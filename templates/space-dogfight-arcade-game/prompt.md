# Space Dogfight Arcade Game

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build [BRAND], a single-page third-person arcade patrol through the bounded asteroid volume [SECTOR]. The default call sign is [CALLSIGN], the only enemies are the [FOE], and the fighter type is [CRAFT]. The first screen is the lit field with the code-built fighter already in a chase view and a dark glass title card beside it, plus a short control legend. The player steers, fires pale ice bolts, holds a lock to send one homing missile, boosts on a cooldown, and rolls for a brief shield shimmer. Numbered waves add a few drones and a little speed, with a short breather between them. The run ends only when the player shield hits zero, on a retry card that names the wave reached. Default audience line: [AUDIENCE].

- Audience: Developers and indie hackers who want a finished static three.js flight game, playable with a keyboard and pointer on a desk and with a thumb stick on a phone, and retitled from variables without changing the flight model.
- Goal: Ship one replayable patrol: a title card over a living asteroid field, a chase camera, boost, roll, bolts, a lock-on missile, a regenerating shield, a circular radar, a wave counter, on-screen flight controls, synthesized tones, and a retry card. A second set of names must keep the same speeds, colors, silhouettes, and HUD.
- Tone: Calm, short, and operational, like a dispatcher. US English. No movie-trailer voice and no slogans from existing games.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Squadron name (default: "Cinder Patrol"). Invented two-word squadron name. Never an existing game, studio, ship, or product.
- [CALLSIGN] — Pilot call sign (default: "Kestrel"). One invented word. Not a known pilot, character, or franchise call sign.
- [SECTOR] — Patrol sector (default: "Harrow Reach"). Invented two-word place name used in the deck and the retry line.
- [FOE] — Drone name (default: "Glass Moths"). A plural invented drone name of two words, so 'Scatter the [FOE]' and 'The [FOE] still hold [SECTOR]' stay grammatical.
- [CRAFT] — Fighter type (default: "Lark"). One invented word for the fighter type. The mesh does not change with this word.
- [AUDIENCE] — Who it is for (default: "A short patrol for night-shift pilots."). One short sentence shown under the deck. Keep the same role in a second naming.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/space-dogfight-arcade-game in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 4 px base. Desktop title card padding 28 px and HUD inset 24 px. Mobile card padding 20 px, HUD inset 16 px, and safe-area padding on the touch row. Buttons are at least 44 px tall. Touch buttons are 64 px. The stick is 116 px. The page itself does not scroll; only the mobile title card scrolls inside itself.
- Radius: Title and retry cards 16 px. Primary and touch buttons 12 px. The radar is a circle. The shield track is 2 px. World meshes stay faceted. The lock reticle is a circle, not a bracket.
- Motion: Variable dt capped at 0.033 seconds. Render once per frame after at most one simulation step.
- Motion: Chase camera smoothing is 1 - exp(-5.5 * dt). Snap the camera on the title pose, on the frame play starts, and on Fly again.
- Motion: A damaging hit shakes the camera for 0.16 seconds at amplitude 0.14. The title fighter yaws at 0.08 rad/s. The lock ring may pulse 5 percent. The shield fill may pulse while below 25.
- Motion: Boost pulls the camera back 1.6 units and shows eight short speed lines. Missile and bolt motion is the game and always stays.
- Motion: With prefers-reduced-motion, cut shake, title yaw, camera pull, speed lines, missile trail, lock pulse, shield pulse, and particle tumble. Use the small particle budget and multiply burst life by 0.35. Snap overlays. Keep steering, firing, lock, waves, and the retry card.
- Imagery: Draw the fighter, drones, asteroids, stars, bolts, missile, bursts, boundary cage, and favicon in code. No photographs, no generated stills, no HDRI, no downloaded spacecraft, and no people. The first-screen anchor is the lit chase view of the fighter among asteroids, with the glass card beside it on desktop and below it on a phone. Hits are tumbling ice and amber squares. There is no fire, smoke, pilot, or blood.
