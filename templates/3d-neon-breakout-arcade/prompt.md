# 3D Neon Breakout Arcade

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build [BRAND], a complete single-page 3D breakout in a gridded night court at [VENUE] in [CITY]. The first screen is the lit arena itself: a cool sky dome, a dark grid floor, the bar in front, and round-one bricks farther back, with a start card beside the view. Five authored rounds change the brick map and ball speed. The player has sparks, points, and three falling pickups: Scatter, Needle, and Girder. Default deck: [TAGLINE].

- Audience: Indie hackers and front-end developers who want a finished static Three.js arcade game, playable with a mouse, arrow keys, and a finger drag, and reskinnable by changing words rather than the court.
- Goal: Ship one replayable five-round cabinet: a start card over a living court, mouse and touch aiming, keyboard nudge, shatter, a hot ball trail, a sci-fi readout, clear and fail cards, pause, synthesized hits, and a local best. A second name set must keep the same maps, speeds, colors, and pickup rules.
- Tone: Dry, specific, and local, like a night attendant talking to a regular. Short US English. No military hull talk, no death slogan, and no padded scoreboard.

## Stack
- Framework: Vite 6 + TypeScript + Three.js
- Styling: Vanilla CSS
- Libraries: three (MIT), @fontsource/syne (OFL), @fontsource/sora (OFL), @fontsource/share-tech-mono (OFL)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Scaffold Vite's vanilla TypeScript template. Scripts: dev=vite, build=tsc && vite build, preview=vite preview. Package name is the slug of BRAND, not a fixed template id. Set vite base to './', build.outDir to dist, build.emptyOutDir to true, and build.sourcemap to false. tsconfig: strict true, noEmit true.
  - Install three at 0.170.0 or newer, plus Vite 6 and TypeScript 5 as dev dependencies, and the three Fontsource packages as runtime dependencies. Do not add React, a physics engine, a game framework, a tween library, an icon package, or a separate postprocessing package.
  - Import Syne weights 700 and 800, Sora weights 400, 500, and 600, and Share Tech Mono weight 400 from those packages. CSS families are Syne, Sora, and Share Tech Mono. Do not load fonts from a CDN.
  - Bloom, desktop only: import EffectComposer, RenderPass, UnrealBloomPass, and OutputPass from three/addons. Strength 0.25, radius 0.32, threshold 0.82. OutputPass is the last pass. Skip the composer under 800px width and after the performance cut below.
  - Use modules src/content.ts, main.ts, arena.ts, levels.ts, sim.ts, hud.ts, audio.ts, and styles.css. Keep every player-facing string in content.ts. Simulation code reads that module and never shows raw tokens.
  - If BRAND, VENUE, CITY, and ATTENDANT are the defaults, use the Glass Pier copy table. If VENUE is Millhouse Rec, use the Millhouse copy table exactly. Any other change to those four rewrites the attendant sentence, the hours line, and the five round names in content.ts to fit the new words. A TAGLINE-only change replaces the deck and keeps the round names. Maps, speeds, colors, coordinates, and pickup cells never change.
  - Persist only the best points integer and the mute flag in localStorage. Key is the slug of BRAND plus -cabinet, so Lumen Dock becomes lumen-dock-cabinet. Slug: lowercase, non-alphanumerics to single hyphens, trim hyphens. No dates. Parse nothing from the network.
  - One index.html, lang=en, viewport with viewport-fit=cover. Document title is BRAND, an em dash, then VENUE. A full-view canvas plus HTML overlays. Body margin 0, overflow hidden, background Ink. No router.

## Design
### Colors
- Ink `#07110F` — Page background, renderer clear, start card, sky zenith, fog
- Court `#0C1714` — Floor plane and readout chips
- Grid `#24584F` — Floor lines and card border
- Horizon `#1C6B62` — Sky-dome horizon
- Teal `#1EC8B8` — Teal bricks, rails, primary button, spark pips, focus ring
- Sea `#7DFFB2` — Sea bricks
- Ice `#9AD7FF` — Ice bricks and chipped lime bricks
- Lime `#E4F27A` — Two-hit bricks before the first chip, Girder pickup, far lip
- Hot `#FF4D1C` — Ball, ball light, center tick, Needle beam, Scatter pickup
- Paper `#E7F3EE` — Headings, HUD numbers, paddle body
- Dim `#8AA39A` — Eyebrow, labels, hours line, credit
- Rail `#12312C` — Side rails and back plate

### Typography
- Cabinet name on the start card: Syne (OFL), weights 700/800
- Body, buttons, and HUD labels: Sora (OFL), weights 400/500/600
- Points, round, and timers: Share Tech Mono (OFL), weights 400

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 4 px base. Desktop card padding 28 px, HUD inset 16 px. Mobile inset 12 px plus safe-area insets. Buttons at least 44 px tall. The start sheet on a phone stays within 46 vh and scrolls inside itself. The center of the court stays clear of chrome during play.
- Radius: Start, pause, clear, fail, and controls cards 12 px with a hard 4 px offset shadow in Grid, no blur. Buttons 8 px. Spark pips are 8 px diamonds. World meshes stay sharp except the ball, the attendant head, and Scatter's small spheres.
- Motion: Fixed step of 1/60 second. Each frame runs at most three steps and drops leftover time. Render with the latest state.
- Motion: On the start card the camera eases 0.04 units on x over 8 seconds. During play the camera x eases toward 0.18 times the paddle x, clamped to 1.1, with smoothing 1 - exp(-4 dt).
- Motion: A brick break offsets the camera by at most 0.045 units for 0.1 seconds, ease out, one shake at a time. The ball trail keeps the last 10 positions spaced at least 0.12 apart.
- Motion: Shatter spawns 14 short-lived boxes. Pickups bob 0.05 units at 1.8 Hz. Overlays fade in 160 ms and do not travel.
- Motion: With prefers-reduced-motion, cut the title drift, camera follow, shake, trail, shatter, pickup bob, and overlay fade. Snap UI in place. Keep the ball, the bar, the beam, and the rounds, because that motion is the game.
- Imagery: Draw the court, sky, bricks, bar, ball, pickups, shatter, and the attendant from original Three.js geometry. No photographs, no generated stills, no HDRIs, no downloaded models, and no brand marks. The lit court is the first-screen anchor, with the start card beside it. The attendant is a featureless figure outside the right rail: a box coat in #C5D2CB, a sphere head in #E6C4A4 with no eyes or mouth, and a Teal cap. The figure is fictional and is not a portrait of any real person. No faces anywhere else.

## Don'ts
- No backend, accounts, database, analytics, external APIs, multiplayer, or a shared leaderboard. The best points integer and the mute flag stay in localStorage under a key built from BRAND.
- No CDN and no remote assets. Self-host fonts, import three from the installed package, use relative paths, and emit minified files to dist/ with source maps off and without copying src/ into dist/. Keep dist under 20 MB.
- The only page is index.html. Do not add a marketing page, a price, or a bare directory link. The first screen is the lit court with the start card beside it, never type on a flat field.
- Do not use the title VOIDBREAK, the clear line LANDED, or the fail line YOU DIED. Fail heading is Out of sparks. Mid-round clear heading is Round clear. The last card heading is the venue plus clear.
- Do not label the readout HULL, LIVES, NOMINAL, CRITICAL, WAVE, BRICKS, or a zero-padded six-digit SCORE. On-screen labels are PTS, RND, BLOCKS, SPARKS, REST, and LIVE. Do not print the control line MOUSE / A D MOVE or SPACE SURGE. Do not bind A or D.
- Do not use these status words: HULL BREACH, HULL CRITICAL, BALL LOST, KEEP LANE, MINE HIT, SAW HIT, IMPACT, SURGE, WIDE, WIDE LOCK, or COMBO. Pickup toasts name Scatter, Needle, and Girder only.
- Do not build EnergyCore, Interceptor, ObjectivePylon, SawDrone, SpikeMine, hull damage, lane hazards, mines, or saws. The court is a bar, balls, bricks, and three pickups.
- Do not copy a one-shot HTML page, a bench prompt, a judge note, a screenshot, or a gameplay video. Do not mention Agent OS, GoldieBench, AI Profit Boardroom, AIPB, Grok, xAI, or Julian Goldie, and do not add a player chrome bar.
- Do not lock the scene to a source palette, and do not use Inter or Courier New. Faces are Syne, Sora, and Share Tech Mono, self-hosted. Colors are the hex values in this spec.
- No real arcade brands, logos, mascots, or celebrity likeness. The attendant has no face and is not a portrait of a real person. No downloaded models, HDRIs, stock photos, or placeholder-image services.
- No shadow maps, no physics engine, and no Math.random. Stars and shatter use mulberry32. Bloom is desktop-only and turns off after the single performance cut.
- Do not hard-code Lumen Dock, Glass Pier, Harborline, Nia Pell, or the default round names outside src/content.ts, and do not leave raw variable tokens on screen.
- Do not invent dates, entry fees, online player counts, or any claim about VibeFather. The only VibeFather presence is the optional [CREDIT] link.
- Honor prefers-reduced-motion by removing shake, shatter, trail, bob, and camera drift without stopping the round. At 390×844 the sheet, readout, and drag control must be usable with no horizontal overflow and no console errors.
- If WebGL fails, show the readable fallback sentence and do not throw in a loop. Freeze the round while paused, hidden, or while a card is open.
