# Mars Colony Builder Game

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build [BRAND], a single-screen turn-based colony game in [CRATER], founded by [EXPEDITION]. The player places habitats, solar arrays, greenhouses, and ice drills on a code-drawn isometric crater, then advances one sol at a time. Oxygen, power, water, and morale tick together. Dust storms last four sols and cut solar yield. Colonists increase only when beds and all four stocks allow it, up to 50. The grid and stocks persist in localStorage. Default tagline: [TAGLINE]. The crater, one habitat, and a dust haze are already on screen between glass columns that hold the title and the HUD.

- Audience: People who enjoy short planning puzzles, including [AUDIENCE], plus indie hackers and front-end developers who want a self-contained canvas game with keyboard and touch controls. The help text stays short, the first storm is on a known sol, and a failed sol can be retried.
- Goal: Ship one complete loop: a title column over a live crater, one structure change per sol, a preview that uses the same tick as Advance sol, storms, growth to 50, a win overlay, a failed-expedition overlay, resume from localStorage, and equivalent play at 1440x900 and 390x844.
- Tone: Calm, practical, and a little dry, like a field log from a small survey. Short US English, concrete yields, no exclamation marks, no epic colonization slogans, and no jokes about abandoning anyone on a planet.

Desktop at 1100px and wider uses two 320px glass columns over a full-bleed canvas, with 20px column padding and 24px top and bottom crater insets. Below 1100px, including 390px, the canvas is the top region and a bottom sheet holds the HUD, with 12px sheet padding, 48px button heights, and safe-area insets. - Radius: 12px glass panels, 14px dialogs, 10px buttons, 999px forecast chips and stock tracks. The canvas is square-cornered and full bleed. World shapes are diamonds, domes, and masts, not rounded app icons.
- Motion: Dust is the only continuous motion: 32 seeded particles drift right at 0.6 CSS pixels per frame and wrap the crater bounds. A storm raises the count to 64 and adds a flat Dust veil at 0.18 alpha.
- Motion: When motion is allowed, stock fills ease width over 160ms. Overlays fade opacity over 140ms with no travel greater than 8px. Buttons do not bounce.
- Motion: With prefers-reduced-motion, draw no particles, snap stock fills, use instant overlays, and keep a storm as the flat Dust veil only. Placement, removal, and sol resolution stay immediate.
- Motion: Stop the animation frame while the tab is hidden. Do not shake the canvas, flash the veil, or autoplay sound.
- Imagery: Draw the crater, rim, boulders, four structure types, selection, dust, storm veil, favicon, and build-bar glyphs in canvas and inline SVG. No photographs, generated images, downloaded textures, or sprites. The first screen's anchor is the centered habitat dome on the rust basin, with the glass HUD already readable around it.

2. **Isometric crater grid** — The grid is 12 by 12, columns c and rows r from 0 through 11. Column increases east and row increases south. For each cell, d2 = (c * 2 - 11) squared plus (r * 2 - 11) squared. If d2 is greater than 128 the cell is void and is not drawn. If d2 is greater than 84 it is rim and cannot be built on. Otherwise it is floor, except the boulder cells (2,4), (8,3), (4,9), (9,7), (3,7), and (7,8), which are blocked floor. That is 62 buildable floor cells, 6 boulders, and 28 rim cells. A correct terrain function must match those counts. The new game places one habitat at (5,6) and nothing else. Tile width is 72 and tile height is 36 before fit. Screen position before offset is x = (c - r) * 36, y = (c + r) * 18. Draw non-void cells in ascending (c + r) order. Floor fill cycles by (c * 3 + r) modulo 3 through Regolith, Regolith mid, and Regolith dark, with a 1px stroke at rgba(62,32,24,0.45). Rim uses Rim for the top diamond and a south face 7px lower in pre-scale pixels filled Rim face. A boulder draws the floor diamond, then three circles around the tile center: radius 10 at (-6,-4) in #6A3E30, radius 8 at (7,-1) in Rim, radius 7 at (0,6) in #4A2C22, plus a Dust highlight on the first circle. The canvas behind the grid is a vertical gradient from #2A1A14 to Basalt and a wide ellipse of #6E3B2C at 20 percent alpha. Fit the bounding box of non-void diamonds into the playable canvas rect with 16px padding, uniform scale capped at 1.35, centered. At 1100px and wider the rect is the viewport inset 320px on the left and right and 24px on the top and bottom. Below that, the rect is the canvas element.
   Layout: One canvas element fills the viewport and sits under the HTML HUD. Set its bitmap size to the CSS size times the capped device pixel ratio and scale the context. Rebuild the fit on resize. Hit testing inverts the fit: fx = x / 36, fy = y / 18, c = round((fx + fy) / 2), r = round((fy - fx) / 2). Ignore void. The status line reports the cell under the pointer or the keyboard cursor.
   Motion: The grid itself does not animate. Only dust, the storm veil, and the selection stroke change between paints.
3. **Four structures and adjacency** — Each structure occupies one floor cell. Orthogonal neighbors count. Diagonals, rim, void, and boulders do not. Shade on a solar is the number of orthogonal habitats and greenhouses. Clear solar yield is max(2, 10 - 3 * shade). Storm solar yield is max(1, 5 - 2 * shade). An open array is 10 clear and 5 in dust. One tall neighbor makes 7 and 3. Two make 4 and 1. Three or four stay at the minimum, 2 and 1. A greenhouse makes 16 oxygen if at least one orthogonal neighbor is a habitat, otherwise 8. It always costs 4 power, 2 water, and gives 2 morale when the grid is powered. A drill makes 20 water, costs 3 power, and costs 1 morale plus 2 more for every orthogonal habitat, when powered. A habitat sleeps 8, costs 2 power and 1 water, and gives 1 morale. Drills do not shade solars. Draw at tile-local pixels for a 72-wide tile, then multiply by the fit scale. Habitat: skirt ellipse (0,6, rx 20, ry 8) in Copper deep, dome (0,-2, rx 18, ry 12) in Copper, door rect (-3,2, 6 by 8) in Rim face, window (-6,-6, 12 by 3) in Parchment. Solar: mast from (0,4) to (0,-14) in Clay, panel through (-16,-18), (10,-18), (18,-8), (-8,-8) filled Array cell and stroked Copper. Greenhouse: base ellipse (0,6, rx 18, ry 7) in Rim face, vault ellipse (0,-2, rx 16, ry 11) in Sage at 85 percent with an Array cell stroke, pane lines at x -5 and 5. Drill: base ellipse (0,8, rx 14, ry 6) in Rim, tower rect (-4,-16, 8 by 24) in Clay, collar (-6,-6, 12 by 4) in Copper, bore ellipse (0,-16, rx 4, ry 3) in Ice. A pending placement draws at 50 percent alpha. The selected cell is a 2px Copper diamond. An illegal cell is a 2px Warning diamond and does not spend the action.
   Layout: Build-bar glyphs are inline SVG in the same silhouettes and hexes, 28px, not Lucide pictures of unrelated objects. The HUD status line uses the keyboard cursor or the hovered cell. Examples: Column 5, row 6 · Habitat. Column 2, row 4 · Boulder. Open floor · Solar yield 10 clear / 5 dust. Rim and void never show a yield.
   Motion: Ghosts and selection have no pulse. Reduced motion does not change the drawings.
4. **Sol tick, storms, growth, win and fail** — Keep all of this in sim.ts and call it for both the preview and Advance sol. Stocks are integers from 0 to 100. New game: sol 1, population 6, oxygen 74, power 46, water 64, morale 68, one habitat at (5,6). A storm is active on sols 8 through 11, 18 through 21, 30 through 33, 44 through 47, 60 through 63, and 78 through 81, then on every run of four sols that starts at 96, 114, 132, and so on, stepping by 18. Supply is the sum of storm yields during a storm and clear yields otherwise. Demand is habitats * 2 + greenhouses * 4 + drills * 3, counted after the pending action. The grid is powered when power + supply is at least demand. If powered, power changes by supply minus demand, water by drill water minus habitat and greenhouse water minus population, oxygen by greenhouse oxygen minus population, and morale by habitat and greenhouse morale minus drill penalties. If not powered, greenhouses and drills idle: they add nothing and cost nothing that sol, power changes by supply minus habitats * 2, water by minus habitats minus population, oxygen by minus population, and morale by habitats minus 6. Then, using population before growth and housing after the action, add 2 morale when population is less than or equal to housing - 8, subtract 4 when population equals housing and housing is positive, and subtract 8 during a storm. Clamp each stock to 0 through 100. If any stock is 0, the tick fails and population does not grow. Otherwise, if the grid was powered and every stock is at least 35 and population is below both housing and 50, population grows by min(2, housing - population, 50 - population). Sol then increases by 1. A failed tick is not committed. The fail dialog shows the clamped outcome and the sol that was just attempted. Retry and Leave discard it. Retry also clears the pending action and returns to the start-of-sol snapshot. Leave deletes the save and returns to the title on the new-game crater. A successful tick commits, replaces the snapshot, clears the pending action, and saves. If population is now at least 50 and the win has not been dismissed this run, open the win dialog. Further sols do not open it again. Required checks for a new game: passing sol 1 lands on oxygen 68, power 44, water 57, morale 69, population 8, sol 2. Placing an unshaded solar and advancing lands on oxygen 68, power 54, water 57, morale 69, population 8, sol 2. A solar orthogonal to the starting habitat, such as (6,6), yields 7 clear, so power lands on 51 with the same other results.
   Layout: The preview row reads O2 74 to 68, PWR 46 to 54, the matching water and morale figures, and population 6 to 8, using the pending action. An unshaded solar shows power 46 to 54. A pass shows power 46 to 44. Numbers that will fall use Clay; a result under 25 uses Warning and the suffix LOW; a result of 0 turns Advance sol into Advance anyway in Warning. The button still works. Do not duplicate the formulas inside the component.
   Motion: Resolution is a single state change plus the stock ease. No sol-by-sol animation and no automatic extra ticks.

## Don'ts
- Do not add a backend, server routes, accounts, a database, analytics, a leaderboard, multiplayer, or any network call. The only persistence is the colony save and the sound flag in localStorage.
- Do not change the stack. No Unity, Godot, Phaser, three.js, Pixi, a physics engine, GSAP, Motion, or a second page. The build is Vite 5, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 3.4, and one canvas.
- Ship static files in dist/ only. Vite base is './', links use file names, assets are relative, the build is minified, source maps are off, src is not copied, and dist stays under 20 MB.
- Do not load scripts, fonts, icons, images, audio, or tiles from a CDN. Do not hotlink or download Mars photography, maps, or textures. The crater, structures, and storm are drawn in code.
