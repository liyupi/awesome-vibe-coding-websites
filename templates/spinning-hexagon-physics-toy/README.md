# Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy

> A neon-pop physics playground with twenty candy-colored balls, moving walls and hands-on controls.

This interactive physics toy turns a simple rotating hexagon into a small, colorful playground. Twenty glossy numbered balls tumble, collide and spin against a moving boundary. Gravity, friction, rotation and ball count can all be changed while the scene runs, so visitors can discover how each setting changes the motion. A shake button adds a fresh burst of energy, and slow motion makes the smaller contacts easier to follow.

The visual direction is deliberately bold. Deep indigo surrounds large hot-pink and tangerine shapes, a cyan rim frames the arena, and lime gives the tuning panel a clear place in the composition. The balls repeat five saturated candy colors, with readable number discs and small highlights drawn directly in canvas. Contact confetti stays tiny and brief. Rounded display lettering gives the page a playful voice, while ordinary HTML controls keep it usable with a keyboard or a phone.

The layout suits a creative studio's break room, a browser-toy collection, an interactive portfolio experiment or an educator's forces workshop. Desktop visitors see the arena beside its controls. On a narrow screen, the arena comes first and the tuning panel expands below it. Reduced-motion visitors start with a still scene and choose when to begin.

The prompt defines the moving-wall and ball-contact calculations, not just their appearance. It also specifies stable time steps, visible ball rotation, responsive drawing and working preset recipes. Change the invented project name, audience, headline and experiment theme to give the same design a new purpose. The alternate case uses classroom language to explore forces, showing how the playful visual system can carry a different story without changing its color identity or underlying physics.

## FAQ

### What can visitors actually change on the Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy page?
Visitors to the Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy can adjust gravity, friction, signed wall rotation, ball count and simulation speed while the scene runs, and they can press a Shake it up button for a fresh burst of energy or switch on slow motion. A How it moves disclosure explains the controls, and the tuning lab sits beside the arena on desktop and below it on a phone.

### How do I rewrite the Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy for a classroom lesson on forces?
Set the variables in the Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy: BRAND to your school or program, TOY_NAME to the playground name students will see, HEADLINE to a short invitation, AUDIENCE to the class, and EXPERIMENT_THEME to forces and motion. The copy lives in one content file, so you rewrite the introduction and preset explanations without touching the arena geometry or the neon palette.

The Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy prompt keeps every English string in one content file, so new values flow into the header, footer, document title, arena label and preset descriptions together. ### How does the Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy handle reduced motion and keyboard use?
The Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy respects prefers-reduced-motion by starting in a polished still state with a Start motion button, and confetti and decorative transitions stay off until the visitor chooses to begin. All controls are ordinary HTML buttons, range inputs and outputs with cyan focus rings, so the toy works with a keyboard as well as a pointer, and Pause and Resume are always available.
