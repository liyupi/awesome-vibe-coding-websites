# Spinning Hexagon Physics Toy

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build [BRAND], an original single-page physics playground called [TOY_NAME]. Twenty glossy numbered balls in hot pink, tangerine, lime, cyan and violet move inside a real rotating hexagon on mandatory deep indigo #1B1464. The first screen is the live toy, a concise invitation and a compact tuning lab, with every contact, ball spin and control implemented rather than staged.
