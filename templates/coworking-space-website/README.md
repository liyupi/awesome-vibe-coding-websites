A single-page website for a coworking space: a hero with the space's promise over a wide lounge photograph and desks-from pricing; the centerpiece — an interactive floor map drawn as clean architectural linework, where hot desks, dedicated desks and private rooms are hoverable (availability tooltips) and clickable (selecting a spot for the tour); three membership plans with monthly and day-pass pricing; an amenities grid; four community events (weekday-named, no dates) with client-side RSVP; an honest 'sounds like' strip answering the noise question; and a tour booking form that carries the map selection through to a client-side confirmation. The default content follows an invented 40-desk space in a converted print works, but the structure suits any shared workspace: change the name, city, desk count and price base and every plan, zone and event still reads true. Availability on the map is clearly-labeled sample state, seeded locally. The look is warm workplace modern: off-white with walnut ink and one coral accent, Sora headlines over Albert Sans, generous cards and calm reveals. Photography shows desks, lounges and booths with no people. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[SPACE]` | Space name | The Print Works |
| `[CITY]` | City | Philadelphia, Pennsylvania |
| `[DESKS]` | Desk count | 40 |
| `[VIBE]` | Vibe phrase | warm, plant-heavy and quietly busy |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add coworking-space-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A design studio collective: set SPACE to your studio name, raise DESKS to your real capacity and rewrite the plans around resident desks and shared meeting rooms.
- A neighborhood cafe with work tables: keep the map and tour form, swap the amenities grid for coffee, Wi-Fi and quiet-hours details, and rename the plans to table passes.
- A small-town shared office: set CITY to your town, lower DESKS, and rewrite the events section around local meetups and skill swaps for remote workers.
- A freelance-friendly workspace: adjust VIBE to your atmosphere, keep the noise-honesty strip and rewrite the tour form's reassurance to match how your space actually sounds.

## FAQ

### How does the interactive floor map on the Coworking Space Website template work?
The Coworking Space Website template draws its floor map as architectural linework, with hot desks, dedicated desks and private rooms as separate zones. Hovering a zone shows an availability tooltip such as how many desks are free, and clicking selects that spot. The selection carries through to the tour booking form, so the visitor's chosen zone is part of the tour request. Availability is clearly labeled sample state seeded locally, so you replace it with your real desk counts.

### What membership plans and pricing can I show with the Coworking Space Website template?
The Coworking Space Website template includes three membership plans with monthly and day-pass pricing, plus a featured card that lifts permanently to draw the eye. Plan names, prices and availability lines are all editable content, so you can set a hot desk plan, a dedicated desk plan and a private room plan for your own space. The hero also shows a desks-from price, which you set alongside the desk count.

### Can I change the desk count, city and space name in the Coworking Space Website template?
Yes. The Coworking Space Website template is driven by four variables: SPACE for the space name, CITY for the location, DESKS for the total desk count and VIBE for a short phrase describing the atmosphere. Changing DESKS updates the hero strip, the map's zone capacities and the plans' availability lines. The default content follows an invented 40-desk space in a converted print works, but the structure suits any shared workspace.

### Does the Coworking Space Website template include event RSVP and a tour booking form?
The Coworking Space Website template includes four community events with client-side RSVP, where clicking toggles a button to Going and updates the count. The tour booking form lets a visitor pick a day, enter their details and confirm, and it carries the floor map selection through to a client-side confirmation. Everything runs in the browser and nothing is submitted anywhere, so you connect the form to your own booking or mailing tool.
