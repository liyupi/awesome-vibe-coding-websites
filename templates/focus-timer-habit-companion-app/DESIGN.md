# Focus Timer & Habit Companion App — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Warm Stone** `#F5F3EF` — Serene daylight app canvas
- **Paper White** `#FFFFFF` — Elevated timer, mixer, and habit cards
- **Deep Charcoal** `#1C1917` — Primary display numerals, headings, and dark-mode background
- **Terracotta** `#D95D39` — Active Focus ring arc, primary Play CTA, and streak highlights
- **Calm Sage** `#4D7C6F` — Break timer mode, completed habit checks, and weekly chart bars
- **Muted Pebble** `#78716C` — Secondary labels, durations, and reflection timestamps

## Typography
- **Warm editorial headings** — Fraunces (OFL), weights 600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Habits, journal reflections, and UI controls** — Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Countdown clock, durations, and focus totals** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Mobile (<768px) uses comfortable 16px horizontal padding with 84px clearance above the fixed 64px tab bar. Desktop (>=1024px) renders a full-height 236px left rail + balanced 2-column focus & habits/telemetry workspace.
- Radius: 20px for timer studio card, 14px for habit and mixer cards, 999px for mode pills and task chips.

## Motion
- Smooth SVG circular countdown arc sweeping counterclockwise as seconds tick.
- Tactile sage check-off transition on daily micro-habits.
- Honors prefers-reduced-motion; all content visible immediately.

## Imagery
100% crisp code-drawn calm visuals: animated SVG circular countdown ring, ambient sound channel meters, weekly focus-minutes SVG bar chart, 28-day sage consistency heatmap, and Lucide icons.

## Tone
Serene, grounded, organic warm minimalism, zero hustle-culture noise.
