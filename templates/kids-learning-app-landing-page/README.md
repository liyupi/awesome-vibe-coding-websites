# Kids Learning App Landing Page

> A bright clay learning app page with playful lessons, age-aware paths, and a useful parent view.

Build a parent-facing learning app page with a world of soft clay shapes and practical product previews. A lilac canvas carries large bubblegum-pink characters, a sky-blue phone, butter-yellow details, and grass-green learning cards. The color appears in the main objects and section surfaces, giving the page a clear identity before a visitor touches a button. Rounded display type, readable dark text, and hand-shaped shadows keep the mood playful without making the page difficult to scan.

The phone is a working interface built in CSS, with separate reading and math lessons, selectable answers, gentle feedback, and a next activity. An age picker changes the lesson difficulty and the learning-path content for three age bands. Word tiles reveal sound breakdowns, with optional device read-aloud when a local English voice is available. Parents can explore an illustrative activity dashboard, change its sample period, and see a specific suggested next step for the selected age.

The lower page explains the reading and number practice, introduces simple safety choices, and compares a starter option with a family plan. Monthly and yearly pricing update together. Plan buttons open a grown-up routine form that creates a local sample schedule without asking for a child’s name, birthday, email, or photograph. The page includes a keyboard-friendly mobile menu, accessible controls, concise FAQs, and reduced-motion behavior.

Use it for an early learning product, a family activity subscription, or a language and number practice app. Replace the brand, audience, lesson focus, story world, session length, and price while keeping the layout and clay assets. A second example shifts the lessons from a tiny garden to a floating workshop, showing how the same companions and visual system support different educational content.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | App brand | Mallowpath |
| `[APP_FOCUS]` | What the app practices | Phonics, first words, and everyday math |
| `[AUDIENCE]` | Families served | Curious kids ages 3–8 and their grown-ups |
| `[STORY_WORLD]` | Lesson setting | A tiny garden of sunbursts, sprouts, and picnic discoveries |
| `[SESSION_MINUTES]` | Suggested session length in minutes | 10 |
| `[MONTHLY_PRICE]` | Family plan monthly price in USD | 8 |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add kids-learning-app-landing-page` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A language practice app: set BRAND to your app name, point APP_FOCUS at bilingual words, and rewrite the word tiles and lesson answers for your language pair.
- A preschool or nursery: set AUDIENCE to your enrolled families, swap STORY_WORLD for your classroom theme, and keep the three age bands for your rooms.
- A family activity subscription: set APP_FOCUS to offline crafts and outings, rewrite the learning path as weekly activities, and set SESSION_MINUTES to your box length.
- An after-school tutoring service: set BRAND to your centre, rewrite the lesson preview around your curriculum, and use the parent dashboard to show a sample progress view.

## FAQ

### How do I change the lessons and story world in the Kids Learning App Landing Page?
The Kids Learning App Landing Page keeps every lesson, question, answer set and word tile in one content file, so you rewrite the reading and math activities to match your own STORY_WORLD and APP_FOCUS. Keep one reading activity and one math activity for each of the three age bands (3–4, 5–6, 7–8) so the age picker still changes difficulty and the learning path.

### What does the phone preview in the Kids Learning App Landing Page actually do?
The phone in the Kids Learning App Landing Page is a working CSS interface, not a screenshot. Visitors pick an age band, switch between reading and math lessons, select answers, get gentle feedback and move to a next activity. Word tiles reveal sound breakdowns, with optional read-aloud through the device voice when a local English voice is available.

### How do I set the price and session length for a kids learning app landing page?
Set MONTHLY_PRICE to your whole-dollar family plan price and SESSION_MINUTES to a value between 6 and 15. The Kids Learning App Landing Page derives every displayed amount from the monthly price, with the yearly total at ten times that value, and builds the routine form and session copy from the session length, splitting reading and math minutes inside it.

### What can parents see in the dashboard section of the Kids Learning App Landing Page?
The Kids Learning App Landing Page includes a sample parent dashboard with an activity view, a changeable sample period and a suggested next step that follows the selected age band. It is an illustrative preview with local sample data, so it shows what a parent view could look like without collecting a child's name, birthday, email or photograph.

### Do I need to collect children's data to use the routine form?
No. The grown-up routine form in the Kids Learning App Landing Page creates a local sample schedule and asks for nothing about a child. It runs entirely in the browser with no storage, accounts or form submission, so you can show a working family routine on a static site without any backend or privacy setup.

### How do I build a kids learning app landing page with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add kids-learning-app-landing-page, replace [BRAND], [APP_FOCUS], [AUDIENCE] and [STORY_WORLD] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
