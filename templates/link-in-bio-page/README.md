A single-page, mobile-first link hub for a creator: a ringed avatar and one-line bio, four big link buttons built for thumbs, the latest video card with a drawn play badge, two product mini-cards with prices, a social row of drawn generic glyphs and an email capture that saves locally and says so. The default content follows an invented ceramicist and slow-living creator, but the page suits anyone with a phone audience — streamers, bakers, musicians, coaches: change the creator, tagline, links and products and every block still reads true. The layout lives in a 420px column that centers on desktop with a soft page backdrop; every tap target is at least 52px tall; buttons bounce gently on press and the email form validates inline. The look is warm creator minimal: cream canvas, charcoal ink, one coral accent, rounded-friendly Baloo 2 display over Karla body — wait, the body is Figtree — and an illustrated monogram avatar rather than a photo, so no face is needed anywhere. Socials are drawn generic glyphs, never real platform marks. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file; links, products and video all read from it.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[CREATOR]` | Creator name | Hearth & Hallow |
| `[TAGLINE]` | One-line bio | Small-batch ceramics, big-batch calm |
| `[NICHE]` | Niche | slow pottery and studio life |
| `[SHOP_HINT]` | Shop hint | the mug shop |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add link-in-bio-page` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A streamer: set CREATOR to your handle, TAGLINE to your schedule line, and point the four link buttons at your stream, clips, merch and Discord.
- A home baker: set NICHE to sourdough and cakes, rename the product minis to your bakes with prices, and swap the video card for your latest bake reel.
- A session musician: set CREATOR to your name, TAGLINE to your instrument and city, and use the link buttons for bookings, latest release and lessons.
- A ceramics teacher: keep the studio tone, set NICHE to wheel-throwing classes, and turn the email capture pitch into a waitlist for your next course.

## FAQ

### What do I need to change to make the Link in Bio Page my own?
The Link in Bio Page template is driven by three variables: CREATOR, TAGLINE and NICHE. Set CREATOR to your handle or brand, TAGLINE to your one-line bio, and NICHE to the phrase your content is about, then rewrite the copy in the single content file so the link titles, video card, product minis and email pitch match your voice. The layout, colors and motion stay as they are.

### Can I use the Link in Bio Page without a photo of myself?
Yes. The Link in Bio Page template uses an illustrated monogram circle built from your creator name rather than a portrait, so no face is needed anywhere. The avatar sits inside a coral ring with a presence dot, and the monogram also acts as the loading fallback if you later add an image, so the page reads complete from the first second.

### How does the email capture on the Link in Bio Page work?
The Link in Bio Page email capture validates the address inline, shakes the field once with a short hint if it is malformed, and on success swaps the form for a Coral Mist panel with a drawn tick and a line to use a different address. Nothing is submitted anywhere: the address is saved in the visitor's browser only, and the page says so.

### Are the social icons on the Link in Bio Page real platform logos?
No. The Link in Bio Page template draws generic glyphs for the social row instead of using real platform marks, so you avoid brand assets entirely. Each glyph is a button with an accessible label, lifts and rotates slightly on hover, and you point the labels and links at whichever profiles you actually run.
