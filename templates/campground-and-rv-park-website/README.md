# Campground and RV Park Website

> A campground site with an interactive map, date availability, trails, and reservation requests.

Campground and RV Park Website is a single-page outdoor hospitality template for an invented woodland park. It suits independent campground owners, RV resorts, cabin operators, and small rural hospitality teams that need to explain both the feeling of a stay and the practical details behind it. The page moves from a scenic photographic hero and quick date search to accommodation choices, an interactive site map, amenities, nearby trails, arrival guidance, park rules, and a focused reservation request.

The visual system uses warm oat, deep spruce, moss, clay, and muted gold with expressive serif headlines and clear interface typography. Six generated photographs form one consistent Pacific Northwest-inspired shoot, covering the park, tent sites, RV pads, cabins, shared spaces, and trails. An original inline SVG map provides the main interactive anchor without external map tiles or a mapping service.

Twelve individual sites have their own capacity, features, sample nightly rate, and blocked date ranges. Visitors can compare tent, RV, and cabin options, filter by dates and useful features, select a site on the map or in the accessible list, review an estimated stay total, and prepare a reservation request. The finished request can be copied or downloaded on device; nothing is transmitted, no site is held, and no payment is collected.

Brand, park type, location, hero line, audience, and contact email are variables. All accommodation records, availability, amenities, trail details, rules, prices, and contact copy live in one content file. Adapt them for another woodland campground, lakeside RV resort, or small cabin park while retaining the same layout and interactions.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |

| `[PARK_TYPE]` | Park description | woodland campground and RV park |
| `[LOCATION]` | Park location | Fern Hollow, Oregon |
| `[TAGLINE]` | Hero headline | Find your patch of quiet. |
| `[AUDIENCE]` | Primary guests | weekend campers, RV travelers, and families planning an easy cabin stay |
| `[CONTACT_EMAIL]` | Reservation email | stay@mossandmeadow.example |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add campground-and-rv-park-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A lakeside RV resort: set PARK_TYPE and LOCATION to your shoreline, swap the tent and cabin records for full-hookup pads, and redraw the SVG map around the water.
- A small cabin rental park: keep the cabin stays, replace the trail section with local walks, and point the reservation request at your own booking inbox.
- A family-run woodland campground: set BRAND and TAGLINE to your park, keep the campfire and trail photography, and rewrite the park rules for your quiet hours.
- A campground group with several parks: duplicate the content file per property, adjust each SVG site map, and use CONTACT_EMAIL to route requests to the right host.

## FAQ

### How does the campground site map and date availability actually work?
The Campground and RV Park Website template draws an original inline SVG park diagram rather than loading map tiles or a mapping service. Twelve sites each carry capacity, features, a sample nightly rate and blocked date ranges. Visitors enter arrival and departure dates, filter by accommodation type and features, pick a site on the map or the accessible list, and see an estimated stay total before preparing a request.

### Does the reservation form send bookings or take payment?
No. The Campground and RV Park Website template builds a reservation request entirely on the visitor's device. The finished request can be copied or downloaded, but nothing is transmitted, no site is held and no payment is collected. Park owners who want live bookings would connect the request to their own booking system or inbox after adapting the template.

### What can I change with the BRAND, PARK_TYPE and LOCATION variables?
Setting BRAND, PARK_TYPE, LOCATION, TAGLINE, AUDIENCE and CONTACT_EMAIL rewrites the wordmark, eyebrow, hero headline, introduction, footer and contact copy across the page. The Campground and RV Park Website template keeps all accommodation records, amenities, trails, rules and prices in one content file, so you change the park's details there without touching the layout.

The Campground and RV Park Website template fits independent campground owners, RV resorts, cabin operators and small rural hospitality teams.
