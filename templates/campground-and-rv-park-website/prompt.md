# Campground and RV Park Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build a polished single-page website for [BRAND], a [PARK_TYPE] in [LOCATION]. Lead with the line '[TAGLINE]' and a scenic park photograph, then let visitors compare tent, RV, and cabin stays, inspect an original SVG site map, filter sample availability by dates and features, and prepare a client-side reservation request. Write for [AUDIENCE] and use [CONTACT_EMAIL] as the fictional contact.

- Audience: The primary visitors are [AUDIENCE]. The template is also for independent owners and small hospitality teams who need an adaptable site that answers stay, arrival, amenity, trail, and policy questions clearly.
- Goal: Help a visitor choose the right kind of stay, understand the park before arrival, find an available sample site for specific dates, and leave with a complete reservation request they can copy or download without a backend.
- Tone: Warm, grounded, practical, and quietly confident. Use concrete details about sites, hookups, trails, and routines rather than vague escape language, urgency, superlatives, or unverified hospitality claims.

## Stack
- Framework: Vite 5 + React 18 + TypeScript
- Styling: Tailwind CSS 3.4
- Libraries: motion (MIT), lucide-react (ISC), @fontsource-variable/fraunces (OFL), @fontsource-variable/inter (OFL)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Set vite.config.ts to use React, base: './', build.outDir: 'dist', and build.sourcemap: false.
  - Configure tailwind.config.ts with content for index.html and src/**/*.{ts,tsx}; define the listed colors, spacing, and font families as theme tokens.
  - Import the self-hosted font packages in src/main.tsx and the Tailwind directives plus small global focus and reduced-motion rules in src/index.css.
  - Put every headline, paragraph, label, site record, blocked range, amenity, trail, rule, FAQ, contact detail, link, and image path in src/content.ts keyed by section; derive variable-dependent copy and the brand-derived contact details from the supplied values.
  - Build small section components and keep quick-search, map-filter, selected-site, accordion, mobile-menu, reservation-drawer, and request-confirmation state in React; use Motion only for the specified reveals and transitions.
  - Represent each blocked range as non-negative arrival and departure day offsets from the visitor's current local date, with departure treated as checkout-exclusive; default the quick search to arrival in 14 days and departure in 17 days, use native date inputs, and format dates in US English without an external date API.
  - Use an original inline SVG viewBox='0 0 960 600' for the park diagram, with every site geometry and label defined in content.ts; do not add a map library, map tiles, geocoding, or real geographic claims.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: Use an 8px base grid, a 1320px maximum content width, 40px desktop gutters, and 20px mobile gutters. Sections have 104px vertical padding on desktop and 68px on mobile. Use 12-column desktop grids with 24px gaps, 16px form-field gaps, and 56–72px between major headings and content. Keep body measure near 68 characters.
- Radius: Use 18px corners on photographs and major cards, 12px on the map and reservation panels, 8px on inputs and site buttons, and fully rounded shapes only for small filter chips and status labels. Avoid nested rounded cards, large floating bubbles, and heavy shadows.
- Motion: On first load, fade the hero copy upward by 18px over 600ms while the photograph settles from scale 1.035 to 1 over 1000ms; stagger the eyebrow, headline, paragraph, and action by 70ms.
- Motion: Reveal section headings and photographic cards with a 20px rise and opacity transition over 500ms when they enter the viewport, once only, with no scroll hijacking.
- Motion: Give map sites, filter controls, and stay-card actions a 160ms fill, border, or color response; update result counts and availability immediately without animated number ticking.
- Motion: Open the reservation drawer with a 240ms opacity and 24px horizontal transition, trap focus, close on Escape and backdrop click, and return focus to the triggering control.
- Motion: Expand accordions over 200ms with height and opacity, and use smooth anchor scrolling only when reduced motion is not requested.
- Motion: Disable nonessential transforms, staggered reveals, smooth scrolling, and drawer movement under prefers-reduced-motion; never delay form errors, site details, or availability text.
- Imagery: Use the six supplied generated images as one coherent editorial outdoor shoot: a wide park hero, one photograph for each accommodation type, a communal campfire area, and a woodland trail. They share warm late-afternoon light, tall pines, natural materials, deep green and honey tones, and an unposed sense of space. Crop with object-fit: cover and keep the hero scene spacious. Add the original SVG site map and small code-drawn trail elevation profiles as informational graphics. Do not add recognizable people, vehicle or equipment brands, readable signs, logos, or identifiable landmarks.

## Don'ts
- Do not add a backend, server code, API routes, authentication, database, third-party API, external map service, analytics, payment collection, or real form transmission; the reservation request remains on device and is explicitly labeled as a demo.
- Do not hotlink images, fonts, icons, scripts, styles, maps, or any other runtime asset. Download the six supplied generated images into public/images and serve everything locally with relative paths.
- Do not use Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Google Images, CC BY-NC or CC BY-SA assets, placeholder-image services, real campground photography, or images with readable signs, logos, recognizable people, branded vehicles or equipment, or identifiable landmarks.
- Do not use real campground names, logos, slogans, mascots, site maps, testimonials, guest identities, reservation records, or branded palettes. The default and alternate parks, the SVG geography, and all site and trail data must remain invented.
- Do not copy website code, exact copy, signature illustrations, protected trail artwork, custom fonts, unique iconography, or restricted component source; implement the visual system and interactions from scratch with the listed permissive packages.
- Do not load fonts from Google Fonts or another CDN and do not use commercial typefaces; self-host only the specified OFL Fontsource packages.
- Do not hard-code variable-dependent copy inside components. Keep all copy, prices, policies, inventory, links, image paths, and contact details in src/content.ts and rewrite them coherently when variable values change, including the alternate park type, location, audience, and email.
- Do not present sample rates, availability, amenities, trail conditions, policies, or the reservation flow as verified live services; retain the fictional-park and sample-content notes.
- Do not collect identity documents, payment details, precise home addresses, or other sensitive guest information. Do not persist personal form fields, and never show a success message claiming a request was sent, a site was held, a gate code was issued, or a booking was confirmed.
- Do not use bare-directory links, client-side routes that require a server fallback, external navigation for core sections, random availability, or an empty state without a reset. Use ./index.html anchor links and deterministic local offset-based availability.
- Do not make the tiny SVG sites the only way to select a stay. Provide keyboard-operable SVG controls, an equivalent accessible list, visible focus, useful empty states, and mobile layouts without horizontal scrolling.
- Do not add scroll hijacking, custom cursors, autoplay media, heavy 3D, continuous parallax, animated availability numbers, or animation that ignores prefers-reduced-motion.
- Do not ship source maps, src/ files, remote dependencies, console errors, or a dist/ over 20 MB. The production site must work at 1440×900 and 390×844 with keyboard navigation, accessible dialog semantics, sufficient contrast, and zero console errors.
