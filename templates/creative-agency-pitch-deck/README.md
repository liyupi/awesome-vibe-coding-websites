Built for independent brand studios, creative directors, and design consultancies pitching flagship hospitality or lifestyle rebrands, this 12-slide presentation deck brings print-magazine poise into an interactive browser showcase. Asymmetric editorial grids pair oversized display serif typography with disciplined grotesque body copy over a warm alabaster canvas grounded by deep charcoal ink, terracotta accents, and quiet olive highlights. Rather than dropping mismatched stock thumbnails onto generic white slides, the deck uses six cohesive studio photographs from one warm Mediterranean architectural shoot across its cover, full-bleed campaign moodboards, case study spotlights, and studio culture slide. Three dedicated visual case study slides let prospective clients toggle live between a dated Before visual direction and the reimagined After editorial art direction, surfacing concrete typography, color, and conversion shifts right inside the presentation. Additional slides map the studio's credentials, a four-phase brand strategy and rollout timeline, named fictional partner and director roster cards, three transparent scope and investment tiers with optional sprint add-on calculations, and an editorial closing slide. Presenter controls remain unobtrusive yet complete: arrow-key, spacebar, swipe, and click navigation, a fine bottom progress hairline, a G-key thumbnail overview grid, an N-key speaker notes drawer with a meeting timer, F-key fullscreen mode, and a clean landscape print stylesheet for PDF leave-behinds. Customize the agency name, hospitality client, project location, and fee anchors in a single content file.

## Make it yours

- An independent brand studio pitching a boutique hotel group: set BRAND and CLIENT_NAME, then swap the three case study before/after pairs for your own rebrand work.
- A creative director preparing a credentials deck for a restaurant launch: keep the four-phase timeline and rewrite each phase for menu, interior and digital rollout.
- A design consultancy bidding on a desert resort rebrand: change LOCATION and INVESTMENT_ANCHOR, and replace the six studio photographs with your own property shoot.
- A founder-led studio pitching lifestyle clients: edit the roster cards to your real partners and directors, and align the three investment tiers to your scope.

## FAQ

### How do the before and after art direction toggles work in the Creative Agency Pitch Deck?
The Creative Agency Pitch Deck includes three case study slides, and each one lets a prospective client switch live between a dated Before visual direction and the reimagined After editorial art direction. The toggle sits inside the slide, so you can talk through concrete typography, color and conversion shifts without leaving the presentation. To adapt it, replace the Before and After notes and the paired visuals in the content file so each case study shows your own work.

### What presenter controls come with the Creative Agency Pitch Deck?
The Creative Agency Pitch Deck supports arrow-key, spacebar, swipe and click navigation, a fine bottom progress hairline, a G-key thumbnail overview grid, an N-key speaker notes drawer with a meeting timer, and an F-key fullscreen mode. A landscape print stylesheet renders all 12 slides one per page for PDF leave-behinds. Speaker notes live in the same content file as the slide copy, so you can rewrite them per pitch.

### Can I use the Creative Agency Pitch Deck for client pitches and paid work?
Yes, with a Pro membership. The Creative Agency Pitch Deck is a Pro template: Pro members see the full prompt and get the source code of the demos, which is what commercial and client use requires. Free templates' prompts and every DESIGN.md are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only. The demo content, including the fictional studio and hospitality client, is sample copy you replace with your own.

### How do I customize the agency name, client and fees in the Creative Agency Pitch Deck?
The Creative Agency Pitch Deck centralizes its content in one file, with variables for BRAND, CLIENT_NAME, LOCATION, INVESTMENT_ANCHOR and CONTACT_EMAIL. Set BRAND to your studio, CLIENT_NAME and LOCATION to the prospect, INVESTMENT_ANCHOR to your flagship fee, and CONTACT_EMAIL to your address. Because the default copy is written for the sample studio and hotel group, rewrite every headline, paragraph and label to match your values while keeping the layout, type and motion.

### What slides and sections are included in the Creative Agency Pitch Deck?
The Creative Agency Pitch Deck has 12 slides: an editorial cover, full-bleed campaign moodboards, three case study spotlights with before/after toggles, a credentials slide, a four-phase brand strategy and rollout timeline, named partner and director roster cards, three scope and investment tiers with an optional sprint add-on, a studio culture slide and an editorial closing slide. Six cohesive studio photographs from one warm Mediterranean architectural shoot run across the deck, and all copy sits in a single content file.
