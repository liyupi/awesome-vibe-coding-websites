# Creative Agency Pitch Deck — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Warm Alabaster** `#F4EFE6` — Primary magazine canvas background
- **Charcoal Ink** `#161513` — Display serif headlines, body copy, and dark editorial plates
- **Terracotta Clay** `#C85A32` — Signature editorial accent, chapter indices, and active toggles
- **Olive Grove** `#4A5D4E` — Secondary natural accent, craft callouts, and selected scope badges
- **Linen Rule** `#DED6C8` — Fine 1px magazine hairlines and grid rules
- **Warm Stone** `#6B645B` — Photo captions, credits, and muted editorial metadata

## Typography
- **Oversized editorial serif headlines** — Instrument Serif (OFL), weights 400. Self-host the font files.
- **Grotesque body and UI controls** — DM Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Slide indices, folio codes, and investment figures** — IBM Plex Mono (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Desktop uses a refined 16:9 editorial plate (max 1320px) with asymmetric 12-column magazine proportions, crisp 1px linen rules, and generous breathing room above a 52px bottom folio bar. Mobile (<768px) stacks imagery and typography cleanly with full-bleed aspect-ratio photos and zero horizontal overflow.
- Radius: 2px to 6px crisp architectural corners on photography frames and editorial tables; 999px only for compact interactive toggle pills.

## Motion
- Calm editorial fade-and-shift transitions (260ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)) honoring prefers-reduced-motion.
- Smooth visual treatment crossfade when toggling Before vs After art direction on Slides 06, 07, and 08.
- Hover focus lift on investment tiers and overview grid thumbnails.
- All content visible by default with zero scroll-clip traps.

## Imagery
Six cohesive warm-daylight architectural and hospitality photographs generated from one shoot in warm alabaster, travertine, terracotta, and olive tones, with zero text, zero logos, and no recognizable faces.

## Tone
Cultured, confident, architectural, tactile, and specific; avoids agency clichés.
