This music app landing page turns its central product preview into something visitors can actually hear. A near-black stage supports large magenta, cyan, and lime holographic shapes, while an opaque application panel keeps prompts and playback controls readable. The result has the visual energy of a music tool without relying on a prerecorded video or a decorative waveform that moves independently of sound.

The supplied demo tracks are original synthesized compositions created by code during the production build and shipped as local WAV files. A user starts playback explicitly. A real Web Audio analyzer then drives the waveform and frequency display, with volume, seeking, pause, and track changes connected to the same player. Genre and mood chips select documented demo variations, and the interface explains that the page is auditioning bundled examples rather than calling an AI generation service. A download button saves the selected original demo file.

Below the player, a track gallery introduces four different musical ideas with lengths, tempos, and practical use notes. A compact process section explains how a creator might move from a brief to a shortlist. Credit-based pricing includes a local plan comparison and arithmetic that visitors can inspect without starting a subscription. FAQ answers distinguish the fictional product concept from the static demonstration and avoid unsupported licensing or commercial-use promises.

All text and track metadata live in one editable module. The alternate example changes the audience from video creators to game teams, including track titles, briefs, use cases, and pricing. Audio remains local and original in both cases. Responsive controls, clear empty states, keyboard operation, and a reduced-motion display make this a useful starting point for music tools, sound libraries, and creative software launches.

## FAQ

### How does the audio player on the AI Music Generator Landing Page actually work?
The AI Music Generator Landing Page ships four original WAV loops that play locally in the browser. A Web Audio analyzer reads the real signal to drive the waveform and frequency display, so the visuals move with the sound. Playback starts only after a visitor presses play, and volume, seeking, pause and track changes all control the same player.

### Are the demo tracks on the AI Music Generator Landing Page real AI-generated songs?
No. The four demo tracks on the AI Music Generator Landing Page are original synthesized compositions created by a build script and saved as local WAV files. The page labels this clearly: it auditions bundled examples rather than calling a generation service. The product itself is a fictional concept, and the interface says so near the player controls.

### How do I change the brand name, product and audience on the AI Music Generator Landing Page?
All copy and track metadata on the AI Music Generator Landing Page live in one editable content module. Set BRAND, PRODUCT, AUDIENCE and STARTER_PRICE to your values, then rewrite every related headline, label, track title, brief and confirmation to match. The alternate demo shows this in practice, moving the audience from video creators to game teams.
