> A cinematic vertical feed with snap navigation, motion stories and tactile social controls.

Short Video Feed App is an original, fully client-side motion-story experience for creators, studios and publishers who want a more focused way to present short visual work. Instead of real video files or a media backend, six locally hosted photographs become cinematic scenes with slow pans, timed captions and optional browser-generated sound. The result is quick to ship as a static site while still feeling alive and immersive.

The interface centers on a portrait feed with deliberate one-story navigation by swipe, wheel or arrow key. A compact action rail supports likes, comments, saves and sharing; counts and added comments persist locally. A slide-in conversation drawer, spinning sound control and animated feedback make the prototype feel complete without pretending to connect to a live service. The following view and creator profile reuse the same content model, and the profile grid returns visitors to any selected story.

Warm charcoal, parchment type and a saffron accent give the app an identity distinct from familiar social platforms. Manrope keeps controls crisp, while Instrument Serif adds an editorial note to story captions. Desktop presents a framed portrait stage with generous dark surroundings; mobile turns the same stage into a full-bleed, thumb-friendly feed.

Change the brand, editorial theme, audience and accent to adapt it for a maker journal, travel collective, food publication or creative community. All copy and sample data live in one content file. The template is especially useful for portfolio experiments, editorial concepts and interactive product prototypes where motion, keyboard accessibility, reduced-motion behavior and responsive composition matter more than backend integration.

## Make it yours

- A travel collective: set [BRAND] to your group's name, make [FEED_THEME] trip dispatches, and swap the six photos and captions for field notes from your routes.
- A food publication: use [BRAND] as the masthead, set [FEED_THEME] to seasonal kitchen stories, and rewrite the profile bio and empty states for your editors.
- A maker journal: point [AUDIENCE] at hobbyist woodworkers, change [ACCENT] to a workshop tone, and replace the creator profile grid with your finished pieces.
- A creative community prototype: keep the feed structure, set [FEED_THEME] to member spotlights, and link the profile grid to each contributor's selected story.

## FAQ

### How do I make a short video feed website with AI if I don't have actual video files?
The Short Video Feed App template turns six locally hosted portrait photographs into cinematic scenes using slow pans, timed captions and optional browser-generated sound, so no video files or media backend are needed. Ask your AI coding agent to build from the published prompt, then replace the six image paths and captions in the single content file. The result builds to static files you can host anywhere.

### What can I change in the Short Video Feed App template to make it match my brand?
The Short Video Feed App template exposes four variables: BRAND sets the wordmark, profile name, document title and derived handle; FEED_THEME sets the editorial theme and rewrites captions, bios and empty states; AUDIENCE sets the voice; and ACCENT is a hex color applied to active states, progress, focused controls and the Follow button. All copy and sample data live in one content file.

### Do the likes, comments, saves and follows in the Short Video Feed App actually persist?
The Short Video Feed App stores likes, saves, follows and comments you add in the browser under one versioned local key, with safe parsing and a graceful fallback. Nothing is sent to a server, so the state is per browser and per device. Sharing copies a link using the clipboard with a local confirmation toast rather than posting to a live service.

### How do visitors move between stories in the Short Video Feed App?
The Short Video Feed App moves exactly one story at a time by swipe, mouse wheel or arrow key, and a compact action rail sits alongside the feed.
