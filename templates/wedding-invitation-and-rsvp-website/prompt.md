# Wedding Invitation and RSVP Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page wedding invitation for [BRAND], marrying at [VENUE] in [CITY] on [DATE]. It must feel like a printed suite come alive: an ivory hero with the names in an elegant serif and a live countdown to [DATE], a four-chapter story, the schedule of the day as a vertical timeline, travel and stay notes with invented hotels, an RSVP form (attending, party size, meal choice, dietary notes, one song request) that saves a thank-you card client-side, a guest FAQ and drawn registry placeholder cards. One page: index.html with sections (#story, #day, #travel, #rsvp, #faq, #registry). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is submitted anywhere.

- Audience: The guest list: college friends, extended family, colleagues and plus-ones, ages 9 to 90, mostly opening the link from a group chat on a phone. They want to know where, when, what to wear, where to sleep and how to say yes — in under three minutes.
- Goal: Get every guest to a submitted RSVP and a booked hotel. Within ten seconds a visitor should know who, where and when, and see the countdown; within two minutes they should have answered the RSVP.
- Tone: Warm, personal and lightly funny, like the couple wrote it themselves on a good writing day. Short paragraphs, concrete details (times, walking minutes, menu names), no formal invitation clichés, no exclamation marks.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — The couple (default: "Elena & Marcus"). The couple's names as they'll appear on the invitation, 'Name & Name'. Used in the hero wordmark, title, hashtag (from the slug), couple initials in the footer and the RSVP's saved key. Always invented people.
- [VENUE] — Venue (default: "Hollowbrook Barn"). The invented venue name. Used in the hero line, the venue sketch label, travel copy and hotel distances. Two common English words; never a real venue.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Hudson, New York"). Where the venue is. Used in the hero line, travel directions (train station, drive times) and the footer. Real towns are fine; streets and hotels stay invented.
- [DATE] — Wedding date (default: "June 12, 2027"). The full future date the countdown runs to, written like 'June 12, 2027'. Drives the countdown, the hero line and the story's last chapter. Always in the future so the countdown is live.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/wedding-invitation-and-rsvp-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base unit. Container max-width 1080px, centered, side padding 48px desktop, 32px tablet, 20px under 640px. Section vertical padding 112px desktop, 80px under 1024px, 64px under 640px. Type scale: H1 (the names) clamp(56px, 9vw, 120px) Gilda Display 400, line-height 1.04, letter-spacing 0.01em; H2 clamp(32px, 4vw, 52px) Gilda 400; H3 24px Gilda 400; ampersand between names set in Sage italic via Cormorant-free fallback (Gilda has no italic — set '&' in Jost 300 italic instead); lead Jost 300 18px / 1.75 Ink at 85%; body Jost 400 16px / 1.8 (max-width 64ch); eyebrow Jost 500 12px uppercase letter-spacing 0.2em Olive with a 16px drawn sprig glyph before; caption Jost 400 13px Olive. Buttons: height 50px, padding 0 28px, Jost 500 14px letter-spacing 0.12em uppercase. Primary: Ink fill, Paper text. Secondary: 1px Olive border, Olive text. Inputs: height 50px, 1px Rule border, Paper fill, 16px Jost, focus border Sage. Focus ring everywhere: 2px Blush outline with 3px offset.
- Radius: 14px on cards, inputs, chips, photos and the RSVP panel. 999px only for chips, the party stepper buttons, timeline dots and the heart.
- Motion: Easing: --ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1) for reveals and the countdown; --ease-io cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for accordions and the RSVP swap. Slow and soft — this is paper, not software.
- Motion: Reveal: blocks wrapped in <Reveal> start at opacity 0, translateY(16px) and animate to rest over 900ms --ease-out when 15% visible (Motion whileInView, viewport once). Siblings stagger by 100ms (index prop).
- Motion: Hero: the two names fade and rise from translateY(20px) with 200ms stagger 400ms after load, then the ampersand sprig draws (stroke-dashoffset to 0) over 1200ms, then the countdown fades in. The hero photo (top half, photo hero-barn.webp) scales from 1.05 to 1.0 over 2600ms; scrolling translates it 0 to 8% (Motion useScroll, offset ['start start', 'end start']).
- Motion: Countdown: the four cells sit on one hairline; each tick crossfades only the changed digit (a 240ms y-slide), never a flip-clock. Under prefers-reduced-motion the digits swap instantly.
- Motion: Story chapters: each chapter card reveals with its marker dot filling Sage; the connecting hairline draws downward as the section scrolls (scaleY 0 to 1, transform-origin top, useScroll on the list).
- Motion: Timeline (schedule): rows reveal with 100ms stagger; each time crossfades when its row enters; the dot pops from scale 0.6 once.
- Motion: RSVP: the attending toggle slides a small drawn heart between 'Joyfully accepts' (Blush) and 'Regretfully declines' (Stone); meal chips fill Sage when selected; party stepper counts with a 200ms scale pulse; submitting slides the form out and the ThankYouCard in (16px rise, 320ms --ease-io, AnimatePresence mode 'wait'); the card's heart draws its outline over 700ms.
- Motion: FAQ accordions: height 0 to auto and opacity 0 to 1 over 340ms --ease-io; the Plus rotates 45 degrees into an X.
- Motion: Registry cards: hover lifts 4px and the drawn ribbon straightens (a tiny 6px rotate settles to 0).
- Motion: Header: transparent over the ivory page with Ink text; after 40px of scroll it gets Ivory at 92% opacity, backdrop-filter blur(8px) and a 1px Rule border, 250ms transition. Anchor nav always visible with .link-draw underlines.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — disable the photo scale and parallax, name rise, sprig draws, count-ups, layout animations (render at rest; countdown digits swap instantly). Color and border hover changes stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Real photography only, soft and romantic, all six made for this template in one consistent style: soft garden light, ivory and sage with blush accents, shallow depth of field, fine grain. Never a person: the venue, florals, stationery, table settings and candles — no faces, no hands, no rings on fingers. No readable text anywhere — stationery stays blank. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 14px corners, no shadow; photos on Ivory get a 1px Rule outline. Captions under photos in Jost 13px Olive. Drawn in code: the botanical corner sprigs, chapter markers, timeline dots, the heart, the venue sketch and the registry placeholder cards. Icons: lucide-react only, stroke width 1.5.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the RSVP and its thank-you card live in the browser only (localStorage); nothing is posted anywhere, and the form says so.
- All links on this single page are anchors (./index.html#story, #day, #travel, #rsvp, #faq, #registry); hotel and registry links are '#' placeholders the couple fills in — never real stores.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- No real venues, hotels, registry stores or real couples; no photos with faces, hands or people; stationery in photos stays blank — no readable writing.
- No dates of any real couple; the countdown targets the [DATE] variable only; the page shows no other dates (the RSVP deadline lives in copy, not a calendar).
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere: disable parallax, the sprig and heart draws, digit slides and staggered reveals; keep color/border hovers.
- The site must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; the RSVP works with keyboard only and every interactive element has a visible focus ring.
- Keep copy concrete and personal: no lorem, no invitation-website clichés ('welcome to our journey'); every time, shop and hotel comes from src/content.ts.
