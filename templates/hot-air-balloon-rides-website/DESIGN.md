# Hot Air Balloon Rides Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Dawn Peach** `#FFC9B3` — Top of the drifting gradient and hero washes.
- **Dawn Lilac** `#C9A8E0` — Bottom of the gradient, accents and ribbons.
- **Sky Cream** `#FFF9F3` — Page ground, cards and panels over the gradient.
- **Dusk Plum** `#46365B` — Headlines, body text and hairlines.
- **Apricot** `#EE8A63` — Primary CTA, focus rings and balloon motifs.
- **Morning Gold** `#F2C94C` — Small highlights: stars, the voucher ribbon, step times.

## Typography
- **Display / headlines** — Cormorant Garamond (OFL), weights 300/400/500. Self-host the font files.
- **Body / UI text** — Jost Variable (OFL), weights 300/400/500. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Airy and light: 112px section padding on desktop and 64px on mobile, a 1160px container, 40px gaps, body line-height 1.8.
- Radius: Soft throughout: 24px cards and photo frames, 999px pills and chips, 12px inputs.

## Motion
- The dawn gradient drifts: a fixed oversized peach-to-lilac layer animates background-position over 32s alternate; frozen to a static blend under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Balloon motifs bob: small drawn SVG balloons float 8px up and down on 7-10s staggered loops; timeline photos shift 6px with scroll parallax.
- Scroll reveals: sections fade and rise 24px over 0.6s with an 80ms stagger; the timeline hairline draws its length whileInView.
- Micro-interactions: month chips glow softly on select, the party-size stepper counts with a 150ms slide, inputs glow Apricot on focus.

## Imagery
Golden-dawn photography in one warm grade — balloons over landscapes, a dewy meadow, the burner glow, a landing toast — with passengers only as distant, unidentifiable figures and balloon envelopes plain or gently striped, never marked. Weather icons and balloon motifs are delicate line-drawn SVG in Dusk Plum with Apricot highlights.

## Tone
Light, calm, a little poetic; specifics do the dreaming — dew, the burner roar, first light. Never over-promise: weather is explained, not forecast. Rewrite all copy to fit the supplied variables.
