> A dawn-soft balloon ride site with a flight timeline, packages, gift vouchers, and a saved booking request.

Hot Air Balloon Rides Website is a dawn-soft landing page for a fictional ride company, aimed at small operators who sell one unforgettable morning. A photograph of balloons over a misty valley at first light anchors the hero, dissolving into a peach-to-lavender gradient that drifts slowly behind every section. Cormorant Garamond sets the headlines in light, elegant strokes; Jost keeps the labels quiet and precise. The page walks the morning in four steps — meet in the meadow, unfold and inflate, lift off at first light, toast on landing — each with its own golden-dawn photograph. Three packages price the dream, a gift voucher card invites the impulse buyer, and a weather explainer sets expectations with four drawn condition cards and sample numbers, explicitly avoiding forecasts or live data. A booking request form with month chips, a party-size stepper and package preselection saves to the browser through localStorage and says so in plain words. Photography stays in one warm dawn grade across six generated images, with passengers only distant and balloon envelopes unmarked. Motion is gentle: the gradient drift, bobbing balloon motifs, a timeline that draws itself on scroll, and soft chip selections, all disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. Four variables re-theme the site — company, region, audience and basket capacity — with all copy in one content file. The alternate case moves the same design to an olive-grove plateau with a smaller basket, showing how the layout absorbs a different operator without losing its calm. The centered, airy composition stays quiet at any width, holding cleanly from a 1440px desktop down to a 390px phone.

## Make it yours

- A small family balloon operator: set BRAND to your company name, REGION to your valley, and rewrite the three packages around your basket size and route.
- A gift-experience seller: keep the dawn hero and voucher card, then rewrite the packages as gift tiers and point the booking form at your own redemption process.
- A photographer-led flight business: set AUDIENCE to photographers, adjust CAPACITY, and rewrite the timeline steps to mention camera setups and light windows.
- A tourism board or hotel concierge page: set REGION to your area, keep the weather explainer, and rewrite packages as partner flight options with a request form.

## FAQ

### How do I change the company name, region and basket size in the Hot Air Balloon Rides Website?
The Hot Air Balloon Rides Website template is themed by four variables: BRAND, REGION, AUDIENCE and CAPACITY. Set BRAND to your company name, REGION to the landscape you fly over, AUDIENCE to the guests you serve and CAPACITY to your basket limit. Every headline, package and FAQ is written for the default values, so rewrite each one to match your new values while keeping the same tone and length.

### How does the booking request form work on the Hot Air Balloon Rides Website?
The booking request form on the Hot Air Balloon Rides Website uses month chips, a party-size stepper and package preselection, then saves the request to the visitor's browser through localStorage under the key booking-request.v1. The page says this in plain words. There is no backend or external API, so requests stay on the device and you would need to connect a real inbox or form service if you want them sent to you.

### What does the weather section on the Hot Air Balloon Rides Website actually show?
The weather section on the Hot Air Balloon Rides Website is an explainer, not a forecast. It shows four drawn condition cards with sample numbers to set expectations about wind, cloud and visibility, and it explicitly avoids live data or forecasts. The copy is written to explain what makes a good flying morning rather than promise conditions, so keep that framing when you adapt the text for your own region.

### What is included in the gift voucher section of the Hot Air Balloon Rides Website?
The Hot Air Balloon Rides Website includes a gift voucher card presented as a split card rotated slightly, aimed at impulse buyers and gift-givers. It sits alongside three flight packages and a booking request form, so a visitor can move from the voucher idea to a concrete request. The voucher is a page section, not a payment or redemption system, so pair it with your own voucher process when you publish.

### Which files do I edit first to make the Hot Air Balloon Rides Website my own?
Start with the single content file that holds all copy, packages, timeline steps, weather cards and FAQs, and change the four variables there. Then adjust the dawn photography and the package prices to match your operation. Layout, colors, type and motion are set in the design tokens and can stay as they are, which keeps the calm dawn look while your words and numbers change.
