# Driving School Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build a clear, upbeat one-page site for [BRAND], an invented driving school in [CITY] serving [AUDIENCE]. The identity is road-sign yellow on warm white with navy accents, rounded confident type, and code-drawn road-marking motifs (dashed center lines, route dots, curve arrows). The page anchors on a friendly car photograph, lays out four lesson packages with per-lesson sample pricing, runs a five-question practice theory quiz with instant feedback and a score readout, introduces three fictional instructors with generated portraits, then adds test-prep tips, service-area chips, and a lesson-booking sample saved locally.

- Audience: [AUDIENCE] — and the parents who usually pay for the packages. Also freelancers and local academies adapting the layout for any coached-session business with packages and a practice test.
- Goal: Get a nervous visitor smiling and booked: win them with the car and the promise in one screen, let the quiz prove the teaching tone in ninety seconds, price the packages plainly, and let them hold a lesson slot in the browser without any signup.
- Tone: Plain, warm, and lightly funny — a patient instructor on a quiet street. Short lines, real prices, no jargon, no fear tactics. Confident about safety basics without lecturing, and honest that the quiz is practice, not the official test.

  - Use Motion from 'motion/react' (motion, AnimatePresence, useReducedMotion) for quiz feedback, chip pops, and reveals; gate every animation on useReducedMotion().

  - Implement the quiz as an explicit state machine: intro, question (locked after answering), results. Store per-question answers in React state only; 'Retake quiz' resets everything.
  - Draw the road-marking motifs in code as reusable inline SVG components: a dashed center-line divider (26px dashes, 34px gaps) and a route-dot line for the tips list. Never photograph them.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Brand (default: "Greenlight Driving School"). Invented school name; a road-sign logomark is derived from it in code.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Riverbend"). The town the school serves; appears in the hero badge, tips, service areas, and footer.
- [AUDIENCE] — Audience (default: "first-time teen drivers and adults getting back behind the wheel"). A plain-language phrase for who the lessons are for.
- [PROMISE] — Promise (default: "Patient instructors, calm cars, no yelling. Ever."). The school's one-line pledge; appears as its own sentence in the hero and again in the footer.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/driving-school-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Design
### Colors
- Warm white `#FFFCF4` — Page ground
- Card white `#FFFFFF` — Cards, quiz panel, booking form
- Sign yellow `#FFC72C` — Primary accent: CTAs, selected chips, check icons, popular card
- Soft yellow `#FFF4D3` — Bands, badges, and tint chips
- Navy `#1E3A6E` — Buttons, links, logomark ring, footers
- Ink navy `#17294D` — Headings and body text
- Slate `#3B4358` — Secondary text and meta
- Dash gray `#CDD3E0` — Hairlines, dashes, and idle borders
- Quiz green `#2F9E63` — Correct-answer fill and recap dots
- Quiz red `#D94848` — Wrong-answer fill and error text

### Typography
- Display: headlines, package names, quiz headings: Fredoka (OFL), weights 500/600
- Body: paragraphs, answers, chips, forms: Figtree (OFL), weights 400/600/700

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base grid. Content shell max-width 1120px with 32px desktop and 20px mobile side padding. Header 72px. Desktop sections 96px vertical padding, mobile 64px. Package grid gap 24px. Prose max 62 characters per line.
- Radius: Everything friendly: 999px pills for buttons, chips, and badges; 20px for cards, photos, and the quiz panel; 12px for inputs. Road-marking SVG dividers are square 3px lines with 26px dashes and 34px gaps.
- Motion: Quiz: a chosen answer settles with a 160ms scale pop; a wrong pick shakes 6px twice over 240ms; the explanation panel slides up 180ms; the results score fills a small drawn dial over 600ms.
- Motion: Road-marking dividers drift slowly left on an 8px-per-second loop — decorative only, and fully static under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Motion: Cards lift 4px on hover (180ms); chips pop to 1.06 on selection; sections reveal once on scroll with a 14px rise and a 420ms ease-out fade, staggered 60ms.
- Motion: With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, every animation above becomes a 100ms opacity change at most and every interaction stays fully functional. No page parallax, no cursor effects, no autoplay.
- Imagery: One photographic set in a consistent warm-morning grade: a cheerful unbranded yellow compact car on quiet suburban streets — a front three-quarter hero view, a cockpit detail, a curb-side drop-off — plus three studio-quality fictional instructor portraits taken outdoors in the same soft light, varying age and ethnicity, depicting no real people. Cars carry no visible badges or readable plates; no bystanders. Road markings, sign-shaped chips, and route doodles are drawn in SVG, never photographed.

## Don'ts
- No real driving schools, DMVs, licensing bodies, or official test banks. The school, instructors, routes, rates, pass figures, and stats are invented samples, and the footer says so.
- Instructor portraits must be AI-generated fictional people who do not resemble any real individual: vary age and ethnicity across the three, never imitate a celebrity or public figure, and keep consistent lighting and quality across the set.
- The quiz questions are invented practice samples: never claim they come from, match, or are endorsed by any official test, and never imply a passing score guarantees a license.
- Photos must show no visible car badges, no readable license plates or signs, no storefronts with branding, and no identifiable bystanders; the three instructor portraits are the only people in the set.
- Booking is local-only: no form submits, no APIs, no email or SMS services, no analytics. Persist to localStorage only and validate restored data.
- No dates tied to the real clock: weekday chips and static sample slots only; never render today's date, a countdown, or time-of-day logic against the visitor's clock.
- No backend, server code, databases, auth, or third-party scripts. Static output in dist/ from the single build command.
- Self-host fonts through the listed Fontsource packages only; never load Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts, or any CDN stylesheet or script.
- No source maps in production, no src/ copied into dist/, keep dist/ under 20 MB, and keep Vite's base relative.
- No Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Google Images, placeholder-image services, or hotlinked files; use only the six generated photos and original code-drawn SVG/CSS art.
- No SEO work: no meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, sitemaps, or keyword-stuffed headings. A plain title with [BRAND] and a favicon are enough.
- Do not let the quiz double-fire on rapid taps (lock after answering), trap keyboard focus, or animate in a way that ignores prefers-reduced-motion; keep the drifting road dashes purely decorative.
- No scroll-jacking, custom cursor, autoplay audio, or page parallax.
- Keep all copy in US English and build brand-derived strings (storage keys, slugs, alt text) from [BRAND] and [BRAND_SLUG] so alternate variable values never leave default-brand remnants.
- Every internal link is a file name or in-page anchor, never a bare directory; the site must render at 1440×900 and 390×844 with zero console errors.
