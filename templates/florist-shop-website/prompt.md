# Florist Shop Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page shop for [SHOP], a corner florist in [CITY] — same-day delivery on orders before [CUTOFF], bouquets from $[BASE_PRICE]. It must sell flowers honestly: an eight-bouquet catalogue with size and vase options priced live, a delivery date picker (next 7 days, Sundays closed, same-day before the cutoff), a gift message with a live notecard preview, a cart drawer with per-line variants and date, and a client-side checkout that says it takes no payment. One page: index.html with sections (#shop, #subscriptions, #care). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is submitted anywhere.

- Audience: Gift-givers in a mild panic and regulars who know what they like: partners, adult children, apology drafters. They shop on phones, care about the delivery date above all, and want the card message to sound better than they can write.
- Goal: Fill the cart and pick the date. Within ten seconds a visitor sees the same-day cutoff, the price range and the first bouquets; within a minute they have a size, a date and a message started.
- Tone: Warm, lightly witty, flower-literate but never gatekeep-y. Concrete stems and seasonal notes, one joke about lilies, no 'express your emotions through flowers'.

## Variables
- [SHOP] — Shop name (default: "Petals & Post"). Invented florist name. Used in the wordmark, title, footer, email (hello@ + shopSlug without hyphens + .com) and localStorage keys. Never a real shop.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Portland, Maine"). Where the shop delivers. Used in the hero, delivery copy, reviews and the footer. Real cities fine; streets invented.
- [CUTOFF] — Same-day cutoff (default: "2pm"). The same-day order cutoff hour, written like '2pm' or '1pm'. Drives the countdown banner, the date picker's same-day chip and one FAQ-style line. Keep it noon–4pm.
- [BASE_PRICE] — Bouquet base price (default: "42"). The standard 'bouquet' size price of the signature arrangement as digits. Posey is ×0.75, abundant ×1.45; every bouquet's sizes derive from its own base around this figure.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/florist-shop-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

- Motion: Notecard preview: the drawn notecard sits beside the message field; typed text appears on the card in Lora italic in real time, with a soft underline draw; the signature line ('— with love, [name]') types once when the name field completes.

- Motion: Links: .link-draw grows a 1px currentColor underline over 280ms on hover and focus-visible.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — no pulses, petals, type-ins or counts; drawer and swaps appear; color/border hover changes stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the cart, messages and order save in the browser only (localStorage); the checkout says it takes no payment; the cutoff countdown uses the client clock and says so.
- All links on this single page are anchors (./index.html#shop, #subscriptions, #care) plus the footer mailto.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- No real florists, delivery brands or payment marks; no photos with people, faces or hands; no readable signage; all stems, prices and reviews invented.
- No dates or years anywhere; the date picker shows weekday chips (Today/Tomorrow then weekday names) without full dates; the site never shows today's date.
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: no pulses, petals, type-ins or counts; the countdown still ticks (it is information, not decoration); color/border hovers stay.
- The page must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; size/date/message controls, the drawer and checkout work with keyboard only and visible focus rings; the drawer traps focus while open.
- Keep copy concrete: no lorem, no 'emotions through flowers'; every price, stem list and hour lives in src/content.ts.
