Property Management Company Website is a single-page site for a residential property management firm, in warm brick, ink navy and sand — a brownstone-office feel instead of corporate landlord blue. The page is built around two clear paths. Owners get services, a fully transparent fee structure (leasing, management, renewal, lease-out guarantee) and a rental income estimator: rent, vacancy weeks, management fee and maintenance reserve produce gross annual, each deduction and net monthly with every line visible. Tenants get a filterable available-rentals grid, four apply steps, and a maintenance request flow with category and urgency that saves to a local 'my requests' list with sample statuses — portal-lite without a login. Owner testimonials carry real-feeling numbers (days-to-lease, collection rate), a drawn service-area map shows towns and unit counts, and a free rental analysis form closes the owner path. Five variables swap firm name, town, broker, phone and years; all fees, listings and people are invented sample data.

## FAQ

### How does the rental income estimator on the Property Management Company Website work?
The rental income estimator on the Property Management Company Website takes four inputs: monthly rent, vacancy weeks per year, management fee percentage and maintenance reserve percentage. It shows gross annual rent, the vacancy deduction, the management fee cost and the reserve cost, then net annual and net monthly income with every line visible. Inputs persist in the browser, and a footnote states the math uses sample assumptions rather than a guarantee of income.

### Can tenants submit maintenance requests through the Property Management Company Website template?
Tenants can submit maintenance requests through the Property Management Company Website template without creating an account. The form asks for the property, a category such as Plumbing or HVAC, an urgency level and a description. Emergency requests show the office phone number instead of the form. Submitted requests appear in a local My requests list with Submitted, Scheduled and Done steps, and the list starts with two labeled sample requests so it is never empty.

### What should I change first after downloading the Property Management Company Website template?
Set the five variables first: BRAND for the firm name, CITY for the home base, FOUNDER for the managing broker, PHONE for the office and emergency line, and EXPERIENCE for the years managing. Then replace the invented fee rows, the eight sample rental listings, the seeded maintenance requests, the testimonials and the six towns on the service-area map with your own real details.

### How do I adapt the Property Management Company Website to a condo management company instead of houses?
The Property Management Company Website template already suits condo management. Change the rental listings from houses and townhomes to condos, adjust the type filter chips, and rewrite the owner services and fee table for condo-specific work such as board reporting and common-area coordination. The income estimator, maintenance flow and rental analysis form stay as they are, since the math and request categories apply to any residential unit.
