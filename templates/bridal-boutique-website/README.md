Bridal Boutique Website is a single-page site for an independent bridal salon, in blush, champagne and deep wine with an editorial serif — a magazine spread that happens to book appointments. Three working pieces: a style quiz (venue, vibe, and the dress you sketch in margins → two suggested silhouettes with a friendly why, linking into the collection), a gown collection grid filterable by silhouette, neckline and price band with live counts, and appointment booking with party size and a 'what to expect' contract (90 minutes, private lounge). An alterations timeline tells the six-step after-sale story every bride worries about, real-wedding stories add proof, and accessories close quietly. Photography shows gowns on forms and the fitting lounge in soft light — no faces, per the house rule. It suits bridal salons, formal-occasion boutiques and suit-ateliers re-pointing the quiz and timeline. Five variables swap boutique name, town, stylist-owner, phone and years; every gown, story and price is invented sample data.

- A suit atelier: keep the quiz and alterations timeline, rename the silhouettes to suit cuts, and swap the eight gowns for made-to-measure jackets with the same filter chips.
- A mother-of-the-bride boutique: set BRAND and CITY to your salon, replace the bridal stories with occasion-wear stories, and keep the party-size appointment chips for family fittings.
- A destination wedding planner: point EXPERIENCE at your years of planning, rewrite the quiz around venue type, and turn the gown grid into a venue and package collection.
- A formal-occasion rental shop: keep the filterable grid, change the price bands to rental tiers, and rewrite the alterations timeline as a fitting and return schedule.

## FAQ

### How does the style quiz on the Bridal Boutique Website template work?
The quiz asks three questions: where the wedding is happening, the vibe you want, and the dress you sketch in margins. Answers map to silhouettes, and the result shows the top two with a one-sentence reason each and a link that filters the gown collection to that silhouette. The quiz is style advice about movement and mood, never body commentary.

### Can I filter the gown collection by silhouette, neckline and price on this bridal template?
The Bridal Boutique Website template has a gown collection grid with silhouette chips (A-line, Ballgown, Mermaid, Sheath), neckline chips (Sweetheart, V, Boat, Off-shoulder) and price bands (Under $2,000, $2,000–3,000, Over $3,000). A live count shows how many gowns match, a clear-filters chip appears when filters are active, and a zero-result state offers the clear chip. The collection ships with eight invented gowns.

### What does the appointment booking include on the Bridal Boutique Website template?
Appointment booking on the Bridal Boutique Website template collects party size (Just me, 2–3 VIPs, The whole parade), a time window (Weekday morning, Weekday afternoon or Saturday), name, phone and an optional wedding-season select. The success message says the salon calls within a day to confirm, and the request is saved in the browser under a name derived from the boutique name.

### Which variables do I change to make the Bridal Boutique Website my own salon?
The Bridal Boutique Website template exposes five variables: BRAND for the boutique name, CITY for the town, FOUNDER for the stylist-owner, PHONE for the salon line, and EXPERIENCE for the years phrase used in the hero. Changing them rewrites the dependent copy, the phone link and the saved-data names, so no default-boutique remnants remain. Gowns, stories and prices are invented sample data you replace.

### Can I use the Bridal Boutique Website template for a suit atelier or formal-occasion boutique?
Yes. The Bridal Boutique Website template is built so formal-occasion and suit boutiques can re-point the style quiz and the six-step alterations timeline at their own fittings. You would rewrite the quiz answers and silhouette names, swap the gown collection for suits or occasion wear, and adjust the alterations steps. The editorial blush-and-wine palette and appointment flow stay as they are.
