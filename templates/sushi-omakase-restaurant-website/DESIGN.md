# Sushi Omakase Restaurant Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Lacquer** `#0D0B09` — Page ground everywhere; base of the hero scrim. Near-black with a warm brown undertone — never pure #000.
- **Charred** `#171310` — Solid header background after scroll, footer ground, confirmation panel ground.
- **Cedar** `#6B4A2F` — Hairlines, section rules, course rail, the wood of the counter diagram. Used at 40–60% opacity for hairlines.
- **Ashwood** `#A9805B` — Warm wood highlight: dimmed seat tokens, captions, the counter bar in the seat diagram.
- **Amber** `#C8873A` — The single accent: section labels, active course tick, selected seat fill, CTA underline.
- **Amber raised** `#E3A85C` — Hover and active accent states only (links, date chips, seat hover hairline).
- **Rice** `#EDE6DB` — Primary text: headlines, course names, body copy. Warm off-white, never pure #fff.
- **Mist** `#9A8F83` — Secondary text: descriptions, captions, nav links at rest.

## Typography
- **Display — headlines, course names, the pull quote (500 italic)** — Cormorant Garamond (OFL), weights 500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body, labels, chips, nav (uppercase labels tracked 0.18em)** — Karla (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Generous and unhurried: sections py-40 (160px) desktop / py-24 (96px) mobile; content max-w-6xl (72rem) with px-6 mobile / px-10 desktop; 64–96px between blocks inside a section; structure carried by 1px cedar hairlines at 45% opacity instead of boxes or cards.
- Radius: Almost none: 2px on controls, date chips and seat tokens; 0px on images and surfaces. No pill buttons, no large rounded cards.

## Motion
- Lenis smooth scrolling (duration 0.9), initialized once in App; fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Course reveal: IntersectionObserver at 25% threshold, fires once per course — opacity 0→1 and translateY 28px→0 over 750ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1); label, name and description stagger 80ms apart.
- Hero: the cover photo scales 1.06→1 over 2.4s ease-out on load and parallaxes at 0.15 of scroll (capped at 12%); headline lines fade up staggered 120ms starting at 300ms; the scrim deepens with scroll.
- Course rail: the tick of the course nearest 40% of the viewport height fills amber over 200ms; past ticks stay amber at 50% opacity.
- Seat picker micro-interactions: hovering a free token lifts it 2px with a 1px amber hairline; selecting fills amber over 200ms; the inline confirmation panel fades in over 300ms. No animation anywhere exceeds 750ms.
- prefers-reduced-motion: Lenis off, parallax and load-scale off, every reveal becomes a plain 200ms opacity fade, micro-interactions keep color changes only.

## Imagery
Every photograph comes from one session: macro food photography on a dark lacquered cedar counter, one warm side light from the left like a low lamp over the counter, deep soft shadows, muted amber highlights, near-black background with faint warm wood grain, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no branded props, no recognizable faces — the chef appears only as hands at work. No stock-photo services; the six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into the project.

## Tone
Hushed, precise, unhurried. Short declarative sentences; luxury communicated through restraint, space and light, never adjectives.
