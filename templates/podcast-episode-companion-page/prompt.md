# Podcast Episode Companion Page

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page, illustrated companion to one long podcast episode, read chapter by chapter. Default story: Episode 47 of [BRAND], '[EPISODE_TITLE]' — [SUBJECT], a fictional family company that sawed a frozen lake into blocks and sold the cold to a harbor city. The page feels like a printed magazine feature laid on warm paper: watercolor paintings open each of five chapters, archival public-domain photographs sit in the text like taped scrapbook prints, eleven numbered exhibits (hand-drawn SVG charts and small interactive widgets) carry the numbers, and six pulled-out lessons can be saved to a notes drawer. A sticky chapter rail shows where the reader is and the matching timestamp in the episode, and a Skim/Read toggle hides the narrative for people who only want the charts and lessons.

- Audience: Listeners of a long-form history and business podcast who want to revisit the episode, skim it before listening, or share one chart or lesson with a friend.
- Goal: Let a visitor understand the whole story in five minutes of skimming or twenty minutes of reading, save the lessons that matter to them, and jump back into the episode at the right timestamp.
- Tone: Warm, curious and precise, like a good magazine feature. Concrete numbers, dates and names; short declarative sentences; no hype words, no exclamation marks, no emoji.

## Stack
- Framework: Vite 5 + React 18 + TypeScript
- Styling: Tailwind CSS 3.4
- Libraries: react (MIT), react-dom (MIT), motion (motion.dev, import from 'motion/react') (MIT), lucide-react (ISC), tailwindcss (MIT), postcss (MIT), autoprefixer (MIT), vite (MIT), @vitejs/plugin-react (MIT), typescript (Apache-2.0), @fontsource-variable/fraunces (OFL-1.1), @fontsource-variable/source-serif-4 (OFL-1.1), @fontsource/ibm-plex-mono (OFL-1.1), @fontsource-variable/caveat (OFL-1.1)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Scaffold with npm create vite@latest using the react-ts template. One page, index.html, with anchored sections: #top, #map, #ch-1 to #ch-5, #recap, #credits.
  - vite.config.ts: base './', plugins [react()], build { outDir: 'dist', sourcemap: false }. Do not copy src/ into dist/.
  - Tailwind 3.4 through PostCSS (tailwindcss + autoprefixer). In theme.extend.colors add every palette color below under kebab-case keys (paper, paper-deep, print, ink, graphite, pencil, rule, indigo, glacier, ochre, rust, moss). In theme.extend.fontFamily add display ['Fraunces Variable', 'Georgia', 'serif'], serif ['Source Serif 4 Variable', 'Georgia', 'serif'], mono ['IBM Plex Mono', 'ui-monospace', 'monospace'], hand ['Caveat Variable', 'cursive'].
  - Import fonts once in src/main.tsx: '@fontsource-variable/fraunces/full.css', '@fontsource-variable/fraunces/full-italic.css', '@fontsource-variable/source-serif-4/index.css', '@fontsource-variable/source-serif-4/wght-italic.css', '@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono/400.css', '@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono/500.css', '@fontsource-variable/caveat/index.css'.
  - All copy, chapter metadata (number, title, years, start and end timestamps in seconds, chapter color), chart data, lessons, image paths, alt texts and credits live in src/content.ts. Components only read from it.
  - Components in src/components/: TopBar, Hero, EpisodeMap, ChapterRail, Chapter, Painting, Print, PullQuote, LessonCard, NotesDrawer, Recap, Credits, Footer, Watercolor (exports WatercolorDefs, Blot, WatercolorScene) and charts/ (BarChart, LineChart, UnitChart, WindowCard, ThicknessGauge, CrossingChart). Charts are hand-written SVG; no chart library.
  - Hooks in src/hooks/: useActiveChapter (IntersectionObserver on the five chapter sections, rootMargin '-35% 0px -60% 0px'), useEpisodeTime (maps scroll position inside the active chapter to a timestamp), useNotes (saved lesson ids in localStorage key 'companion-notes'), useSkim (boolean in localStorage key 'companion-skim').
  - src/index.css holds the Tailwind layers, the paper grain background, drop-cap and tape styles, the .skim rules and a prefers-reduced-motion block that disables transitions and the painting parallax.
  - Write scripts/fetch-assets.sh with one 'curl -fL -o <target> <url>' line per image in Assets, run it once, and keep the downloaded files in public/images/. The site never hotlinks.
  - Expected dist/ size: under 6 MB (self-hosted woff2, six webp paintings, four archival JPEGs).

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Show name (default: "The Long Ledger"). The podcast (or book club, newsletter) the companion belongs to. Used in the top bar wordmark, the hero eyebrow, the <title>, credits and footer.
- [EPISODE_TITLE] — Episode or book title (default: "Frozen Assets"). Short title set as the giant hero headline and in the <title>. Two or three words read best.
- [SUBJECT] — What the story is about (default: "The rise and melt of the Halvorsen Ice Company, 1856–1934"). One line under the headline. Drives every chapter, chart, lesson and image; when it changes, rewrite the five chapters, eleven exhibits and six lessons for the new story with the same structure.
- [HOSTS] — Hosts or narrators (default: "Maren Okafor & Teddy Lindqvist"). Invented names. Shown in the hero eyebrow, pull-quote attributions and credits.
- [RUNTIME] — Episode length (default: "4 h 12 min"). Shown in the hero eyebrow. The chapter timestamps add up to this length (04:12:00); rescale them if it changes.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/podcast-episode-companion-page in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base. Page container 1280px with 40px side padding (24px under 768px). Body text measure 680px; wide figures and charts 880px. Sections 128px apart on desktop, 88px on mobile; chapter openers get 160px top padding on desktop. Body 19px/1.7 Source Serif (17px/1.65 on mobile), paragraphs 24px apart.
- Radius: Paper-like and mostly square: 2px on prints, lesson cards and panels; 6px on buttons and tooltips; 999px only for the Skim/Read pill, chapter chips and the notes count badge.
- Motion: Reveal: text blocks, prints, lesson cards and charts fade in and rise 16px over 600ms with ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1] when 30% visible, once. Lists stagger by 80ms.
- Motion: Watercolor bloom: every Blot scales 0.85 → 1 and fades from 0 to its opacity over 900ms when it enters the viewport, once.
- Motion: Chart draw-in when 40% visible, once: line paths animate pathLength 0 → 1 over 1.4s ease-in-out; bars and unit squares grow from the baseline over 700ms with a 60ms stagger; Caveat annotations and their hand-drawn arrows appear after the data (arrow path draws over 500ms, text fades in 300ms).
- Motion: Painting parallax (≥1024px only): chapter paintings move from translateY(24px) to translateY(-24px) across their pass through the viewport, via useScroll + useTransform.
- Motion: Reading progress: a 2px Lake Indigo bar under the top bar, scaleX bound to page scroll progress through useSpring (stiffness 120, damping 30).
- Motion: Prints rest at rotate(-1.5deg) or rotate(1.2deg) (alternating) and straighten to 0deg with translateY(-4px) and a deeper shadow on hover, 250ms.
- Motion: Top bar chapter label and rail timestamp cross-fade (opacity + 6px y) in 200ms when the active chapter changes.
- Motion: Notes drawer slides in from the right (x 100% → 0) in 320ms ease-out over a 30% Ink backdrop; on mobile it is a bottom sheet rising from y 100%. Saving a lesson pops the count badge (scale 1 → 1.25 → 1, 300ms).
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: no reveals, blooms, parallax or draw-ins; charts render fully drawn; the drawer appears without sliding.
- Imagery: Three layers, never mixed inside one frame. (1) Six watercolor paintings (hero + one per chapter) shown with mix-blend-mode: multiply so their white paper merges into the page, and a feathered edge: mask-image radial-gradient(ellipse 75% 72% at 50% 50%, #000 60%, transparent 100%). (2) Four archival public-domain images shown as scrapbook prints: Print-colored border 10px with 36px at the bottom, image filter grayscale(1) sepia(0.3) contrast(1.05), two Ochre tape strips at the top corners (72×22px, rotated ±6°, 28% opacity, jagged ends via clip-path), shadow 0 14px 24px -14px rgba(31,36,48,0.35). (3) Watercolor washes drawn in code (Blot component) behind numerals, quotes, the hero headline and in charts. A paper grain covers the page background: an inline SVG data-URI with feTurbulence (baseFrequency 0.9, numOctaves 2) at 5% opacity, tiled at 240px, set as body background-image over Paper.

## Don'ts
- No backend: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, analytics or third-party APIs. Notes and the Skim setting live only in localStorage. There is no audio player and no form that submits anywhere.
- No fonts from fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any CDN. Self-host through the Fontsource packages listed.
- No CDN <script> or <link> tags. Every script, style, font, icon and image is bundled or downloaded into the project. Never hotlink the image URLs at runtime.
- No source maps in the production build (build.sourcemap: false), and do not copy src/ into dist/.
- Relative asset paths only (Vite base './'). Internal links are hash anchors on this page (#top, #map, #ch-1 … #ch-5, #recap, #credits) or file names like ./index.html, never a bare directory.
- Do not change the stack (Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind 3.4 + Motion + Lucide + Fontsource) unless the user asks. No chart library: every chart is hand-written SVG.
- Do not reuse any real podcast's name, hosts, episode titles, branding or copy, and do not make a real company the subject. The default story, people, places and numbers are fictional and must stay that way.
- No real people's likeness: no portraits of hosts or founders, no recognizable faces in the paintings, and no faces as the hero image.
- No brand logos, product names or AI-tool branding anywhere, and no lettering, signage or company names inside the images.
- No Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay or other stock-site images, no placeholder-image services, nothing CC BY-NC or CC BY-SA. Only the listed generated watercolors and public-domain / CC0 images, all recorded in ASSETS.md.
- No blue-purple gradients, neon glows, glassmorphism cards or drop-shadowed glowing buttons. Color comes from the paper palette and the watercolor washes only.
- Do not stretch the narrative over the charts: body text stays at a 680px measure and the exhibits at 880px. No full-width text blocks.
- Must work at 1440×900 and 390×844 with zero console errors and no horizontal scroll. Respect prefers-reduced-motion as described.
- No SEO extras (meta descriptions, Open Graph, JSON-LD, sitemap). A plain <title> and the favicon are enough.
