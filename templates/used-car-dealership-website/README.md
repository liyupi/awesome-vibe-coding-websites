Used Car Dealership Website is a full storefront for an independent used-car dealer — the most app-like template in the local-business family. It ships a 24-car inventory with filters that really work (make, body type, fuel, price, mileage, year, plus sort), a vehicle detail view per car with photo gallery, spec table, inspection highlights and an amortizing payment calculator (price, down payment, term, APR → monthly with the math shown), a three-car compare tray and table, saved cars in localStorage, a trade-in estimator with a sample value range, and an honest four-step financing explainer. The look is showroom restraint: graphite ground, clean white panels and exactly one loud color — signal yellow — reserved for prices, active states and CTAs. Every car is a fictional make and model photographed generically with no badges or plates. It suits any inventory business — boats, RVs, bikes, equipment — after re-pointing the filter list and the photo set. Five variables swap brand, city, owner, phone and years; prices, values and terms are sample data in one content file.

## FAQ

### How does the payment calculator on the Used Car Dealership Website work?
The Used Car Dealership Website template includes an amortizing payment calculator on each vehicle detail view. Shoppers set the price (prefilled from the car), a down payment slider from $0 to $15,000, a term of 36, 48, 60 or 72 months and an APR slider from 3.9% to 14.9%. It shows the monthly figure in signal yellow plus amount financed, total interest and total of payments, with the note that it is an estimate, not an offer of credit.

### Can shoppers compare cars and save favourites on the Used Car Dealership Website?
Yes. The Used Car Dealership Website template lets a shopper add up to three cars to a compare tray that stays fixed at the bottom of the screen, then open a side-by-side table covering price, year, miles, body, fuel, transmission, inspection score and the one-line note, with differing values marked in signal yellow. A heart toggle saves cars to localStorage, and the saved page lists them or shows an empty state that links back to the inventory.

### How do the inventory filters work on the Used Car Dealership Website?
The Used Car Dealership Website template ships 24 fictional cars with live filters for make, body type and fuel as multi-select chips, plus sliders for maximum price, maximum mileage and minimum year, and a sort menu for price, mileage or newest year. A live count shows how many cars match, any active filter shows a Clear all chip, and a zero-result state says no cars match and invites the shopper to loosen a filter rather than showing an empty page.

### How do I adapt the Used Car Dealership Website for boats, RVs or equipment?
The Used Car Dealership Website template suits any inventory business once you re-point the filter lists and the photo set. In the content file, replace the six fictional makes with your brands, change the body types to your categories, and swap the four body-type photos plus the interior and showroom shots for your own. The compare table, saved cars and calculator follow the same fields, so the storefront keeps working without structural changes.

### What should I change first in the Used Car Dealership Website template?
Start with the five variables: set BRAND, CITY, PHONE, FOUNDER and EXPERIENCE, then rewrite the dependent copy in the content file so no default brand remains. Next replace the 24 invented vehicles, prices and terms with your real listings and the trade-in sample figures with your own ranges. Finally swap the generic car photos for your lot photography, keeping the body-type mapping so cards and detail galleries stay consistent.
