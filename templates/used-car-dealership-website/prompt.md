# Used Car Dealership Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build a single-page app for [BRAND], the independent used-car dealership run by [FOUNDER] in [CITY], [EXPERIENCE] in the business. The site is a working storefront: a 24-car inventory of fictional makes with live filters and sort, a detail view per vehicle with gallery, specs and a payment calculator, compare for up to three cars, saved cars, a trade-in estimator and a financing explainer. Graphite and white with signal yellow as the only loud color; bright showroom photography of generic, badge-free cars. All vehicles, prices, values and terms are invented sample data.

- Audience: Primary: used-car shoppers around [CITY] comparing listings on their phone at night — people who want the monthly number, the mileage and the return policy without talking to anyone first. Secondary: RV, boat, bike and equipment dealers re-pointing the inventory fields.
- Goal: Move a visitor from browsing to a saved car and a submitted trade-in or test-drive request: make the inventory genuinely filterable, make the money question answerable in the calculator, and make the purchase risk feel small (inspection count, return window, warranty months).
- Tone: Calm, specific, zero pressure — spec-sheet English with tabular numbers. Never a countdown, never ALL-CAPS urgency, never 'deal of the day'.
