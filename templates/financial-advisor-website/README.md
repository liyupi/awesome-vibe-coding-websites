A single-page website for an independent financial advisor: the advisor's plain promise beside a professional portrait (an AI-generated fictional person users swap for their own photo); a retirement savings calculator with age, monthly contribution and expected-return inputs that draws an illustrative growth chart — every figure labeled sample math, never advice; a fee-transparency table in plain English; three services (retirement planning, tax-aware investing, small-business owners); a credentials strip with invented designations; and an intro-call booking form with day chips that confirms client-side. The default content follows an invented fee-only advisor, but the structure suits any independent practice: change the name, city and fee lines and every number still reads true. The look is quiet wealth: soft charcoal and warm white, a deep green accent, one brass tick, humanist serif headlines over a crisp sans, generous margins and tabular numerals. A visible 'illustrative, not financial advice' note sits with every figure. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## Make it yours

- A solo tax-focused advisor: set FOCUS to tax-aware investing, rewrite the first service card around year-end planning and keep the calculator labeled as sample math.
- A small-business planning practice: set FOCUS to business owners, swap the second and third service cards for cash-flow and exit planning, and point the booking form at your calendar.
- A newly independent fee-only planner: set ADVISOR and CITY, replace the invented designations with your real ones, and rewrite the fee table around your flat planning fee.
- An advice practice in another city: change CITY in the title, hero line and footer, then adjust the credentials strip to the licenses and designations your region uses.

## FAQ

### How does the retirement calculator on the Financial Advisor Website template work?
The calculator takes three inputs: your age, your monthly contribution and an expected annual return. It projects a year-by-year balance to age 85 using monthly compounding and draws an illustrative growth chart in the browser. Every figure carries an 'illustrative, not financial advice' note. The projection logic is a pure function with its assumptions exported for display, so you can adjust the starting balance or the age cap and the chart follows.

### Can I use the Financial Advisor Website template for a real advisory practice and its clients?
The Financial Advisor Website template is a Pro template, and Pro covers commercial and client use, so you can publish it for a real advisory practice. You get the full prompt plus the demo source code. Before going live, replace the invented advisor name, the invented credentials and the sample fee lines with your own, and keep the 'illustrative, not financial advice' note beside every figure.

### What do I need to change first to make the Financial Advisor Website template mine?
Start with the four variables: ADVISOR, CITY, FOCUS and HOURLY. Setting ADVISOR also drives the email address and the localStorage keys, and HOURLY drives the first fee-table row and the transparency copy. Then rewrite the hero promise, the three service cards, the credentials strip and the FAQ in the single content file so the tone matches your practice. The layout, colors and motion stay as they are.

### How does the intro-call booking work on the Financial Advisor Website template?
The booking section shows day chips plus name and email fields. When a visitor picks a day and submits, the form confirms client-side and stores the selection in the browser's localStorage under a key prefixed with the advisor slug. Nothing is submitted anywhere, so there is no backend and no external API. To receive real requests, connect the form to your own scheduling or email service.

### Where does the Financial Advisor Website template show its fees and credentials?
The Financial Advisor Website template has a fee-transparency table written in plain English, with the hourly planning-session rate as its first row, and a credentials strip of drawn badges carrying invented designations. Both sit on the single page alongside the calculator, the three service cards and the booking form. Swap the fee lines and designations for your real ones and the structure still reads true.
