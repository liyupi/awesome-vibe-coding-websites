# Black Hole Interactive Explainer — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Deep Space** `#050508` — Primary background canvas
- **Obsidian Instrument** `#0E1019` — Lab cards and telemetry panels
- **Hot Disk Orange** `#FF8A2A` — Accretion plasma and primary interactive accent
- **Incandescent Gold** `#FFD27A` — Innermost stable orbit photon ring highlight
- **Cool Starlight Blue** `#9DB8FF` — Relativistic Doppler approach shift and data metrics

## Typography
- **Display Title & Chapter Headings** — Space Grotesk (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Explanatory Prose** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Equations, Clocks & Telemetry** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Full-bleed interactive ray-marched WebGL hero with docked glass HUD, followed by centered 1152px max-width alternating chapter sections with generous vertical rhythm.
- Radius: 16px precision obsidian instrument panels with 1px starlight-tinted borders.

## Motion
- Continuous real-time ray-marched swirling accretion plasma and relativistic Doppler glow with 360° drag orbit.
- Live ticking dual clocks driven by gravitational time dilation sqrt(1 - rs/r).
- Smooth interactive sliders for light-ray impact parameter, infall tidal gradient, VLBI dish toggling, and cosmic mass scale.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion by pausing auto-orbit rotation while keeping drag orbit and all lab sliders fully responsive.

## Imagery
100% procedural GLSL fragment shader black hole, starfield, and code-drawn interactive SVG/Canvas physics instruments.

## Tone
Awe-inspired scientific clarity—precise observatory instrumentation paired with lucid plain-English explanations.
