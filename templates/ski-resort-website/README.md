Ski Resort Website is a four-page site for a fictional mountain resort, built around the decisions a visitor makes before a ski trip. The homepage pairs a panoramic alpine photograph with a clearly labeled sample conditions report, then moves into an original interactive SVG trail map. Difficulty filters, selectable trails, lift status, and route details make the map useful rather than decorative. Dedicated tickets and rentals, lessons, and lodging pages continue the same planning journey with working client-side calculators and request states.

The visual system combines cold snow whites, deep spruce, ice blue, and restrained trail-rating colors. Bricolage Grotesque gives headlines a confident editorial shape, while Inter and IBM Plex Mono keep prices, labels, and mountain data easy to scan. Large photography, fine contour lines, compact data strips, and short reveal transitions give the site a polished alpine character without imitating an existing resort.

This template suits ski areas, snowboard resorts, winter retreats, and mountain hospitality businesses that need to explain conditions, terrain, prices, instruction, and stays in one coherent experience. All conditions and availability are explicitly illustrative; there is no backend, live feed, payment flow, or real reservation. The ticket estimator, lesson finder, lodging quote, filters, accordions, and mobile navigation all run locally.

The defaults describe Alderpeak Resort in invented Juniper Valley, Colorado. Five variables control the brand, location, hero tagline, audience, and adult day-ticket price. A second case can become a quieter family mountain resort while preserving the same structure. Copy, prices, trail data, FAQs, and image references live in one typed content file, so adapting the resort does not require restructuring components. Six coordinated generated photographs are downloaded into the project, with an original code-drawn trail map and local icons and fonts.

## Make it yours

- A boutique alpine lodge: set BRAND and LOCATION to your property, swap the trail map for local ski touring routes, and replace the lift ticket estimator with room and breakfast rates.
- A snowboard park and terrain school: keep the trail map filters, change trail ratings to park features, and rewrite the lesson finder around freestyle coaching and equipment rental.
- A regional tourism board: set AUDIENCE to first-time winter visitors, replace lodging with nearby stays, and use the conditions report to point people to several mountains.
- A mountain guiding company: keep the conditions bar, replace lift tickets with guided day rates, and turn the lesson request form into a private guide booking enquiry.

## FAQ

### How does the interactive trail map in the Ski Resort Website template work?
The Ski Resort Website template includes an original interactive SVG trail map with difficulty filters, selectable trails, lift status and route details. Visitors can filter by trail rating, click a trail to see its details, and check which lifts are running. The map is code-drawn rather than an image, so the trail data, ratings and lift status all live in the same typed content file you edit to match your mountain.

### Can the Ski Resort Website template take real lift ticket payments or bookings?
No. The Ski Resort Website template has no backend, payment flow or real reservation system. The ticket estimator, rental picker, lesson request form and lodging stay estimate all run locally in the browser and are explicitly illustrative. To take real money or bookings you would connect the forms and calculators to your own booking or payment service after adapting the template.

### What do I change first to turn the Ski Resort Website into my own resort?
Start with the five variables: BRAND, LOCATION, TAGLINE, AUDIENCE and TICKET_PRICE. The Ski Resort Website template derives every ticket category and multi-day rate from TICKET_PRICE, so setting your adult day ticket updates the whole pricing table. Then replace the trail records, lift statuses, conditions report, lesson options, lodging details and the six photographs in the typed content file.

### Is the Ski Resort Website template suitable for a small family mountain or a snowboard-focused area?
Yes. The Ski Resort Website template suits ski areas, snowboard resorts, winter retreats and mountain hospitality businesses. One of its live demos, built by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, is a quieter family mountain resort that keeps the same structure. Set AUDIENCE to your visitors, adjust trail ratings and lesson options for your terrain, and rewrite the copy for a family or snowboard-focused tone.
