A tactile, classroom-inspired biology explainer featuring a complete cutaway 3D animal cell built procedurally in Three.js with zero external model files. Rendered in a warm risograph print aesthetic with pastel fills and confident ink outlines, readers can orbit freely, click or scroll through 10 organelles (nucleus, rough and smooth ER, Golgi apparatus, mitochondria, lysosomes, ribosomes, centrioles, cytoskeleton, and channel-studded membrane), trace the 'Follow a Protein' pathway from DNA to secretion, toggle 3D labels, and test comprehension with an interactive quiz.

## FAQ

### What is included in the 3D Animal Cell Explainer template?
The 3D Animal Cell Explainer template is a single-page interactive biology explainer built in Three.js with no external model files. It includes a cutaway 3D animal cell you can orbit, a field guide to 10 organelles with plain-language captions and scale bars, a Follow a Protein pathway from DNA to secretion, a labels toggle, and a 5-question quiz. All copy lives in one content file.

### How do I change the brand name, title and course code on the 3D Animal Cell Explainer?
The 3D Animal Cell Explainer uses four variables: BRAND, TITLE, SUBTITLE and COURSE_TAG. Set BRAND to your school, studio or imprint, TITLE to your headline, SUBTITLE to the deck under it, and COURSE_TAG to your course or catalog label. The prompt asks you to rewrite every headline, caption and label to match those values in the same tone and length.

### Is there a free 3D animal cell model I can use to teach biology with AI?
The 3D Animal Cell Explainer is a free template on VibeFather whose cell is generated procedurally in Three.js, so there are no model files to download or license. Free templates' prompts and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0. Commercial or client teaching use needs Pro. The page also carries a note that the model is simplified and proportions are enlarged.

### How does the Follow a Protein pathway work in the 3D Animal Cell Explainer?
The Follow a Protein mode animates a glowing coral-butter cargo bead along a 3D path through the cell in five steps: DNA transcription in the nucleus, ribosome folding on rough ER, COPII vesicle shuttle, Golgi sugar tagging and sorting, then exocytosis at the plasma membrane. You can press Play or step through 1 to 5, and the camera tracks the bead.

### Can I add my own quiz questions and organelle captions to the 3D Animal Cell Explainer?
Yes. The 3D Animal Cell Explainer keeps all organelle captions, scale metrics, protein journey steps and quiz questions in one content file, so you can swap them without touching the 3D scene. If you change BRAND or TITLE, rewrite the copy for your course in the same length and tone so the layout and motion stay as designed.

### How do I build a 3D animal cell explainer with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add 3d-animal-cell-explainer, replace [BRAND], [TITLE], [SUBTITLE] and [COURSE_TAG] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.

### Which AI model built the 3D Animal Cell Explainer demo?
The Cellfolio Press · Inside the Animal Cell case was built by Gemini 4 Neon in Antigravity; the Biofield Studio · The Living Cell Cutaway case was built by GPT-6.2 Sol in Codex. Each build used the published prompt with nothing added, so you can compare the models side by side.

### What tech stack and pages does the 3D Animal Cell Explainer template use?
3D Animal Cell Explainer is a single-page site built with React, Three.js and Vite, with 3d model, scroll camera and interactive diagram. The prompt fixes the stack so every AI tool builds the same kind of project, and the result runs as a static site you can host anywhere.

### Can I use the 3D Animal Cell Explainer template for a client or a business?
The 3D Animal Cell Explainer prompt and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial projects under CC BY-NC 4.0. Client work and commercial use need VibeFather Pro, which also includes the source code of the live demo.

### Does the 3D Animal Cell Explainer template work on mobile?
Yes. Every 3D Animal Cell Explainer build was tested on a phone-sized screen as well as desktop before it went live, checking layout, text size and touch targets. Open the live demo on your phone, or switch the preview on this page to mobile view, to check it yourself.
