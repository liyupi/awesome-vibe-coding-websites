# Home Energy Dashboard

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
[BRAND] is a dark, single-screen analytics dashboard for [FOCUS] at [SITE_NAME], run by [OPERATOR_NAME]. It is an app shell with a fixed left sidebar, a sticky top bar holding a Day / Week / Month / Year range switcher, four KPI tiles with count-up numbers and sparklines, a hero consumption-vs-solar SVG line/area chart that morphs between data sets, a live-load SVG gauge, an SVG bar chart (by room or by hour) with staggered grow-in, a device list with real toggle switches that update the live totals, a time-of-use tariff strip and an activity timeline. All data is fake, deterministic and kept in one data file, and a single range state swaps every view. Default copy is written for a family home with solar panels and a heat pump. When the variable values differ, rewrite every piece of copy (greeting, site details, device names, rooms, tips, activity items) so it fits the new site, and keep the same structure, counts and number formats.

- Audience: People who run a home or small site with solar, a heat pump or heavy appliances and want to see in seconds where energy goes, what it costs and what is running right now. Builders use it as a starting screen for IoT, energy or building-management products.
- Goal: Make energy use readable at a glance and feel live: change the time range, hover a chart, flip a device off and watch the totals react. The screen should show careful, controlled SVG animation and a responsive app shell that works from 1440px desktop down to a 390px phone.
- Tone: Calm, precise, quietly technical. Short labels, sentence case, numbers first. Friendly but never cute. No exclamation marks.

## Variables

- [SITE_NAME] — Monitored site (default: "Juniper Lane house"). The home, shop or building being monitored. Shown in the site switcher and the top-bar subline.

- [CURRENCY_SYMBOL] — Currency symbol (default: "€"). Prefix for every cost and tariff value.
- [FOCUS] — What is monitored (default: "Home energy"). Short noun phrase, e.g. 'Home energy' or 'Kitchen energy'. Used in the top-bar subline and the page title.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/home-energy-dashboard in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 4px base unit. Desktop main area: padding 28px top, 32px sides, 40px bottom, max-width 1320px. 12-column grid with a 20px gap. Card padding 20px, card header to content gap 16px. Mobile (<768px): padding 16px, grid gap 12px, card padding 16px, and 88px bottom padding to clear the tab bar. All numbers use tabular figures (.num).
- Radius: Cards 16px. Buttons, inputs, icon tiles and nav items 10px. Segmented controls 12px outer, 9px inner pill. Chips and switches fully rounded. Bars 6px top corners (rooms) and 3px (hours). Tooltips 10px.
- Motion: UI easing cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1). Data tweens use easeInOutCubic (morphs) or easeOutCubic (count-ups and grow-ins), driven by requestAnimationFrame.
- Motion: On load, cards fade up 12px over 450ms, staggered 60ms in DOM order (KPI tiles first).
- Motion: KPI values count up from the previous value to the new one over 900ms easeOutCubic. Delta chips cross-fade over 200ms.
- Motion: Usage chart: on first view the usage line draws with stroke-dashoffset from full length to 0 over 1200ms, and the area fills from opacity 0 to 1 over 600ms after a 300ms delay. On range change, both series and the y-scale max morph over 700ms, and x labels fade out over 150ms and back in over 200ms.
- Motion: Bars grow from scaleY(0) with transform-origin at the bottom over 600ms easeOutCubic, staggered 40ms, the first time the card is 30% in view. On range change their heights tween over 500ms. On mode switch the chart cross-fades over 150ms and the bars grow in again.
- Motion: The range switcher pill slides with translateX over 320ms. The switch knob slides 16px over 180ms. The gauge arc and center number tween over 600ms. Device wattage counts to its new value over 400ms, and the toggled row flashes rgba(198,255,61,0.06) for 600ms.
- Motion: The live dot (top-bar pill and tariff now-marker) loops the pulse-ring animation every 2s.
- Motion: Drawers slide in over 280ms with a 200ms overlay fade. Toasts use toast-in and auto-dismiss after 3000ms.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: no draw-ins, morphs, staggers, pulses or slides. Values and positions change instantly, and the live numbers still update every 2s without tweening.
- Imagery: No photos or bitmaps. Everything visual is SVG or CSS drawn in code: charts, sparklines, gauge, tariff strip, the logo mark, and initials avatars on Raised circles. Lucide icons are used at 16–18px with a 1.75 stroke. Depth comes only from 1px borders, soft radial lime glows behind the chart cards (radial-gradient(600px circle at 30% 0%, rgba(198,255,61,0.08), transparent 60%)) and drop-shadow glows on active data points. Tooltips and drawers use the shadow 0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.4). No light theme.

## Don'ts
- No backend: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, third-party APIs or forms that submit anywhere. All data is local and fake, in src/data/energy.ts.
- No chart or visualisation libraries (Recharts, Chart.js, D3, Nivo, ECharts, ApexCharts, Victory) and no animation libraries. Every chart and tween is hand-written SVG and requestAnimationFrame code.
- Do not add dependencies beyond the listed stack, and do not change the stack unless the user asks.
- No Tailwind Plus / Tailwind UI, Aceternity UI, ThemeForest or purchased template source.
- No real energy-company, utility, solar, smart-home or app names, logos or device brands. Use [BRAND] and the generic device names given.
- No copy, slogans or metric wording copied from existing energy apps. Use only the copy written here, rewritten to fit the variables.
- No brand palettes from existing dashboards. Use only the listed hex values, keep Lime as the single accent and Amber only for peaks and warnings. No light theme.
- No photos of people and no avatar images. Avatars are code-drawn initials only.
- No commercial or custom fonts, and no font loading from fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any CDN. Fonts come only from the Fontsource packages.
- No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no hotlinked files, no analytics or tracking.
- No Math.random and no system-clock dates. Every number and date is fixed in the data file so every load looks identical.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, nothing from src/ copied into dist/, relative asset paths (base './'), dist/ under 20 MB.
- Internal navigation uses in-page hash anchors (#usage, #rooms, #devices, #tariffs) or file names like ./index.html, never bare directory links. No dead links.
- Zero console errors and no horizontal page scroll at 1440×900 and 390×844.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: disable draw-ins, morphs, staggers, pulses, slides and smooth scrolling.
- No SEO extras: no meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD or sitemaps. Just the <title> and favicon.
- No mention of VibeFather anywhere in the site except an optional credit link if the prompt adds one.
