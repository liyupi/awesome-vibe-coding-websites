# Sticker Shop Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Cherry red** `#FF2D55` — Large hero half, catalog dividers and original artwork. WCAG relative-luminance calculations: #141126 text on #FF2D55: 5.06:1; #141126 text on #FFF35C: 16.02:1; #141126 text on #FFFFFF: 18.45:1; #FFFFFF text on #254DFF: 5.86:1; #141126 text on #19C37D: 8.04:1. Use these pairs for every normal-size label/body/button. Decorative overlaps/gradients carry no text; readable text gets a solid backing.
- **Lemon** `#FFF35C` — Large hero half and pack-building desk.
- **Ink** `#141126` — Body and controls on cherry/lemon/white.
- **White** `#FFFFFF` — Die-cut border and product reading panels.
- **Cobalt** `#254DFF` — Secondary solid panels and selected pack actions.
- **Leaf green** `#19C37D` — Decorative sticker art and selected-count chips.

## Typography
- **Display** — Baloo 2 (OFL), weights 700. Self-host the font files.
- **Body** — Nunito Sans (OFL), weights 400/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: 1200px max, 40px desktop/20px mobile gutters, 8px unit, 88px/52px sections. 4col catalog/2 mobile, pack desk 55/45 with 3×2 visible slots. H1 92px/54px.
- Radius: 20px product photos/panels, 12px controls, 999px category tags. Stickers have contour-following 6–10px white die-cut borders and soft shadows, not square image frames.

## Motion
- Sticker hover/focus tilt maximum 3deg and a small corner curl for 160ms; tap Preview peel button toggles the same effect without adding a product. Drag can be an optional enhancement, never required to fill slots.
- Cart drawer 200ms slide, reduced motion instant; no floating sticker loop, cursor replacement or motion that hides prices.

## Imagery
Four unified photorealistic tabletop product images with hard-soft daylight, cherry red/lemon colored surfaces, unbranded notebooks and mailers, original abstract rainbow die-cut stickers with clean white edges. No existing characters, faces, logos, slogans or lettered sticker designs. Product thumbnails are original inline SVG artworks with the exact recurring original motifs, not random emoji. Use photography for physical storefront credibility and SVG for precise selectable items. Any visible hand belongs to a fictional person, no face.

## Tone
Playful, specific and useful about size, materials and price; no fake sales urgency or real checkout claims.
