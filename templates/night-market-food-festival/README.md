# Night Market Food Festival

Night Market Food Festival is a neon-night single-page template for an invented street-food market. The first screen is all atmosphere: a lantern-lit stall photograph after dusk under a deep indigo sky, a headline with one amber-glow word, and the market's hours and yard spelled out in chips. From there the page does a festival's real work — helping visitors plan a night and helping cooks apply for a stall.

The centerpiece is a code-drawn vendor map: a stylized top-down layout of the yard with two stages and eighteen numbered stalls, filterable by eight cuisine chips. Pins dim and glow as filters change, and tapping one opens a card with the vendor's signature dish and token price. A two-stage schedule lays out a typical weekend in weekday-pattern time blocks, a tickets section explains the three pass tiers and the token economy, and a vendor application form validates and saves locally with an honest demo note.

The look is one consistent dusk grade across six photographs: deep indigo ground, lantern amber, restrained neon pink, cream type, and CSS glow used sparingly on accents only. Bricolage Grotesque headlines over Plus Jakarta Sans body keep the night friendly rather than cyberpunk.

This template suits night markets, food-truck rallies, lantern festivals, and cultural street fairs. Swap the brand, city, venue, and hours to retune it; vendors, stalls, and the schedule are records in one content file. The stack is Vite, React, Tailwind, and Motion with self-hosted fonts, static output, no payments, no submissions, and nothing tied to a real calendar.

## FAQ

### How does the vendor map and cuisine filter work on the Night Market Food Festival template?
The Night Market Food Festival template draws its map in code: a stylized top-down view of the yard with two labeled stages and numbered pins for eighteen stalls, plus a tokens booth and first aid. Eight cuisine chips filter the pins — matches keep full opacity with an amber glow ring, non-matches fade to 25%, and tapping a pin opens a card with the vendor's signature dish and token price.

### Can I change the vendors, stalls and schedule to match my own market?
Vendors, stalls and schedule blocks are records in one content file, so you can rewrite names, cuisines, signature dishes, token prices and stall numbers without touching the layout. The two-stage schedule uses weekday-pattern time blocks for both stages. Change the brand, city, venue and hours variables and the dependent copy, storage key and map intro follow.

### What are the token ticket tiers and does the template take payments?
The Night Market Food Festival template explains three pass tiers and a token economy in a tickets section, with token prices shown on each vendor card. It is a static page: there are no payments, no submissions and nothing tied to a real calendar, so visitors read the tiers and plan their night rather than buying online.

### How does the vendor application form work if there is no backend?
The vendor application form validates entries and saves them to the browser's local storage under a key derived from the brand name, with an honest demo note on the page. Nothing is sent anywhere. To collect real applications you would connect the form to your own form service or mailing list after adapting the template.
