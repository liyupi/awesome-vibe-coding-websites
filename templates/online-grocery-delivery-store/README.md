An online grocery shop that behaves like one. The home page opens on a banana-yellow panel beside a crate of fresh produce, then drops into the aisles: forty-five everyday products in a dense grid with a quantity stepper on every card, so adding two avocados and a loaf takes two clicks. Search filters as you type and aisle chips narrow the grid. The basket page has a free-delivery meter, a delivery slot picker with seven days of two-hour windows, and substitution preferences, and ends in a sample order that is saved to your past orders. Recipes are the shortcut: each one lists its ingredients from the shop, shows three short steps and has one button that adds everything to the basket. Past orders can be reordered in one click. All product photographs come from one bright studio shoot with plain packaging and no labels, and the palette is leaf green, tomato red and banana yellow on white with a rounded display face. Everything is stored in the browser. Change the shop name, delivery town and free-delivery threshold in the content file, and edit products and prices in the seed; layout, palette and motion stay as described.

## Make it yours

- A village greengrocer that delivers: set BRAND and CITY to your shop and town, then trim the forty-five products down to the produce you actually stock each week.
- A farm shop box scheme: raise FREE_DELIVERY to your box threshold, rewrite the recipes around seasonal produce and use the delivery slot picker for your two-hour drop windows.
- A small health food store: keep the aisle chips but rename them to supplements, grains and dairy-free, and rework the recipes into simple everyday meals from your shelves.
- A market stall going online: set BRAND to the stall name, keep the quantity steppers for weighed items and link the past orders page so regulars can reorder their usual basket.

## FAQ

### How do I change the shop name, delivery town and free-delivery threshold in the Online Grocery Delivery Store template?
The Online Grocery Delivery Store template keeps three variables in its content file: BRAND for the shop name, CITY for the delivery town and FREE_DELIVERY for the basket total that unlocks free delivery. Change those three values, then rewrite every headline, label and paragraph that mentions the old ones so the copy stays consistent across the shop, recipes, basket and orders pages.

### How do I edit the products, prices and aisles in the Online Grocery Delivery Store template?
Products, prices, aisle names, recipes, delivery slots and past orders all live in the seed data file. Edit the product entries to match your own stock, set each price, and rename or regroup the aisle chips so the search and filter still make sense. The product photographs crop from studio image sheets, so swap in your own images and update the cell positions for each tile.

### How does the recipe-to-basket feature work in the Online Grocery Delivery Store template?
Each recipe in the Online Grocery Delivery Store template lists ingredients that come from the shop, shows three short steps and has one button that adds everything to the basket at once. Recipes are a shortcut for shoppers who do not want to search aisle by aisle. To adapt them, replace the recipe entries in the seed with your own dishes and point each ingredient at the matching product.

### Can I use the Online Grocery Delivery Store template for a client's grocery or farm shop site?
Commercial and client use of the Online Grocery Delivery Store template needs Pro. Pro templates show the brief, stack and variables publicly and give Pro members the full prompt plus the source code of the demos. Free prompts and every DESIGN.md are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only, so a paid client build requires the Pro route.

### Where is the basket and order data stored in the Online Grocery Delivery Store template?
The Online Grocery Delivery Store template stores the basket, past orders and delivery preferences in the browser using localStorage, keyed by a slug built from the shop name. There is no backend, server code or external API, so placing an order saves a sample order to the past orders list rather than sending it anywhere. Connecting a real checkout or order system would need work beyond the template.
