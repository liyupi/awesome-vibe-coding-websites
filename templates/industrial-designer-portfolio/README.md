A portfolio for an industrial designer of household objects, built around process rather than a gallery of pretty renders. The first screen pairs a condensed technical headline with a studio group shot on a graphite panel, annotated with orange dimension lines. Each project page walks through the whole job: research notes, pencil sketches drawn in code, a photograph of the hand-shaped foam model, a colour, material and finish board with real swatches, the final render and a spec table. Two of the four objects also sit in a 3D turntable built in code with three.js: drag to rotate, and switch the body between chalk matte, safety orange and graphite. A graphite process band, a material library of named swatches, an awards and press timeline and a one-click CV download complete the site. Renders come from one clean studio shoot, the palette is concrete grey, graphite and a single safety orange, and the type is a condensed display face with a plain text face and a mono for labels. Change the name, city and featured product in the content file, swap in your own renders under the same file names, and edit the CV text; layout, palette and motion stay as described.

## Make it yours

- A furniture designer: set BRAND to your studio, swap the kettle and lamp for seating and tables, and rewrite the CMF boards with your own timber and textile swatches.
- A housewares startup founder: use the process pages to show one product from research to spec, and point the CV download at a deck or investor one-pager instead.
- A design studio with several people: set BRAND to the studio name, replace the studio group shot and add each designer's projects to the work index with their own spec tables.
- A design graduate building a first portfolio: keep the four objects, rewrite the research notes for student projects and use the awards timeline for competitions and press.

## FAQ

### What does the Industrial Designer Portfolio template include besides the project pages?
The Industrial Designer Portfolio template includes a home page and a work index, plus project pages that walk through research notes, pencil sketches, a foam model photograph, a CMF board with real swatches, a final render and a spec table. It also has a graphite process band, a material library of named swatches, an awards and press timeline, a one-click CV download and a 3D turntable for two of the four objects.

### How do I turn this industrial design portfolio into my own site?
Change BRAND, CITY and FEATURED_PRODUCT in the content file, then swap your own renders in under the same file names and edit the CV text. All copy, products, process steps, swatches, awards and press live in one typed content file, so you can rewrite every headline and paragraph for your own objects without touching the layout, palette or motion.

### Can I use the 3D turntable for my own product models?
The 3D turntable in the Industrial Designer Portfolio template is built in code with Three.js from primitives, not loaded model files. Two of the four objects rotate on drag and switch between chalk matte, safety orange and graphite. To show your own product you would rebuild its geometry in the same code-built approach, since the template does not load external 3D files.

### Is this portfolio template good for a freelance industrial designer looking for clients?
The Industrial Designer Portfolio template is written for design directors at housewares brands, startups preparing a first product and studios looking for a freelance industrial designer. It leads with process rather than finished renders, so the CV download and the enquiry path sit at the end of a walkthrough that shows research, sketches, CMF boards and specs for each object.

### What do I need to change first in the Industrial Designer Portfolio template?
Start with BRAND, CITY and FEATURED_PRODUCT in the content file, then replace the four objects and their renders under the same file names. After that, rewrite the research notes, CMF swatches and spec tables for your own products, and update the awards, press timeline and CV text so the site reads as your studio rather than the default designer.

### How do I build an industrial designer portfolio with AI?
Copy the full prompt (Pro) from this page or run npx vibefather add industrial-designer-portfolio, replace [BRAND], [CITY] and [FEATURED_PRODUCT] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.

Industrial Designer Portfolio is a 2-page site built with React, Tailwind CSS and Three.js, with 3d / webgl and micro-interactions. The prompt fixes the stack so every AI tool builds the same kind of project, and the result runs as a static site you can host anywhere.
