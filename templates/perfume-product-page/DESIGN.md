# Perfume Product Page — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Blush** `#F7E4E0` — Page ground — soft pink porcelain, the whole site's wash.
- **Blush deep** `#EFCFC9` — Alternate surfaces, the pyramid's heart band, chip hovers.
- **Silk** `#FDF8F5` — Panels, cards, ingredient chips, size chips at rest — warm near-white.
- **Amber** `#C9863B` — The primary accent: CTAs, active size chip, hairline golds, the pyramid base, leader lines.
- **Amber light** `#E0B273` — Hover and gradient end; underline accents on links.
- **Iris mauve** `#9C7F96` — Secondary accent: quiet links, the heart-note chips, captions' hover.
- **Cocoa** `#4A3833` — Primary text — warm dark brown, never black.
- **Rose smoke** `#A98F88` — Secondary text: intros, captions, sample notes.

## Typography
- **Display — fragrance name, headlines, prices (airy serif, often italic)** — Cormorant Garamond (OFL), weights 500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body, labels, chips (airy geometric sans, tracked labels in uppercase)** — Josefin Sans (OFL), weights 300/400. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Soft and generous: sections py-32 (128px) desktop / py-20 mobile; content max-w-5xl — a narrow, intimate column; the hero keeps 120px clear around the bottle; body copy capped at 48ch; hairlines are amber at 35% opacity.
- Radius: Round and gentle: 999px pills on buttons, chips and size pickers; 18px on panels, images and the upsell card; nothing square.

## Motion
- Hero: the bottle photograph parallaxes at 0.06 of scroll and slowly scales 1.03→1 over 2.5s on load; the fragrance name fades up 300ms behind it.
- Scent pyramid: each band unfolds with scaleX 0→1 from center (900ms ease-out) as it crosses 40% of the viewport, top first, then heart, then base; each band's ingredient chips slide in beside it 150ms later, connected by leader lines that draw in (500ms).
- Mood strip: the three photographs fade up staggered 120ms and drift 0.04 parallax; captions fade last.
- Size picker: switching chips cross-fades the price line (300ms) and fills the active chip amber with silk text (250ms); the demo bag button shows its inline note with a 300ms fade.
- Reveals: sections, quotes and cards fade up (y 22→0, 800ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)) with 100ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once.
- prefers-reduced-motion: the pyramid renders fully unfolded and static with all chips visible, parallax and load-scale off, every reveal a plain 200ms opacity fade.

## Imagery
One warm studio session: soft editorial still-life photography — the glass bottle with a strictly blank label on draped blush silk, folds of blush satin, rough amber resin, a single iris blossom, the amber stopper in macro, three sample vials — all in the same blush-and-amber grade, one warm diffused light, soft rose shadows, airy high-key warmth, gentle filmic softness, shallow depth of field. No people, no readable text anywhere, no logos. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
Soft, sensual, a little literary — short poetic lines that describe feelings, not chemistry; honest about being sample copy.
