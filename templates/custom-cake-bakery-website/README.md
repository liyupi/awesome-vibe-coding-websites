Custom Cake Bakery Website is a warm, editorial storefront built around a working cake configurator rather than a static contact form. It suits independent bakeries, home-based cake studios, and patisseries that want customers to explore sizes, tiers, flavors, frosting, and decorations before making an inquiry. The default content follows an invented garden-inspired bakery, while a second variable set can turn it into a retro celebration-cake shop without changing the layout.

The opening screen pairs concise ordering guidance with a large, naturally lit cake photograph. A styled gallery establishes the range of finishes, and the main builder turns choices into a responsive SVG cake with visible tiers, filling bands, frosting colors, sugar decorations, and an optional inscription. Every option updates an itemized estimate. A pickup field enforces the bakery's lead time, and the final summary collects the practical details needed for an order request. Because the finished demo is static, the final action prepares a copyable request instead of pretending to submit an order or take payment.

Fraunces headings, DM Sans body copy, cream paper tones, cherry accents, and restrained botanical details give the design a handmade but polished character. Motion is limited to useful feedback, gentle section reveals, and smooth preview changes. The desktop builder uses a sticky visual summary; mobile layouts keep controls large and the cake preview easy to reach.

Adapt the brand, city, specialty, lead time, pickup address, and order email through six variables. Cake options, prices, opening hours, testimonials, FAQs, and image references remain centralized for straightforward editing. Six coordinated generated photographs provide the hero, gallery, and process imagery, while the cake preview and icons are drawn locally in code.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Bakery name | Fable & Frost |
| `[CITY]` | City and region | Portland, Oregon |
| `[SPECIALTY]` | Cake specialty | garden-inspired celebration cakes |
| `[LEAD_TIME_DAYS]` | Minimum notice in days | 4 |
| `[PICKUP_ADDRESS]` | Pickup address | 18 Marigold Lane, Portland, Oregon |
| `[ORDER_EMAIL]` | Order email | orders@fableandfrost.example |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add custom-cake-bakery-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A home-based cake studio: set BRAND and CITY to your kitchen, raise LEAD_TIME_DAYS to your real notice, and swap the gallery entries for your own finishes and flavors.
- A wedding cake specialist: point SPECIALTY at tiered wedding cakes, keep the tier and decoration options, and adjust the estimate copy to mention tastings and consultation.
- A patisserie with a shop counter: set PICKUP_ADDRESS and opening hours to the storefront, and add collection guidance to the visit section and copied request.
- A seasonal holiday bakery: rewrite the gallery and flavor options around your current range, and set ORDER_EMAIL so requests land in the inbox you check daily.

## FAQ

### How does the cake configurator on the Custom Cake Bakery Website work?
The Custom Cake Bakery Website template's builder turns your choices for size, tiers, flavor, frosting and decorations into a live SVG cake drawn in code, not a photo. Every option updates an itemized estimate with a running total, and an optional inscription appears on the cake. Visitors finish by preparing a copyable pickup request rather than a real checkout or payment.

### What should I change first after downloading the Custom Cake Bakery Website template?
Set the six variables first: BRAND, CITY, SPECIALTY, ORDER_EMAIL, LEAD_TIME_DAYS and PICKUP_ADDRESS. These feed the header, hero, pickup guidance, visit section and the copied order request. Then replace the six generated photographs and edit the cake options and prices, opening hours, testimonials and FAQs, which stay centralized in the content and cake options files.

### Can customers actually order and pay through the Custom Cake Bakery Website template?
No. The Custom Cake Bakery Website template is a static site with no backend, so the final step prepares a copyable request showing the bakery's order email instead of submitting an order or taking payment. That keeps it honest for a small bakery: customers send the request, then you confirm details and payment directly with them.

### How does the lead time and pickup date work in the Custom Cake Bakery Website template?
The Custom Cake Bakery Website template uses the LEAD_TIME_DAYS variable to enforce a minimum notice period on the pickup date field, so a visitor cannot choose a date inside that window. The same number appears in the hero detail line and ordering copy. Set LEAD_TIME_DAYS to your real notice period, and set PICKUP_ADDRESS to where customers collect.

### What is included in the Custom Cake Bakery Website template besides the cake builder?
The Custom Cake Bakery Website template is a single page with a sticky header and navigation, a hero with a large cake photograph, a gallery of four finishes, the configurator, a process section explaining how ordering works, a visit section with pickup details, plus opening hours, testimonials and FAQs. Six coordinated generated photographs cover the hero, gallery and process, while the cake preview and icons are drawn in code.

Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add custom-cake-bakery-website, replace [BRAND], [CITY], [SPECIALTY] and [LEAD_TIME_DAYS] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
