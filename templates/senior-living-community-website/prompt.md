# Senior Living Community Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build an original single-page website for [BRAND], a [COMMUNITY_TYPE] in [LOCATION]. It serves [AUDIENCE] with large readable type, a persistent text-size control, a clear comparison of independent living, assisted living, and memory care, a filterable daily-life gallery, sample dining menus, selectable floor plans, and a client-side tour request. Use the default English copy below, and rewrite every brand-specific, location-specific, audience-specific, and care-specific detail when variable values change.

- Audience: Older adults researching their own next home and adult children, partners, or caregivers comparing communities with them. The secondary audience is a senior-living operator who needs an accessible, trustworthy marketing and tour-request site.
- Goal: Make care options and daily life easy to understand, let visitors explore at their own reading size and pace, answer practical questions about meals and residences, and provide a clear tour request without backend services or false submission claims.
- Tone: Warm, calm, respectful, and concrete. Speak to residents as independent adults, never as patients or children. Use plain US English, specific routines and inclusions, no fear-based selling, no clinical promises, and no exaggerated claims.

## Stack
- Framework: Vite 6 + React 19 + TypeScript
- Styling: Tailwind CSS 4
- Libraries: react (MIT), react-dom (MIT), tailwindcss (MIT), @tailwindcss/vite (MIT), motion (MIT), lucide-react (ISC), @fontsource-variable/fraunces (OFL-1.1), @fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible (OFL-1.1)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Configure vite.config.ts with base './', build.sourcemap false, and the React and Tailwind Vite plugins. The production entry is index.html and every asset URL must be relative.
  - Use Tailwind CSS 4 with a small src/index.css theme for the exact colors, font families, focus rings, text-size variants, keyframes, print rules, and reduced-motion overrides. Keep reusable interface pieces in src/components.
  - Put all variable defaults, headlines, paragraphs, navigation labels, care descriptions, comparison values, gallery captions, menu items, floor-plan details, reviews, FAQs, contact details, and asset paths in src/content.ts. Rewrite default-specific content when variables change; never leave the previous brand, location, audience, community type, or phone number behind.
  - Keep shared state in src/App.tsx. Implement text-size preference, gallery filters, lightbox state, care and dining tabs, floor-plan selection, mobile navigation, and the tour-request draft in React. Persist only text-size preference and the local tour request in localStorage, with safe fallbacks when storage is unavailable.
  - Import self-hosted Fraunces Variable and Atkinson Hyperlegible 400/700 in src/main.tsx. Use Motion for React through motion/react and Lucide React for interface icons; do not load any CDN resource.
  - Create an original favicon from a rounded architectural arch and two simple evergreen leaf shapes. Do not imitate a real community logo or use a recognizable facility emblem.
  - Use semantic landmarks, a skip link, labelled controls, keyboard-operable tabs and dialogs, visible focus, and an aria-live confirmation. All tap targets are at least 44×44px and primary form controls are at least 52px high.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Community name (default: "Avenbrook Community"). An invented senior-living community name used throughout the site.
- [LOCATION] — Community location (default: "Asheville, North Carolina"). A city and state or region; adapt the address and local references to match.
- [COMMUNITY_TYPE] — Community type (default: "full-service senior living community"). A short lowercase phrase describing the community model.
- [AUDIENCE] — Primary audience (default: "older adults and their families"). A short lowercase phrase describing who the community welcomes.
- [PHONE] — Tour phone number (default: "(828) 555-0146"). A display and telephone-link number; use fictional sample contact details.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/senior-living-community-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Design
### Colors
- Ivory `#F7F3EA` — Main page canvas and warm negative space.
- Paper `#FFFDF8` — Header, cards, form controls, menus, and lightbox surfaces.
- Evergreen `#183C34` — Primary text, dark buttons, footer, active controls, and strong icon strokes.
- Soft Pine `#52665D` — Secondary copy at 16px or larger; never use for faint microtype.
- Sage `#DEE7D9` — Selected tabs, care highlights, schedule rows, and soft information panels.
- Clay `#984B34` — Small editorial accents, underlines, and secondary emphasis; pair with Ivory or Paper text only where contrast passes.
- Honey `#E6B65C` — Active marker and small decorative highlight behind Evergreen text, never body text on Ivory.
- Linen Line `#CBD1C5` — One-pixel borders, input outlines, comparison rules, and separators.

### Typography
- Display, section headings, and large quotations: Fraunces Variable (OFL), weights 500/600/700
- Body, navigation, controls, tables, and numbers: Atkinson Hyperlegible (OFL), weights 400/700

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: Use a centered 1200px maximum container with 32px desktop gutters and 20px mobile gutters. Build on an 8px base grid. Desktop sections have 96px vertical padding and mobile sections 64px. The hero has 48px top and 64px bottom padding. Cards use 24–32px padding, form rows 20px gaps, and body copy a maximum measure of 68 characters.
- Radius: Use 28px corners for hero and gallery photography, 20px for major panels and dialogs, 14px for cards and inputs, and full pills only for text-size, filter, and time-slot controls. Use 1px Linen Line borders and very restrained shadows rather than floating every section.
- Motion: Reveal major sections once with Motion whileInView: opacity 0 to 1 and y 14 to 0 over 420ms, stagger direct children by 60ms, viewport amount 0.16.
- Motion: Use 180–220ms transitions for tabs, gallery filtering, mobile navigation, accordion height, buttons, and focus states; never delay reading while animation completes.
- Motion: Open the gallery lightbox with a 180ms opacity and 8px scale transition; support Escape, left/right arrows, focus trapping, and restoring focus to the originating thumbnail.
- Motion: Crossfade the selected care detail, dining menu, and floor-plan drawing over 180ms while keeping panel dimensions stable enough to avoid layout jumps.
- Motion: When prefers-reduced-motion is enabled, remove transforms and smooth scrolling, show final states immediately, and keep every interaction functional.
- Imagery: Use six coordinated photorealistic editorial images generated specifically for this template. The shared look is soft late-morning light, muted sage, cream, honey oak, and terracotta, realistic residential materials, subtle 35mm film grain, and calm documentary composition. Show an operating community through spaces, activities, and meals rather than institutional corridors or posed clinical scenes. People are fictional, varied in age and ethnicity, and photographed only from behind or at a distance with no recognizable face. The hero is a spacious courtyard scene, never a face close-up. Download every image locally and list each in ASSETS.md with the supplied generated-asset credit. Do not add other photographs, logos, readable signs, watermarks, or branded objects.

## Don'ts
- Do not use a backend, server code, API routes, database, authentication, external form service, third-party API, analytics, or real network submission. The tour request is local-only and must say that no message was sent.
- Do not produce anything other than a static production build in dist/ with one npm run build command, relative asset paths, minified output, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, and total size under 20 MB.
- Do not use bare-directory links, broken routes, placeholder-image services, CDN scripts, CDN stylesheets, remote font loads, hotlinked images, or remote media. All assets must ship locally and internal navigation must use working section anchors on index.html.
- Do not use Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Google Images, real community photography, copied floor plans, or any asset with a restrictive or non-redistributable license. Use only the six supplied generated images, original SVG plans, the listed icon library, and self-hosted fonts.
- Do not show recognizable people, real resident or staff likenesses, celebrities, tight face close-ups, logos, branded uniforms, readable facility signs, watermarks, or trademarked emblems. Generated people must remain fictional and faces must not be identifiable.
- Do not use real community names, existing slogans, branded typography, mascots, signature illustrations, unique iconography, copied copy, medical outcome promises, unverified awards, accreditation seals, review-platform badges, or claims about real availability.
- Do not make the site feel clinical, institutional, childish, or fear-driven. Avoid hospital-blue palettes, medical-cross branding, exaggerated smiling stock portraits, pressure countdowns, and language that treats older adults as dependents.
- Do not hide essential information behind hover, autoplay, animation, or horizontal page scrolling. No autoplay carousel, continuous parallax, custom cursor, scroll-jacking, or motion required to understand the content.
- Do not use low-contrast text, placeholder-only form labels, icon-only controls without accessible names, tiny targets, keyboard traps, clipped large text, or color-only status encoding. Maintain visible focus, 44×44px minimum targets, reduced-motion support, and zero console errors at 1440×900 and 390×844.
- Do not leave default-specific brand, location, audience, community type, phone number, address, rates, menus, care copy, or testimonials unchanged when variables differ. Keep all such content in src/content.ts and rewrite it coherently.
- Do not add SEO metadata, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, sitemap work, keyword-stuffed headings, publication dates, or unrelated pages. A plain title containing [BRAND] and the original favicon are sufficient.
- Do not mention VibeFather inside the site except the optional [CREDIT] footer link, and never claim that the local demo form contacted or confirmed anything with staff.
