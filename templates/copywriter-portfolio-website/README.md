A portfolio for a freelance copywriter in which the words are the design. The first screen sets an oversized serif headline against a copy-deck card drawn in code, where a tired line is struck through and replaced by a sharper one, so the value of the work is visible before a single sample is read. The samples sit in one filterable list grouped by type: websites, product pages, email, taglines and long-form. Each sample shows the brief, the before and the after side by side, and a row of results marked as sample numbers. A tone-of-voice playground lets a visitor pick a personality, warm, wry, direct, playful, premium or plain, and watch the same sentence change, with a note on what each voice does and a button to copy the result. Services are three plain packages with prices and turnaround, followed by client quotes, a newsletter sign-up and a contact form. The palette is cream, ink and a single highlighter green used like a marker on key words, the type pairs a characterful serif display with a clean sans and a mono for briefs and numbers, and there are no photographs at all. Change the name, city and specialty in the content file and swap in your own before and after pairs; the layout, palette and motion stay as they are.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Copywriter's name | Ada Whitlock |
| `[CITY]` | City | Philadelphia |
| `[SPECIALTY]` | What you write | websites, product pages and email |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add copywriter-portfolio-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A brand voice consultant: keep the tone-of-voice playground, set SPECIALTY to brand voice and messaging, and swap the samples for voice guidelines before and after.
- A UX writer: set SPECIALTY to product and interface copy, then replace the samples with button, onboarding and error-message pairs grouped under product pages.
- An email marketing freelancer: set SPECIALTY to email and lifecycle copy, and rebuild the sample list around welcome, win-back and launch emails with briefs.
- A content strategist: keep the long-form samples, set BRAND and CITY to your studio, and rewrite the packages around audits, editorial calendars and article writing.

## FAQ

### How do I swap in my own writing samples and before and after pairs?
The Copywriter Portfolio Website template keeps every sample in one content file as typed objects, so you replace the brief, the before line, the after line and the result chips for each entry without touching the layout. The samples are grouped by type: websites, product pages, email, taglines and long-form. Keep the before and after pairs short and specific, and label results as sample numbers rather than claiming real outcomes.

### What does the tone-of-voice playground actually do?
The tone-of-voice playground on the Copywriter Portfolio Website template lets a visitor pick a personality, warm, wry, direct, playful, premium or plain, and watch one sentence rewrite itself in that voice. Each voice carries a short note on what it does, and a button copies the result. The sentences and notes live in the content file, so you can point the playground at your own example line.

### Is there a free copywriter portfolio website template I can build with AI?
Yes. The Copywriter Portfolio Website template on VibeFather is free, and its prompt and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0. Commercial and client work needs Pro, and Pro members also get the source code of the demos. The page is a single home page with samples, the tone playground, services, client quotes, a newsletter sign-up and a contact form.

### What should I change first to make this portfolio my own?
Set BRAND, CITY and SPECIALTY in the content file first, since the wordmark, the hero eyebrow and the intro paragraph all read from them. Then replace the client names with brands you have actually worked with and rewrite the samples around your own briefs. The layout, the cream and ink palette, the highlighter green and the motion stay as they are.

### How do the services packages and turnaround work on this template?
The Copywriter Portfolio Website template shows services as three plain packages with prices and turnaround, written as sample content in the content file. You edit the package names, the included work and the turnaround to match what you actually offer. The packages sit after the samples and before the client quotes, and the header button and hero buttons lead visitors down to the contact form.
