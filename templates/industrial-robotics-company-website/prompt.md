# Industrial Robotics Company Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A one-page, steel-grey B2B site for [BRAND] Robotics, a maker of original industrial robot arms for [PRIMARY_USE]. The hero is a full-bleed factory-bay photograph with the arm as anchor and mono spec chips. Then: a four-stat band; tabbed spec sheets for three arms (TL-120, TL-190, TL-260) each paired with an original code-drawn SVG side-profile arm; four industry cards with proof lines; a full-width install band photo; an ROI calculator (operators and shifts sliders, local math from the selected arm, spelled out exactly); three case studies with photos; and a client-side demo-request form. Specs, prices and ROI outputs are invented samples, labeled as such.

- Audience: [AUDIENCE] evaluating capital equipment, plus hardware founders and automation integrators adapting the template for any spec-driven machine.
- Goal: Put hard numbers first — specs, proof, payback — and convert plant teams into demo requests through the #demo form.
- Tone: Industrial and precise. Short declarative sentences, engineering units, mono-labeled data. Confident without slogans; every claim is a labeled sample number.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Brand name (default: "Torvan"). Invented company name; the site brands itself '[BRAND] Robotics'. Never a real robotics company, and never a name or mark that evokes one.
- [AUDIENCE] — Who buys it (default: "plant managers at mid-size US factories"). Plural noun phrase. Used in the hero subhead, industries intro and demo-form copy.
- [PRIMARY_USE] — Main use case (default: "palletizing and machine tending"). Lowercase phrase for what the arms do all day; drives the hero, industries, cases and footer line. Swap for welding, dispensing, lab handling and rewrite the proof lines to match.
- [LABOR_RATE] — Sample labor rate (default: "$38"). Fully-loaded hourly rate used as the ROI calculator's default (digits after a currency symbol). A sample number the calculator labels as editable.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/industrial-robotics-company-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

- Radius: Sharp industrial: cards and photos 6px; chips 3px; buttons and inputs 4px; slider tracks square. No pills anywhere.
- Motion: Arm tabs: the profile SVG and spec table crossfade (opacity 0 -> 1, 240ms) with the table rows staggering in 30ms apart; the active tab's orange left bar slides 200ms.
- Motion: ROI calculator: outputs tween between values (Motion animate on a motion value, 300ms, rounded display) as sliders move; slider tracks fill orange to the knob.
- Motion: Install band: the background photo translates 0 -> 6% over the band's scroll (parallax, transform only).
- Motion: Scroll reveals: fade up 20px, 0.6s, ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1], once, stagger 0.06-0.08s.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: no crossfades (instant swap), no tweening (instant numbers), no parallax; sliders still work as direct manipulation.
- Imagery: Six generated photographs that read as one facility: clean modern factory bays, soft even daylight from skylights, polished concrete, cool grey and white machinery with small safety-orange accents; no people, no readable text, no logos anywhere in frame. The robot arms in photos are generic unbranded designs. On the page, the arms themselves are drawn as original SVG side profiles — capsule links, sphere joints, cream housing, orange joint rings — geometry invented for this template that deliberately resembles no real maker's arm.

## Don'ts
- No backend of any kind: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, email services, analytics or third-party APIs. The demo form calls preventDefault and only changes local UI state; the ROI calculator is pure local math; nothing is submitted or sent.
- No real robotics companies, product names, logos or arm designs anywhere — never name or evoke a real robot maker, and never draw an arm that imitates a recognizable industrial arm silhouette (bulky orange cabinets, square castings, red or yellow liveries). The drawn arm stays the original capsule-and-sphere geometry specified in the Arms section.
- Specs, prices, proof metrics and ROI outputs are invented samples; keep the sample labels and footnotes specified in the Arms, ROI and Cases sections, and never present them as certified or measured data.
- No safety or quality certifications claimed: no CE, UL, ISO or standards badges, marks or numbers anywhere.
- Photos show factory bays and machinery only — no people, no faces, no readable text or logos in frame; use exactly the six listed files, no stock photos, no placeholder-image services.
- Do not load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any CDN. Self-host through the three Fontsource packages only. No commercial fonts.
- No CDN script or link tags; every script, style, font, icon and image is installed or downloaded into the project and bundled into dist/. Never hotlink the media.vibefather.ai URLs at runtime; download them first.
- No source maps in the production build (build.sourcemap false), and src/ is not copied into dist/.
- Relative asset paths (Vite base './', images as ./images/<file>). Internal links are hash anchors (#arms, #industries, #roi, #cases, #demo, #top) or ./index.html, never a bare directory or a leading slash.
- No dates or years anywhere on the site: no copyright year, no founded-in, no install or event dates. The footer copyright line carries only companyName.
- Do not use code or components from Aceternity UI, Tailwind Plus / Tailwind UI or any purchased template for the tabs, sliders, parallax or calculator. Re-implement everything as described here.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: no crossfades, tweening or parallax; sliders and the form keep working as direct interaction.
- Animate only transform and opacity on HTML elements; never animate width, height, top or left directly (slider fills use a background gradient, not an animated width).
- Must work at 1440x900 and 390x844 with zero console errors and no horizontal scroll; the ROI panel must fit one mobile screen without horizontal scrolling of its chips.
- Do not mention VibeFather anywhere in the site except the optional [CREDIT] footer link.
