A single-page website for a private-practice therapist: a warm one-line welcome over a calm interior photograph; the approach in three plain paragraphs; six specialty areas framed as 'a space to explore' (anxiety, life transitions, relationships, work stress, grief, identity — never diagnoses); fees and insurance answered plainly with a sliding-scale note; an honest four-step 'what the first session looks like' section that lowers the barrier to write; a contact form (name, email, one sentence about what brings you here) that confirms client-side with a no-obligation note; and practice details covering telehealth and in-person options. The default content follows an invented licensed therapist, but the structure suits any solo practice: change the name, city and fee and every paragraph still reads true. The look is soft sanctuary: warm sand and soft sage, deep warm-gray ink, one muted rose accent, Lora headlines over Nunito Sans, rounded corners and breathing whitespace. Language stays experiential throughout — no clinical claims, no outcomes promised, and a gentle crisis note pointing to emergency services generically. Photography shows rooms, chairs, plants and light — no people. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[THERAPIST]` | Therapist name | Elena Marsh |
| `[CITY]` | City | Santa Fe, New Mexico |
| `[FEE]` | Session fee | 140 |
| `[MODES]` | Session modes | in person and by telehealth |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add therapist-private-practice-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A couples counselor: set THERAPIST and CITY to your practice, rewrite the approach paragraphs around working with two people, and swap the six areas for communication, conflict, trust, transitions, intimacy and family.
- A telehealth-only therapist: set MODES to by telehealth, rewrite the practice details block to explain how video sessions work, and keep the first-session steps focused on joining a call.
- A grief and loss counselor: keep the calm sand and sage palette, set FEE to your sliding scale, and rewrite the areas section around loss, bereavement, caregiving and life after change.
- A newly licensed therapist building a first site: set THERAPIST, CITY and a modest FEE, shorten the approach to two paragraphs, and link the contact form to your own email before launch.

## FAQ

### How do I change the therapist name, city and session fee in the Therapist Private Practice Website template?
The Therapist Private Practice Website template drives the name, city, fee and session modes from four variables: THERAPIST, CITY, FEE and MODES. Swap those values and the title, hero line, fees section, sliding-scale note, practice details and footer all follow. The template's copy is written for the default values, so rewrite any paragraph whose wording referenced the old ones, keeping the same tone and length.

### What sections does the Therapist Private Practice Website template include?
The Therapist Private Practice Website template is one page with five anchored sections: approach, areas, fees, first session and contact. It opens with a hero over a calm interior photograph, explains the approach in three plain paragraphs, frames six specialty areas as a space to explore, answers fees and insurance with a sliding-scale note, walks through what a first session looks like in four steps, and closes with a contact form and practice details covering telehealth and in-person options.

### Where does the contact form send enquiries on a therapist site built with AI?
The contact form in the Therapist Private Practice Website template confirms client-side only: name, email and one sentence about what brings the visitor here are saved in the browser and a gentle confirmation appears, with a Write again option to clear it. Nothing is submitted anywhere and no backend or external API is involved, so you connect the form to your own email or booking tool before the site goes live.

### How do I write the specialties and fees copy without making clinical claims?
The Therapist Private Practice Website template keeps language experiential throughout: the six areas are framed as a space to explore (anxiety, life transitions, relationships, work stress, grief, identity) rather than diagnoses, and fees are stated plainly with insurance and a limited number of sliding-scale places. Keep that frame when you rewrite: describe what a client might bring to a session, not outcomes, conditions or promised results, and leave the gentle crisis note pointing to emergency services generically.

### Can I add a couples counseling or group practice version of this therapist template?
The Therapist Private Practice Website template suits any solo practice and the live demos include a couples counseling practice built from the same prompt. To adapt it, set THERAPIST and CITY for the practice, rewrite the approach and areas copy around the work you do, adjust FEE and the sliding-scale line, and update MODES. For more than one therapist, the single-page structure still works but the byline and first-person paragraphs need rewriting.
