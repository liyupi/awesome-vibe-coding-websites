A single-page website for a construction and renovation company: the firm's promise over a finished-project hero with a quote CTA; four trades (renovations, additions, kitchens & baths, exteriors) with flat-from pricing; a six-project gallery filterable by category with before/after notes; a safety and credentials strip with an invented license line, insurance and a cleanup pledge; a five-step process from walkthrough to final clean; a quote-request form (project-type chips, budget band, timeline, contact) confirming client-side; and a site-lead portrait (AI-generated fictional person) anchoring the crew section. The default content follows an invented family firm, but the structure suits any general contractor or renovation company. The look is blueprint honest: white and blueprint-navy with a safety-orange accent, sturdy condensed headlines over a workmanlike sans, jobsite photography with hard details and drawn blueprint motifs. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A commercial contractor: set COMPANY and CITY to your firm, swap the four trades for your build categories and rewrite the gallery projects as commercial sites.
- A kitchen and bath remodeler: set TRADE to kitchen and bath remodeling, keep the four service cards but lead with kitchens and baths, and price the gallery around room sizes.
- A roofing and exteriors specialist: set TRADE to exteriors, replace the service list with roofing, siding and windows, and use the before-and-after gallery to show storm damage and repairs.
- A solo general contractor: set COMPANY to your own name, keep the five-step process as your personal promise and use the crew section to introduce yourself by name.

## FAQ

### What sections does the Construction Company Website template include?
The Construction Company Website template is a single page with five anchored sections: services, work, safety, process and quote. Services lists four trades with from-pricing, work is a six-project gallery filterable by category with before-and-after notes, safety holds the license and insurance credentials, process runs five steps from walkthrough to final clean, and quote is a request form with project-type chips, budget band, timeline and contact fields.

### How do I change the company name, city, trade and years in business?
The Construction Company Website template drives those through four variables: COMPANY, CITY, TRADE and YEARS. Set them to your firm's details, then rewrite every headline, service card, project note and footer line in the single content file so the copy matches the new values instead of the default family firm. The email address and saved form data follow the company name automatically.

### Does the quote form on the Construction Company Website actually send requests anywhere?
No. The quote form on the Construction Company Website template confirms in the browser only and stores the entry locally, so nothing is submitted to a server or inbox. To receive real requests, connect the form to your own form service or email endpoint, or keep it as a front-end demo until you wire up a backend.

### Can I filter the project gallery to show only kitchens, additions or exteriors?
Yes. The Construction Company Website template's gallery holds six projects and filters by category through chips above the grid, with a live count that updates as you narrow the list. Cards animate in and out as you switch categories, and an empty state appears if a combination has no projects, so you can add more projects later without breaking the layout.

### What should I change first when adapting the Construction Company Website for my own firm?
Start with the four variables (COMPANY, CITY, TRADE, YEARS), then replace the six gallery projects and their before-and-after notes with your own finished work. Rewrite the four service cards and their from-prices, update the safety and credentials strip with your real license and insurance lines, and adjust the five process steps to match how your firm actually runs a job.
