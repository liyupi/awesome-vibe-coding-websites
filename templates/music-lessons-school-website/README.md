Music Lessons School Website is a single-page site for a neighborhood music school, in plum, brass and cream with sunlit instrument photography — the warmth of a good lesson room, not a corporate academy. Two working tools anchor it: a lesson finder that matches instrument, student age, level and format (in-person or online) to teachers with their weekly openings, and a practice tracker that logs minutes into a week grid with a streak counter and a gentle goal bar, saved in the browser — the retention feature no template ships. Around them: an instruments-and-teachers grid across six instruments, honest pricing by lesson length with family and online discounts, a recitals-and-events page framed as community (two recitals a year, monthly coffee-house nights), and a trial lesson booking that carries the finder's choices. It suits music schools, tutoring centers and art studios re-pointing the finder. Five variables swap school name, town, director, phone and years; every teacher, opening and price is invented sample data.

## Make it yours

- A neighborhood tutoring center: set BRAND and CITY, swap the six instruments for school subjects, and keep the age, level and format filters on the lesson finder.
- An art studio for children and adults: rename the instrument chips to drawing, painting and ceramics, and relabel the practice tracker as weekly studio minutes.
- A private piano teacher growing a studio: use FOUNDER and EXPERIENCE for your own story, trim the teacher grid to yourself, and point the trial booking at your phone line.
- A community choir or voice school: keep Voice and Piano in the finder, rewrite the recital events as concerts and open nights, and adjust pricing by lesson length.

## FAQ

### How does the lesson finder on the Music Lessons School Website template match students to teachers?
The Music Lessons School Website lesson finder takes four choices: instrument (Piano, Guitar, Violin, Voice, Drums, Cello), student age, level and format (in-person, online or either). It returns every teacher who teaches that instrument and fits the filters, showing each teacher's weekly openings as bookable chips. Choosing an opening prefills the trial booking form with the teacher and time, so a parent can go from question to booked trial in a few taps.

### What does the practice tracker on the Music Lessons School Website template actually do?
The Music Lessons School Website practice tracker is a Monday-to-Sunday grid where tapping a day cycles through 10, 20, 30 and 45-plus minutes. It totals the week, fills a goal bar toward 100 minutes and counts consecutive logged weeks as a streak. Everything is saved in the visitor's browser under a name derived from the school name, and the page says plainly that the streak lives only in that browser.

### What should I change first after generating the Music Lessons School Website template?
Start with the five variables: BRAND, CITY, FOUNDER, PHONE and EXPERIENCE. Changing them rewrites the hero, footer, phone link and storage keys, but the four teachers, their weekly openings, the pricing tiers and the recital events are invented sample data, so replace those in the content file with your own staff, times and prices. Check the lesson finder after the swap so every teacher still matches the instruments you offer.

### Can the Music Lessons School Website template be adapted for a tutoring center or art studio?
The Music Lessons School Website template suits any small teaching business that matches students to instructors. A tutoring center can re-point the finder's instrument chips to subjects and keep the age, level and format filters. An art studio can swap instruments for media such as drawing, painting and ceramics, then relabel the practice tracker as studio time. Pricing by session length, the trial booking and the events page carry over unchanged.
