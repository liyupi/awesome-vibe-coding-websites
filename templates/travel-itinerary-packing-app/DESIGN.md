# Travel Itinerary & Packing App — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Sunlit Sand** `#FDFBF7` — Warm sunlit paper app canvas
- **Card White** `#FFFFFF` — Timeline stops, budget cards, and packing groups
- **Ocean Ink** `#0F2537` — Primary headings, hero overlays, and deep maritime cards
- **Mediterranean Teal** `#0D9488` — Active day tabs, route lines, packed checks, and primary CTAs
- **Sunset Coral** `#F97316` — Booking badges, FX highlights, dining pins, and add-stop CTA
- **Warm Driftwood** `#64748B` — Secondary notes, durations, and confirmation codes

## Typography
- **Editorial travel titles and place headings** — Fraunces (OFL), weights 600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Schedule descriptions, checklists, and UI** — Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Stop times, booking refs, currency rates, and packing counts** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Mobile (<768px) clears the fixed 64px bottom tab bar cleanly. Desktop (>=1024px) renders a full-height 236px sidebar + split timeline left & stylized coastal route map / trip readiness right.
- Radius: 18px for hero banner and place cards, 14px for timeline stop cards, 999px for Day 1–Day 5 pills and FX currency toggles.

## Motion
- Smooth expandable booking details accordion on every schedule stop.
- Live packing progress meter and instant currency conversion updates.
- Honors prefers-reduced-motion; all content visible immediately.

## Imagery
Six cohesive golden-hour coastal travel photographs shot in warm editorial 35mm light (turquoise cove, cliffside terrace hotel, wooden sailboat harbor, sea-view cafe dining, rosemary cliff footpath, sunset sail), zero text or logos.

## Tone
Sunlit, serene, editorial, organized coastal optimism.
