A two-page website for a physical therapy clinic: a home page with the move-better promise over a bright treatment room, eight common conditions in plain words (never diagnosis language), a three-step approach, three therapist profiles with AI-generated working portraits (fictional people users swap for their own staff photos), an insurance block answering networks and self-pay honestly, and an outcomes note kept experiential; and a new-patient page with an intake form (condition select, history checkboxes, contact) confirming client-side, hours with an open-now indicator and a getting-here note. The default content follows an invented clinic, but the structure suits any outpatient PT or rehab practice. The look is clinical warm: white and light slate with a calm teal primary and one coral tick, rounded friendly headlines over a humanist sans, treatment-room photography and gentle reveals. Outcomes are described as typical journeys with sample labels — never guarantees or cure claims. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[CLINIC]` | Clinic name | Bridge Physical Therapy |
| `[CITY]` | City | Minneapolis, Minnesota |
| `[FOCUS]` | Who they treat | adults with stubborn joints |
| `[SELF_PAY]` | Self-pay visit | 95 |

## Make it yours

- A sports rehab clinic: set FOCUS to runners and athletes, rewrite the eight conditions for knees, ankles and shoulders, and point the new-patient CTA at your intake form.
- A post-operative rehab practice: keep the three-step approach, swap the condition cards for recovery milestones, and adjust the outcomes note to describe typical therapy journeys.
- A mobile or home-visit PT service: change CITY to your coverage area, rewrite the getting-here note for travel, and set SELF_PAY to your home-visit rate.
- A cash-pay wellness and mobility studio: set FOCUS to desk workers, replace the insurance block with a simple pricing note, and keep the intake form for first bookings.

## FAQ

### What sections are included in the Physical Therapy Clinic Website template?
The Physical Therapy Clinic Website template has two pages. The home page carries the move-better promise over a treatment room photo, eight common conditions written in plain words, a three-step approach, three therapist profiles with AI-generated placeholder portraits, an insurance block covering networks and self-pay, and an outcomes note framed as typical journeys. The new-patient page holds an intake form, hours with an open-now indicator and a getting-here note.

### How do I change the clinic name, city, focus and self-pay rate in this PT website?
The Physical Therapy Clinic Website template exposes four variables: CLINIC, CITY, FOCUS and SELF_PAY. Set CLINIC to your practice name, CITY to where you treat, FOCUS to a lowercase phrase naming who you serve, and SELF_PAY to your per-visit rate as digits. Because every string lives in one content file, rewriting the headlines and paragraphs for your values keeps the layout and tone intact.

### Is the new-patient intake form connected to a real backend or does it just confirm in the browser?
The intake form on the Physical Therapy Clinic Website template confirms client-side only. It collects a condition select, history checkboxes, name and phone, then shows a confirmation panel, and nothing is submitted anywhere. To receive real submissions you need to connect the form to your own scheduling or email service, or have patients call and email the clinic using the contact details you publish.

### How do I replace the AI therapist portraits with photos of my own staff?
The three therapist profiles in the Physical Therapy Clinic Website template use AI-generated portraits of fictional people as placeholders. Swap each image file for a real photo of your own clinicians, keep the same file names and aspect ratio, and update the names, credentials and short bios in the content file. Use photos you have permission to publish, since the defaults are not real staff.

### How does the insurance block answer network and self-pay questions?
The insurance section of the Physical Therapy Clinic Website template lists accepted networks with ticks and states the self-pay rate from the SELF_PAY variable, so visitors see the honest math before they call. Edit the network rows and the self-pay sentence to match your contracts and pricing. The template avoids promising coverage, so keep the wording factual when you adapt it to your clinic.
