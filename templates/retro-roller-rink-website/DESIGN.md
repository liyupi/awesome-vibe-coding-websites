# Retro Roller Rink Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Burnt orange** `#E8590C` — Hero large field and full-width wavy stripes. WCAG relative-luminance calculations: #32170E text on #E8590C: 4.64:1; #32170E text on #F2B807: 9.20:1; #FFFDF5 text on #32170E: 16.31:1; #FFFDF5 text on #546B24: 5.88:1; #32170E text on #FFFDF5: 16.31:1. Use these pairs for every normal-size label/body/button. Decorative overlaps/gradients carry no text; readable text gets a solid backing.
- **Mustard** `#F2B807` — Hero large field, form selections, tickets.
- **Chocolate** `#32170E` — All dark text; footer and deep rules.
- **Avocado** `#546B24` — Schedule feature panels and wave strip.
- **Paper** `#FFFDF5` — Reading/form panels, not the primary hero background.

## Typography
- **Display** — Fredoka (OFL), weights 700. Self-host the font files.
- **Body** — DM Sans (OFL), weights 400/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: 1180px max width, desktop 40px/mobile 20px gutters. 8px spacing unit, 88px section gaps desktop and 56px mobile. Hero 55/45 split, schedule 3-column, party 3-column.
- Radius: 36px photo arch corners, 20px package panels, 999px pill buttons, 3px chocolate borders. Wavy dividers are authored SVG, never cover labels.

## Motion
- Decorative disco sparkles slowly change opacity between .65 and 1 over 3 seconds without flashing; Pause decoration toggle persists.
- Cards lift 3px for 180ms on hover/focus, section reveal maximum 16px once; reduced motion removes both. No horizontal auto-marquee.

## Imagery
Five consistent photorealistic warm editorial photographs of one fictional 1970s roller rink, burnt orange/mustard/avocado/chocolate and polished maple flooring, soft tungsten lighting with mild film grain. Hero is wide rink architecture, not a face. No text, logos or branded equipment. One family action scene shows only legs/skates from behind, no identifiable faces. Photos are AI fictional venue placeholders, not a real address representation. Never replace with vector-only business imagery.

## Tone
Friendly, clear, community-minded, with useful safety and age information.
