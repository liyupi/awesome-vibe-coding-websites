# Retro Roller Rink Website

> A groovy roller rink with warm photography, session filters, rental sizes, and a working party planner.

This roller rink website brings the warmth of a neighborhood leisure venue into a bold orange and mustard layout. Large curved stripes, rounded display lettering, chocolate rules, and small disco highlights establish a recognizable visual language. The first screen includes a substantial photograph of a polished wooden rink, so visitors see the place before they read through its details. The same photographic treatment carries through skate rentals, party seating, a family skating scene, and the snack counter.

The page is useful as well as expressive. A recurring weekday schedule can be filtered for adults, families, or lessons. Session cards show practical start and end times, admission prices, age rules, and rental costs. Choosing a session carries that choice into a local booking estimate. A rental panel offers youth and adult size ranges with a clear fit note, while party packages feed a form with guest counts and an itemized total. The snack menu includes prices and dietary notes without pretending to accept online food orders.

All bookings are explicitly local demonstrations. The form checks its fields, displays the selected session and package, and states that no reservation, message, or payment was sent. The second example uses another invented rink, a different neighborhood, a revised schedule, and new package names, with the same realistic photo set functioning as a fictional venue placeholder.

A single content module holds the venue information and sample data. Fonts and photographs are bundled locally, and the mobile layout keeps the session controls and form accessible without horizontal scrolling. Reduced-motion mode freezes decorative sparkle and stripes. It suits independent roller rinks, skate halls, and community recreation spaces that need a lively site with clear practical information.

## Make it yours

- A community ice or inline skate hall: set [BRAND] and [ADDRESS], swap the wooden rink hero for your floor, and relabel the Adults and Learn filters to match your own session types.
- A family entertainment center adding skating: keep the party planner and package cards, rename Little Wheels, Big Groove and Full Orbit after your own party tiers, and adjust the extra-guest price.
- An independent skate shop running weekly rollouts: use the recurring session tickets for your meetups, point the rental panel at your loaner quads, and link the snack bar section to your counter menu.
- A recreation department or youth club: set [CITY] to your district, keep the age rules and spectator note visible, and rewrite the beginner story to name your floor hosts and safety policy.

## FAQ

### How do I change the rink name, city and address on the Retro Roller Rink Website?
The Retro Roller Rink Website template keeps all editable content in a single content module, with BRAND, CITY, ADDRESS and TAGLINE as the four variables. Set BRAND to your rink name, CITY and ADDRESS to your venue, and TAGLINE to your welcome line, then rewrite every headline, ticket label, photo caption and confirmation that references them so the whole page reads as one venue.

### Does the Retro Roller Rink Website actually take bookings or payments?
No. The Retro Roller Rink Website template's visit planner is a local demonstration: it validates fields, shows the selected session or party package, calculates admission, rentals and extra-guest totals, and then states that no reservation, message or payment was sent. The session list is also recurring weekly sample data, not live capacity, so you would connect a real booking system separately.

### How do the session filters and skate rental sizes work on the roller rink template?
The Retro Roller Rink Website template filters its weekday schedule by session type (All, Family, Adults, Learn) and by day, with a results count and a no-result state. The rental panel offers youth Y8–Y13 and adult US 1–13 size buttons, resets incompatible sizes when you switch group, and carries the selected size into the planner, with a note that sizes are a guide rather than live inventory.

### What do the party packages include and how is the total calculated?
The Retro Roller Rink Website template lists three packages: Little Wheels at $180 for 8 guests, Big Groove at $280 for 12 guests and Full Orbit at $390 for 16 guests, all including admission and rentals, with extra guests at $18 each up to 24. Choosing a package fills the party form, and the total is the package base plus extra guests beyond the included count.

### Can I use the Retro Roller Rink Website photos for my own venue?
The five warm editorial photographs in the Retro Roller Rink Website template are AI-generated placeholders for one fictional 1970s rink, not a real address. Replace them with your own venue photography in the same burnt orange, mustard and avocado palette and keep the arch-cropped hero as rink architecture rather than a face, so the layout and lighting treatment stay consistent.
