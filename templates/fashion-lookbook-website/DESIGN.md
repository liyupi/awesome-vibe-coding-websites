# Fashion Lookbook Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Ink** `#111214` — Page ground everywhere — near-black neutral, the paper of the issue.
- **Graphite** `#1B1D21` — Overlay and spec-card ground, film placeholder, footer band.
- **Fog line** `#2E3136` — Hairlines, spec-card rules, caption underlines — the only borders.
- **Bone** `#EDEDEA` — Primary text: oversized titles, captions, credits.
- **Ash** `#6E7176` — Muted text: sub-captions, stockist meta, sample-copy notes.
- **Accent** `var(--accent) #D8461F` — The single collection accent from [ACCENT]: chapter numbers, progress line, play glyph, look tags, link hovers.

## Typography
- **Display — cover title, chapter titles, look numbers (oversized condensed, uppercase, tight tracking)** — Oswald (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — captions, spec cards, notes, stockists (quiet sans; labels uppercase tracked 0.14em)** — Manrope (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Editorial and architectural: content max-w-[1600px] px-6/px-10; the lookbook track pads x 8vw with 6vw gaps between frames; chapter interludes take 40vw of track; notes and stockists py-36; captions sit 16px beneath frames with a fogline rule.
- Radius: Zero everywhere — frames, cards, buttons, overlays; the only geometry is the accent play triangle.

## Motion
- Horizontal chapters: vertical scroll drives the pinned track's translateX linearly (no spring); wrapper height = 100vh + the track's scrollable width, so the scrub is exactly 1:1 with the wheel.
- Chapter titles drift at 0.6 of the track's speed, so type lags behind photographs as they pass.
- Inner parallax: each frame's image slides -6%→6% inside its frame across its chapter's progress, images slightly larger than their masks (scale 1.12).
- Progress line: a 2px accent line across the top of the pinned viewport fills with scrollYProgress; the current chapter number ('01 / 02') in accent updates as chapters change.
- Cover: the photograph scales 1.05→1 over 3s on load; the collection title rises 40px once; the scroll cue line loops 3s.
- Look overlays: fade in 300ms with the photograph sliding 24px into place; Escape and backdrop close.
- Reveals: notes and stockists fade up (y 30→0, 900ms) with 120ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once.
- prefers-reduced-motion: no pinning and no transforms — chapters render as a vertical stack of frames and titles; the cover and overlays become plain fades; the progress line is static at full.

## Imagery
One campaign shoot in one light: high-fashion editorial photography, near-monochrome — charcoal, bone and ink grays with at most one muted accent tone — hard directional side light carving sculptural silhouettes, deep shadows, subtle film grain, medium-format look, full-length compositions with generous negative space. Faces are never identifiable: models are turned away, in shadow, or cropped above the shoulders — silhouette over portrait. No readable text, no logos anywhere in frame. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
austere, declarative, magazine-flat — captions like credits, notes like manifestos; no exclamation, no softness in the words.
