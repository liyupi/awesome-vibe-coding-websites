# Private K-12 School Website

> A bright red-and-navy school site with day-in-the-life timelines per division and a tuition-and-aid estimator that shows its math.

Private K-12 School Website is a single-page site for an independent day school, in school red, navy and chalk white with a confident crest-free wordmark — institutional tradition with modern grid discipline. The page answers what parents actually navigate: three divisions (lower, middle, upper) each with a six-stop day-in-the-life timeline told in concrete details; admissions as a five-step rhythm with seasonal deadline anchors; a tuition-and-aid estimator (division, household size, income band → estimated tuition after sample aid, every factor visible and labeled illustrative); athletics and arts as living program grids; campus tour booking; and a news feed that reads maintained. Photography shows classrooms, a library, labs and fields with people far away or from behind — sunlit, orderly, alive. It suits private day schools, charter networks and faith-independent academies. Five variables swap school name, town, head of school, phone and age; every stat, staff name and event is invented sample data.

## Make it yours

- A charter network: set BRAND to the network name, relabel the three divisions to your grade bands and rewrite the admissions steps with your lottery and enrolment dates.
- A faith-independent academy: keep the wordmark and red rule, swap the day-in-the-life stops for your chapel, seminar and service blocks, and retune the estimator tables.
- A small independent day school: set CITY and FOUNDER, shorten the athletics and arts grids to your real offerings, and point the tour booking at your admissions calendar.
- A school marketing lead: replace the sample news feed with your own announcements, keep the estimator labeled illustrative, and link the phone number to the admissions line.

## FAQ

### How does the tuition and aid estimator on the Private K-12 School Website show its math?
The Private K-12 School Website estimator takes three inputs — division, household size and income band — and prints every line of the calculation in the result panel, such as the division base tuition, the aid percentage for the income band, the household-size multiplier and the estimated yearly figure rounded to the nearest $10. It also labels the result illustrative only, since aid decisions are made by committee with a full application.

### What is a day-in-the-life timeline and how do the division tabs work on this school template?
The Private K-12 School Website gives each of its three divisions — lower, middle and upper — a timeline of six stops with fixed times of day, from morning circle and reading workshop in the lower school to Harkness English and ensembles in the upper school. Tabs switch between divisions with a short crossfade, and the navy timeline line draws in with red nodes as it enters view.

### What do I need to change first after downloading the Private K-12 School Website template?
Start with the five variables: set BRAND to your school name, CITY to your town, FOUNDER to your head of school, PHONE to your admissions line and EXPERIENCE to your years of operation. Then replace the invented sample stats, staff names, news items and event dates in the content file, and re-point the division descriptions and estimator tables to match your own program.

### How do I book campus tours and capture parent enquiries with this school website template?
The Private K-12 School Website includes a campus tour booking section with tour options, and tour requests persist in the browser under a storage key derived from the school name. Because the template builds to static files with no backend, you connect the tour form and the admissions call to action to your own email service or scheduling tool before launch.
