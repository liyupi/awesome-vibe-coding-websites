Danmaku Video Platform is a Bilibili-inspired media community built around the moment a viewer reaction becomes part of the film. It includes a searchable home feed, six original video thumbnails, and a watch view where a code-drawn Canvas scene plays beneath timed comments. A density map reveals where conversations gather, while playback, comment filters, saving, following, and sharing all respond without a server.

The visual system uses deep ink surfaces, warm amber accents, compact metadata, and crisp Space Grotesk and Inter typography. On the home page, a large featured film and a live conversation panel establish the product immediately. The watch page keeps the player dominant, with related films nearby and an uploader card, comment composer, and local activity states adding context. Comments enter real lanes instead of appearing as a decorative overlay. On smaller screens, the same controls reflow into touch-friendly rows, and reduced-motion settings pause the scene until the viewer chooses to play.

This template suits creators, community builders, and developers exploring interactive media or social playback. Change the brand, tagline, content focus, audience, and featured title to adapt the catalogue to another editorial theme. Keep the included imagery and rewrite the surrounding titles to fit what each frame shows, or replace the local assets with an equally coherent set. The second case demonstrates a travel-and-food publication using the same layout and interactions.

Everything runs as a static Vite and React build. User comments, saved films, follows, and viewing state remain in the browser, making the template useful for prototypes, portfolio projects, and client-side product demos without exposing credentials or requiring external video services.

## FAQ

### How do the timed comments in the Danmaku Video Platform template stay in sync with the video?
The Danmaku Video Platform template plays a code-drawn Canvas scene instead of a video file, and each comment carries a timestamp. During playback the scene advances with requestAnimationFrame, and comments whose timestamps fall into the current window move across real lanes rather than sitting as a decorative overlay. Seeking the timeline repositions the stream, and the density map updates to show where reactions gather.

### Can I add a comment to the Danmaku Video Platform and see it appear immediately?
Yes. The watch view includes a comment composer, and anything you post is injected into the stream and reflected in the density timeline without a server. Locally added comments are kept in localStorage alongside saved films, followed channels and comment preferences, so they survive a reload. If storage is unavailable, the template falls back safely and keeps the session working in memory.

### What do I change first in the Danmaku Video Platform template to make it my own?
Start with the five variables: BRAND, TAGLINE, AUDIENCE, CONTENT_FOCUS and FEATURED_VIDEO. The default brand is Rillcast with the tagline Watch together. Think out loud. After that, rewrite the six film titles and descriptions so they match their thumbnails, and update the uploader card and related films. All copy lives in one content file, so the catalogue can be swapped without touching the layout.

### Does the Danmaku Video Platform require a backend or an external video service?
No. The Danmaku Video Platform template is a static Vite and React build with no server code and no external APIs. Playback, comment filters, saving, following, sharing, search, sorting and related-film navigation all respond in the browser. Playback time stays in memory while comments, saved films and follows persist in localStorage, which makes it suitable for prototypes, portfolio projects and client-side product demos.
