# Company Careers Page

> Careers site with mission and culture, team stories, filterable open roles and a structured application form.

A careers site for one employer, a fictional company that makes home heat pumps. The first screen is a large photograph of the team at work under a headline about doing work that matters, and the page then earns the claim with specifics: a short mission, a benefits grid of concrete perks such as a grant toward a heat pump in your own home, and three team stories from people in different roles. Open roles can be filtered by department and location, and each role page sets out the work, the requirements, the pay range and the hiring process before an application form that asks structured questions and takes a CV as a file chip. A hiring timeline shows what happens after applying, and a talent community sign-up catches people who are interested but not ready. All photographs come from one shoot, with people far away or seen from behind. Forest green, mint and sunshine yellow on white with a rounded display face. Change the company name, city and mission in the content file and swap in your own roles and photographs; layout, palette and motion stay as described.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Company name | Everwarm |
| `[CITY]` | Where the office is | Burlington |
| `[MISSION]` | The mission in one line | Heat every home without heating the planet. |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add company-careers-page` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A heat-pump installer hiring field engineers: set [BRAND] and [CITY], keep the benefits grid, and rewrite the role pages for installation and service technician openings.
- A climate-tech startup recruiting its first team: use [MISSION] as the hero line, trim the roles list to a few openings, and keep the talent community sign-up for speculative applicants.
- A solar or home-retrofit company: swap the team stories for your own installers and designers, and change the heat-pump benefit to a staff discount on your own installations.
- A design or engineering studio with a playful tone: keep the forest and sunshine palette, replace the roles with your disciplines, and rewrite the benefits as learning budget and flexible hours.

## FAQ

### How do I change the company name, city and mission on the Company Careers Page template?
The Company Careers Page template keeps the company name, city and mission in one content file as [BRAND], [CITY] and [MISSION]. Set [BRAND] to your company, [CITY] to your office location and [MISSION] to your one-line purpose, then rewrite the headlines, paragraphs, labels and image captions that mention the default values so the whole page reads as one employer.

### What does the application form on the Company Careers Page template include?
The Company Careers Page template gives each role its own page with the work, requirements, pay range and hiring process, followed by a structured application form that asks set questions and takes a CV as a file chip. The form is a local sample that says so: no file is stored or sent, so you connect it to your own hiring system before using it for real candidates.

### Can I filter the open roles by department and location in this careers template?
Yes. The Company Careers Page template lists open roles on a dedicated roles page and lets visitors filter them by department and location, with rows fading out and in as filters change. Roles, departments and locations all live in the content file, so you add or remove a role there and the filters follow without touching the layout.

### Is the Company Careers Page template free to use for my own company?
The Company Careers Page template is free. Its prompt and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0, and the live demos are built from that prompt. Commercial use, including a careers page for a company you work for or a client, needs Pro, which also brings the full prompt and the demo source code.

### How do I add a talent community sign-up and hiring timeline to a careers site built with AI?
The Company Careers Page template already includes both: a hiring timeline that shows what happens after someone applies, and a talent community sign-up for people who are interested but not ready to apply. Both are local samples in the content file, so you keep the wording and layout and point the sign-up at your own mailing list or applicant tracking tool.

### How do I build a company careers page with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add company-careers-page, replace [BRAND], [CITY] and [MISSION] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
