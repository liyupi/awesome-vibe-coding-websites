# Company Careers Page — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **forest** `#1B4332` — Forest green: hero scrim, dark band, text on mint and sunshine, primary buttons with white text
- **mint** `#95D5B2` — Mint: cards, chips and secondary buttons with forest text
- **mintTint** `#E9F5EE` — Pale mint panels
- **sunshine** `#FFD23F` — Sunshine yellow: the main call to action and highlights, always with forest text
- **white** `#FFFFFF` — Page ground and cards
- **ink** `#12261F` — Text
- **muted** `#4F6159` — Secondary text
- **line** `#DDE6E1` — Borders and dividers

## Typography
- **Display / headlines** — Red Hat Display Variable (OFL), weights 600/800. Self-host the font files.
- **Body / UI** — Manrope Variable (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Mono / labels, pay ranges, step numbers** — IBM Plex Mono (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Section padding py-24 desktop / py-16 mobile; container max-w-[1200px] px-6; cards p-6 with a 16px radius; 8px rhythm.
- Radius: Cards 16px; buttons pill; chips pill; photos 16px; inputs 10px.

## Motion
- Scroll reveals: sections fade up 20px over 450ms, once (Motion whileInView); content is visible by default.
- Role filters: leaving rows fade 150ms, entering rows fade up 250ms with a 40ms stagger.
- Hero photograph settles from scale 1.04 over 1.2 seconds.
- prefers-reduced-motion: no reveals or scale; filters change instantly.

## Imagery
Bright documentary photographs from one shoot of a small climate-tech company: a sunlit office full of plants, people at work far away or seen from behind, an engineering bench with heat-pump parts and a rooftop with solar panels. Green, mint and pale wood tones. No front-facing close-up portraits, no readable text, no logos.

## Tone
Warm and plain, like a colleague explaining the job: specific perks, real names, no recruiting slogans.
