The default content sells an invented tiny-games workshop, but the anatomy suits any skill course — lettering, photography, spreadsheets: change the course name, topic, instructor and price and every module, result and answer still reads true. The look is warm workshop editorial: cream and walnut surfaces, espresso ink, one marigold accent, Domine headlines over Assistant, result figures in tabular numerals, long-scroll rhythm with quiet reveals. Photography shows desks, tools and materials with no faces and no readable text. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file; modules, results, plans and FAQ all read from it.

## FAQ

### How do I change the course name, topic, instructor and price on this sales page?
The Online Course Sales Page template reads every string from one content file, so you edit four variables there: COURSE, TOPIC, INSTRUCTOR and PRICE. The template ships with the invented Tiny Games Workshop, Theo Marsh and a $149 price. Change those and the header enroll button, hero promise, curriculum, results, pricing card and final call to action all follow. Rewrite the paragraphs to match your topic at the same length and tone.

### How does the full-pay versus three-payment toggle work on the pricing section?
The Online Course Sales Page template keeps one plan state shared by the pricing card, the sticky header button and the final call to action, so all three show the same figure. At the default $149 price the three-pay plan is 3 × $55 for $165 total, stated plainly. If you set a different price, the plan prices recompute in the content file as round thirds plus $16 with the total shown. The choice is remembered in the browser.

### Can I use this template to sell a course for clients or my own business?

The brief, stack and variables are public, the full prompt and the demo source code come with Pro membership, and free prompts and every DESIGN.md are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only. The page itself is a static front end: nothing is submitted anywhere, so you connect enrollment and payment to your own checkout.

### What does the video frame in the hero actually play, and how do I replace it with my own course trailer?
The Online Course Sales Page hero shows a drawn video frame with a poster, a play affordance and a duration badge, and clicking it opens an honest placeholder modal rather than a real video. To use your own trailer, replace the poster image in the content file and point the modal at your hosted video. The instructor note in the modal is written as a short message from the teacher, so rewrite it in your instructor's voice.

### How honest are the sample results and outcome figures, and what should I change before publishing?
The Online Course Sales Page labels its outcome strip and three student-result stories as sample figures, and the copy avoids income claims. Before publishing, replace those numbers with real outcomes from your own students, or keep them marked as samples. The curriculum accordion, results, payment plans and eight-question FAQ all read from the content file, so swap the invented tiny-games material for your own modules, lesson run times and answers.
