# Hair Salon and Barbershop Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [BRAND], an independent salon and barbershop on [STREET] in [CITY], open since [FOUNDED] with four stylists and six chairs. It must feel like a real neighborhood shop: full-bleed photography of the room and the tools (never faces), a price list with durations, stylist profiles with chairs and specialties, a gallery of finished work, reviews, opening hours with a live open-now indicator, and a four-step booking flow — service, stylist, day and time, name — that ends in a client-side confirmation card with a reference number. Pages: index.html (home), booking.html (book a chair). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

- Audience: Locals within a ten-minute walk deciding between three shops on the same street, plus lapsed regulars checking whether their stylist still works Sundays. Adults of every hair type; men coming for fades and shaves, women for color and long-hair cuts, parents booking kids' cuts. Phone-first, wary of phone-call bookings.
- Goal: Fill the chairs. Within ten seconds a visitor should know what a signature cut costs, who cuts hair there, whether the shop is open right now, and be one tap from a booked slot.
- Tone: Confident, warm and unpretentious, like a stylist who has heard every hair confession. Short sentences, concrete numbers (minutes, dollars, chairs), no beauty-industry buzzwords, no exclamation marks.

- [CITY] — City (default: "Chicago, Illinois"). Where the shop is. Used in the hero eyebrow, the getting-here line and the footer. The street stays invented-sounding; only the city may be real.
- [STREET] — Street (default: "Rowan Street"). The invented street the shop sits on ('118 Rowan Street'). Drives the address, the hero eyebrow and the footer. Keep it common English words, not a real address.
- [FOUNDED] — Year opened (default: "2019"). Four-digit year. Drives the hero eyebrow, the wordmark line 'Est. [FOUNDED]', the story line and the first review's 'regular since' year.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/hair-salon-and-barbershop-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.

- Motion: Marquee: one continuous strip of pull quotes on Forest scrolling left at a constant 40px/s, pausing on hover, duplicated content for a seamless loop (CSS animation; disabled under reduced motion).
- Motion: Service rows: hover fills Oat at 60% and slides a 24px x 2px Brass rule in from the left over 250ms; the 'Book' affordance fades in at the right edge.
- Motion: Stylist cards: on hover the photo scales to 1.04 over 700ms --ease-out and the card border turns Ink; the 'next free slot' pill crossfades to 'Book with [name]' linking to ./booking.html?stylist=<id> (the booking page preselects that stylist; opened without a query it starts unselected — never an error).

- Motion: Booking steps: step changes crossfade with a 20px horizontal slide, 300ms --ease-io (AnimatePresence mode 'wait'). Selecting a service, stylist, day or slot fills the chip over 150ms; the sticky summary price counts to the new total over 400ms. The confirmation card flips in (rotateX -8deg to 0, opacity 0 to 1, 420ms --ease-out) and the reference number types character by character over 700ms.
- Motion: Hours: the open-now dot pulses (scale 1 to 1.25, 1600ms loop) only while open.
- Motion: Header: transparent over the photo hero with Card text; after 40px of scroll it gets Cream at 94% opacity, backdrop-filter blur(10px), Ink text and a 1px Rule bottom border, 250ms transition. On booking.html it is solid from the start.
- Motion: Links: .link-draw grows a 1px currentColor underline from 0% to 100% width over 300ms --ease-out on hover and focus-visible.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — disable the hero scale and parallax, H1 rise, marquee (show a static centered quote), count-ups, type-in and layout animations (render at rest). Color and border hover changes stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Real photography only, warm and natural, all six made for this template in one consistent style: deep green walls, warm cream light, brass details, leather and wood, documentary editorial look, fine grain. Never a face: interiors, tools, chairs, backs of heads. No readable text, logos or branded tools anywhere. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 10px corners, no shadow; photos on Cream get a 1px Rule outline. Captions below photos in Karla 13px Muted. Drawn in code: the favicon, the wordmark (type only), duration and price pills, the confirmation card layout, dividers and the stepper. Icons: lucide-react only, stroke width 1.5.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the booking, its confirmation and every choice live in the browser only (localStorage); nothing is charged or sent.
- Every internal link points to a file name (./index.html, ./booking.html, ./booking.html#policies, ./index.html#services) — never to a bare directory.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- No real salon or chain names, no real addresses, no map tiles or embeds, the stylist portraits are AI-generated fictional people used as placeholders; elsewhere no faces, hands or readable branding.
- No dates or years anywhere except 'Est. [FOUNDED]' and review attributions; the site never shows today's date.
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere: disable parallax, marquee, count-ups, type-in and layout animations; keep color/border hovers.
- The site must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; the booking flow works with keyboard only, every step has a visible focus ring, and booking.html opened directly starts at step 1 with nothing preselected unless a query param says so.
- Keep copy concrete: no lorem, no filler, no superlatives without evidence; prices, durations and hours always come from src/content.ts.
