# Surf School Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Sand** `#F4EDE3` — Page ground everywhere; the hero photo melts into it with a bottom gradient.
- **Dune** `#E7DCCC` — Raised surfaces: level cards, coach cards, forecast cells, footer band.
- **Driftwood** `#B9A88F` — Hairlines, card borders, rules; darkens on hover.
- **Faded blue** `#8FB4C2` — Primary faded ocean accent: links, active chips, wave arcs, forecast highlights, the map's sea.
- **Deep tide** `#3E6474` — Strong accent: primary buttons, level card headings, the map pin outline.
- **Ink** `#2E3438` — Primary text — soft near-black with a blue undertone, never pure #000.
- **Fog** `#6E7A78` — Secondary text: intros, meta lines, captions, sample-data label.
- **Sunbleach** `#DFA95C` — The one warm highlight: the map pin fill, underline accents, the tide dot — used sparingly.

## Typography
- **Display — headlines, camp names, coach names (laid-back, slightly quirky)** — Bricolage Grotesque (OFL), weights 600/700/800. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body copy, lists, form labels** — Archivo (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Mono — forecast readouts, small uppercase labels, prices** — Space Mono (OFL), weights 400/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Loose and unhurried: sections py-28 (112px) desktop / py-20 (80px) mobile; content max-w-6xl px-6/px-10; cards breathe with 28px padding; hairlines (1px driftwood) carry structure instead of shadows.
- Radius: Soft but not pill: 12px on cards and photos, 8px on buttons and inputs, 999px only on tiny chips; nothing sharper, nothing perfectly round.

## Motion
- Reveals: sections and cards fade up (y 20→0, 600ms ease-out) with 80ms stagger via Motion whileInView, firing once.
- Hero: the wave photo scales 1.05→1 over 3s on load and parallaxes at 0.1 of scroll; the headline rises 120ms after the photo settles; the sand gradient at the bottom keeps the melt into the page static.
- Forecast strip: each wave arc drifts (translateY ±3px) on a 4s ease-in-out loop, staggered 200ms per cell; wind arrows ease from 0° to their rotation on reveal (400ms).
- Card and row hovers lift 2px and darken the driftwood hairline to fadedblue (200ms); chips fill dune and the label shifts to Space Mono 700.
- The beach map pin pops in (scale 0→1, 300ms spring) when the section enters; the map itself is static.
- The grain overlay never animates — it is the constant of the page.
- prefers-reduced-motion: parallax, load zoom and all loops off; reveals become plain 200ms opacity fades; hover effects become color changes only.

## Imagery
One roll of film: 35mm photographs on sun-bleached color negative stock — faded ocean blues and sandy neutrals, warm visible grain, hazy soft highlights, slightly lifted blacks. Empty waves, boards on sand, and people only small in frame or from behind with faces not identifiable. No text in frame, no logos, no recognizable breaks or landmarks. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather, downloaded into public/images, with the site-wide warm grain overlay sitting on top of them too.

## Tone
Laid-back and warm with dry humor; short sentences that sound like they were said squinting into the sun; competence shown through specifics, not adjectives.
