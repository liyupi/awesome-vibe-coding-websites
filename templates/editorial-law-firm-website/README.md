# Editorial Law Firm Website

> Editorial two-page law firm site: big serif type, case results in figures and a multi-step intake form.

A two-page website for a small litigation and employment law firm, built to read like a serious publication rather than a brochure. The home page runs as a set of numbered sections: a masthead hero with an oversized high-contrast serif headline and a ledger of three firm figures, six practice areas with what each covers and its typical outcome, case results set as large figures on a dark ground, attorney profiles with monogram avatars, education and bar admissions, a four-step consultation intake, an insights list that opens inline, a fee table with an FAQ, and a contact block that shows whether the office is open right now. The second page goes deep on each practice area with a sticky index that tracks your position as you read. It suits small law practices, solo attorneys, and other professional services that sell judgement and track record: tax advisers, mediators, consultancies, architects. The look is warm paper, near-black ink and a single oxblood accent, with hairline rules, small mono labels and real photographs of offices, corridors, libraries and documents treated in a muted, slightly desaturated way and captioned like figures in a report. People's faces never appear; attorneys are shown as typographic monograms. Motion is quiet: headlines rise out of a mask, figures count up once, links draw their underline, and everything stops under reduced-motion settings. The intake form validates each step, shows a review screen and ends in a success state with a reference number, all in the browser. To adapt it, change the firm name, the practice focus, the city and the clients you serve; the prompt asks the builder to rewrite every headline, figure, profile and answer to match, and to keep all copy in one content file so later edits stay simple.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Firm name | Wrenfield & Osei |
| `[PRACTICE_FOCUS]` | Practice focus | litigation and employment law |
| `[CITY]` | City | Portland, Oregon |
| `[CLIENTS]` | Who the firm represents | employees, executives and growing companies |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add editorial-law-firm-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A solo employment mediator: set BRAND to your name, PRACTICE_FOCUS to workplace mediation, and rewrite the six practice areas and fee table for mediation sessions and hourly rates.
- A tax advisory firm: keep the editorial layout, set CLIENTS to owner-managed businesses, and replace case results with settlement figures and the fee table with engagement models.
- An architecture studio: use the numbered sections for project types, swap attorney monograms for studio principals, and turn case results into built-area and project figures.
- An immigration and family practice: set PRACTICE_FOCUS to immigration and family law, rewrite the intake matter types, and change the bar admissions and insights to match your jurisdictions.
