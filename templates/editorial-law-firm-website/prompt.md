# Editorial Law Firm Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [BRAND], a small [PRACTICE_FOCUS] firm in [CITY] that represents [CLIENTS]. It reads like a serious editorial publication: oversized high-contrast serif headlines on warm paper, numbered sections separated by hairline rules, case results set as big figures, attorney profiles with monogram avatars, real documentary photographs of the office, the city, corridors, libraries and documents (never faces), and a client-side four-step consultation intake with a review step and a success state. Page 1 (index.html) is the firm's home; page 2 (practice-areas.html) explains each practice area in depth with a sticky index.

- Audience: People in [CITY] facing a dispute at work or in business ([CLIENTS]) who are comparing two or three firms late at night, often anxious, and want to see quickly what the firm handles, what it has won, who would represent them, what it costs and how to start.
- Goal: Turn a worried visitor into a consultation request: make the firm's scope, record and fees legible in under a minute, then make the four-step intake form feel safe and easy to finish. Secondary goal: send readers to the practice-areas page to confirm the firm handles their exact problem.
- Tone: Serious, plain-spoken and confident. Short declarative sentences, concrete numbers, no legal jargon without an explanation, no hype words, no exclamation marks. The firm sounds like a calm senior partner explaining things across a table.

- [CLIENTS] — Who the firm represents (default: "employees, executives and growing companies"). Lowercase list of client types. Used in the hero paragraph, the intake 'Who are you?' choices and the brief tone of the practice areas.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/editorial-law-firm-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

- Motion: Easing: --ease-out cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1) for reveals; --ease-io cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for accordions and form steps.
- Motion: Reveal: blocks wrapped in <Reveal> start at opacity 0, translateY(16px) and animate to rest over 700ms --ease-out when 15% visible (Motion whileInView, viewport once). Siblings in the same list stagger by 70ms (pass index prop).
- Motion: Headline mask: H1 and every H2 are split into lines in content.ts (an array of line strings, the italic phrase marked with *asterisks*). Each line sits in an overflow-hidden block; the inner span rises from translateY(105%) to 0 over 900ms --ease-out, 90ms stagger, when the heading enters the viewport (H1 on load after 120ms).
- Motion: Count-up: numeric parts of result figures and hero ledger figures count from 0 to their value over 1400ms easeOutCubic when 40% visible, once (useCountUp with requestAnimationFrame). Prefix and suffix ('$', 'M', '%', ' months') stay static; decimals keep their precision (4.2, 48.6). Non-numeric figures ('Dismissed') only fade in.
- Motion: Hero photo: on load it scales from 1.06 to 1 over 1600ms --ease-out; while scrolling, the image inside its frame translates from 0 to -6% of its height (Motion useScroll + useTransform on the frame's scroll progress). The frame itself does not move.
- Motion: Link underline: .link-draw uses background-image linear-gradient(currentColor, currentColor), background-size 0% 1px at bottom left, no-repeat; on hover and focus-visible it grows to 100% 1px over 300ms --ease-out.
- Motion: Rows and cards: practice rows and insight rows fade their background to Paper Deep over 200ms on hover, the arrow icon moves 6px right, the number turns Oxblood. Attorney monogram tiles swap to Ink background with Ivory initials over 300ms.
- Motion: Accordions (FAQ, insights): height animates with Motion (AnimatePresence, height 0 → auto, opacity 0 → 1) over 320ms --ease-io; the Plus icon rotates 45° into an X.
- Motion: Intake steps: the outgoing step fades and moves 24px left, the incoming step enters from 24px right, 260ms --ease-io (AnimatePresence mode 'wait'); going Back reverses the direction. The progress bar width animates over 400ms.
- Motion: Header: transparent with no border at the top of the page; after 24px of scroll it gets Paper at 92% opacity, backdrop-filter blur(8px) and a 1px Rule bottom border, 200ms transition.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — disable the headline mask, reveals, parallax, hero scale, count-up (show final values), step slide (crossfade 0ms) and smooth scrolling. Hover color changes stay.
- Imagery: Real documentary photography only: the firm's own conference room, reception, consultation room, desk with case files, the view from its window and its street entrance (six photos made for this template), plus four public-domain / CC0 photos of a courthouse corridor, a law library, legal stacks and a nineteenth-century handwritten deed. No people or faces anywhere. ## Don'ts
- No backend of any kind: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, analytics, email services or third-party APIs. The intake form calls preventDefault and only changes local React state; it never submits, stores or sends anything.
- Do not load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any CDN. Self-host through the Fontsource packages only.
- No CDN <script>, <link> or import maps. Every script, style, font, icon and image is in the project and bundled into dist/.
- No hotlinked images: every photo is downloaded into public/images/ once and served from there, and every third-party file is listed in ASSETS.md.
- No source maps in the production build (build.sourcemap false), and src/ is not copied into dist/.
- Relative asset paths (Vite base './'). Internal links are hash anchors or file names such as ./index.html, ./index.html#consult and ./practice-areas.html#wage-and-hour, never a bare directory or a leading slash.
- Do not change the stack (Vite 5 + React 18 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS 3.4 + Motion + lucide-react + Fontsource, two HTML entries) unless the user asks. No router library, no UI kit, no 3D.
- No real law firm's name, logo, crest or tagline; no real attorneys' names, photos or biographies; no real case names, docket numbers or rulings. Every person, case, school, figure and street is invented. Real states and federal districts may appear only as bar admissions.
- No state bar, court, government or university seals, scales-of-justice clip art, gavels or column icons. The firm has no logo mark beyond its typeset name and the favicon ampersand.
- Never promise outcomes. Keep the 'Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome' disclaimer under the results and the attorney-advertising disclaimer in the footer, and the required acknowledgment checkbox in the intake form.
- No photos with people's faces, handshakes, readable signage, logos or recognizable landmarks. Use only the listed images, and no Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay or other stock sites with their own licenses. Attorneys are monograms, never photos or AI portraits.
- No commercial or custom fonts: only Bodoni Moda, Public Sans and IBM Plex Mono (OFL).
- Only dependencies under MIT, ISC, BSD, Apache-2.0, 0BSD, CC0 or Unlicense (OFL for fonts). No Aceternity UI, Tailwind Plus / Tailwind UI or purchased template code; write every effect from scratch.
- One accent only: Oxblood (and its lighter #C4544A on dark backgrounds). No gradients, glows, glassy cards, drop shadows, rounded cards or second accent color.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion exactly as described under Design motion. Every interactive element is keyboard reachable with a visible 2px Oxblood focus ring; accordions and the menu use aria-expanded; form errors use role alert.
- Must render at 1440×900 and 390×844 with zero console errors and no horizontal page scroll (only the mobile practice-index chip row scrolls inside itself).
- Keep dist/ under 20 MB (target under 10 MB).
- No lorem ipsum, filler or hype words. Use the exact headlines, figures, fees, hours and CTAs given here for the default values. No meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD or sitemap: only the <title> and favicon.
