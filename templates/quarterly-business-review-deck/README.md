# Quarterly Business Review Deck

> A 12-slide executive SaaS QBR presentation deck with SVG revenue waterfall, filterable OKRs, and print CSS.

Designed for SaaS operations leaders, chief operating officers, and revenue teams running quarterly board or leadership meetings, this 12-slide browser presentation turns complex operating metrics into a crisp, interactive executive review. Rather than exporting static slide images that lose context during live Q&A, the deck keeps key charts and department views interactive right inside the presentation canvas while preserving a clean one-slide-per-page landscape print layout for PDF distribution. The visual language pairs a clean studio-white background with deep graphite typography, crisp indigo data anchors, and warm amber attention flags so leadership can spot both momentum and risk in seconds. Opening slides present the quarter badge, executive narrative headline, and a high-contrast KPI scoreboard tracking ARR, Net Revenue Retention, Gross Logo Churn, and CAC Payback with animated counter polish and period-over-period deltas. Mid-deck slides feature a code-drawn SVG revenue waterfall chart breaking down beginning ARR through new business, expansion, contraction, and churn, alongside an interactive OKR progress board filterable across Product, Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success with traffic-light status badges. Additional slides cover key shipped wins, a structured risk and mitigation matrix, headcount and burn runway tracking, regional pipeline mix, and a next-quarter strategic priorities roadmap. Full presentation ergonomics come built in: arrow-key, spacebar, and swipe navigation, bottom slide progress bar, G-key thumbnail grid overview, N-key speaker notes drawer with a live elapsed timer, and F-key fullscreen mode. Edit the company name, quarter label, and financial baseline in one structured data module to prepare your next executive review.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Company name | StrataPulse |
| `[QUARTER_LABEL]` | Review period badge | Q3 Business Review |
| `[HEADLINE_ARR]` | Ending ARR figure | $24.8M |
| `[TEAM_SCOPE]` | Operating scope | Global SaaS Operations |
| `[LEAD_PRESENTER]` | Presenter name and title | Elena Vance, COO |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add quarterly-business-review-deck` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A fintech payments team: set BRAND and HEADLINE_ARR to your processed volume, relabel the waterfall segments for net revenue and swap the OKR departments for Risk, Compliance and Partnerships.
- A startup COO preparing a board pack: keep the KPI scoreboard, replace the wins gallery with fundraising milestones and use the risk matrix for hiring and runway decisions.
- A revenue operations lead: point TEAM_SCOPE at your GTM organization, rewrite the pipeline velocity slide for your segments and filter the OKR board by Sales and Customer Success only.
- A marketing leadership review: set LEAD_PRESENTER to the CMO, replace the product delivery slide with campaign performance and use the regional mix slide for channel attribution.

## FAQ

### What slides are included in the Quarterly Business Review Deck template?
The Quarterly Business Review Deck template has 12 slides: a cover with an executive snapshot, a four-metric KPI scoreboard, an SVG ARR revenue waterfall, a department-filterable OKR board, go-to-market and pipeline velocity, product delivery and reliability, a key wins gallery, a risk and mitigation matrix, headcount and burn runway, regional pipeline mix, next-quarter priorities, and a closing slide. Every slide lives on one page with hash-based navigation.

### How do I change the company name, quarter and ARR figures in the QBR deck?
The Quarterly Business Review Deck template centralizes its content in one structured data module. Set BRAND to your company name, QUARTER_LABEL to the review period, HEADLINE_ARR to your ending ARR, TEAM_SCOPE to your operating scope and LEAD_PRESENTER to the presenter's name and title. All slide copy, waterfall segments, OKRs, risks and speaker notes are edited in the same place, so nothing needs touching in the slide components.

### Can I print the Quarterly Business Review Deck to PDF for the board?
Yes. The Quarterly Business Review Deck template includes print CSS with a landscape page size and one slide per page, and the top header has a Print PDF button that hides the toolbar and navigation chrome before printing. The result is a clean PDF of all 12 slides for board packs or email distribution, while the live version keeps the interactive waterfall, OKR filters and speaker notes available during the meeting.

### How do the speaker notes and presentation controls work in the QBR deck?
The Quarterly Business Review Deck template supports arrow keys, spacebar and swipe to move between slides, plus a 12-segment progress bar and a fullscreen mode. Press G for a thumbnail overview grid and N for a speaker notes drawer with talking points per slide and a start, pause and reset timer, so you can rehearse timing before the executive review. All controls sit in the bottom presentation bar.
