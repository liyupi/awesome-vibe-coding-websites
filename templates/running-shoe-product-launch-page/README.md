An oversized electric-blue and volt-yellow composition gives this running shoe launch a clear identity before a visitor scrolls. The centerpiece is an original procedural shoe model with a squared toe, a sculpted two-arch sole, an exposed lace bridge, and small oval ventilation details. It rotates through side, front, heel, and top views as the page moves. Dedicated angle buttons provide the same views without requiring a mouse gesture or scroll effect.

The shoe is an invented product rather than a recreation of an existing design. Consistent photographic-style assets show the fictional shoe in a studio, its construction details, and a runner’s lower legs. These images give the page the material detail of a real product launch, while the interactive model provides a useful way to inspect its form. Reduced-motion mode switches to static angle views and final specification values.

Below the hero, cushioning, stack height, drop, and illustrative weight figures are explained in plain language. A colorway control changes the model materials and selection summary. A size finder uses foot length and a transparent size table to suggest a starting point, and a local bag records the chosen size and quantity with an accurate total. It never pretends to accept an order or payment.

A visible countdown is explicitly a browser-only demonstration. It starts from a relative duration, expires honestly, and can be replayed without showing a calendar date or suggesting real stock pressure. All product text and values come from one module. The alternate example changes the fictional brand, product, specifications, and launch story while preserving the original shoe architecture and bold layout. The template suits footwear concepts and other physical product launches that benefit from real inspection controls.

## FAQ

### Can I put the running shoe 3D model on a page for my own shoe brand?
The Running Shoe Product Launch Page template ships an invented shoe built from original geometry, not a real design, so you can reuse the architecture and the angle controls for your own concept. Set BRAND and PRODUCT in the content file, then rewrite the specifications and launch story to match your shoe. The prompt tells the agent to keep the original silhouette and avoid real brand emblems or copied commercial profiles.

### How does the size finder and bag work without a checkout?
The Running Shoe Product Launch Page template includes a size finder that takes foot length and suggests a starting point from a transparent size table, plus a local bag that records size and quantity and totals it. Both stay in the browser: the bag never accepts an order or payment. Treat them as a fit and pricing preview, and connect your own store later if you need real transactions.

### What can I change first in the Running Shoe Product Launch Page template?
Start with the four variables: BRAND, PRODUCT, WEIGHT_G and PRICE. The Running Shoe Product Launch Page template keeps every headline, label, alt text and confirmation in one content file, so changing those values updates the hero, the specification section and the bag summary together. Then adjust the colorway materials and the launch story copy to match your product.

### Is the countdown on the shoe launch page a real drop timer?
No. The countdown in the Running Shoe Product Launch Page template is a browser-only demonstration that starts from a relative duration and expires honestly, with no calendar date and no claim of real stock pressure. It can be replayed. If you need a real launch timer, that requires your own scheduling logic outside the template's demo behavior.

### What happens on the shoe page when a visitor prefers reduced motion or has no WebGL?
The Running Shoe Product Launch Page template switches to discrete static angle views and final specification values when reduced motion is on, so nothing is scrubbed by scrolling. If WebGL is unavailable, the page falls back to static photographic-style assets. Angle buttons also provide Side, Front, Heel and Top views without a mouse gesture, and a Follow scroll toggle restores scroll control after dragging.
