A single-page website for an independent bookstore: the shop's promise over the stacks in warm light; a staff-picks carousel of six invented books (covers generated as abstract blank-spine art — no real titles or authors); a weekday-named events calendar (readings, signings, story hour); this month's book club pick with a join note; the shop in three photographs (the stacks, the counter, the reading nook); a bookseller portrait anchoring the staff note; hours with an open-now indicator; and an order-for-pickup form (title/author, pickup day, contact) confirming client-side. The default content follows an invented corner shop, but the structure suits any indie bookstore. The look is warm bibliophile: paper cream and ink, a mustard accent, literary serif headlines over a friendly sans, wood-shelf photography and ticket-stub event cards. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[SHOP]` | Shop name | Dog-Eared & Sons |
| `[CITY]` | City | Portland, Maine |
| `[GENRE]` | Specialty | fiction, poetry and the odd map |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add independent-bookstore-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A poetry-only bookshop: set [GENRE] to poetry and small press, swap the six picks for chapbooks, and rewrite the book club note around a monthly reading circle.
- A sci-fi and comics store: keep the ticket-stub events for signings and launch nights, and change the shop photographs to shelf walls of genre paperbacks.
- A used and rare books dealer: use the staff note for your buying policy, and reword the pickup form to ask for a title you should look out for.
- A children's bookstore: point [GENRE] at picture books and young readers, and rename the weekday events to story hour and craft mornings.

## FAQ

### What sections come with the Independent Bookstore Website template?
The Independent Bookstore Website template is one page with a hero over the stacks, a staff-picks carousel of six invented books, a weekday-named events calendar, this month's book club pick, three shop photographs, a bookseller portrait and note, hours with an open-now indicator, and an order-for-pickup form that confirms in the browser. All copy sits in one content file.

### How do I change the bookstore name, city and specialty in the prompt?
The Independent Bookstore Website template uses three variables: SHOP for the shop name, CITY for the location and GENRE for what the shop stacks deepest. Set them before you generate, then rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image caption in the content file so the copy matches your values instead of the invented defaults.

### Do the staff picks and book club use real books?
No. The Independent Bookstore Website template ships six invented titles with abstract blank-spine cover art and one invented book club pick, so nothing points at a real author or publisher. Replace the picks in the content file with your own shelf favorites, and keep the one-line review format so each card still reads like a bookseller's note.

### Where do orders from the pickup form go?
Nowhere on its own. The order-for-pickup form in the Independent Bookstore Website template validates the title, author, pickup day and contact details, then confirms client-side and saves the entry in the browser's local storage. To receive real requests, connect the form to your own email service or booking tool before you publish the site.

### How do the events calendar and open-now hours work?
The Independent Bookstore Website template names events by weekday and draws each one as a ticket-stub card, so you edit the event list in the content file rather than a calendar system. The open-now indicator reads the hours table against the visitor's clock, so set accurate hours for each day or the pill will show the shop as closed.

### How do I build an independent bookstore website with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add independent-bookstore-website, replace [SHOP], [CITY] and [GENRE] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
