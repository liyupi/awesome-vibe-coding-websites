# Dental Clinic Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [BRAND], an independent practice offering [SPECIALTY] in [CITY], a mid-size town. The look is calm, clean and trustworthy, clinical but warm: linen and soft sage surfaces, deep teal ink, one clay accent, generous whitespace, rounded cards and real photographs of the clinic's rooms and equipment (never patients). index.html carries the whole visit decision: emergency banner, hero with a live open-now card, reassurance strip, new-patient offer ([NEW_PATIENT_OFFER]), six treatment cards with price ranges, a clinic gallery, the team as monogram cards with credentials, patient reviews, insurance and payment plans, a client-side appointment request form with a day strip, time-slot chips and a success state, opening hours with an open-now indicator, a drawn map, an FAQ about pain and cost and a footer. treatments.html lists every treatment by category with a price range, duration, insurance note and what to expect, a clear aligner feature, a payment plan calculator and a closing call-to-action. It must read like a real practice that has been open for years: concrete prices, hours, an invented street address, named (invented) clinicians and patients, and plain-spoken answers.

- Audience: Local adults booking for themselves or their family: parents with young children, people who have avoided the dentist for years and are nervous, and people comparing costs because they have no insurance or a basic plan. Most visit on a phone, often from a search, and want to know three things fast: is it near me, what will it cost, and can I get in soon.
- Goal: Get the visitor to send an appointment request. Primary CTA everywhere: 'Book a visit' (to ./index.html#book). Secondary: call [PHONE] (tel: link) and 'See treatments & prices' (./treatments.html). For emergencies the banner and booking side panel push the phone call instead of the form.
- Tone: Warm, plain and reassuring, like a good receptionist on the phone. Short sentences, real numbers, no jargon without a plain explanation, no fear tactics, no hype words (no world-class, state-of-the-art, cutting-edge, best-in-class, revolutionary). Address the reader as 'you'; the practice speaks as 'we'.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Practice name (default: "Wrenfield Dental"). Shown in the nav wordmark, titles, footer, booking success text and FAQ answers. The care plan is called '[BRAND] Care Plan' with the trailing word Dental dropped ('Wrenfield Care Plan').
- [CITY] — Town or city (default: "Calder Springs"). An invented or real town name. Used in the hero eyebrow, titles, the address block and the footer.
- [SPECIALTY] — Practice type (default: "family & cosmetic dentistry"). Lowercase phrase. Drives the hero eyebrow, the titles and all treatments, prices, team credentials, FAQ answers and photos. Rewrite those for another kind of practice.
- [NEW_PATIENT_OFFER] — New-patient offer (default: "$89 first visit: exam, cleaning & X-rays"). Price first, then what it includes. Feeds the offer section, the hero trust row, the booking reason list and the FAQ.
- [PHONE] — Phone number (default: "(320) 555-0142"). Use a 555-01xx number for demos. Shown in the emergency banner, nav, booking panel, footer and as tel: links (digits only in the href, e.g. tel:+13205550142).
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/dental-clinic-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

- Spacing: 8px base unit. Container max-width 1200px, side gutter clamp(20px, 4vw, 40px). Section vertical padding 112px on desktop, 72px under 768px. H3 Figtree 600 20px/1.3 Ink. Body Figtree 400 17px/1.65 Ink (16px under 768px); lead paragraphs 19px Slate. Eyebrows Figtree 600 13px uppercase, letter-spacing 0.12em, Teal at 80%, with a 6px Clay dot 10px before the text. Prices Fraunces 500 with font-variant-numeric tabular-nums. Section headers: eyebrow, H2 and a max-width 56ch lead, 48px below the header before content.
- Radius: Cards and photo tiles 24px, the new-patient offer card and the booking form card 28px, inputs and select 14px, buttons, chips, the day strip chips and time-slot chips fully rounded (999px), icon circles and monogram avatars are circles. Primary buttons are 52px tall pills (44px in the nav), padding 0 26px, Figtree 600 16px, Clay background, Linen text, with a lucide ArrowRight 18px that moves 3px right on hover. Secondary buttons are the same size with a 1.5px Teal border, Teal text, transparent background, Sage 100 fill on hover.
- Motion: Easing everywhere: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Durations: 200ms for hovers and chip selection, 300ms for accordions and menus, 600ms for reveals.
- Motion: Reveal: elements with class .reveal start at opacity 0 and translateY(16px) and go to rest over 600ms when 15% visible, once (one shared IntersectionObserver in src/lib/reveal.ts adds .is-in). Siblings in a grid stagger by 70ms via a --i CSS variable (transition-delay calc(var(--i) * 70ms)). Elements already in the viewport on load reveal immediately with the same stagger.
- Motion: Card hover (treatment cards, team cards, plan cards, gallery tiles): translateY(-3px) and shadow card to lift over 200ms; gallery images also scale to 1.03 over 600ms inside their overflow-hidden tile.
- Motion: Open-now dot: an 8px Open circle with a ring that pulses (keyframes pulse-dot: box-shadow 0 0 0 0 rgba(47,125,91,0.45) to 0 0 0 8px rgba(47,125,91,0), 2000ms, infinite). When closed the dot is Slate and does not pulse.
- Motion: Accordions (FAQ, what-to-expect, mobile menu groups): height animates with the CSS grid trick (grid-template-rows 0fr to 1fr, inner overflow hidden) over 300ms; the lucide Plus icon rotates 45 degrees when open.
- Motion: Chips: day and slot chips scale to 0.97 on press; the selected state swaps background and text color over 200ms.
- Motion: Emergency banner dismiss: collapses height to 0 and fades over 250ms, then unmounts.
- Motion: Booking success: the form fades out (200ms), the success card fades in and rises 12px over 400ms, and the page scrolls so the card's top sits 96px below the viewport top.
- Motion: Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce): reveals show immediately with no transform, no stagger, no hover lift or image zoom, no dot pulse, accordions open and close instantly, smooth scrolling is replaced with instant jumps, the banner disappears without animation.
- Imagery: Real photographs of the clinic's rooms, furniture and equipment in soft daylight: sage walls, light white oak, cream upholstery, plants in terracotta. Never patients, never faces, never before/after mouths. Six photos are made for this template and hosted by its author; three CC0 photos (a quiet treatment-room lounge, handpieces on a dental unit, bamboo toothbrushes) add real-world detail. Photos are always shown in 24px-radius tiles with object-fit cover and the exact object-position given, never full-bleed behind text. People are shown as monogram avatars. Icons come from lucide-react; the favicon, wordmark glyph, the map, rating bars and decorative arches are drawn in SVG or CSS. No illustrations, no stock smiling-model photos, no 3D, no gradients other than the subtle ones specified.

## Don'ts
- No backend of any kind: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, payments, email services, analytics or third-party APIs. The booking form calls preventDefault, validates in the browser and only swaps to the success state; it never submits, sends or stores anything.
- No map embeds or map tile services; the map is the inline SVG described.
- Do not load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any CDN. Self-host through the two Fontsource packages only.
- No CDN <script>, <link> or import maps. Every script, style, font, icon and image is in the project and bundled into dist/.
- No hotlinked images: every photo is downloaded into public/images/ once and served from there, and every third-party file is listed in ASSETS.md.
- No source maps in the production build (build.sourcemap false), and src/ is not copied into dist/.
- Relative asset paths (Vite base './'). The site is two real HTML files; every internal link points to ./index.html, ./treatments.html or a hash on them, never to a bare directory or /.
- Do not change the stack (Vite 5 + React 18 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS 3.4 + lucide-react + Fontsource) unless the user asks. No animation library, no UI kit, no router: two entries and hash anchors.
- No real clinic's name, logo, slogan or copy, and no real dentists' names or credentials. All clinicians, patients, reviews, prices and the address are invented.
- No photos or illustrations of people: no faces, no patients, no before/after mouths, no stock smiling models. Team members appear only as monogram avatars.
- No real insurance companies, dental brands, equipment makers or product names anywhere in copy or images. Describe insurance only as PPO, HMO / DHMO and out-of-network.
- No medical claims beyond what is written here: no guarantees, no 'painless', no success-rate numbers. Keep the advice to go to the emergency room for swelling with trouble breathing or swallowing.
- Only the listed images, with the exact crops given (lounge.jpg always at object-position 78% 50% so its left side stays hidden). No Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay or other stock sites with their own licenses, no placeholder-image services.
- Clay is the only accent and is reserved for booking buttons, the emergency banner and notices, stars, the map pin and the focus ring. No other bright colors, no blue medical gradients, no glassmorphism, no dark mode.
- No commercial or custom fonts: only Fraunces and Figtree (OFL).
- Only dependencies under MIT, ISC, BSD, Apache-2.0, 0BSD, CC0 or Unlicense (OFL for fonts). No Aceternity UI, Tailwind Plus / Tailwind UI or purchased template code.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion exactly as described under Design motion.
- Must render at 1440×900 and 390×844 with zero console errors and no horizontal page scroll (only the day strip and the mobile category pills scroll inside themselves). Every interactive element is keyboard reachable with a visible Clay focus ring.
- Keep dist/ under 20 MB (target under 8 MB).
- No lorem ipsum, filler or hype words. Use the exact headlines, prices, hours and CTAs given here for the default values. No meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD or sitemap: only the <title> and favicon.
