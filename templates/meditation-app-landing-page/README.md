A single-page landing for a meditation app, built entirely from gradients, soft shapes and type — no photography anywhere. The first screen is a slow gradient sky that drifts from dawn rose to midday lavender to dusk plum over three minutes, and in front of it the interactive centerpiece: a working 4-7-8 breathing circle. Press Begin and the circle expands for four counts, holds for seven and settles for eight, with a cue line, a progress ring and a cycle counter — visitors try the product before reading a word about it. Below, the page keeps the same calm: a sessions library preview with focus, sleep and anxiety cards and duration chips; a dusk-gradient sleep-stories band with a waveform drawn in code and a play chip that gently changes its amplitude; a CSS phone mockup showing one session screen; sample pricing with a monthly/yearly toggle; and three short, experiential testimonials. The look is locked: warm porcelain ground, sky rose, dawn gold and dusk lavender, deep plum text, one still-teal accent, Quicksand display over Figtree, pill buttons and 20px card radii, whitespace measured in breaths rather than pixels. Every animation is slow — the sky loop, the circle, the waveform — and every one has a calm static fallback under prefers-reduced-motion. Copy stays experiential (settle, rest, quieter days) with no therapeutic claims, and pricing is sample copy with no checkout. Swap brand, price, library size and free minutes in one content file; the second demo case turns the same page into a deep-work focus timer.

## Make it yours

- A breathwork studio: set BRAND to your studio name, rewrite SESSIONS as your class library and link the Begin button area to your booking page.
- A sleep-audio app: keep the dusk sleep-stories band, swap narrators and story titles in the content file, and set FREE_MINUTES to your free trial allowance.
- A deep-work timer for indie makers: reframe sessions as focus blocks, change the cue words to start and rest, and keep the still-teal accent.
- A corporate wellness offering: set BRAND to the program name, replace testimonials with employee quotes and make PRICE_MONTH a per-seat figure.

## FAQ

### How does the 4-7-8 breathing circle on the Meditation App Landing Page work?
The Meditation App Landing Page includes a working 4-7-8 breathing circle: press Begin and the circle expands for four counts, holds for seven with a soft ripple ring, and settles back over eight, while cue words crossfade, a progress arc tracks each phase and a cycle counter increments after every exhale. Pressing Rest stops it, so visitors can try the practice before reading any copy.

### What can I change in the Meditation App Landing Page template without touching the design?
The Meditation App Landing Page template keeps brand, sessions, price and free minutes in one content file. Set BRAND to your app name, SESSIONS to your library size, PRICE_MONTH to your monthly price and FREE_MINUTES to your free daily allowance, and the wordmark, hero sub-line, library intro, pricing rows and header CTA all update from those values while layout, colors and motion stay fixed.

### Does the Meditation App Landing Page template use photos or stock imagery?
The Meditation App Landing Page template uses no photography at all. The calm comes from three CSS gradient skies that drift from dawn rose to midday lavender to dusk plum, one barely moving sun ellipse, the breathing circle and a code-drawn waveform on the sleep-stories band. You never need to source, license or replace images to launch the page.

### Can the Meditation App Landing Page template be turned into a focus timer or a different wellness app?

Change the section copy in the content file toward focus and rest breaks, keep the teal accent and gradient sky, and the page reads as a timer app rather than a meditation one.
