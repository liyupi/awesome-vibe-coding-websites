# Hospital Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Warm Clinical White** `#FCFBF8` — Primary calm page canvas
- **Hospital Blue** `#1356A8` — Primary trust accent, links, and clinical headers
- **Calming Mint** `#3CC2A0` — Availability badges, wellness highlights, and wayfinding
- **Alert Coral** `#FF6F59` — Emergency wait-time emphasis and urgent care indicators
- **Deep Slate Ink** `#142136` — High-contrast primary body text

## Typography
- **Headings & Wayfinding Titles** — Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Readable Body Copy & Portal Data** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Clear persistent top emergency strip, clean header nav, and generous 1280px max-width cards with big touch targets and accessible line-height.
- Radius: 16px soft architectural cards with clean hairline borders.

## Motion
- Instant client-side filtering across doctors, departments, health articles, and mock portal tabs.
- Smooth drawer/detail panels for department services and physician profiles.

## Imagery
Four cohesive sunlit architectural & clinical photos from one shoot: bright glass atrium, daylight recovery suite, modern diagnostic imaging console detail, and calm landscaped campus walkway—staff strictly distant or from behind, zero close-up faces, zero readable text.

## Tone
Reassuring, transparent, modern civic healthcare—plain English, generous spacing, high contrast.
