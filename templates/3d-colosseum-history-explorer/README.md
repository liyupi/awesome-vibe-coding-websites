This history explorer presents the Colosseum as a structure you can take apart. A warm limestone model appears immediately, with open arcades, rising seats, an arena floor and a network of spaces below it. Drag to inspect the outer walls, zoom toward the center or choose a feature from the accompanying list. The model and interface are built from code, so the example does not depend on a downloaded architectural asset.

A state slider follows the building from foundations through rising arches and a completed interpretation to a weathered ruin. The labels describe physical states rather than calendar years. A cutaway removes a front section and exposes how the seating, arena and underground routes fit together. Four learning stops explain the arena floor, hypogeum, seating tiers and shade canopy. Each moves the camera to a useful view and pairs the scene with a short note. Visitors can explore freely or follow a guided tour with pause, previous, next and exit controls.

The layout feels like a compact architectural exhibit. Mineral paper, fine rules, a readable serif and restrained technical labels frame the model without competing with it. On a phone, the stage, timeline and explanations become a natural vertical sequence with touch controls. Reduced motion keeps every learning feature available through immediate state changes.

The prompt suits educators, museum prototypes and architecture studies that need a spatial explanation rather than a long article. Change the studio name, exhibit title, audience and accent to make a separate edition. Copy and labels stay in one content module. The model clearly identifies reconstructed details as interpretive and its proportions as approximate, with a link to the archaeological park for further reading.

## FAQ

### How do I change the studio name, exhibit title and audience on the 3D Colosseum History Explorer?
The 3D Colosseum History Explorer template keeps four variables in one content module: BRAND for the learning studio name, EDITION for the exhibit title, AUDIENCE for who the exhibit addresses, and ACCENT for the interface color. Set those values and rewrite every headline, paragraph and label to match, keeping the same length and tone. The architectural subject stays the Colosseum.

### What does the construction state slider in the 3D Colosseum History Explorer actually show?
The 3D Colosseum History Explorer template includes a state scrubber that moves the model from foundations through rising arches and a completed interpretation to a weathered ruin. The labels describe physical states rather than calendar years, so no dates appear. Each state pairs the model with a short explanation, and the cutaway stays available as the structure changes.

### What are the four learning stops in the 3D Colosseum History Explorer?
The 3D Colosseum History Explorer template includes four learning stops covering the arena floor, the hypogeum below it, the seating tiers and the shade canopy. Selecting a stop moves the camera to a useful view and shows a short note beside the scene. Visitors can explore freely or follow a guided tour with pause, previous, next and exit controls.

### Does the 3D Colosseum History Explorer need downloaded 3D models or external services to run?
The 3D Colosseum History Explorer template builds the entire amphitheater from code using Three.js, with original procedural meshes, canvas textures and shaders rather than a downloaded architectural asset. A settled high-quality model renders immediately on load.
