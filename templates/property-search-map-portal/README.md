# Property Search Map Portal <sup>PRO</sup>

> Home search portal with a code-drawn price map, URL-synced filters, listing pages with a mortgage estimate.

A home search portal built around the two things people actually do: scan a list and look at a map. The first screen splits the page between scrolling listing cards and a map drawn entirely in code, with coral price pins that cluster together when zoomed out, pan and zoom controls, and a preview card above a pin when it is clicked. Hovering a card lifts its pin on the map. Filters for price, beds, type, neighbourhood and more are written into the URL, so any search can be bookmarked, shared or saved by name and applied again later. Each listing page has a photo gallery, the facts, a price history chart, school and commute cards and a mortgage estimate with sliders for down payment and rate. Homes can be saved, and a tour request form with time slots ends the page. Forty fictional listings across six neighbourhoods give the list real density, and all photographs come from one bright, airy shoot with no house numbers or signs. White, deep teal, coral pins and sand, with a serif for prices and headings. Change the portal name, city and agent in the content file and swap in your own listings and photos; layout, palette and motion stay as described.

## FAQ

### How does the map work if it doesn't use a map service like Google Maps?
The Property Search Map Portal draws its map entirely in code as one inline SVG: sand blocks, white streets, a water band, three park shapes and neighbourhood labels. Each of the forty fictional listings has an x and y position, so coral price pins sit on the right block. Pins cluster when zoomed out, pan and zoom are clamped so the map cannot be dragged away, and clicking a pin opens a preview card. No external APIs are called.

### Can I share or bookmark a specific property search?
Yes. The Property Search Map Portal writes every filter into the URL, so a search like price, beds, type, neighbourhood and sort order can be bookmarked, pasted into a message or opened later and applied again. Visitors can also save a search by name, and saved homes and saved searches persist in the browser through localStorage under a key built from the portal name.

### How do I estimate the monthly mortgage cost on a listing page?
Each listing page in the Property Search Map Portal includes a mortgage estimate with sliders for down payment and interest rate. The estimate is calculated in the browser from the listing price, so moving a slider updates the monthly figure immediately. There is no backend and no lender integration; the numbers are an illustration to help a visitor judge what a home might cost each month.

### What do I need to change to make this portal my own brokerage's site?
Set the three variables: BRAND for the portal name, CITY for the city and AGENT_NAME for the person who runs tours. Then replace the forty fictional listings, neighbourhoods, schools and commute lines in the data file, and swap the photographs for your own shoot. All copy, filter options, tour slots and form labels live in one content file, so you can rewrite the wording without touching the layout, palette or motion.
