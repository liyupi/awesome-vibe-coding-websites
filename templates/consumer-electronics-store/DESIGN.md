# Consumer Electronics Store — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Studio White** `#FFFFFF` — Primary storefront background
- **Graphite Chassis** `#1C1F24` — Primary typography, dark spec headers, and structural contrast
- **Electric Blue** `#2D6BFF` — Primary Add-to-Cart, active filters, and compare highlights
- **Sale Red** `#FF3B30` — Deals savings badges and low-stock indicators
- **Cyclorama Grey** `#F3F5F8` — Product studio card staging backdrop

## Typography
- **Product Names & Headlines** — Space Grotesk (OFL), weights 600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Body, Reviews & Filters** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Prices, SKUs & Specs** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Crisp top utility strip + expandable Mega Menu header + 1280px structured product grids with generous studio breathing room.
- Radius: 16px clean hardware cards on #F3F5F8 studio plinths with sharp 1px borders.

## Motion
- Smooth Mega Menu hover/click panel, slide-over Cart Drawer with instant subtotal + protection-plan math, and live spec filtering.

## Imagery
Five brand-free invented industrial hardware products (matte smartphone, ultra-slim aluminum laptop, over-ear studio headphones, compact rangefinder camera, cylindrical woven smart speaker) photographed in one clean neutral-grey studio shoot with zero logos or readable text.

## Tone
Precision industrial design—uncluttered, technical specs presented cleanly with crisp visual restraint.
