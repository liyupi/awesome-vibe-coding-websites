# Y2K Chrome Fashion Drop Page

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A one-page limited drop site for [BRAND], which makes [PRODUCT_LINE] in [HOME_BASE]. The page announces [DROP_NAME]: four pieces, [EDITION_SIZE] of each, no restock. The first screen is one huge liquid-metal 3D wordmark on near-black with cyan, lilac and pink light, and a live countdown to the drop. Below it: marquee, draggable product carousel with die-cut stickers, product detail with size picker, lookbook strip, front-end-only waitlist that ends in a queue ticket, drop rules and footer.

- Audience: People who follow small labels and creator capsules, shop on their phones, know what a drop is and will set an alarm for one. On the template side: indie fashion labels, merch runs and creators selling a small batch on a set day.
- Goal: Get the visitor to pick a piece and a size, then join the waitlist before the countdown ends. Every section feeds the same selection, and the page ends in a ticket the visitor would want to screenshot.
- Tone: Loud, glossy and a little cocky, with short sentences and specific numbers. It sounds like a small crew that knows its run sizes, not a retailer. No exclamation marks, no 'elevate', no 'curated'.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Brand name (default: "Moonfoil"). Becomes the 3D wordmark in capitals. Latin letters, one word of up to 9 letters looks best; two words break at the space on phones.
- [DROP_NAME] — Name of this drop (default: "Afterglow Capsule")
- [PRODUCT_LINE] — What the brand makes (default: "small-run heavyweight streetwear")
- [HOME_BASE] — City and region (default: "Portland, Oregon")
- [EDITION_SIZE] — Pieces per style (default: "200"). A number. Feeds the '1 OF ...' stickers and the scarcity copy.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/y2k-chrome-fashion-drop-page in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Design
### Colors
- Void `#07060D` — Page ground, hero, carousel, lookbook, footer; text on holographic surfaces
- Ultraviolet `#160B36` — Ground for the piece detail and rules sections, so the page shifts color as it scrolls
- Electric Cyan `#27F2FF` — Hero light sweep, wireframe globe, focus rings, in-stock dots, cyan stickers
- Hot Pink `#FF2FB3` — Hero light sweep, low-stock states, pink stickers, lookbook color tile
- Lilac `#B79CFF` — Middle of the holographic gradient, floor grid, secondary borders
- Acid Yellow `#E8FF3A` — Tail of the holographic gradient and the loudest stickers. Small doses only
- Chrome Light `#F2F5FC` — Primary text on dark grounds, sticker outlines
- Steel `#A9B0C6` — Secondary text and labels on dark grounds

### Typography
- Display: section headlines, product names, marquee, and the outlines of the 3D wordmark. Uppercase with -0.02em tracking. H2 is clamp(34px, 5vw, 72px) at 1.02 line height: Unbounded (OFL), weights 700/900
- Text: body at 17px/1.55, buttons at 15px weight 600, form inputs: Hanken Grotesk (OFL), weights 400/500/600
- Mono: countdown digits, stickers, prices, size chips, spec labels, ticket. Labels are uppercase at 12px with 0.08em tracking: Martian Mono (OFL), weights 500/700

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base. Sections use 120px vertical padding on desktop and 72px on mobile. Content max-width is 1320px with 40px gutters on desktop and 16px on mobile. Card gap is 24px.
- Radius: Everything is a capsule or a soft window: 999px on buttons, chips and countdown panels, 28px on product cards, 20px on pill windows, 16px on inputs. Stickers keep their die-cut shapes.
- Motion: Wordmark: continuous ripple through the shader, plus damped tilt toward the cursor (rotation y up to 0.22 rad, x up to 0.14 rad, THREE.MathUtils.damp with lambda 4). On touch devices it sways slowly on sin(t*0.4) instead.
- Motion: Hero light: the three radial gradients drift by up to 6% over 18s, alternating, in CSS.
- Motion: Countdown digits: each changed digit slides in from y -12px over 200ms with Motion AnimatePresence.
- Motion: Marquee: CSS translateX(0 to -50%) over 28s linear, paused on hover.
- Motion: Product cards: perspective tilt up to 6deg and a holographic sheen following the pointer, 120ms transition.
- Motion: Stickers: spring pop on hover (scale 1.14, stiffness 520, damping 14) with a small corner fold appearing.
- Motion: Sections: content fades up 24px over 500ms with Motion whileInView, once, staggered by 60ms.
- Motion: Pixel stars twinkle with steps(3) over 2.4s. Globe meridians animate scaleX 1 to -1 over 14s with stagger, which reads as a spin.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: wordmark renders once with frameloop 'demand' and no ripple or tilt, marquee is static, drift, twinkle, globe, confetti and fade-ups are off, digits swap instantly and sheen sits still at 25% opacity.
- Imagery: One shoot throughout: garments and models under cyan and magenta rim light on a near-black seamless backdrop, glossy and high contrast, so photos melt into the page ground. Six generated photos: four products and two lookbook frames. Everything else is drawn in code: the 3D wordmark, gradients, stickers, globes, pixel stars, floor grid, the locked fifth card.

## Don'ts
- No backend: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, checkout, payments or third-party APIs. The waitlist form never submits anywhere and makes no network request.
- No CDN script or link tags, no fonts.googleapis.com or fonts.gstatic.com, no analytics. Every font, image, HDRI and script ships inside dist/.
- Do not use drei Environment presets or any other helper that fetches files from a remote host at runtime. The HDRI loads only from public/hdri/.
- No hotlinking. Download each listed file into the project. No placeholder-image services and no Unsplash, Pexels or Pixabay.
- No source maps, no src/ in dist/, relative asset paths only. Keep dist/ under 20 MB. Internal links are section anchors on index.html, never bare directories.
- Zero console errors at 1440x900 and 390x844, and no horizontal page scroll at either size (the rotated marquee sits inside an overflow-hidden wrapper). If WebGL or the font fetch fails, fall back quietly to the DOM wordmark.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion as described in the motion list.
- Do not use logos, box marks, monograms or label designs of real streetwear, luxury or sportswear brands. Garments carry no logos, text or graphics.
- Do not reproduce recognizable garment or sneaker silhouettes or signature prints from real products and collaborations. No sneakers on this page.
- No commercial or custom chrome display fonts. The chrome comes only from the 3D material on the OFL font's outlines, plus the CSS gradient text in the listed fonts.
- No real celebrities, musicians or models, or their likeness, in lookbook shots. No faces in close-up.
- No trademarked drop-platform names, retailer badges, payment badges, or real slogans and campaign lines.
- No icons, mascots, window controls or characters tied to a real Y2K-era operating system, game console, phone maker or media player. Pill windows use only the star and x described here, never a row of three colored dots.
- No code from restricted effect libraries for holographic cards or metallic text. Write the tilt, sheen and chrome effects from scratch as described.
- Do not fall back to a cream or muted palette, and do not swap the near-black ground for a light one. Cyan, pink and lilac must be visible on the first screen.
- No mention of the template source inside the site, apart from the optional credit link. No dates shown other than the weekday and time of the drop.
