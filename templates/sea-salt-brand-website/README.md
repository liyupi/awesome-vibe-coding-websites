# Sea Salt Brand Website <sup>PRO</sup>

> A tidal water surface opens a tactile salt shop, harvest story and marsh visit.

This sea salt brand website starts with a surface you can disturb. Move a pointer or a finger across the opening scene and the water breaks into small ripples over a submerged wordmark. As the page moves, the waterline falls, exposing clay and clusters of salt. The material becomes the introduction to a fictional coastal producer, rather than a decorative background behind a sales pitch.

The rest of the page makes that opening useful. A three-part harvest story pairs short explanations with a changing overhead diagram and a photograph of the work. Three salt products each show a distinct texture, a tin size, a price and specific cooking suggestions. A visitor can add tins, change quantities, remove items and review an itemized demo order. A separate marsh walk section combines a coastal photograph, a small illustrated map and a local-only request form.

The design uses gray coastal light, mineral paper, fine dividing rules and a rounded serif paired with a clear sans serif. Photographs are deliberately unbranded, so a second fictional producer can use the same visual system while changing its name, coast, harvesters and salt collection. All copy and product data sit in one content module. The water wordmark changes with the producer as well.

This is suited to a small food maker that needs both atmosphere and understandable products. The finished example is static, with bundled fonts and images, touch-friendly controls and a reduced-motion presentation. Checkout and visit requests are clearly marked demonstrations; they do not send information or collect payment. Replace those interactions with an appropriate service when adapting the design to a working business.

## Make it yours

- A small coastal salt maker: set BRAND to your producer name, COAST to your shoreline, FOUNDERS to your harvesters and COLLECTION to your three salts, then rewrite the harvest story and product pairings.
- A spice or smoked-flavor producer: keep the water opening but set COLLECTION to three smoked or blended seasonings, rewrite cooking suggestions and use ACCENT to match your packaging.
- A farm or food maker running coastal visits: keep the marsh walk section, set COAST and FOUNDERS to your place and people, and point the visit request form at your own booking service.
- A designer building a portfolio piece for a food client: use the BRAND, COAST and ACCENT variables to show a second fictional producer, and swap the unbranded photographs for the client's own product shots.

## FAQ

### Can I use the Sea Salt Brand Website template for a real business, not just a demo?
The Sea Salt Brand Website template is a Pro template, so commercial and client use is covered once you are a Pro member. The finished demo is static and its checkout and marsh visit request are labeled demonstrations that do not send information or collect payment. To run it as a working business, replace those interactions with an appropriate service before launch.

### How do I change the brand name, coast and harvesters in the Sea Salt Brand Website template?
All copy, product data, image paths and links for the Sea Salt Brand Website template sit in one content module. Set the BRAND variable to your producer name, COAST to your fictional or real coast, FOUNDERS to your harvesters and COLLECTION to your three salts. The water wordmark and every headline, paragraph and label update from those values, so rewrite the default copy to match your new brand and coast.

### What does the interactive water opening on the Sea Salt Brand Website actually do?
The Sea Salt Brand Website opens with a tidal water surface built with Three.js. Moving a pointer or a finger across the scene breaks the water into small ripples over a submerged wordmark. As the page scrolls, the waterline falls to expose clay and clusters of salt. The scene appears immediately with a visible fallback while loading and has a reduced-motion presentation.

### How does the cart work on the Sea Salt Brand Website template?
The Sea Salt Brand Website template includes a working local-only cart. Visitors can add tins of the three salts, change quantities, remove items and review an itemized demo order. Checkout is clearly marked as a demonstration and does not collect payment or send information. For a live store, connect the cart to a payment and order service that suits your business.

### What should I change first when adapting the Sea Salt Brand Website to my own salt or food product?
Start with the BRAND, COAST, FOUNDERS and COLLECTION variables in the content module, then rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image path so no default brand or coast remains. Set ACCENT to a muted botanical or coastal hex color that fits your product. After that, replace the demo checkout and marsh visit request with real services, and check the water scene and controls on a phone.
