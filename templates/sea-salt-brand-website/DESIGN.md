# Sea Salt Brand Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Paper** `#ECEBE5` — Lower-page mineral paper background
- **Ink** `#263832` — Text and dark buttons
- **Water** `#6E8588` — Gray coastal water
- **Clay** `#77716A` — Revealed textured salt-pan base
- **Salt** `#F9F6ED` — Crystal highlights
- **Accent** `[ACCENT]` — Botanical product detail

## Typography
- **Display and wordmark** — Fraunces (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.
- **Body and UI** — Manrope (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: 8px base. Desktop 32px outer gutters and max-width 1320px below hero. Sections 112px apart; mobile 20px gutters and 64px section spacing. Headlines 72px desktop / 42px phone, body 16–18px. Generous legible lines.
- Radius: 0–3px; rectangular photographs, fine rules, no pill-card grid.

## Motion
- An initial 700ms water settle and 160ms nav reveal; copy never stays hidden waiting for animation.
- Water ripples respond to pointer/touch at their actual location with decay; native scrolling lowers tide continuously and reveals instanced crystal clusters.
- Harvest diagram changes in three readable progress states. Product button feedback is 140ms. Reduced motion uses settled states.

## Imagery
Five supplied photographic images in one cool overcast coastal shoot. Photographs show unbranded ingredients, rake and landscape without faces or text. The hero is original code-built WebGL water over clay, with the live brand wordmark refracted below. Do not substitute photographs for the interactive hero or treat code-drawn products as a substitute for the three supplied product photos.

## Tone
Quiet, specific, unhurried US English. Warm craft without exaggerated claims.
