Personal Chef Service Website is an intimate, candlelit template for an invented private chef. The first screen anchors on a moody macro of a plated dish in candlelight, the chef's name set in an elegant serif, and one quiet promise — then the page walks a client through everything they need to book: sample menus, how a dinner actually runs, what kinds of gatherings work, and what it costs.

Menus are the heart: three tasting menus as course cards with per-guest sample rates, from a four-course garden menu to a seven-course long table. A four-step timeline explains the rhythm of a dinner — the consult, the market run, the cook, the table — and three quiet event cards pair photography with realistic group sizes. The interactive centerpiece is a per-guest pricing calculator: slide the guest count, pick a menu tier, and the estimate updates with an honest small-party supplement and a ten-guest floor on the long table. An inquiry form validates and saves locally.

The look is chiaroscuro restraint: near-black warm ground, candlelight amber accents, cream type, Cormorant Garamond headlines over Karla body text, and six photographs from one candlelit shoot — plated dishes, table settings, hands at work, no faces.

Swap the chef, city, cuisine, and promise to tune the voice; menus, tiers, steps, quotes, and rates are records in one content file. This template suits private chefs, supper clubs, catering studios, and tasting-event hosts. The stack is Vite, React, Tailwind, and Motion; static output, self-hosted fonts, no booking backend, no payments, and no dates tied to the real clock.

| `[CITY]` | City | Bridgehaven |
| `[CUISINE]` | Cuisine | Mediterranean coastal |
| `[PROMISE]` | Promise | One menu, written for your table — nothing reheated, nothing rushed. |

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add personal-chef-service-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A supper club host: set BRAND to your club name, CUISINE to your rotating theme, and swap the three tasting menus for your upcoming dinner dates and per-seat rates.
- A catering studio: keep the calculator but change the menu tiers to your buffet, plated and family-style packages, and rewrite the event cards for weddings, corporate lunches and private parties.
- A tasting-event organizer: replace the four-step dinner timeline with your tasting flow, and turn the inquiry form into a waitlist note for your next seated event.
- A weekly meal-prep chef: set PROMISE to your weekly service, change the menu cards to weekly plans, and adjust the calculator tiers to per-week pricing instead of per-guest dinners.

## FAQ

### How does the per-guest pricing calculator on the Personal Chef Service Website template work?
The Personal Chef Service Website template includes an interactive calculator with a guest slider from 2 to 30 people and three menu tiers. The Garden menu is $78 per guest, Cellar is $95, and Long Table is $135. A $60 kitchen supplement applies to parties under six, and the Long Table tier has a ten-guest minimum. The estimate updates as you move the slider or change tiers.

### What do I need to change to make the Personal Chef Service Website template my own?
Set the four variables in the Personal Chef Service Website template: BRAND for the chef or studio name, CITY for where you cook, CUISINE for your cooking style, and PROMISE for your one-line creed. Then rewrite the sample menus, course lists, per-guest rates, four-step dinner timeline, event cards and client quotes in the single content file so they match your real service.

### Can the Personal Chef Service Website template take real bookings or payments?
No. The Personal Chef Service Website template has no booking backend and no payment processing. The inquiry form validates entries and saves them locally in the browser, but it never submits anywhere. To take real bookings you would connect the form to your own email service, booking tool or payment provider after adapting the template.
