Space Tech Startup Website is a deep-space marketing site for an invented small-satellite company. A near-black star field with two parallax layers runs behind every section, drawn on a single canvas that answers scroll. The first screen is anchored by an animated orbit diagram in SVG: a planet with a fine graticule and shaded night side, three thin elliptical orbits, and small code-drawn satellites circling with fading signal-blue telemetry trails; mono labels tick altitude, velocity and link figures beside it, marked as simulated values. The mission overview states why in plain language, a mono spec table lists the spacecraft bus with design-target disclaimers, and a stylized globe with abstract continents shows ground stations pinging under orbit arcs. A vertical rail carries the launch manifest: six milestones with ring nodes that light up as the rail draws on scroll, statuses limited to complete, building and scheduled, and no calendar dates anywhere. An invented, text-only investors row and a three-role careers strip close the page. The palette is disciplined: near-black, off-white, one signal blue. Space Grotesk sets tracked-caps headlines, Space Mono carries telemetry, and motion is calm and mechanical rather than cinematic. Everything is drawn in code, so there are no photographs, no people and no bitmaps beyond the favicon. It suits space, robotics, climate-hardware and other mission-driven startups that need to look credible to enterprise customers, investors and engineers. The default case is a telemetry constellation; the alternate case is an orbital propellant depot, proving the layout survives a different mission entirely. Change the variables, rewrite one content file, and the structure holds.

## Make it yours

- A small-satellite operator: set BRAND and CONSTELLATION to your program, write SERVICE as your downlink offering, and replace the manifest milestones with your own launch phases and statuses.
- An orbital logistics startup: keep the deep-space palette but rewrite the spec table for your transfer vehicle and turn the coverage globe into docking or depot locations.
- A robotics hardware founder raising a round: use the mission overview and investors row to state the problem plainly, and point the careers strip at the three roles you are hiring.
- A climate-hardware company: swap the orbit diagram labels for sensor network figures, set HQ_LINE to your facility, and describe SERVICE as the environmental data your hardware delivers.

### How do I change the company name and mission in the Space Tech Startup Website template?
Set the BRAND, CONSTELLATION, SERVICE and HQ_LINE variables in the Space Tech Startup Website template. BRAND drives the wordmark, page title, footer and mailto links; CONSTELLATION appears in the hero eyebrow, mission headline and manifest entries; SERVICE is the lower-case noun phrase describing what the constellation delivers; HQ_LINE is the footer address and its last segment shows on careers role cards. When values differ from the defaults, rewrite the copy in the single content file to fit, keeping the same lengths, structure and tone.

### Can I use the Space Tech Startup Website template for a robotics or climate hardware company?
Yes. The Space Tech Startup Website template suits space, robotics, climate-hardware and other mission-driven startups that need to look credible to enterprise customers, investors and engineers. ### How does the scroll animation work on the Space Tech Startup Website template?
The Space Tech Startup Website template draws a two-layer parallax star field on one fixed canvas behind the page, with the layers drifting at different speeds and responding to scroll. The orbit diagram's satellites and telemetry labels run on a shared animation clock, and the launch manifest rail draws as you scroll with ring nodes lighting up. Section reveals use scroll-triggered motion. When reduced motion is requested, the page renders one static frame and stops.

### Are the telemetry numbers and launch milestones on the Space Tech Startup Website template real?
No. The Space Tech Startup Website template is a marketing design for an invented company. Telemetry labels tick simulated values and are marked as simulated, spacecraft specs are labeled design targets, the investors row is text-only and invented, and the launch manifest uses statuses limited to complete, building and scheduled with no calendar dates. Replace the sample content in the content file with your own verified figures and milestones before publishing.
