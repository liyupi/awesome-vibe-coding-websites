> A warm, code-drawn smart home landing page with a clickable cutaway house showing devices and automations.

This is a warm, soft-light landing page for a smart home starter brand, built with React, Tailwind CSS and Motion, and drawn entirely in code — there is not one photograph in it. The first screen sells with an interactive cutaway house: a hand-built SVG of a two-story home with an attached garage. Click or tab a room — living, kitchen, bedroom or garage — and it highlights in calm teal while the card beside it lists the devices inside and the one automation that room runs. The lineup section pairs six device cards with a second, simplified house map: hover a card and its dots pulse where that device lives. Two CSS phone mockups show the app, and the second phone plays the signature scene — Leaving Home — checking off four devices in sequence, with a replay chip. A facts strip, three sample-priced bundles, an FAQ and a reserve-your-kit email band finish the page.

It suits hardware founders and indie makers launching a device kit, a sensor set or any calm consumer product that needs demonstrating rather than describing. The look is warm white with rounded, friendly cards, generous radii and exactly one teal accent reserved for interaction, highlights and primary buttons. Motion stays gentle: room crossfades, softly popping device pins and calm scroll reveals, all still and complete under reduced-motion.

To adapt it, change the brand, the product, the audience and the name of the signature scene; the prompt asks the builder to rewrite every headline, device, automation and FAQ to fit, while the house, phones and layout stay put. Room contents are data-driven from one content file, so swapping what lives in each room takes minutes, not a redesign.

## Make it yours

- A sensor startup: set BRAND and PRODUCT to your sensor kit, rename SIGNATURE_SCENE to your flagship routine, and rewrite each room's device list to your sensors and the automation they trigger.
- A lighting maker: keep the cutaway house but swap the six lineup cards for your bulbs and strips, and set the room automations to dimming and wake-up scenes that fit your app.
- A home security brand: point SIGNATURE_SCENE at your away or night routine, replace the garage room contents with entry sensors, and rewrite the bundles to your starter kits.
- An indie hardware founder: set AUDIENCE to your backers, use the reserve-your-kit email band as a waitlist, and rewrite the three bundles to your pre-order tiers with sample prices.

## FAQ

### What is the interactive cutaway house on the Smart Home Product Landing Page and how does it work?
The Smart Home Product Landing Page hero centers on a hand-built SVG of a two-story home with an attached garage. Visitors click or tab a room — living, kitchen, bedroom or garage — and that room highlights in teal while a card beside it lists the devices inside and the one automation that room runs. Room contents come from one content file, so swapping what lives where is a data edit, not a redesign.

### How do I change the rooms, devices and automations in the cutaway house to match my own product?
The Smart Home Product Landing Page keeps every room, device and automation in a single typed content file, and the house component reads from that array rather than hard-coding copy. Edit the room entries to change which devices appear and which automation each room runs, then update the device lineup cards to match. The house geometry and layout stay put while the contents follow your product.
