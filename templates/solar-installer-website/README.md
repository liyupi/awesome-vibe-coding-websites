A single-page website for a residential solar installer: the promise over sunlit rooftop panels with the savings calculator above the fold — a monthly-bill slider and roof-size select computing system size, yearly savings and payback years as clearly-labeled sample math; five how-it-works steps from assessment to switch-on; three financing options compared plainly (loan, lease, cash); a six-roof project gallery with sizes and first-year output (sample); a free assessment form (address, bill band, roof age, contact) confirming client-side; a trust strip of warranties and certified installers; and an FAQ answering the real questions — winter, hail, moving home, and payback honesty. The default content follows an invented two-crew installer, but the structure suits any residential solar or energy company: change the company, city and rate assumptions and every number, step and gallery line still reads true. The look is sunlit modern: warm white and sky, deep navy ink, one solar-amber accent, Manrope display over Public Sans, big sky-and-panel photography. All savings figures carry a sample-data caption with stated assumptions. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

| `[CITY]` | City | Denver, Colorado |

| `[SEGMENT]` | Segment | homeowners |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add solar-installer-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A two-crew residential installer: set COMPANY and CITY, keep the bill slider, and replace the six gallery roofs with your own installs and first-year output.
- A commercial solar contractor: set SEGMENT to warehouses and small businesses, widen the roof select, and rewrite the financing cards for larger loan terms.
- A solar plus battery installer: keep the calculator, then swap the how-it-works steps to cover battery sizing and rewrite the FAQ to answer backup and outage questions.
- A regional energy cooperative: set RATE to your published rate, name the CITY service area, and use the assessment form to route members to a callback.

## FAQ

### How does the live savings calculator on the Solar Installer Website work?
The calculator takes a monthly bill slider and a roof-size select, then computes system size, yearly savings and payback years in the browser. The sample utility rate comes from the RATE variable and every figure carries a sample-data caption with stated assumptions. Nothing is submitted anywhere, so visitors can move the slider freely before booking the free assessment.

### How do I change the company name, city and utility rate in the Solar Installer Website template?
The Solar Installer Website template is driven by four variables: COMPANY, CITY, RATE and SEGMENT. Set COMPANY to your installer name, CITY to where your crew installs, RATE to your sample utility rate in dollars per kWh, and SEGMENT to who you serve. All copy and image paths live in one content file, so you can swap them without touching components.

### What sections and pages does the Solar Installer Website template include?
The Solar Installer Website template is a single page with a hero over rooftop photography, the savings calculator, five how-it-works steps, three financing options, a six-roof project gallery with sizes and first-year output, a free assessment form that confirms client-side, a trust strip of warranties and certified installers, and an FAQ covering winter, hail, moving home and payback honesty.

### Can I adapt the Solar Installer Website template for a commercial solar company?
Yes. The Solar Installer Website template suits any residential solar or energy company, and one of its live demos was built for a commercial installer. Set SEGMENT to small businesses and warehouses, rewrite the hero line, gallery captions and FAQ voice, and adjust the rate and roof assumptions so the calculator math still reads true for larger roofs.

### What should I change first in the Solar Installer Website template?
Start with the four variables: COMPANY, CITY, RATE and SEGMENT. Then rewrite the hero headline, the five how-it-works steps and the three financing options for your own process, and replace the six gallery projects with your real installs. Every savings figure needs your stated assumptions behind it, so keep the sample-data caption honest.
