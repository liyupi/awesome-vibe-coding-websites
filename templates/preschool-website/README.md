A single-page website for a preschool: the school's promise over a bright classroom photograph with a tour-booking CTA; three programs by age (toddlers, preschool, pre-K) with daily rhythms and ratios; a horizontal day-in-the-life timeline from arrival to pickup; three teacher profiles with AI-generated portraits (fictional people users swap for their own staff photos); a transparent tuition table by days per week; a parent FAQ answering the real questions (potty training, naps, food, separation, screen time); and a tour-booking form with day chips and a child-age select confirming client-side. The default content follows an invented neighborhood preschool, but the structure suits any early-childhood school. The look is playful calm: warm white and soft sky, a crayon-orange accent, rounded storybook headlines over a friendly sans, empty-classroom photography (never children's faces) and a drawn day-timeline. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[SCHOOL]` | School name | Bluebird Garden Preschool |
| `[CITY]` | City | Boulder, Colorado |
| `[FOCUS]` | Focus | play-based learning with real mud |
| `[RATIO_NOTE]` | Ratio note | 1:8 in every room, 1:6 for toddlers |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add preschool-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A bilingual preschool: set SCHOOL and CITY to your school, rewrite the three program cards in both languages, and use FOCUS to name the dual-language approach.
- A forest or nature kindergarten: change FOCUS to outdoor learning and rewrite the day timeline stops around woodland walks, shelter-building and muddy boots.
- A home-based childminding service: lower the program count to your real age groups, set RATIO_NOTE to your own ratios, and replace the teacher portraits with your own photos.
- A Montessori toddler room: rewrite the tuition table for half-day and full-day sessions, and adjust the parent FAQ to answer questions about independence and mixed-age rooms.

## FAQ

### How do I add a wine club signup to a winery website built with AI?
The Preschool Website template has no wine club section, so you would add one to the single content file and place it near the tour form. A winery version would swap the tour-booking form for a mailing-list signup with name and email fields, keep the client-side confirmation pattern, and link it from the hero CTA. The form stays in the browser and submits nothing unless you connect it to your own list.

### What sections does the Preschool Website template include?
The Preschool Website template is one page with five anchor sections: programs by age, a horizontal day-in-the-life timeline from arrival to pickup, three teacher profiles, a tuition table by days per week, and a tour-booking form. A hero with a classroom photograph and tour CTA sits above them, and a parent FAQ covers potty training, naps, food, separation and screen time.

### Which parts of the Preschool Website template do I change first for my own school?
Start with the four variables: SCHOOL, CITY, FOCUS and RATIO_NOTE. SCHOOL drives the wordmark, page title, footer and email address. FOCUS is the lowercase pedagogy phrase in the hero and program copy, and RATIO_NOTE is the staff-to-child line in the trust strip and program cards. Then replace the AI-generated teacher portraits with real staff photos and rewrite the tuition rows.

### How does the tour-booking form on the Preschool Website template work?
The tour form has day chips and a child-age select, and it confirms client-side only. On a valid submit it swaps to a booked state with a drawn sticker, a line naming the child, and an echo of the chosen day and age, plus a change-my-tour option that comes back pre-filled. Nothing is sent to a server, so you need to connect it to your own booking or email tool.

### Can I use the Preschool Website template for a bilingual or non-preschool early-years program?
Yes. The structure suits any early-childhood school, and one of the live demos is a bilingual preschool. Set FOCUS to your language or pedagogy phrase, rewrite the program cards for your age groups, and adjust RATIO_NOTE to your actual staff-to-child ratios. The day timeline, teacher profiles, tuition table and parent FAQ all carry over with new copy in the content file.

### How do I build a preschool website with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add preschool-website, replace [SCHOOL], [CITY], [FOCUS] and [RATIO_NOTE] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
