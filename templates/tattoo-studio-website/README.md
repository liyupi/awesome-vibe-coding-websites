# Tattoo Studio Website

> A dark editorial studio site with artist portfolios, SVG flash, consultations, and aftercare.

Tattoo Studio Website is a dark, editorial multi-page site for an independent tattoo practice that needs to show artistic range and make its booking process easy to understand. It suits resident-artist studios, small collectives, and solo tattooists who want more than a generic image gallery. The homepage pairs a cinematic studio photograph with large serif type, then moves through artist introductions, original flash, a four-step consultation process, practical visit information, and aftercare. Artist pages combine professional portraits with style tags, personal approaches, session minimums, and selected vector artwork. A filterable flash library uses original SVG drawings rather than copied tattoo imagery, with size, placement, pricing, and a direct path into the consultation form. The form collects the details a studio actually needs: artist preference, custom or flash work, placement, approximate size, color, budget, availability, and contact information. It validates each step, saves a draft locally, and clearly explains that the static demo does not transmit requests. Dedicated deposit, rescheduling, aftercare, and FAQ content answers common concerns without hiding important policies. The visual direction uses ink-black surfaces, warm bone typography, oxblood accents, thin rules, and restrained Motion transitions. Cormorant Garamond and Manrope are self-hosted through Fontsource. All business copy, artists, designs, and sample details live in one content file. Responsive layouts, accessible dialogs, reduced-motion support, and explicit file-based navigation keep the finished static site practical to deploy and maintain. Five variables change the brand, city, studio focus, audience, and booking deposit while preserving the layout, making the template straightforward to adapt to another tattoo practice or a closely related appointment-led creative studio.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Studio name | Duskhaven Tattoo |
| `[CITY]` | Studio city | Portland, Oregon |
| `[STUDIO_FOCUS]` | Studio specialties | ornamental blackwork, fine-line botanicals, and small illustrative pieces |
| `[AUDIENCE]` | Ideal clients | adults seeking thoughtful custom tattoos and collaborative artist-led design |
| `[BOOKING_DEPOSIT]` | Booking deposit | $150 |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add tattoo-studio-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A solo fine-line tattooist: set BRAND to your name, narrow STUDIO_FOCUS to one style, and cut the artist roster to a single profile page.
- A piercing and body-jewellery studio: keep the dark editorial layout, swap flash designs for jewellery pieces, and rewrite aftercare for piercing healing.
- A small tattoo collective: keep all three artist pages, give each artist distinct style tags, and point the consultation form at a shared booking inbox.
- A permanent-makeup artist: set AUDIENCE to clients seeking brow and lip work, replace flash with treatment examples, and adjust the deposit policy.

## FAQ

### How do I add a wine club signup to a winery website built with AI?
The Tattoo Studio Website template does not include a wine club signup, so a winery version would need a new section rather than a renamed one. The template's consultation form collects artist preference, custom or flash work, placement, size, color, budget and availability, and saves a draft locally without sending requests. For a winery, replace that form with a mailing-list field and keep the deposit and aftercare pages as policy content.

### What can I change with the five variables in the Tattoo Studio Website template?
The Tattoo Studio Website template exposes five variables: CITY, BRAND, AUDIENCE, STUDIO_FOCUS and BOOKING_DEPOSIT. Changing them rewrites the studio name, city and address, the client group the copy welcomes, the style tags and flash categories, and the deposit amount used in consultation, policy and FAQ copy. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as designed, so the site still reads as a dark editorial studio.

### How does the consultation form on the Tattoo Studio Website template handle requests?
The consultation form on the Tattoo Studio Website template validates each step, saves a draft in the browser and clearly states that the static demo does not transmit requests. It collects artist preference, custom or flash work, placement, approximate size, color, budget, availability and contact information. To receive real enquiries you connect the form to your own form service or email endpoint, since the template ships with no backend or external APIs.

### Where do I edit the artists, flash designs and prices in the Tattoo Studio Website template?
All business copy, artist records, flash designs, prices, policies and FAQ entries in the Tattoo Studio Website template live in one content file, so you edit text and sample data there rather than inside page components. Artist pages read an id from the URL and fall back to the first artist when it is missing or unknown. Flash artwork is original black-line SVG drawn in code, so new designs are added as SVG paths.

### What should I change first when adapting the Tattoo Studio Website template to my own studio?
Start with the five variables, then rewrite the artist bios, style tags, flash titles, prices, address details and calls to action to match. The Tattoo Studio Website template keeps the default studio name, city and deposit consistent across the header, footer, consultation confirmation, deposit and rescheduling pages and FAQ, so those strings need to agree after the change. Replace the six documentary photographs and the flash SVGs with your own work.
