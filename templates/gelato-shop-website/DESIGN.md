# Gelato Shop Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Cream** `#FFF6EA` — Page ground everywhere; footer ground.
- **Milk** `#FFFDF9` — Cup body in the SVG, panel fills, chip inner surfaces, input fields.
- **Pistachio** `#CBE3B6` — Primary pastel surface: hero photo frame, hero blob, active states' tints.
- **Pistachio deep** `#A9C98E` — Drips, borders and pressed states on pistachio surfaces.
- **Raspberry** `#F4AFC0` — Secondary pastel surface: shop panel two, tag pills, blob accents.
- **Raspberry deep** `#E790A4` — Drips and borders on raspberry surfaces; the single drip on the hero cup photo.
- **Butter** `#FFE1A0` — Third pastel: highlight chips, one blob per section, hero flavor-dot accent.
- **Cocoa** `#4A3528` — All text and outlines; primary buttons are cocoa grounds with cream text.
- **Latte** `#8A7565` — Secondary text: intros, captions, the honest allergen note.

## Typography
- **Display — headlines, flavor names, prices (chunky rounded)** — Baloo 2 (OFL), weights 600/700/800. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body, chips, tags, inputs (rounded sans)** — Nunito (OFL), weights 400/600/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Roomy but bouncy: sections py-24 (96px) desktop / py-16 (64px) mobile; content max-w-6xl px-6/px-10; headings get 8–16px of breathing room below; pastel panels use 32px inner padding so drips never crowd text.
- Radius: Rounded everywhere: 24px (rounded-3xl) on panels and photo frames, 16px (rounded-2xl) on chips, buttons and inputs, 999px pills on filter tags; the only straight lines in the design are the SVG drips hanging down.

## Motion
- Hover squish on every chip, button and flavor swatch: scale 1.04 with a spring (stiffness 400, damping 15), whileTap scale 0.96 — implemented once in src/lib/motion.ts.
- Scroll reveals: sections and grid items fade up (y 24→0, 400ms ease-out) with 60ms stagger via Motion's whileInView, firing once.
- Hero: the cup photograph bobs slowly (y ±6px, 6s ease-in-out loop) and the two background blobs drift on 20s loops; the flavor-dot row pops in sequentially on load (150ms apart).
- Flavor wall filter: non-matching swatches animate to opacity 0.25, grayscale 0.6 and scale 0.96 over 250ms; matching chips stay full color.
- Cup builder: each added scoop pops in (scale 0.6→1 spring); a removed scoop pops out (200ms); the price line slides up 80ms after any change; with two or more scoops a drip path grows below the rim (scaleY 0→1, 350ms).
- Drip dividers idle: one blob per divider slowly stretches (scaleY 1→1.15) on a 5s loop, staggered per section.
- prefers-reduced-motion: every loop (bob, blob drift, drip idle) stops, springs become 200ms opacity fades, squish becomes a color shift only.

## Imagery
One studio session throughout: gelato still-life photography on seamless pastel backdrops (pistachio green, raspberry pink, cream, butter yellow), soft diffused daylight from the front-left, one gentle shadow, candy-pastel palette, high-key minimal compositions with lots of empty backdrop. No text or labels in frame, no menu boards, no logos, no people or faces. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
Sunny, chatty, a little silly — short punchy lines and gentle puns; the playfulness comes from color and shape, never from exclamation marks.
