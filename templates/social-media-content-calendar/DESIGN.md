# Social Media Content Calendar — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Bubblegum** `#FF8FC7` — Full next-post band, first campaign and its labels. Ink on pink.
- **Sky** `#7CC8FF` — Second campaign card fills, selected media and chart accents. Ink on blue.
- **Butter** `#FFE17A` — Third campaign cards and active drop slots. Ink on yellow.
- **Ink** `#1A1A2E` — Headlines, icons, card titles, focus outlines and filled primary action.
- **Paper** `#FFFFFF` — Canvas, panels and empty calendar cells.
- **Rule** `#DADBE5` — Fine grid, rail divider and neutral borders.
- **Secondary ink** `#55566B` — Captions, weekday metadata and inactive navigation on paper.

## Typography
- **Page and feature titles** — Bricolage Grotesque (OFL), weights 600/700/800. Self-host the font files.
- **Content, controls and tabular figures** — DM Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: At 1440px use a 200px left rail, a 32px main inset, an 8px rhythm and a calendar spanning the remaining width. Page title 40px/1.05, feature title 28px, card titles 14px/1.25. Keep the entire first week and most of the next two visible within a 900px viewport. At 390px use 16px side insets and stack the agenda.
- Radius: Use a 14px outer radius on the pink feature, 8px on actions and calendar tickets, and 4px on small chips. Square grid corners inside a fine bordered frame retain the printed-planner feel.

## Motion
- A focused ticket gains a 2px ink outline. Dragging lifts it with a small shadow. Only the active destination receives an inset outline.
- Slide the editor in over 150ms and retain the page underneath. Announce completed moves in a polite live region. With reduced motion, all states change immediately.

## Imagery
Use the five supplied studio photographs as the complete stationery shoot. A pink tape dispenser, green pencil cup, yellow pad, cobalt trays and pink binder clips have distinct silhouettes even at small sizes. The next-post band gets a large physical object shot. Use a few inline crop strips among colored calendar tickets and larger square contact prints in the library. Original app chrome uses simple functional icons and a small folded-paper glyph.

## Tone
Use plain US English and short, specific titles. Write like the maker who knows why the tray lip is rounded and why the tape roll lifts out.
