# Racing Driver Personal Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Carbon Graphite** `#121417` — Primary dark editorial backdrop and studio floor
- **Electric Cobalt** `#2F6BFF` — Primary brand accent, active telemetry, and Season I livery
- **Safety Orange** `#FF6A1A` — High-speed accents, apex kerbs, and Season II livery
- **Track Chalk** `#F2F2EF` — Crisp editorial headings, light contrast panels, and chalk accents
- **Paddock Slate** `#1C2027` — Card surfaces, dashboard borders, and telemetry instruments

## Typography
- **Driver Wordmark, Race Numbers & Display Headlines** — Barlow Condensed (OFL), weights 600/700/800. Self-host the font files.
- **Editorial Copy & Store Details** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Lap Times, Speed, Gear & Points Telemetry** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Full-bleed 3D helmet studio hero followed by alternating dense telemetry dashboards and crisp editorial bands inside a 1280px max-width grid with zero overflow on 390px mobile.
- Radius: 14px technical beveled cards with 1px subtle chalk borders; crisp pill buttons for season liveries and section anchors.

## Motion
- Pointer-drag 360° inertial helmet rotation with smooth livery color & rim-light morphing synced to scroll progress and livery pills.
- Scroll-scrubbed + slider-scrubbed glowing car marker lapping the code-drawn circuit with live Speed, Gear, RPM, and G-force updates.
- Subtle magnetic tilt hover on behind-the-scenes paddock cards and store replicas.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion by disabling inertial spin and magnetic hover while keeping all controls fully interactive.

## Imagery
100% code-built Three.js 3D sculpted racing helmet with canvas-painted livery textures, interactive code-drawn globe calendar, vector circuit telemetry map, graphic paddock art cards, and SVG replica helmet store previews.

## Tone
Kinetic, precision-engineered, confident motorsport editorial.
