# Coffee Shop Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A three-page website for [BRAND], an independent coffee shop on [STREET] in [CITY], roasting beans from [BEAN_ORIGIN] since [FOUNDED]. It must feel like a real neighborhood cafe: full-bleed photography of the room, the counter and the drinks, three signature drinks with tasting notes, the roasting story with concrete numbers, a menu with category tabs, dietary filters and 8/12/16 oz pricing, opening hours with a live open-now indicator, transit notes, an SVG-drawn map of the block, and an order-ahead pickup builder that ends in a client-side confirmation ticket. Pages: index.html (home), menu.html (menu), visit.html (visit and order). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

- Audience: People who live or work within walking distance of [STREET] deciding where to get their morning coffee, remote workers looking for a seat and an outlet, and weekend visitors hunting for good pastries. Mostly 22–55, phone-first, impatient with fancy coffee jargon but appreciative of good beans.
- Goal: Get locals through the door and let regulars skip the line with order-ahead pickup. Within ten seconds a visitor should know what the shop smells like, what a small latte costs, whether it is open right now, and how to order ahead.
- Tone: Warm, plain and a little playful, like the person behind the counter who remembers your order. Short sentences, concrete numbers (sizes, prices, hours, grams), no coffee-snob vocabulary without a plain-word gloss, no exclamation marks.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Cafe name (default: "Marigold Coffee House"). Invented cafe name. Used in the wordmark, titles, footer, email (hello@ + brandSlug without hyphens + .com), localStorage keys and pickup order numbers (initials, e.g. MCH-1042). Never a real cafe or chain.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Portland, Oregon"). Where the shop is. Used in the hero eyebrow, getting-here copy and the footer. The street and every landmark stay invented-sounding; only the city may be real.
- [STREET] — Street (default: "Alder Row"). The invented street the shop sits on ('14 Alder Row'). Drives the address, the SVG block map (street names), the transit and parking copy and the house blend's name '[STREET] Blend'. Keep it two common English words, not a real address.
- [BEAN_ORIGIN] — Bean origin (default: "Huila, Colombia"). The current single origin the shop roasts. Drives the bean story, the menu's single-origin section, the retail bag and the FAQ. Real growing regions are fine; farms and importers stay invented.
- [FOUNDED] — Year opened (default: "2016"). Four-digit year. Drives the hero eyebrow, the wordmark line 'Est. [FOUNDED]' and the first line of the bean story; the roaster and the blend timeline shift to fit.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/coffee-shop-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

H1 lines rise from translateY(26px) and opacity 0 with 120ms stagger, starting 150ms after load.
- Motion: Signature drink cards: on hover the photo scales to 1.04 over 700ms --ease-out and the card border turns Espresso; the price ticks up 4px and settles. 'Order this' click: the button crossfades to a Check icon and 'Added' for 1400ms.
- Motion: Menu filtering: rows use Motion layout with AnimatePresence mode 'popLayout'; leaving rows fade and slide 8px left over 200ms, remaining rows glide to position over 380ms --ease-io. Tab switch slides a 2px Terracotta underline between tabs with Motion layoutId.
- Motion: Order builder: step changes crossfade with a 16px horizontal slide, 280ms --ease-io (AnimatePresence mode 'wait'). Selecting size, milk or extras fills the chip over 150ms; the running total counts to the new value over 400ms; the ticket replaces the form with a fade and a dashed border draw, and the order number types in character by character over 800ms.
- Motion: Hours: the open-now dot pulses (scale 1 to 1.25, 1600ms loop) only while open; 'Opens at 6:30am' lines crossfade when the day changes at midnight.
- Motion: Block map: on entering view the streets draw (stroke-dashoffset to 0) over 900ms, then the park block fills, then the shop pin drops from scale 0.6 with a soft overshoot and the transit, bike and parking markers pop in with 120ms stagger.
- Motion: Header: transparent over the photo hero with Foam text; after 40px of scroll it gets Cream at 94% opacity, backdrop-filter blur(10px), Espresso text and a 1px Rule bottom border, 250ms transition. On menu.html and visit.html it is solid from the start.
- Motion: FAQ accordions: height 0 to auto and opacity 0 to 1 over 300ms --ease-io; the Plus icon rotates 45 degrees into an X.
- Motion: Links: .link-draw grows a 1px currentColor underline from 0% to 100% width over 300ms --ease-out on hover and focus-visible.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — disable the hero scale and parallax, H1 rise, street draw, pin drop, count-ups, pulse and layout animations (render at rest; tabs and accordions switch instantly). Color and border hover changes stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Real photography only, warm and natural, all six made for this template in one consistent style: morning window light, oak and terracotta palette, documentary editorial look, fine grain. No people, no faces, no hands, no readable text, logos, cup branding or menu boards with writing. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 14px corners, no shadow; photos on Cream get a 1px Rule outline. Captions below photos in Nunito 13px Crema. Drawn in code: the favicon, the wordmark (type only), the illustrated block map, size dots, dietary badge icons, the ticket and dividers. Icons: lucide-react only, stroke width 1.5.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the pickup order, its confirmation and every choice live in the browser only (localStorage), and the order builder never charges anything.
- Every internal link points to a file name (./index.html, ./menu.html, ./visit.html, ./visit.html#order) — never to a bare directory.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- No real cafe or chain names, no real addresses, no real map tiles or embeds (the block map is drawn in SVG), no photos with faces, hands or readable logos, cups or signage.
- No dates or years anywhere except 'Est. [FOUNDED]' and the story copy; the site never shows today's date.
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere: disable parallax, count-ups, pin drops and layout animations; keep color/border hovers.
- The site must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; the order builder works with keyboard only and every interactive element has a visible focus ring.
- Keep copy concrete: no lorem, no filler, no superlatives without evidence; prices, hours and sizes always come from src/content.ts.
