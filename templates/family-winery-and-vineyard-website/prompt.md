# Family Winery and Vineyard Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A three-page website for [BRAND], a family-run winery in [REGION] owned by the [FAMILY] family since [FOUNDED], growing [VARIETIES] on its own hillside. It must look and work like a real estate winery: full-bleed real photographs of the vineyard, grapes, cellar and hillside, photos of the estate's own unlabeled bottles, a wine shop with a slide-over client-side cart and checkout, a tasting and tour booking widget, the estate's story as a timeline, a harvest calendar, wine club tiers, visit information with an SVG-drawn estate map, and an age check on first visit. Pages: index.html (home), wines.html (shop), visit.html (tastings and visit). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

- Audience: Wine drinkers within a day's drive of [REGION] planning a weekend visit, and past visitors and club members across the country reordering bottles. Mostly 30–65, curious about how the wine is made, comfortable buying online, and allergic to pretentious wine talk.
- Goal: Sell bottles, mixed cases and club memberships online, and fill tasting-room bookings. Within ten seconds a visitor should know where the estate is, what it grows, what a bottle costs and how to book a tasting; the cart and booking flows must feel finished and trustworthy.
- Tone: Warm, grounded and precise, like a winemaker talking at the barn door. Concrete numbers (acres, cases, months in barrel, prices, hours), plain words for tasting notes, a little dry humor. No wine-snob jargon without explanation, no hype words, no exclamation marks.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Winery name (default: "Wexbury Hollow"). Invented estate name. Used in the wordmark, titles, footer, email address (cellar@ + brandSlug without hyphens + .com), localStorage keys and order and booking references (initials, e.g. WH-4821). Never a real winery.
- [FAMILY] — Family name (default: "Marlowe"). The owning family's surname. Drives the timeline, the hero paragraph, the estate intro and the story. All first names are invented to match.
- [REGION] — Wine region (default: "Oregon's Willamette Valley"). Where the estate is. Used in the hero eyebrow, titles, directions, shipping copy and the harvest calendar timing. The road, the town address and the creek stay invented-sounding; only the region and state are real.
- [VARIETIES] — Grape varieties (default: "Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Gris"). The grapes the estate grows. Drives the eight wines, the vine blocks on the map, the mixed cases, the tasting experiences and the FAQ. Keep 3 varieties and 8 wines (3 red, 3 white, 1 rosé, 1 sparkling or the closest equivalent).
- [FOUNDED] — Year the family bought the land (default: "1952"). Four-digit year. Drives the hero eyebrow, the wordmark line 'Est. [FOUNDED]' and the first timeline entry; later timeline years are spaced to fit.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/family-winery-and-vineyard-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Don'ts
- No backend of any kind: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, payment providers, email services, analytics or third-party APIs. Checkout and booking forms call preventDefault and only change local state and localStorage; nothing is submitted, charged or sent.
- Do not load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any CDN. Self-host through the two Fontsource packages only.
- No CDN <script>, <link> or import maps. Every script, style, font, icon and image is in the project and bundled into dist/.
- No hotlinked images: every photo is downloaded into public/images/ once and served from there, and every third-party file is listed in ASSETS.md.
- No source maps in the production build (build.sourcemap false), and src/ is not copied into dist/.
- Relative asset paths (Vite base './'). Internal links are hash anchors or file names such as ./index.html, ./index.html#club, ./wines.html#cases and ./visit.html#book, never a bare directory or a leading slash.
- Do not change the stack (Vite 5 + React 18 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS 3.4 + Motion + lucide-react + Fontsource, three HTML entries) unless the user asks. No router library, no UI kit, no date-picker or map library, no 3D.
- No real winery, estate, vineyard or wine brand names, no appellation-protected label names, no real people. Every person, wine, block, road, creek and figure is invented; only the region and state may be real.
- No real bottle labels, logos, crests, cooperage stamps or signs in any image, and no AI-made label art. Bottles are shown only in the listed unlabeled photos. The estate has no logo beyond its typeset name and the favicon.
- No copy, tasting notes or slogans from any real winery's site; use only the copy given here for the default values.
- No people's faces in photos and no recognizable landmarks used as branding. Use only the listed images; no Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay or other stock sites with their own licenses.
- No real maps or map tiles (Google Maps, Mapbox, OpenStreetMap embeds or iframes): the estate map is drawn in SVG by hand.
- No commercial or custom fonts: only Cormorant Garamond and Hanken Grotesk (OFL).
- Only dependencies under MIT, ISC, BSD, Apache-2.0, 0BSD, CC0 or Unlicense (OFL for fonts). No Aceternity UI, Tailwind Plus / Tailwind UI or purchased template code; write every effect from scratch.
- One accent only: Oxblood (with Oxblood Light on dark backgrounds). Olive, Oak and Sage are for small marks, never for buttons. No gradients except the photo overlays given, no glows, no glassy cards, no drop shadows, no rounded pill cards.
- The age gate must appear on all three pages until confirmed and must not be skippable by scrolling; keep the 'enjoy responsibly' and 21+ notices in the footer and the 21+ checkbox in checkout.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion exactly as described under Design motion. Every interactive element is keyboard reachable with a visible 2px Oxblood focus ring; dialogs trap focus; accordions use aria-expanded; the calendar and month strip support arrow keys; form errors use role alert.
- Must render at 1440×900 and 390×844 with zero console errors and no horizontal page scroll (only the mobile filter chips scroll inside themselves). localStorage access is wrapped in try/catch so private mode never throws.
- Keep dist/ under 20 MB (target under 12 MB).
- No lorem ipsum, filler or hype words. No meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD or sitemap: only the <title> and favicon.
