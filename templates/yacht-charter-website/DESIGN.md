# Yacht Charter Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Navy** `#12263A` — Page ground on both pages — deep nautical navy.
- **Navy raised** `#1B3350` — Hover tints on navy, filter-chip grounds, footer band.
- **White** `#FCFBF7` — Panels, fleet cards, spec tables, deck plans and route map — the chart paper.
- **Deck line** `#D8DCE0` — Hairlines on white: table rules, card borders, SVG deck strokes.
- **Deck gray** `#8C959D` — Mono labels on white and secondary text on navy; the SVG landmass and deck fills.
- **Brass** `#B98F4E` — The accent: route lines and stop numbers, prices, CTAs, leader lines, active chips.
- **Brass light** `#D3AC6C` — Hover and emphasis on brass; hero label on photography.
- **Ink** `#1B2B3A` — Primary text on white — navy-black ink for headlines and body.
- **Haze** `#9FB4C2` — Secondary text on navy: captions, sample notes, meta lines.

## Typography
- **Display and body — headlines, card names, copy (crisp sans; labels uppercase tracked 0.14em)** — Archivo (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Mono — every measurable thing: spec lines, filters, prices, stop numbers, notes (deck-gray labels)** — Roboto Mono (Apache-2.0), weights 400/500/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Chart-table orderly: sections py-28 (112px) desktop / py-20 mobile; content max-w-6xl px-6/px-10; white panels carry 28px padding with 1px deckline borders; spec rows sit on hairlines with 14px mono padding; the route map panel gets full content width.
- Radius: Ship-shape restraint: 6px on panels, cards and photos; 999px pills on the two CTAs and filter chips; the SVG hull and deck regions keep 4px corners.

## Motion
- Fleet re-sort: AnimatePresence with layout animations — cards glide to their new positions (350ms) while filtered-out cards scale to 96% and fade (250ms); counts in the mono caption update instantly.
- Route map: switching itineraries crossfades the dotted brass route (300ms) and the numbered stop circles pop in staggered 60ms; the coastline stays put.
- Hero: the photograph parallaxes at 0.06 of scroll (the page's only parallax) and settles 1.03→1 over 2.5s; the mono label leads the headline by 120ms.
- Deck plan: cabin rects fade in staggered 80ms on first view; leader lines draw 400ms.
- Reveals: sections and cards fade up (y 20→0, 700ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)) with 100ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once.
- prefers-reduced-motion: layout re-sorting and crossfades become instant, parallax and settles off, all reveals plain 200ms opacity fades.

## Imagery
One sea, one light: bright Mediterranean yacht photography in brilliant midday clarity — white hulls against deep azure water, salt haze on the horizon, crisp nautical contrast, uncluttered compositions. Yachts at anchor and under way, teak decks with brass fittings, and an unnamed coastline from the air; crew appear only as distant figures with no identifiable faces. No readable text anywhere in frame, no logos, no flags or ensigns of any kind. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
Chart-table precise and salt-cured: short declarative lines, mono numbers, no adjectives doing the work of a photograph.
