# Isometric 3D Room Portfolio

> A developer portfolio set in a cozy 3D room where each object opens a section and a lamp turns on night.

A single-page developer portfolio staged as a cozy isometric 3D room floating on a soft pastel gradient. The room is the whole first screen, and every object in it is a way in: the bookshelf opens About, the desk monitor opens Projects, the guitar in the corner opens Music and the little wall mailbox opens Contact. Hover an object and it lifts, brightens and shows a small floating label; click it and the camera glides in on it while a side panel slides open. Close the panel and the camera eases back home. A desk lamp doubles as a light switch: click it, or the sun and moon toggle in the corner, and the room crossfades to a deep blue night where the lamp throws warm light, the monitor glows and a strand of fairy lights twinkles along the wall.

It suits front-end developers, creative technologists, game makers and designers who code, anyone who wants a portfolio that people remember and share rather than scroll past. Everything in the scene is modeled from code primitives with flat, toy-like materials, so there are no model files to license or replace. The panels hold real content: a portrait and short bio with stack and "now" lists, four project cards with code-drawn thumbnails that expand into details, a playlist with a working silent player for your side music project, and contact rows that copy to the clipboard.

A plain HTML navigation mirrors the four objects for keyboard and screen-reader users, Escape always returns to the room, and reduced-motion mode swaps every camera move for an instant cut. On phones the room is re-framed to fit the width and the panels become bottom sheets. To make it yours, change your name, role, city, focus and music alias; the bio, projects and tracks are rewritten to match while the room, palette and interactions stay the same.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Your name | Iris Calloway |
| `[ROLE]` | Role | Creative front-end developer |
| `[LOCATION]` | City | Portland, Oregon |
| `[FOCUS]` | What you build | interactive data stories and small web toys |
| `[MUSIC_ALIAS]` | Music project name | Low Tide Radio |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add isometric-3d-room-portfolio` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- An indie game developer: set ROLE to game developer, swap the four project cards for your released titles and link the desk monitor to your itch page.
- A creative technologist at a studio: set BRAND and FOCUS to your practice, rewrite the About bio around client work and point Contact at your studio email.
- A designer who codes: keep the room and palette, replace the project thumbnails with your interface work and use the guitar panel for your type or sound experiments.
- A freelance front-end developer: set LOCATION to your city, rewrite the four projects as client case studies and make the mailbox email the one you want enquiries sent to.

## FAQ

### What do I change first to make the Isometric 3D Room Portfolio my own?
Start with the five variables: set BRAND to your name, ROLE to your job title, LOCATION to your city, FOCUS to the phrase that completes "I build ...", and MUSIC_ALIAS to your side music project. The name chip, title, intro line, bio, contact rows and storage keys all follow from these. Then rewrite the project cards and tracks in the content file to match.

### Do I need 3D model files or licensed assets to use the Isometric 3D Room Portfolio?
No. Every object in the Isometric 3D Room Portfolio scene, from the bookshelf and desk monitor to the guitar and wall mailbox, is modeled from code primitives with flat toy-like materials. There are no model files to buy, license or replace. The template does download two images into the public images folder before the first build, and the build itself never fetches anything.

### How does the day and night switch work in the Isometric 3D Room Portfolio?
The desk lamp doubles as a light switch. Click the lamp, or the sun and moon toggle in the corner, and the Isometric 3D Room Portfolio crossfades to a deep blue night: the lamp throws warm light, the monitor glows and a strand of fairy lights twinkles along the wall. Colors and light intensities are defined as day and night pairs, so you can retune the palette.

### Is the Isometric 3D Room Portfolio accessible and keyboard friendly?
Yes. A plain HTML navigation mirrors the four room objects, so keyboard and screen-reader users can reach About, Projects, Music and Contact without the 3D scene. Escape always returns to the room, and reduced-motion mode swaps every camera move for an instant cut. On touch devices hover effects are skipped and a tap focuses an object directly.

### Can I turn the Music panel into something other than a music project?
The Music panel in the Isometric 3D Room Portfolio holds a playlist with a silent player and track list, opened by the guitar. If you have no music hobby, keep it as a "what I listen to while coding" playlist of invented tracks. You can also rewrite the tracks and MUSIC_ALIAS for another side project and keep the same player layout.

### How do I build an isometric 3D room portfolio with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add isometric-3d-room-portfolio, replace [BRAND], [ROLE], [LOCATION] and [FOCUS] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.

Isometric 3D Room Portfolio is a single-page site built with React, Three.js and Tailwind CSS, with 3d / webgl and micro-interactions. The prompt fixes the stack so every AI tool builds the same kind of project, and the result runs as a static site you can host anywhere.

Yes. Every Isometric 3D Room Portfolio build was tested on a phone-sized screen as well as desktop before it went live, checking layout, text size and touch targets. Open the live demo on your phone, or switch the preview on this page to mobile view, to check it yourself.
