Designed for VP of Product leaders, staff PMs, and founders presenting multi-quarter software strategy to executives, engineering, and design stakeholders, this 12-slide dark-mode browser deck replaces static roadmap slides with live interactive planning views. Built on a deep graphite stage accented by crisp white typography, electric blue functional highlights, and sharp lime velocity signals, the presentation flows cleanly from product vision and strategic bets into concrete execution detail. Early slides synthesize customer discovery insights through a code-drawn SVG feedback theme bar chart before opening an interactive Now, Next, and Later roadmap board that filters live across Engineering, Design, and Growth tracks. Three dedicated deep-dive slides showcase upcoming flagship capabilities using crisp, interactive UI component mockups built directly in code rather than blurry flat screenshots. Later slides map cross-team release dependencies on an SVG timeline, display beta cohort adoption and retention telemetry, and finish with an interactive stakeholder feedback prompt slide where attendees can tag priorities or copy a session summary. Complete presentation controls come standard: arrow-key, spacebar, swipe, and click navigation, a bottom progress bar, a G-key thumbnail overview grid, an N-key presenter notes drawer with a live talk timer, F-key fullscreen mode, and a print-ready landscape PDF stylesheet. Edit the product name, company, planning horizon, and roadmap tracks in one TypeScript content file to tailor the deck for your next product review.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |

| `[PRODUCT_AREA]` | Core platform focus | Multiplayer Planning & Docs |
| `[HORIZON_LABEL]` | Planning horizon badge | 3-Quarter Product Roadmap |
| `[BETA_COHORT]` | Design partner cohort size | 185 Customer Teams |
| `[PRODUCT_LEAD]` | Presenter name and title | Priya Nair, VP Product |

## Make it yours

- A VP of Product at a developer tools company: set BRAND and PRODUCT_AREA to your platform, then replace the three feature mockups with your next API and dashboard releases.
- A startup founder preparing a board review: set HORIZON_LABEL to your planning window, trim the roadmap board to two tracks and rewrite the strategic bets slide around funding milestones.
- A staff PM running a design partner session: set BETA_COHORT to your partner count, swap the retention telemetry for your own cohort numbers and use the feedback prompt slide to collect priorities.
- A design lead presenting cross-team dependencies: keep the SVG timeline, replace the Engineering, Design and Growth tracks with your teams and add speaker notes for each release handoff.

## FAQ

### What slides are included in the Product Roadmap Presentation Deck?
The Product Roadmap Presentation Deck has 12 slides. Early slides cover product vision and strategic bets, plus a customer discovery feedback theme bar chart drawn in SVG. A Now, Next and Later roadmap board filters across Engineering, Design and Growth tracks. Three deep-dive slides show code-built UI mockups, then an SVG dependency timeline, beta cohort adoption and retention telemetry, and a stakeholder feedback prompt slide close the deck.

### How do I change the product name, planning horizon and roadmap tracks in the deck?
The Product Roadmap Presentation Deck keeps its content in one TypeScript content file. Set BRAND to your product name, PRODUCT_AREA to your platform focus, HORIZON_LABEL to your planning horizon, BETA_COHORT to your design partner cohort size and PRODUCT_LEAD to the presenter's name and title. Roadmap items, feedback data, dependencies, beta metrics and speaker notes live in the same file, so swap them there and every slide updates.

### Can I present the Product Roadmap Presentation Deck live and print it as a PDF?
The Product Roadmap Presentation Deck supports arrow-key, spacebar, swipe and click navigation, a bottom progress bar, a G-key thumbnail overview grid, an N-key presenter notes drawer with a talk timer, F-key fullscreen and Escape to exit. A print stylesheet lays out all 12 slides one per landscape page, so you can export a PDF from the browser for handouts or async review.

### Can I use the Product Roadmap Presentation Deck for a client product review?
Commercial and client use of the Product Roadmap Presentation Deck needs Pro. Pro templates show the brief, stack and variables publicly and the full prompt to Pro members, and source code of the demos comes with Pro. Free templates' prompts and every DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0.

### How do I add my own roadmap items and beta metrics to the deck?
The Product Roadmap Presentation Deck reads roadmap items, customer feedback data, dependencies, beta metrics and speaker notes from a single TypeScript content file. Add or edit entries there, keep the Now, Next and Later horizons and the Engineering, Design and Growth tracks consistent, and the filterable board, dependency timeline and telemetry slides pick them up. Rewrite headlines and labels for your values in the same pass.

### How do I build a product roadmap presentation deck with AI?
Copy the full prompt (Pro) from this page or run npx vibefather add product-roadmap-presentation-deck, replace [BRAND], [PRODUCT_AREA], [HORIZON_LABEL] and [BETA_COHORT] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
