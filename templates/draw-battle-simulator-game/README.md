A playful cartoon battle sandbox set on a bright meadow. Pick from 10 original units (Pikemen, Archers, Catapults, Shield Walls, Goat Cavalry, Orb Wizards, Splitting Slimes, Giant Snowmen, Horn Drummers, and Balloon Bombers), drag lines across the left or right half to deploy formations, paint slowing rivers, hills, and forests, and press Fight to watch hundreds of toy soldiers clash smoothly and pop into festive confetti. Includes slow motion, follow-action camera, instant replay, URL battle sharing, and 12 coin-budget Challenge Puzzles.

## FAQ

### How do I draw armies and place soldiers in the Draw Battle Simulator Game template?
You drag a line across the left half of the meadow to deploy [RED_TEAM] units and across the right half for [BLUE_TEAM] units, with a live sketch trail previewing exactly where soldiers will land. Pick any of the 10 units from the bottom toybox dock first, then stroke to spawn a row. Selecting a terrain brush switches the same drag gesture to painting River, Forest, Hill or Grass Eraser onto [MAP_NAME].

### What are the 12 coin-budget Challenge Puzzles in this battle simulator?
The Draw Battle Simulator Game template includes 12 handcrafted tactical puzzles, each with a fixed [BLUE_TEAM] defense and a strict coin budget on the [RED_TEAM] side, such as Shield Breaker at 32 coins, Goat Charge at 40 coins, River Crossing at 48 coins and Snowman Siege at 70 coins. You open them from the Puzzles drawer, solve each one, and completion stars are saved in your browser.

### How do slow motion, follow camera and replay work in the Draw Battle Simulator Game?
The top command bar of the Draw Battle Simulator Game template has a Slow-Mo toggle that drops the battle to 0.35x speed, a Follow Cam toggle that smoothly zooms and pans toward the densest front-line clash, and a Replay button that reruns your drawn formations. Fight and Pause control the live simulation, and Clear wipes the field so you can redraw both armies.

### Can I share a battle I set up in the Draw Battle Simulator Game with a friend?
Yes. The Share URL button in the Draw Battle Simulator Game template serializes your drawn units and painted terrain into a compact hash parameter, so anyone opening that link sees the exact same battle recreated on [MAP_NAME]. The template has no backend, so the whole setup travels inside the URL itself and needs no account or server to load.
