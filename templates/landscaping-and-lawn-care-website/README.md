Landscaping and Lawn Care Website is a single-page site for a design-and-maintain landscaping company, in deep fern green, fresh lime and warm terracotta over soft stone — with garden photography doing the selling. The signature tool is a lawn care plan builder: pick lot size, visit frequency and add-ons like aeration, leaf removal or bed weeding, and it prices a real monthly program with every line item shown. Around it: services from design to irrigation, a project gallery filterable by job type, a five-step process timeline from soil test to year-round care, and a twelve-month seasonal calendar that tells customers what the crew actually does in February. A clean-cut guarantee, three specific reviews and a site-visit request form close the page. It suits lawn care companies, garden designers and property-care services of any size, and adapts to pool care or snow removal by re-pointing the builder's price table. Five variables swap brand, city, founder, phone and years; all prices, clients and towns are invented sample data in one content file.

## Make it yours

- A pool care company: keep the plan builder but set lot tiers to pool sizes, swap add-ons for chemical balancing and opening or closing visits, and reword the seasonal calendar for swim season.
- A snow removal service: re-point the builder to driveway length and visit frequency, replace summer add-ons with salting and roof clearing, and rewrite the calendar around winter storms.
- A garden designer working alone: set BRAND and FOUNDER to your studio, use EXPERIENCE in the hero, and replace the lawn program add-ons with planting design and bed installation lines.
- A property management firm: keep the gallery and guarantee, set CITY to your service area, and use the site-visit form as the intake for multi-property maintenance contracts.

## FAQ

### How does the lawn care plan builder on the Landscaping and Lawn Care Website work out a monthly price?
The lawn care plan builder on the Landscaping and Lawn Care Website prices a monthly program from three inputs: lot size, visit frequency and add-ons. Lot tiers set a base visit price, frequency sets visits per month, and the monthly base is rounded to the nearest $5. Add-ons like bed weeding, aeration, fall leaf removal and holiday lighting add fixed monthly amounts. Every line item is shown, with a season note and a quoted yearly, billed monthly disclaimer.

### What can I change on the Landscaping and Lawn Care Website without editing the layout?
The Landscaping and Lawn Care Website runs on five variables: CITY, BRAND, PHONE, FOUNDER and EXPERIENCE. All copy, prices, gallery items, calendar entries, reviews and form options sit in one content file, so you can swap the brand name, home city, founder, phone line and years of experience and rewrite dependent copy there. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as designed.

### Can the Landscaping and Lawn Care Website plan builder be repointed to pool care or snow removal?
Yes. The plan builder on the Landscaping and Lawn Care Website reads its price table from the content file: lot size tiers with base visit prices, visits per month per frequency, add-on monthly prices and season notes. A pool care or snow removal company can replace those tiers, frequencies and add-ons with its own service lines, and the builder will price and display the new program the same way.

### What sections come with the Landscaping and Lawn Care Website template?
The Landscaping and Lawn Care Website is a single page with a header and garden hero, a services section covering design through irrigation, the lawn care plan builder, a project gallery filterable by job type, a five-step process timeline from soil test to year-round care, a twelve-month seasonal calendar, a clean-cut guarantee, three reviews and a site-visit request form. All prices, projects and clients are invented sample data.

### How do I turn the gallery and seasonal calendar on the Landscaping and Lawn Care Website into my own project photos and schedule?
The gallery on the Landscaping and Lawn Care Website is a data-driven grid with category chips for All, Design & build, Lawn programs, Hardscape & paths, Lighting and Irrigation, so you replace the gallery items in the content file with your own photos, categories and locations. The seasonal calendar is a twelve-cell grid where each cell holds a month name, one crew task and one garden note, so you edit those entries to match what your crew actually does each month.
