Self Storage Facility Website is a single-page site for a storage facility, in bold royal blue and sunflower on white — warehouse-clean instead of chain-link beige. The signature tool is the size guide: pick what you're storing (studio, 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom home, garage or business stock) and an SVG unit draws itself to scale with furniture and box outlines inside, the recommended size badge and a plain fit verdict. Around it: a unit grid filterable by size, climate control, drive-up access and price with live counts; a three-step reserve-a-unit flow with relative move-in timing and no payment, ending in a held-reservation code; concrete security and access facts; a supplies-shop teaser with a reserved-guest discount; a drawn location map with directions; and reviews. It suits storage facilities of any size, and adapts to RV/boat storage or mailbox shops by re-pointing the unit table. Five variables swap facility name, town, manager, phone and years; every unit, price and review is invented sample data.

## Make it yours

- An RV and boat storage yard: set BRAND and CITY, then replace the nine units with covered and open bays, keeping the size guide for length-based fits.
- A mailbox and package shop: re-point the unit table to mailbox sizes and rental terms, and swap the supplies teaser for packing and shipping supplies.
- A small-business stock room renter: set FOUNDER as the manager, use the garage and business stock preset, and show pallet and shelving units in the grid.
- A family-run facility in a new town: set CITY, PHONE and EXPERIENCE, rewrite the directions to your own landmarks, and replace the sample reviews with real ones.

## FAQ

### How does the size guide on the Self Storage Facility Website work?
The size guide on the Self Storage Facility Website asks what you are storing: a studio, a one-bedroom home, a two-bedroom home, a garage or business stock. An SVG unit then draws itself to scale with furniture and box outlines inside, shows the recommended size badge and a plain fit verdict such as room to spare. A link filters the unit grid to that size.

### Can visitors reserve a storage unit without paying on this template?
Yes. The Self Storage Facility Website runs a three-step reservation flow: the chosen unit and monthly total, move-in timing chips from as soon as possible to just pricing for now, then name, phone and email with inline validation. Success shows a held-reservation code and states the unit is held for 48 hours with no payment taken in the demo.

### What can I change with the five variables in the Self Storage Facility Website template?
The five variables are CITY, BRAND, PHONE, FOUNDER and EXPERIENCE. They swap the facility name, town, manager, phone number and years open across the hero, map labels, directions, reservation copy and reviews. Every unit, price and review is invented sample data, so rewrite those in the content file to match your real facility.

### Can the Self Storage Facility Website template be adapted for RV and boat storage or a mailbox shop?
Yes. The Self Storage Facility Website template is built so the unit table can be re-pointed for RV and boat storage or a mailbox shop. That means replacing the nine sample units, their dimensions, monthly prices and climate or drive-up tags with your own inventory, and adjusting the size-guide presets so the drawn outlines match what you actually rent.
