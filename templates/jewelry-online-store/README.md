> An editorial jewelry shop with filters, personalization, gift wrapping and a complete client-side checkout.

Jewelry Online Store is a four-page storefront for an independent jeweler selling a small, carefully edited collection. The home page combines a photographic campaign hero with featured pieces, materials and making notes, gift services, and a local newsletter interaction. A collection page lets shoppers combine metal and stone filters, sort by price or name, see live result counts, and recover from an empty filter combination. Each product page includes a responsive gallery, variant and size controls, an accessible ring-size guide, optional engraving with a live preview, gift wrapping, a gift message, care details, and related pieces. The shared cart drawer persists selections in localStorage and calculates personalization, gift wrap, quantities, shipping, and the order total. Checkout moves through details, delivery, and review before producing an explicitly labeled mock confirmation; it never collects card details, sends an order, or takes payment. The visual direction is warm editorial luxury: ivory surfaces, oxblood accents, fine rules, Cormorant Garamond display type, Manrope interface text, nearly square corners, and restrained reveals. Six coordinated studio photographs give the collection a consistent art-directed look without relying on stock imagery. The default content is an invented gold-and-silver jewelry brand, while the alternate values shift the name, city, collection story, materials, and shipping threshold. To adapt the template, change those variables and rewrite the centralized content file; products, filters, policies, cart keys, and page copy all follow the same data. It suits independent jewelers, small studios, and designers who need a polished shopping journey that remains entirely static without a backend.

- A one-person goldsmith studio: set BRAND and CITY to your workshop, replace the collection with your current pieces, and rewrite the material story around your own metals and stones.
- A keepsake and memorial jeweler: keep the engraving field and gift message, and rewrite the collection copy around personalization rather than a seasonal collection.
- A wedding band designer: lean on the ring-size guide and variant controls, and set SHIP_FREE to a threshold that suits higher-value orders.
- A gift-focused retailer: expand the gift wrapping and gift message options, and point the newsletter interaction at your own mailing list.

## FAQ

### How does checkout work on the Jewelry Online Store template if there is no backend?
Checkout on the Jewelry Online Store template runs entirely in the browser through three steps: details, delivery, and review, ending in a confirmation that is explicitly labeled as a mock. It never collects card details, sends an order, or takes payment. To sell for real, you connect the confirmed order to your own payment processor or send it to your fulfillment process.

### Can shoppers add engraving and gift wrapping to a piece in the Jewelry Online Store template?
Yes. Each product page in the Jewelry Online Store template includes an optional engraving field with a live preview, plus gift wrapping and a gift message. The cart drawer recalculates as these are added, so personalization and gift wrap carry through quantities, shipping, and the order total. The price logic is centralized, so you change the charges in one place.

### How do the metal and stone filters and sorting work on the collection page?
The collection page of the Jewelry Online Store template combines metal and stone filters, shows a live result count, and sorts by price or name. Empty filter combinations return a recovery state rather than a dead end. Because products, variants, and filters all read from the same centralized content, adding a piece or a new metal updates the filter options without touching the layout.

### How do I make the Jewelry Online Store template my own brand?
Set the five variables: BRAND for the wordmark, titles, footer and storage keys, CITY for the studio story and shipping copy, COLLECTION for the hero and featured pieces, MATERIAL for the material and care copy, and SHIP_FREE for the free shipping threshold. Then rewrite the centralized content file so products, prices, policies and page copy follow your collection.

### What should I change first when adapting the Jewelry Online Store template for a real jeweler?
Start with the variables and the product data in the centralized content file, since the collection grid, filters, product pages, ring sizing, cart and checkout all read from it. Replace the six studio photographs with your own pieces, then adjust the engraving, gift wrap and shipping charges so the cart totals match how you actually sell.
