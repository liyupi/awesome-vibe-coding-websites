Interactive Product Tour Page is a single-page SaaS site built around a working, code-drawn product demo rather than a passive screenshot. A split hero introduces the product and places a complete guided workspace beside it. Three chapter tabs lead visitors through nine focused steps, with anchored hotspots, masked spotlights, concise tooltips, progress controls, optional autoplay, and a conversion prompt after every chapter. The interaction works with mouse, touch, and keyboard, and the mobile version moves guidance below the interface so nothing becomes a tiny desktop mockup.

The visual system pairs warm paper, editorial serif headlines, and coral guidance markers with a deep ink application canvas. It suits SaaS founders, product marketers, and indie teams that need to explain a workflow quickly and give prospects a useful next step. Below the tour, chapter cards, a decision-trail feature section, practical FAQs, and a final call to action make the page feel complete without diluting the demo. Chapter completion states create natural pauses for a contextual next step, while manual navigation lets returning visitors skip directly to the workflow they care about most.

The default content follows Nookline, a client approval workspace for creative studios. Swap four variables to reposition it for another product and audience; all interface labels, steps, sample projects, and calls to action are centralized for rewriting. An alternate property-care case demonstrates the same structure in a different industry. Everything is drawn with React, CSS, SVG, and Lucide icons, with no photographs, remote APIs, or backend. Motion is restrained and reduced-motion aware, while the static build keeps fonts and every other asset local.

- An indie analytics product: keep the chapter tabs but swap the sample projects for your dashboards, and rewrite tooltips to explain each chart and filter.
