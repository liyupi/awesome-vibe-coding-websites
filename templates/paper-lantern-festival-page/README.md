# Paper Lantern Festival Page

> Write a private wish, fold a glowing paper lantern, and release it above an illustrated midnight river.

Paper Lantern Festival Page is a single-page interactive ritual built around an illustrated river at night. A visitor writes a wish, watches a sheet of paper fold into a glowing lantern, and releases it into a sky of gentle ambient lights. The personal gallery keeps each released wish on the same device, with controls to launch it again or remove it.

The scene is drawn entirely with Canvas 2D, SVG, and CSS. Deep indigo hills, slow mist, warm paper panels, river reflections, and a seeded field of lanterns create depth without image downloads or a 3D engine. A compact wind control changes horizontal drift; pointer parallax moves the landscape by only a few pixels. The three-step composer remains an ordinary DOM interface beside the scene on desktop and below it on mobile, so the central ritual is never pointer-only.

This template suits creators, designers, educators, community projects, and anyone building a reflective digital event or small interactive gift. Variables cover the project name, festival title, welcome headline, fictional river, audience, and wish themes. The alternate case turns the same interaction into a harbor gathering about friendship, courage, and homecomings. All supporting copy and ambient wishes are specified as editable content rather than scattered through components.

The build uses Vite, React, TypeScript, vanilla CSS, and locally bundled fonts and icons. Wish storage is validated, versioned localStorage with a clear local-only disclosure; there is no public feed or pretense of live visitors. Fixed lantern counts, capped pixel density, keyboard-operable controls, and a complete reduced-motion mode keep the static site practical. No generated images are required, and nothing needs a backend, account, external API, analytics service, or remote asset host.

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add paper-lantern-festival-page` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A school or library workshop: set BRAND to your program, make AUDIENCE students and families, and choose WISH_THEMES like hopes for the year so the composer feels easy for first-time visitors.
- A memorial or remembrance gathering: use the harbor alternate case, set FESTIVAL_NAME to the occasion, and pick WISH_THEMES around memory and homecoming while keeping the local-only wish disclosure visible.
- A designer's seasonal greeting: set HEADLINE as a short personal message, name a fictional RIVER_NAME, and tune WISH_THEMES so released lanterns read as a small interactive gift rather than an event.
- A neighborhood association's New Year page: set BRAND and AUDIENCE to residents, choose WISH_THEMES about the coming year, and use the wind control labels Still, Gentle and Brisk to set the mood.

## FAQ

### What happens to a wish after I release a lantern on the Paper Lantern Festival Page?
A released wish is saved in the personal gallery on the same device, stored as validated, versioned localStorage. The template keeps up to 24 released wishes locally, with Release again and Remove actions for each one. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no public feed, and the page states plainly that wishes stay in the current browser.

### How do I change the festival name, river and welcome headline in this template?
The Paper Lantern Festival Page template exposes BRAND, FESTIVAL_NAME, RIVER_NAME, HEADLINE, AUDIENCE and WISH_THEMES as variables. You set them once, and every dependent wish, label, storage namespace and instruction is rewritten to match. Swapping the audience also reshapes the welcome copy, wish prompts, helper text and the ambient wishes drawn from your chosen themes.

### Can visitors use the wish composer with a keyboard or screen reader instead of the canvas?
Yes. The three-step composer stays an ordinary labeled interface beside the illustrated river on desktop and below it on mobile, so write, fold and release all work through standard fields and buttons. Canvas motion and pointer parallax are decorative only, and a complete reduced-motion mode shows the finished lantern immediately instead of animating the fold.

### What does the Paper Lantern Festival Page look like without any images or 3D library?
The Paper Lantern Festival Page draws its whole scene with Canvas 2D, SVG and CSS: deep indigo hills, two mist bands, a river with current lines and reflection streaks, a seeded field of lanterns, 96 faint stars and a plain moon. Lanterns are original six-panel paper shapes in cream and amber with soft halos, so the page needs no image downloads and no remote asset host.

### How do I adapt the Paper Lantern Festival Page for a community project or memorial gathering?
Start from the alternate case in the template, which turns the same ritual into a harbor gathering about friendship, courage and homecomings. Set BRAND and FESTIVAL_NAME to your project, give RIVER_NAME a fitting fictional waterway, point HEADLINE and AUDIENCE at your participants, and choose WISH_THEMES that match the tone you want visitors to write in.

### How do I build a paper lantern festival page with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add paper-lantern-festival-page, replace [BRAND], [FESTIVAL_NAME], [HEADLINE] and [RIVER_NAME] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
