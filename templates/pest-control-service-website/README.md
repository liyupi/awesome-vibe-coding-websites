Pest Control Service Website is a single-page site for a local pest control company, in fresh green, deep ink and amber with rounded friendly type — designed to solve problems at 8 a.m., not sell fear at 2 a.m. The signature tool is the pest identifier quiz: three plain questions (what you saw, where, when) branch through a visible logic table to the likely pest, a confidence note and a recommended plan tier, with code-drawn pest icons instead of gross photos. Around it: services from ants to exclusion, a plan comparison with what re-treatment coverage actually means, an honest pet-and-kid safety section (drying times, tamper-resistant bait stations, product classes explained), a seasonal pest calendar, a concrete re-treat-free guarantee, and a free inspection booking that can attach the quiz result. It suits pest control companies, lawn-care-plus-pest hybrids and wildlife removal services. Five variables swap company name, town, entomologist-founder, phone and years; every price, plan and review is invented sample data.

## FAQ

### How does the pest identifier quiz decide which pest I have?
The Pest Control Service Website quiz asks three plain questions: what you saw, where you saw it, and when. Each answer carries a weight map to seven outcomes — ant, bed bug, termite, mouse, flea, wasp and spider — and the highest total wins. The result shows a confidence line, a two-sentence plain-language description and a recommended plan tier, and it can ride along into the free inspection booking.

### What should I change first after downloading the Pest Control Service Website template?
Start with the five variables: BRAND, CITY, FOUNDER, PHONE and EXPERIENCE. The Pest Control Service Website template rewrites every dependent headline, the tel: link, the footer and the quiz storage key from those values, so setting them first leaves no default-brand remnants. Then replace the invented prices, plan inclusions and reviews with your real ones before you publish.

### How do I adapt the pest identifier quiz for wildlife removal or lawn care?
The Pest Control Service Website quiz logic lives in one content table that maps each answer to weighted outcomes. To adapt it for wildlife removal, swap the seven pest outcomes for animals such as raccoons, squirrels and bats and keep the same three-question structure. For lawn care, point the questions at grubs, chinch bugs and moles, then adjust the recommended plan tiers to your own services.

### How does the free inspection booking carry the quiz result into the form?
After the quiz finishes, the Pest Control Service Website template stores the last result in the browser under a key derived from your brand name. The booking form then shows it as an amber chip reading something like 'Identifier: Ant colony — most likely', with an X to detach it. The inspection request is also saved locally, so a visitor who reloads keeps both.
