# New Home Development Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Warm Limestone** `#EDE6DA` — Primary editorial page backdrop and architectural cards
- **Natural Oak** `#B07A4F` — Warm wood borders, reserved lot badges, and timber accents
- **Deep Architectural Green** `#23403A` — Primary dark header, CTAs, available lot fills, and high-contrast ink
- **Warm Terracotta** `#C65D3B` — Accent highlights, selected lot outline, and upgrade tags
- **Studio Paper** `#FAF7F2` — Clean form surfaces, floor plan drawings, and calculator cards

## Typography
- **Community Wordmark & Editorial Headings** — Fraunces (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Property Specs, Descriptions & Controls** — Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Lot Numbers, Square Footage, Prices & Commute Times** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Full-bleed architectural golden-hour hero followed by generously spaced limestone-and-paper sections (1280px max width) with clean responsive stacking on 390px mobile.
- Radius: 14px refined architectural cards with hairline oak/green borders; crisp pill buttons for filters and 3D room stops.

## Motion
- Smooth Three.js camera glide between Dollhouse overview and First-Person eye-level interior room views.
- Instant material & lighting update in the 3D rooms as buyers switch Kitchen Finish, Flooring, and Flex Space upgrades.
- Smooth lot selection highlight on the 48-lot SVG site plan.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion by jumping camera viewpoints immediately without inertial drift.

## Imagery
Four cohesive golden-hour architectural WebP photographs from one shoot (street view, sunlit show-home living room, landscaped greenway path, courtyard terrace) paired with code-drawn 48-lot site plan, 2D floor plans, walkable Three.js 3D interior, and vector commute map.

## Tone
Warm, calm, architectural craftsmanship—tactile limestone, oak, terracotta, and transparent pricing.
