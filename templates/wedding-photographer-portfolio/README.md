A four-page portfolio for a wedding and portrait photographer: a cinematic home with the photographer's statement and featured weddings, a gallery index of four full weddings, one story page per wedding told in a film-like sequence of frames with captions, and an info page with packages, testimonials and an inquiry flow that checks the date against a booked list. The default content follows an invented photographer working the Hudson Valley for a decade, but the structure suits any photographer or two-person studio: change the name, region, start year and yearly wedding count and every page still reads true. The home hero is one full-bleed frame under near-black chrome with the photographer's mark in wide-caps film serif; wedding cards are letterboxed with place and season; story pages mix full-bleed and half-bleed frames with quiet captions; the lightbox runs full-screen with keyboard navigation and slow crossfades. Packages spell out hours, delivered frames, second shooter, travel and album; the inquiry form checks a chosen date against an invented booked list and ends in a client-side confirmation — no backend. Italiana sets the film-serif display, Manrope the quiet body, both self-hosted. Photography is detail-driven — venues, backs of heads, dresses on windows, tablescapes, empty dance floors — with no faces anywhere. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file; weddings, packages, booked dates and testimonials all read from it.

## Make it yours

- An elopement photographer: set REGION to your mountain or coastal area, cut the wedding list to three intimate stories and rewrite the packages around half-day coverage and travel.
- A portrait and family photographer: keep the story structure, relabel the four weddings as sessions, and swap the packages for family, newborn and milestone shoots with delivered frame counts.
- A two-person studio: set BRAND to the studio name, add the second shooter to every package and split the statement between both photographers.
- A destination wedding photographer: widen REGION to the places you travel, add travel terms to each package and note passport and permit details in the FAQ answers.

## FAQ

### How do I turn the Wedding Photographer Portfolio into my own site with my name, region and wedding count?
The Wedding Photographer Portfolio template reads four variables: BRAND, REGION, FOUNDED and YEARLY. Set BRAND to your name, REGION to where you shoot, FOUNDED to your first paid wedding year and YEARLY to your weddings per year between 10 and 24. The wordmark, page titles, email address, statement line and footer all follow those values, and the rest of the copy lives in one content file you rewrite to match.

### How does the inquiry form check a couple's wedding date against your booked dates?
The inquiry form on the Wedding Photographer Portfolio compares the date a couple picks against an invented booked list stored in the content file, then ends in a client-side confirmation. Nothing is submitted anywhere and no backend is involved, so the date check is a demonstration rather than a live calendar. To use it for real, edit the booked dates in the content file and connect the form to your own email or booking service.

### Can I use the Wedding Photographer Portfolio template for client work or a two-person studio?
The Wedding Photographer Portfolio is a Pro template, so commercial and client use is covered by Pro access, which also includes the full prompt and the demo source code. A two-person studio can use it as-is: change BRAND, REGION, FOUNDED and YEARLY, add the second shooter to the packages copy, and rewrite the statement and testimonials in the content file so both photographers are represented.

### What should I change first in the Wedding Photographer Portfolio template?
Start with the four variables, then replace the sample weddings. The Wedding Photographer Portfolio ships with four invented Hudson Valley weddings, each with its own story page, frames and captions, plus three packages, testimonials and a booked-dates list. Swap the image files and captions for your own weddings, rewrite the packages with your real hours, delivered frames, second shooter and travel terms, and update the photographer statement to your own voice.

### How do the story pages and lightbox work in the Wedding Photographer Portfolio?
The Wedding Photographer Portfolio gives each wedding its own story page, opened by a query in the address, and shows the first wedding when opened without one. Each story page runs a film-like sequence of full-bleed and half-bleed frames with quiet captions, and a full-screen lightbox with keyboard navigation and slow crossfades opens over them. All four pages are static, so you can host the built files on any static host.
