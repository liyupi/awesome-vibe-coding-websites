# Mechanical Keyboard Configurator — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Graphite studio** `#16171B` — Studio background and shell
- **Warm cream** `#F3E9D2` — Default alphas and high-contrast text
- **Signal orange** `#FF6B35` — Escape key, primary action with dark text, selected tab
- **Machined teal** `#2EC4B6` — Accent key, focus and saved state

## Typography
- **Display and controls** — Barlow (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Part codes and prices** — IBM Plex Mono (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: 64px utility header. A centered 1440px-wide bench with a 2:1 stage/inspector split, 28px gutters, 20px panel padding and a bottom configuration strip. On phones the stage precedes a full-width inspector.
- Radius: 5px controls, 10px panels and 1px separators.

## Motion
- Orbit by pointer or touch with damping and bounded zoom. Snap to front, top and perspective views.
- Depress keys on pointer hover and physical keystrokes, then release. Keep motion restrained under reduced motion.

## Imagery
Use an original code-drawn product model. Sculpt tapered caps with a slight top dish and row profiles. Print readable legends on cap tops via a shared canvas atlas. Add case bevels, feet, edges, a recessed plate, soft contact shadows and a bright seam against the graphite ground.

## Tone
Precise industrial product photography translated into procedural geometry. Short, direct labels.
