# Nonprofit Donation Page

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page fundraising campaign site for [BRAND], a small volunteer group [CAUSE] in [PLACE]. It reads like a documentary newspaper feature: big serif headlines, hairline rules, numbered sections, oversized figures and line-drawn SVG illustrations on warm paper. Interactive pieces do the persuading: an animated donation meter toward the [GOAL] goal, a slider-driven impact calculator based on [IMPACT_RATE], a scroll-linked timeline of past seasons, a volunteer sign-up form with client-side validation and a ticket-style success state, and an accordion FAQ. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

- Audience: Local residents, past volunteers and small donors who find the page through a newsletter or a shared link, mostly on their phones, and want to know quickly what the money does and how to help in person.
- Goal: Get the visitor to pledge a donation (the impact calculator's Donate button) or sign up for a clean-up day (the volunteer form). Secondary: share the page.
- Tone: Plain-spoken, warm and factual, like a good local reporter. Short sentences, concrete numbers, no exclamation marks, no guilt, no buzzwords.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Organisation name (default: "Fernmouth River Crew"). An invented group name. Used in the header wordmark, hero copy, form and footer.
- [CAUSE] — What the campaign does (default: "clearing litter from the banks of the River Fern"). A short phrase that completes “a volunteer group …”. Drives the hero, the map figure and the timeline drawings.
- [PLACE] — Town or area (default: "Ashby Vale"). Invented, never a real town presented as the campaign’s home.
- [GOAL] — Fundraising goal (default: "$48,000"). Sets the meter target and milestones (25/50/75/100%).
- [IMPACT_RATE] — What one unit of money achieves (default: "Every $12 removes 1 bag of litter and clears 2 metres of riverbank"). One price and two outcomes; the calculator computes both from the slider amount.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/nonprofit-donation-page in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Design
### Colors
- Paper `#F4EFE6` — Page background, text on dark bands
- Paper Deep `#EAE2D3` — Meter band and volunteer section background, illustration panels
- Ink `#1B2422` — Body text, headlines, closing band and footer background
- River `#1F4E4A` — Impact calculator band background, drop caps, river strokes, form submit button, links
- River Light `#6E9A93` — Contour lines, vignette strokes, unreached timeline dots
- Silt `#8A6E4B` — Captions, section numbers, timeline numerals, unreached milestone labels
- Rule `#CFC4B1` — 1px hairlines, meter track, input borders
- Signal `#E2582B` — Only for donate buttons, the meter fill, the slider fill and map site dots
- Error `#A8321E` — Form error text and invalid field borders

### Typography
- Display (headlines, big figures, FAQ questions): Fraunces Variable (OFL), weights 400/600/800
- Text (body, form fields, buttons): Public Sans Variable (OFL), weights 400/500/600
- Mono (captions, section numbers, nav, labels): IBM Plex Mono (OFL), weights 400/500

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 12-column grid inside a 1200px max-width container, 24px gutters; side padding 40px desktop, 20px mobile. Sections are separated by 128px vertical padding on desktop and 80px on mobile. Every section starts with a SectionLabel: mono 12px uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em, Silt, e.g. “01 — The record so far”, followed by a full-width 1px Rule hairline and 32px gap before the heading. Section headings are Fraunces 600, clamp(32px, 4vw, 52px), line-height 1.05, letter-spacing -0.015em. Body columns never exceed 62ch.
- Radius: Square editorial edges: 0 on panels, cards, meter track and images; 2px on buttons, inputs and chips; full circle only for the slider thumb, timeline dots and map dots.
- Motion: Default ease is [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]. Reveal: elements fade from opacity 0 and y 16px to 0 over 0.6s when 30% in view, once; siblings stagger 0.08s.
- Motion: Big figures count up once with CountUp (1.4s); the meter fill grows from 0 to its percentage over 1.8s when 40% in view, and re-animates smoothly (0.6s) whenever raisedTotal changes.
- Motion: SVG line drawings draw themselves with Motion pathLength 0 to 1 (river map 1.6s, vignettes 1.2s, check stamp 0.5s).
- Motion: The timeline’s vertical progress line is scroll-linked: useScroll on the Dispatches section (offset ['start 60%', 'end 60%']) drives scaleY of a River-coloured line from 0 to 1, transform-origin top.
- Motion: Hover: buttons darken 8% and lift 1px over 150ms; text links animate an underline from 0 to 100% width over 200ms.
- Motion: With prefers-reduced-motion: no reveals, count-ups, path drawing, scroll-linked line or water drift; everything renders in its final state and smooth scroll is off.
- Imagery: No photographs. All imagery is original line art drawn in SVG components: a contour-line map of the river stretch, six small vignettes for the timeline, a bag glyph for the calculator, a check stamp, and drifting water lines. Strokes are 1.5px, round caps, River Light or River, on Paper Deep panels, each with a mono figure caption (“Fig. 1 — …”). No people, faces, animals with personality, or real places.
