# Youth Sports Club Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build a polished four-page website for [BRAND], an invented youth soccer club in [LOCATION] for players ages 6–13. The club emphasizes [PROGRAM_FOCUS]. Include age-group squads, fixtures and results, a sortable league standings table, three coach profiles, transparent registration fees, a validated client-side registration form, and a dedicated parent information page. Use the exact default copy and sample data below, rewriting every club-specific detail when variable values change.

- Audience: Parents and caregivers comparing youth clubs on coaching, schedules, cost, safety, and fit, plus players who want to see teams and match activity. It also suits volunteer club administrators adapting a complete static front end for their own community.
- Goal: Help a family find the right age group, understand the match rhythm and total cost, trust the people and policies behind the club, and complete an explicitly illustrative registration request without any backend.
- Tone: Energetic, welcoming, organized, and specific. Sound like a capable community club rather than a professional franchise. Use short direct sentences, concrete times and fees, no hype, no guaranteed development claims, and no pressure tactics.

- Spacing: Use an 8px base grid and a 1240px maximum content width with 40px desktop gutters, 28px tablet gutters, and 18px mobile gutters. Major sections use 96px vertical padding desktop and 64px mobile. Use a 12-column desktop grid with 24px gaps. Focus uses a 2px Volt outline plus a 2px Forest outer ring on light surfaces.
- Radius: Use 18px radii on photographic frames and major cards, 12px on tables and forms, 8px on inputs, and fully rounded chips and buttons. Cards use 1px Line borders rather than heavy shadows; only the registration summary gets a restrained 0 16px 44px Forest shadow at 8% opacity.
- Motion: On the home hero, reveal the eyebrow, headline lines, body, and CTAs with y 24 to 0 and opacity 0 to 1 over 600ms, staggered by 75ms; settle hero-match.webp from scale 1.035 to 1 over 900ms.
- Motion: Reveal section headings and card groups once with y 18 and opacity over 480ms and a 60ms sibling stagger. Content remains visible without IntersectionObserver and is never hidden while waiting for animation.
- Motion: Filter fixture cards with a 180ms opacity and y 8 transition; animate standings row order with a 240ms layout transition and rotate the active sort arrow without animating the numbers.
- Motion: Mobile navigation opens with opacity and height over 220ms. Accordions use height and opacity over 200ms. Selected squad, kit, and payment controls use color and border transitions only.
- Motion: Registration success replaces the form in place over 220ms and moves focus to its heading. Do not shake invalid fields or animate fee totals with counting effects.
- Motion: Respect prefers-reduced-motion through Motion's reduced-motion hook and CSS overrides: remove transforms, smooth scrolling, staggers, and nonessential transitions while preserving all final states and interactions.
- Imagery: Use the six supplied documentary editorial sports photographs as one coherent shoot: natural grass, plain forest-green and chalk unbranded kits, warm late-afternoon sunlight, realistic texture, and subtle 35mm grain. Youth players are visible only from behind, at distance, or in non-identifiable profile; no recognizable child appears. The three adult coaches are fictional people who do not resemble any real individual. No image contains readable text, squad numbers, logos, sponsors, branded products, or recognizable venues. Use object-fit cover with the specified focal positions and descriptive alt text; never place text over a face. Field lines, the corner-flag favicon, score rules, icons, and small diagrams are drawn in code.
