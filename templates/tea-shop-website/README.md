# Tea Shop Website

> Calm tea shop with flavor notes, brewing guides, a working steep timer and sampler sets.

This template builds a calm, earthy shop for a loose-leaf tea seller: warm paper textures, muted green and clay tones, quiet serif headings, and photography graded warm throughout — amber liquor, curling steam, leaves on linen. The hero is a tea-pour macro with steam rising over the shop name, setting the unhurried tone the whole page keeps. It fits tea shops, coffee roasters, spice merchants and any small maker selling taste and ritual rather than urgency. Teas are grouped by type with flavor-note chips and filtered live by type and caffeine. Product pages are query-routed — the first tea appears when no query is given — and carry a brewing guide with temperature and leaf amount, plus a working steep timer: a real mm:ss countdown with pause and reset that customers can actually run while their pot draws. Sampler sets appear as calm cards, a subscription tin is explained quietly as sample copy with no payments, and a brewing-basics strip keeps the teaching light. The stack is entirely client-side — React, Tailwind and Motion — with fonts self-hosted through Fontsource and every photograph bundled. Four variables (brand, city, specialty line and subscription price) rewrite the copy from one content file. Reveals are slow and steam-like, the timer breathes quietly while it runs, and prefers-reduced-motion returns a still page. The template also works well beyond tea: swap the variables to a coffee roaster or a spice house and the same calm rhythm carries. Six photographs from one warm-graded shoot ship with the template: pours, leaves and pots, no faces, no readable packaging.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Shop name | Amber & Ash |
| `[CITY]` | City | Burlington, Vermont |
| `[SPECIALTY]` | Specialty line | small-lot oolong and black teas |
| `[SUB_PRICE]` | Subscription price | $19/month |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add tea-shop-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A craft coffee roaster: set BRAND and CITY to your roastery, rewrite the tea entries as single-origin beans with roast notes, and keep the steep timer for pour-over times.
- A spice merchant: point SPECIALTY at your blends, rename the type filters to heat levels, and swap brewing guides for toasting and grinding notes.
- A tea subscription club: set SUB_PRICE to your monthly tin, rewrite the subscription copy around your shipping rhythm, and link the shelf to your mailing list.
- A gift shop for tea drinkers: turn the sampler set cards into curated gift boxes, adjust the set names and prices, and keep the brewing-basics strip as a teaching insert.

## FAQ

### How does the steep timer on the Tea Shop Website template work?
Each tea page on the Tea Shop Website template carries a working steep timer: a real mm:ss countdown with pause and reset that customers can run while their pot draws. It ends exactly at 00:00, drains a thin clay progress ring, and breathes the digits once per second while running. It makes no sound and sends no notification, and it pauses its tick when the tab is hidden.

### How do I change the shop name, city and subscription price in the tea shop template?
The Tea Shop Website template rewrites its copy from four variables: BRAND, CITY, SPECIALTY and SUB_PRICE. Set BRAND to your shop name, CITY to where you trade, SPECIALTY to your lowercase specialty line such as small-lot oolong and black teas, and SUB_PRICE to your monthly tin price string. All copy lives in one content file, so the variables propagate through the hero, subscription line and footer address.

### Can the Tea Shop Website template be adapted for a coffee roaster or spice merchant?
The Tea Shop Website template works for any small maker selling taste and ritual rather than urgency. One live demo already rebuilds it as a craft coffee roaster. Swap the variables to your roastery or spice house, rewrite the tea entries in the content file as beans or blends with their own flavor notes, brewing temperatures and steep times, and the calm layout and warm photography rhythm carry over.

### What is included with the free Tea Shop Website template?
The Tea Shop Website template is free and ships two pages, six bundled photographs from one warm-graded shoot, a tea grid filtered live by type and caffeine, query-routed product pages with brewing guides, a working steep timer, sampler set cards, subscription sample copy and a brewing-basics strip. The prompt and every DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0; commercial and client use needs Pro.

### How do customers save teas to their shelf on the Tea Shop Website template?
The Tea Shop Website template gives each visitor a local shelf: a Bookmark button on every tea page adds that tea to a list stored in the browser under a key built from your brand name. The header shows the live count as customers browse, and the shelf needs no account, backend or payment setup, so it works on the static build as shipped.
