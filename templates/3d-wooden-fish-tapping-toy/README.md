A sculpted wooden percussion fish sits at the center of a quiet, amber-lit room. Tap its polished surface, use the large strike button, or press the spacebar to bring down the padded mallet. Each hit produces a small physical rebound, an expanding gold ring, drifting dust, and a resonant wooden note synthesized directly in the browser. The toy is ready on the first screen, with no introduction to dismiss.

The composition gives the object room to breathe. A narrow identity rail, a large serif invitation, and neatly aligned counters surround the scene without covering it. Fine procedural wood grain, a carved sound slit, soft contact shadows, and a gently lit circular plinth make the instrument feel like a crafted object rather than a generic floating sphere. A restrained control dock keeps sound, ambience, and automatic tapping close at hand.

Visitors can make their own rhythm or start a slow automatic pulse. A forgiving combo counter recognizes repeated taps while the local daily total records the small ritual across visits. Sound starts only after interaction, the ambience is separately controlled, and both remain optional. Pointer, touch, and keyboard input share the same feedback. Reduced-motion preferences keep the instrument playable while removing idle drift and busy particle effects.

Use this prompt for an experimental personal site, a playful break page, or a small interactive portfolio piece. Change the invented brand, wood finish, room setting, and short floating message to give the same structure a different mood. All copy lives in one content file, dependencies and fonts ship locally, and the finished site is a small static build with no accounts, tracking, or backend.

### How does the tapping interaction work on the 3D Wooden Fish Tapping Toy?
Visitors strike the 3D Wooden Fish Tapping Toy by tapping the wooden fish itself, pressing the large Strike the wood button, or pressing Space on a keyboard. Each hit swings the mallet into the carved sound slit, dips and rebounds the body, sends out an expanding amber ripple, drifts a few warm particles and plays a resonant wooden note synthesized in the browser. Sound starts only after the first interaction and can be turned off.
