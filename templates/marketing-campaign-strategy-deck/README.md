# Marketing Campaign Strategy Deck

> An 11-slide Go-To-Market campaign strategy deck with clickable personas, budget scenario allocator, and Gantt timeline.

Created for growth marketers, product marketing managers, and consumer hardware founders preparing a flagship launch plan, this 11-slide web presentation turns a Go-To-Market campaign strategy into an energetic, interactive browser deck. Instead of locking budget tables and audience personas inside static slides, stakeholders can inspect buyer segments, toggle paid-versus-organic allocation scenarios, and review an eight-week launch schedule directly inside the presentation canvas. The visual palette pairs a crisp snow-white surface and deep charcoal ink with vivid coral primary accents and sunshine-yellow highlights, delivering bold consumer-brand energy while maintaining crisp readability and WCAG AA contrast. Opening slides define the campaign big idea and launch thesis before moving into an interactive audience persona deck where clicking any buyer tab updates purchase triggers, objections, and priority channels. A full-funnel acquisition SVG diagram maps reach through waitlist, pre-order, and referral conversion targets, followed by a live channel budget allocator chart where switching between Balanced, Paid-Heavy, and Organic-Led presets immediately recalculates channel spend bars, projected reach, and blended customer acquisition cost. Remaining slides present an eight-week launch Gantt timeline drawn in code, three creative messaging pillars, an influencer and press embargo cadence, a launch KPI target scorecard, and an interactive launch-readiness checklist saved in browser memory. Complete deck ergonomics include keyboard, spacebar, and swipe navigation, a progress bar, a G-key thumbnail overview grid, an N-key presenter notes drawer with timer, F-key fullscreen mode, and a clean print stylesheet for PDF handoffs.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Hardware brand name | Kinetix Audio |
| `[PRODUCT_NAME]` | Flagship product name | Pulse Mini Studio Monitor |
| `[CAMPAIGN_THEME]` | Campaign big-idea tagline | Studio Sound Anywhere You Set Up |
| `[BUDGET_TOTAL]` | Quarterly campaign budget | $450,000 |
| `[LAUNCH_TARGET]` | Primary launch goal | 18,000 Pre-Orders |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add marketing-campaign-strategy-deck` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A consumer audio startup: set BRAND and PRODUCT_NAME to your device, point LAUNCH_TARGET at your pre-order goal, and rewrite the three persona dossiers for your actual buyers.
- A growth marketing lead pitching to a CFO: set BUDGET_TOTAL to the real quarterly figure and adjust the three channel presets so the allocator matches your approved channel mix.
- A product marketing manager planning a retail drop: keep the eight-week Gantt but rename the workstreams and milestone markers to your embargo, seeding and shelf dates.
- A founder preparing an investor update: set CAMPAIGN_THEME to your launch thesis and replace the KPI scorecard targets with the numbers your board already tracks.

## FAQ

### What slides and interactive parts does the Marketing Campaign Strategy Deck include?
The Marketing Campaign Strategy Deck is an 11-slide web presentation covering a campaign big idea, three clickable audience personas, an SVG acquisition funnel, a channel budget allocator with Balanced, Paid-Heavy and Organic-Led presets, an eight-week Gantt launch timeline, creative messaging pillars, an influencer and press cadence, a KPI scorecard and a launch-readiness checklist. Navigation works by keyboard, spacebar and swipe, with a progress bar, a thumbnail overview grid and a presenter notes drawer.

### How do I change the budget, product name and launch goal in this campaign deck?
The Marketing Campaign Strategy Deck is driven by five variables: BRAND, PRODUCT_NAME, CAMPAIGN_THEME, BUDGET_TOTAL and LAUNCH_TARGET. Set them in the content file the prompt describes, then rewrite every headline, label and paragraph that mentions the old values so the budget allocator, funnel numbers, Gantt milestones and KPI scorecard stay consistent with your own campaign.

### Can I export the strategy deck to PDF for a budget review meeting?
Yes. The Marketing Campaign Strategy Deck includes a print stylesheet that sizes pages to landscape and prints all 11 slides one per page, with the toolbar controls hidden. Use the Print PDF button in the header to produce a handoff file for CMOs, retail buyers or founders. Interactive states such as the selected budget scenario and persona tab print as they currently appear on screen, so set them before printing.

### How does the budget scenario allocator work in the Marketing Campaign Strategy Deck?
The channel budget allocator on the fifth slide lets you switch between Balanced, Paid-Heavy and Organic-Led presets. Each preset redraws the SVG bars for Paid Social and Video, Creator Seeding and Sponsorships, Search and Shopping, PR and Launch Events, and Lifecycle Email and Referral, and recalculates projected pre-orders, blended customer acquisition cost and expected ROAS. It is a planning view, not a live ad platform connection, so the numbers come from the campaign data you write.
