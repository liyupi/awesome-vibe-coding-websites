> Motion design portfolio with a code-drawn showreel, looping project thumbnails and frame-by-frame case pages.

A portfolio for a motion designer where the work itself moves. The first screen is a showreel drawn live in code: a twenty-second loop of five kinetic scenes in acid yellow and electric violet, with play and pause, a progress bar and an optional synthesized sound track that stays off until asked for. Below it, six projects sit in a grid whose thumbnails come alive on hover, each a small looping animation built from the same drawing functions. The case pages are the heart of the template: a three-second title animation plays in a player you can scrub frame by frame, with a frame counter, timecode, single-frame step buttons and a storyboard strip of eight frames that seek the player when clicked. Three easing curves are plotted beside it, each with a play button that sends a square along a track, so the timing can be felt rather than described. Services and rates follow in three plain cards with a day rate you can change, then a tools strip set in plain text and a short contact form. Everything on screen is drawn in code, so there are no video files to host and nothing to license, the whole site stays well under a megabyte, and every animation rests on a still frame when a visitor prefers reduced motion. The palette is off-black, acid yellow and electric violet with a bold grotesk and two mono labels, and the layout is a strict grid with square corners. To make it yours, change the name, city, featured project and day rate in the content file and redraw the scenes with your own timing; the type, palette and motion system stay as they are.

## Make it yours

- A freelance motion designer: set BRAND to your name, CITY to where you work and HERO_PROJECT to your strongest piece, then redraw the showreel scenes with your own timing.
- A studio pitching to festival organizers: keep the case player and storyboard strip, swap the projects for event title sequences and set DAY_RATE to your project rate.
- A UI motion specialist: replace the six project thumbnails with interface transitions and rewrite the services cards around product and interface animation.
- A design graduate building a first portfolio: set BRAND and CITY, feature one student project as HERO_PROJECT and keep the easing curve cards to show you understand timing.

## FAQ

### How do I change the name, city and day rate in the Motion Designer Portfolio template?
The Motion Designer Portfolio template keeps every piece of copy in one content file, so you set BRAND to your name, CITY to your studio city and DAY_RATE to your own rate there rather than editing components. HERO_PROJECT names the featured project shown first. After changing the values, rewrite the headlines and paragraphs that mention the old ones so the copy still reads consistently.

### Can I use the Motion Designer Portfolio template for client work?
The Motion Designer Portfolio template is a Pro template, so commercial and client use is covered by Pro membership, which also includes the full prompt and the source code of the demos. Free templates' prompts and every DESIGN.md are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only. If you are building a portfolio for a paying client, keep the work under Pro.

### What does the case page in the Motion Designer Portfolio template actually show?
Each case page in the Motion Designer Portfolio template plays a three-second title animation in a player you can scrub frame by frame, with a frame counter, timecode and single-frame step buttons. A storyboard strip of eight frames seeks the player when clicked, and three easing curves are plotted beside it, each with a play button that sends a square along a track so the timing can be felt.

### Do I need to host video files for the showreel in the Motion Designer Portfolio template?
No. The showreel in the Motion Designer Portfolio template is drawn live in code as a twenty-second loop of five kinetic scenes, and the six project thumbnails are small looping animations built from the same drawing functions. There are no video files to host and nothing to license, and the whole site stays well under a megabyte.

### How do I add my own project animations to the Motion Designer Portfolio template?
The Motion Designer Portfolio template draws its scenes with pure drawing functions that take a time value, so any frame can be rendered on demand. To make the work yours, redraw the showreel and project scenes with your own shapes and timing while keeping the type, palette and motion system as they are, then update the project titles and descriptions in the content file.
