# Yoga Studio Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [BRAND], a neighborhood yoga studio on [STREET] in [CITY], open since [FOUNDED], teaching [PRACTICE]. It must feel like a real, calm studio: airy photography of the empty room, mats and props, the three practice styles explained in plain words, an intro offer, teacher bios, a membership comparison with per-class math, a first-timer FAQ, a weekly schedule filterable by level and style, and a booking modal that confirms client-side with a saved spot. Pages: index.html (home), schedule.html (the week). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

- Audience: Adults within a short drive deciding to try a first class, plus lapsed members comparing schedules at 10pm. Mixed ages, mostly women, wary of being the newest person in the room; they want to know exactly what a first visit feels like and what it costs.
- Goal: Fill mats with the intro offer and keep them with memberships. Within ten seconds a visitor should feel the room's calm, see the intro price, and find a beginner-friendly class this week.
- Tone: Warm, unhurried and specific, like a teacher greeting a first-timer at the door. Short sentences, concrete numbers (minutes, dollars, mats), no wellness jargon, no medical claims, no exclamation marks.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Studio name (default: "Stillwater Yoga"). Invented studio name. Used in the wordmark, titles, footer, email (hello@ + brandSlug without hyphens + .com) and localStorage keys. Never a real studio or franchise.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Boulder, Colorado"). Where the studio is. Used in the hero eyebrow, the getting-here line and the footer. The street stays invented-sounding; only the city may be real.
- [STREET] — Street (default: "Linden Court"). The invented street the studio sits on ('9 Linden Court'). Drives the address and hero copy. Common English words, not a real address.
- [PRACTICE] — Practice styles (default: "Vinyasa, Yin and Restorative"). The three styles the studio teaches. Drives the three style cards, the class names in the week grid (keep 3 styles and 20 weekly classes), the filter chips and the teacher specialties. Keep exactly three comma-separated style names.
- [FOUNDED] — Year opened (default: "2018"). Four-digit year. Drives the hero eyebrow, the wordmark line 'Est. [FOUNDED]', the story line and teacher 'practicing since' spans.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/yoga-studio-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

- Motion: Reveal: blocks wrapped in <Reveal> start at opacity 0, translateY(16px) and animate to rest over 900ms --ease-out when 15% visible (Motion whileInView, viewport once). Siblings stagger by 100ms (index prop).
- Motion: Hero photo: on load the image scales from 1.05 to 1.0 over 2600ms --ease-out; while scrolling, the image translates 0 to 8% of its height as the hero scrolls out (Motion useScroll, offset ['start start', 'end start']). H1 lines rise from translateY(24px) and opacity 0 with 160ms stagger, starting 300ms after load.
- Motion: Breathing dot: a 10px Clay circle under the hero headline scales 1 to 1.5 and back on a 5000ms ease-in-out loop (inhale/exhale), pausing under reduced motion to a static dot.
- Motion: Class cards: hover lifts 4px and softens the border to Fern over 300ms; clicking opens the BookingModal. Filter changes fade cards out (opacity, 8px y) and re-stagger them in over 350ms --ease-io.
- Motion: Booking modal: Night overlay fades to 45% over 300ms; the Card panel rises 24px and fades in over 380ms --ease-out; closing reverses in 260ms. The confirmation view crossfades inside the panel and the spot number types in character by character over 600ms. Escape and overlay click close.
- Motion: FAQ accordions: height 0 to auto and opacity 0 to 1 over 340ms --ease-io; the Plus icon rotates 45 degrees into an X.
- Motion: Membership tiers: the middle (featured) tier is outlined 1px Clay and lifted 8px; on hover each tier's per-class math line counts the price down/up to the effective per-class value over 500ms.
- Motion: Header: transparent over the photo hero with Card text; after 40px of scroll it gets Sand at 94% opacity, backdrop-filter blur(10px), Fern text and a 1px Rule bottom border, 250ms transition. On schedule.html it is solid from the start.
- Motion: Links: .link-draw grows a 1px currentColor underline from 0% to 100% width over 300ms --ease-out on hover and focus-visible.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — disable the hero scale and parallax, H1 rise, breathing dot, count-ups, type-in and staggered filters (render at rest). Color and border hover changes stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Real photography only, calm and airy, all six made for this template in one consistent style: soft daylight, pale wood, linen and plants, empty rooms and still-life props, documentary editorial look, fine grain. Two photo kinds, never mixed: room and prop stills (the hero, the three style cards, the evening candle shot — no people, no hands, no faces) and teacher portraits (AI-generated fictional people, head-and-shoulders headshots only, natural light, never full-body poses, never hands touching props, never resembling any real individual; they are placeholders users swap for their own staff). Schedule and class avatars are never photos: initials circles drawn in code. No readable text or logos anywhere. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 18px corners, no shadow; photos on Sand get a 1px Rule outline. Captions below photos in Mulish 13px Moss. Drawn in code: the favicon, the wordmark (type only), level pills, teacher initials circles, the breathing dot, dividers and the modal spot card. Icons: lucide-react only, stroke width 1.5.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: bookings, capacity and confirmations live in the browser only (localStorage); nothing is charged or sent.
- Every internal link points to a file name (./index.html, ./schedule.html, ./schedule.html?style=yin, ./index.html#intro) — never to a bare directory; schedule.html opened without a query shows all classes.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- Portraits and studio photographs are AI-generated fictional people (not resembling any real individual) used as template placeholders; no real people appear anywhere.
- No medical or therapeutic claims anywhere; experience language only ('leave lighter', never 'heals' or 'treats').
- No dates or years anywhere except 'Est. [FOUNDED]'; the week grid is date-free and repeats weekly; the site never shows today's date.
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere: disable parallax, the breathing dot, count-ups, type-in and staggered filter animations; keep color/border hovers.
- The site must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; the modal works with keyboard only, focus-traps while open, and every interactive element has a visible focus ring.
- Keep copy concrete: no lorem, no filler, no wellness clichés; times, prices and capacities always come from src/content.ts.
