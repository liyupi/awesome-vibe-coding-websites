A portfolio for a landscape and editorial photographer who does not shoot weddings. It opens on one photograph filling the whole screen, with the title set in a light serif over a soft gradient and the image changing with the arrow keys or a swipe. Four series, coastlines, deserts, alpine lakes and night skies, each have their own page with a full-bleed viewer, short field notes and a place on a map drawn in code. A details panel opens beside any photograph with the camera, lens and settings it was made with. The print shop offers every picture on three papers in three sizes, with a cart that remembers itself, and a licensing form covers editorial and commercial use. Workshops are listed with dates, prices and places left, and a short journal collects field notes. The palette is a near-black gallery, warm white and one quiet ochre accent, the display face is a refined serif, and all photographs share one muted, atmospheric look. Swap in your own images under the same file names and edit the notes in the content file; layout, palette and motion stay as they are.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Photographer's name | Greta Lindholm |
| `[CITY]` | Home town | Bozeman |
| `[HERO_TITLE]` | Title of the featured photograph | Sea Stack, First Light |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add landscape-photographer-portfolio` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A coastal fine-art photographer: set BRAND and CITY, keep the Coastlines series, and rewrite the print shop papers and sizes to match your own editions.
- A desert and night-sky shooter: make Deserts and Night skies the lead series, set HERO_TITLE to your strongest frame, and cut the other two.
- A photography workshop leader: keep the series pages as proof of work and rewrite the workshops list with your own locations, group sizes and prices.
- An editorial photographer who licenses images: keep the licensing form and details panel, and trim the print shop to a small set of available prints.

## FAQ

### Can I sell prints from a Landscape Photographer Portfolio built with AI?
Yes. The Landscape Photographer Portfolio template includes a print shop where every photograph is offered on three papers in three sizes, with a cart that saves itself in the browser. Buyers pick paper and size, then check out through your own process. A separate licensing form covers editorial and commercial use, so print sales and rights enquiries stay apart.

### How do I swap in my own photographs and field notes?
Replace the images under the same file names listed in the template and edit the copy in the content file, where photographs, series, print options, workshops and journal entries are stored as typed objects. Your titles, capture details, places and notes go there. Layout, palette and motion stay as they are, so the site keeps its look.

### What does the details panel show for each photograph?
The details panel opens beside any photograph with the camera, lens and settings it was made with, plus place, weather and a one-line note. For the featured image the sample reads medium format, 45 mm, f/8, 1/60 s, ISO 100, tripod. Each entry also links to buy the print or open the series it belongs to.
