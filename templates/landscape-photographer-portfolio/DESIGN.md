# Landscape Photographer Portfolio — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **night** `#0F0F10` — Page ground
- **nightSoft** `#19191B` — Panels, cards, cart drawer
- **warm** `#F5F1E8` — Text, buttons with night text
- **stone** `#A8A39A` — Secondary text, capture details
- **ochre** `#C9A227` — The quiet accent: eyebrows, pins, active states, the solid call-to-action plate
- **line** `#2C2C2F` — Hairlines

## Typography
- **Display / titles and pull quotes** — Cormorant Garamond (OFL), weights 300/500. Self-host the font files.
- **Body / UI** — Figtree Variable (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.
- **Mono / capture details, index counter, captions** — IBM Plex Mono (OFL), weights 400. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Section padding py-24 desktop / py-16 mobile; container max-w-[1240px] px-6; gallery gaps 24px; 8px rhythm.
- Radius: Corners are square: 0px on images, cards and buttons; the details panel has 4px.

## Motion
- Viewer: photographs cross-fade 700ms; the caption text fades 300ms after the image has settled.
- Scroll reveals: sections fade up 16px over 500ms, once (Motion whileInView); content is visible by default.
- Hover on a thumbnail: it brightens from 80% to 100% and gains a 1px ochre line at the bottom (200ms).
- prefers-reduced-motion: photographs switch with a 150ms fade, no reveals, no hover motion.

## Imagery
Landscape photographs with one consistent look: muted natural colours, deep blacks, soft atmospheric light, long exposures on water, medium-format clarity with fine grain. No people in the landscape photographs; one working shot of the photographer from behind. No text, watermarks, logos or recognizable buildings or landmarks in any image.

## Tone
Quiet and exact, like a caption written in a hut after a long walk: place names, weather and light, no grand statements.
