# Music Streaming App with Lyrics

> A complete listening room with original previews, synchronized lyrics, charts, comments, and a persistent queue.

Music Streaming App with Lyrics is a working, client-side listening experience rather than a static music-dashboard mockup. It opens into an editorial library of invented artists and original cover art, then carries the same playback state through sample charts, an immersive now-playing view, song comments, and a queue drawer. It suits music-product concepts, creator portfolios, design experiments, and developers who want a substantial audio interface without a backend.

Deep plum surfaces, warm apricot controls, expressive Fraunces lyric lines, and compact DM Sans navigation create a nocturnal listening-room character. Six coordinated mixed-media covers give every fictional release its own identity. The now-playing stage pairs a circular vinyl treatment with large, time-synchronized lyrics; selecting any lyric line seeks the actual local audio preview.

Six deterministic, procedurally composed WAV previews make every track genuinely playable. Play, pause, previous, next, seek, volume, queue selection, removal, reordering, and automatic advancement all operate on one shared audio state. The mini player remains available while visitors move between Library, Charts, and Now Playing. Sample comments and chart figures are clearly labeled, while visitor comments, likes, followed playlists, and queue changes stay only on the current device.

Five variables change the service name, listening mood, audience, tagline, and featured playlist. The content model keeps track titles, fictional artists, original lyric previews, comments, artwork paths, synthesis settings, and interface copy together so another musical direction can replace the defaults coherently. The alternate case becomes a sunlit indie-soul service with entirely rewritten songs and lyrics. All fonts, images, and audio ship locally, sound begins only after a user gesture, and reduced-motion users receive a static vinyl and immediate lyric updates.

## FAQ

### How do the synchronized lyrics work in the Music Streaming App with Lyrics template?
The Music Streaming App with Lyrics template times six lyric lines per track against the actual local audio preview. The active line is derived from playback time, kept centered in the lyric viewport, and selecting any line seeks playback to that line's exact start. Reduced-motion users get immediate lyric updates without smooth travel, and lyrics stay aligned because the timings live in the same content model as the songs.

### Is there real audio in this music streaming template, or is it just a visual mockup?
The Music Streaming App with Lyrics template ships six original locally generated audio previews, so every fictional track is genuinely playable. Play, pause, previous, next, seek, volume, mute, queue selection, removal, reordering and automatic advancement all act on one shared audio element. Sound starts only after a visitor presses play, and nothing autoplays on load or when moving between Library, Charts and Now Playing.

### How do I change the artists, songs and lyrics to fit my own music project?
Set the five variables in the Music Streaming App with Lyrics template: BRAND, TAGLINE, AUDIENCE, MUSIC_MOOD and FEATURED_PLAYLIST. Then rewrite the song titles, fictional artists, lyric previews, playlist descriptions and comments in the content file to match the mood. The alternate demo shows this in practice, turning the late-night service into a sunlit indie-soul catalog with entirely rewritten songs and lyrics while keeping the same layout and motion.

### Where do likes, comments and queue changes get saved in the Music Streaming App with Lyrics template?
Visitor comments, likes, followed playlists and queue changes in the Music Streaming App with Lyrics template are stored only on the current device, in a local storage namespace derived from the service name. Sample comments and chart figures are labeled as sample data. Stored items are validated against the content, and a reload restores the last selected track paused at the start rather than playing sound unexpectedly. A Reset local demo action clears everything.

### What should I change first to make this music app template feel like my own service?
Start with BRAND and MUSIC_MOOD in the Music Streaming App with Lyrics template, because the wordmark, document title, playlist labels, storage namespace and footer all follow the brand, while the mood drives artists, song titles, lyrics, comments and audio settings. Then set FEATURED_PLAYLIST, AUDIENCE and TAGLINE, and replace the six covers before touching layout or colors.
