# Dusk Track Racing Game

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build [BRAND], a single-page third-person race at [PLACE] on the closed circuit [TRACK]. The player drives the code-built [CAR] for 3 laps at golden hour, against three rivals named in [RIVALS], with checkpoint gates, a drift boost, a lap ghost saved in the browser, a minimap, and a podium. The live scene is the first screen.

- Audience: Developers and indie hackers who want a finished, reskinnable Three.js arcade race that runs as static files and plays with a keyboard or on-screen touch controls.
- Goal: Ship one complete heat: a start panel, a countdown, a full race, a pause state, and a podium. The player can beat the rivals, save a best lap, and race that lap as a ghost, on both 1440×900 and 390×844.
- Tone: Warm, plain, and specific. US English. Club-heat language, no taunts, no military wording, and no real series, manufacturers, or drivers.

## Stack
- Framework: Vite 6 + TypeScript 5
- Styling: Vanilla CSS
- Libraries: three (MIT), @fontsource/big-shoulders-display (OFL-1.1), @fontsource/outfit (OFL-1.1), @fontsource/share-tech-mono (OFL-1.1)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Scaffold Vite's vanilla TypeScript app. Scripts: dev=vite, build=tsc && vite build, preview=vite preview. Do not add React, a physics engine, a postprocessing stack, or a game framework.
  - In vite.config.ts set base to './', build.outDir to dist, build.emptyOutDir to true, and build.sourcemap to false. Enable strict TypeScript.
  - Install three and the three Fontsource packages above. Import Big Shoulders Display 500, 700, and 800, Outfit 400, 500, and 600, and Share Tech Mono 400 only.
  - Use index.html plus src/content.ts, main.ts, track.ts, cars.ts, drive.ts, race.ts, hud.ts, input.ts, audio.ts, and styles.css, plus public/favicon.svg and ASSETS.md.
  - Put every name, button label, and sentence in src/content.ts. Read [BRAND], [PLACE], [TRACK], [CAR], [DRIVER], and [RIVALS] from that file. Split [RIVALS] on commas into exactly three trimmed names.
  - Keep laps at 3, player number 14, rival colors #2f6f62, #d7a441, and #3d4c7a, and rival numbers 7, 22, and 3 for every variable set. A new place changes copy, not the biome, the clock time, the mesh, or the palette.
  - Gate labels other than the start line stay North Gate, Sweeper, and Hairpin. The start line label is [TRACK] plus the word line.
  - Derive the localStorage key and the tree seed from the lowercased brand and track, with spaces turned into hyphens. Never hard-code the default names outside content.ts.
  - Wait for document.fonts.ready before drawing canvas number plates or the hut sign. Set those textures to SRGBColorSpace. Set document.title from content.ts to [BRAND] — [TRACK], and match the static title in index.html to the default.

- [TRACK] — Circuit name (default: "Barleycut"). Invented circuit. Subtitle, start-line callout, podium, and the other half of the save key.
- [CAR] — Car name (default: "Skiff 4"). Invented closed-wheel car. Shown next to the circuit name. The mesh does not change.
- [DRIVER] — Player name (default: "Nell Harp"). Invented driver. Podium sentence and the player's result row.
- [RIVALS] — Three rivals (default: "Nia Pell, Omar Voss, Bess Calder"). Three invented names, comma-separated, in teal, gold, and dusk-blue order.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/dusk-track-racing-game in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Design
### Colors

- Paper `#f6f1e7` — Text on ink, edge lines, start-stripe light squares, focus rings
- Coral `#c4512c` — Player body, primary buttons, player minimap triangle
- Gold `#e7c27a` — Center dashes, boost fill, minimap ribbon, drift button
- Sun `#ffb067` — Directional light, gate veil, ghost emissive, boost glow
- Horizon `#e7a15a` — Sky horizon band and fog tint
- Zenith `#1e3a5f` — Sky top and the page background behind the canvas
- Asphalt `#3a342e` — Racing surface
- Runoff `#6b5344` — Dirt shoulders
- Grass `#6d7a45` — Ground plane beyond the runoff
- Pine `#3e5340` — Tree cones
- Teal `#2f6f62` — First rival
- Rival gold `#d7a441` — Second rival
- Dusk blue `#3d4c7a` — Third rival
- Danger `#d8452f` — Taillights, wrong-way banner, red start lamps

### Typography
- Club name, position, speed, and podium place: Big Shoulders Display (OFL), weights 500/700/800
- Body, buttons, and HUD labels: Outfit (OFL), weights 400/500/600
- Lap times: Share Tech Mono (OFL), weights 400

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 4 px base. Desktop panel padding 28 px and HUD inset 20 px. Mobile inset 14 px plus safe-area. Buttons at least 44 px, coarse-pointer controls at least 72 px. The start sheet on a phone stays within 58 vh and scrolls inside itself.
- Radius: 18 px on the start, pause, and podium panels. Pill buttons. The minimap and steer pad are circles. The boost track is a 999 px capsule. World meshes stay faceted, not filleted like UI.
- Motion: Chase camera eases with exponential smoothing and may roll up to 4 degrees against the steer. A boost widens FOV by 6 degrees for 1.2 seconds.
- Motion: Countdown numerals use a 160 ms scale from 0.92 to 1. Drift dust is 12 short-lived specks. The armed boost bar pulses once.
- Motion: Overlays fade over 180 ms and move no more than 8 px. Checkpoint callouts last 0.7 seconds.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion removes roll, FOV kick, dust, pulses, overlay travel, and countdown scale. The race, ghost, and rivals still move, because that motion is the game.
- Imagery: Draw the whole world in Three.js: sky shader, banked ribbon, closed-wheel wedge, gates, bales, tires, cones, pines, hills, a hut, and a grandstand of featureless box spectators. No photographs, generated images, HDRIs, downloaded models, or brand marks. Golden-hour light is one low warm sun and long shadows. The parked car on the ribbon is the first-screen anchor, with the start panel beside it rather than instead of it.

2. **Banked golden-hour circuit** — Axes: +x east, +y up, +z south. Build a CatmullRomCurve3 with closed set to true and tension 0.5, through these x,z meters: (0, 0), (72, 6), (136, 32), (168, 96), (128, 152), (56, 168), (-8, 136), (-56, 88), (-72, 32), (-32, -16), then back to the start. Scale x and z about the origin until the curve length is 1000 m. Sample 250 evenly spaced points. Yaw 0 faces +x. Positive yaw turns toward +z. With the nose built on local +z, set the yaw pivot's rotation.y to Math.PI / 2 - yaw. Right vector is normalize(cross(tangent, up)), so positive lateral is right of travel. Build each ribbon cross-section in the right/up plane, rotate it around the tangent by the bank angle, and keep the center point at y = 2. Gameplay lateral is the signed x,z distance to the centerline, not the distance along the tilted surface. Bank is +16 degrees from arc 200 to 500 and +8 degrees from arc 720 to 850, blended with smoothstep over 30 m, and 0 elsewhere. Positive bank raises the right edge. Asphalt half-width is 8 m, color #3a342e. Paper edge lines sit 0.4 m in from each edge. Gold center dashes are 4 m paint and 6 m gap. A start stripe at arc 0 is 0.8 m ink and paper squares across the asphalt, with no logo. Runoff continues to 18 m at 0.35 times the bank, color #6b5344. Beyond that, a grass plane #6d7a45. Curbs on the banked spans only: 0.6 m stripes of #c4512c and #f6f1e7.
   Layout: Gates at arcs 0, 250, 500, and 750. Each gate is two tapered pylons, 0.7 m at the base and 0.4 m at the top, 5.2 m tall, color #c4653a, placed 1.2 m outside each asphalt edge, with a 0.45 m cream beam at 4.2 m. The arc-0 beam uses the ink-and-paper stripe. A Sun-colored vertical plane between the pylons is opacity 0.22 and does not write depth. Callout names: [TRACK] line, North Gate, Sweeper, Hairpin. Hay-bale stacks, three boxes each in #c4a15a, at arcs 780, 805, and 830, lateral -6.6 m. Two tire stacks of four 8-sided rings, #2c2a28 with a #d4722a band, at arcs 460 and 478, lateral +7.2 m. Four cones, 0.7 m, #e07a2f with a Paper band, at arcs 560, 578, 596, and 614, laterals +6.6, -6.6, +6.6, -6.6. A wood grandstand of three steps, 18 m long, #8a6244, at arc 45, lateral +22, with 18 featureless spectator boxes in Coral, Teal, Rival gold, Dusk blue, Paper, and Ink. No faces. A hut at arc 45, lateral -22, Ink walls and a Coral roof, with one canvas sign reading [BRAND]. A five-lamp gantry at arc 70 is visual only. Fourteen pines and five hill domes must sit at least 22 m from the centerline, placed with a mulberry32 seed from [TRACK], never Math.random. Forty grass tufts may sit in the runoff. The grandstand, hut, gantry, trees, hills, and grass have no collision. Only bales, tires, cones, rivals, and the asphalt edge affect the car.
   Motion: The world does not rotate on its own. Start lamps are Ink housings, all Danger red during 3, 2, and 1, then #d6e38a for 1.5 seconds on GO, then dark. Reduced motion switches the lamp color without a glow pulse. Shadow casters are the car, gates, hut, and grandstand.
3. **Player car and arcade driving** — Build one closed-wheel wedge and clone it. It is not an open-wheel car and not a known road car: blunt two-step nose, short cabin, small lip spoiler, rectangular lamps, no giant wing, no sponsor livery. Local +z is the nose. Parent the mesh under the yaw pivot so that when yaw is 0 the nose points along world +x. Sill box 4.20 × 0.28 × 1.78 at y 0.36. Nose box 1.35 × 0.32 × 1.62 at z 1.25, y 0.55. Nose tip 0.70 × 0.18 × 1.40 at z 1.85, y 0.48. Tail box 0.85 × 0.36 × 1.70 at z -1.55, y 0.62. Cabin 1.45 × 0.55 × 1.28 at z -0.15, y 1.02, color #241c18. Windshield 0.08 × 0.42 × 1.18, rotated -32 degrees on x, at z 0.55, y 1.08, color #b7d4d8, opacity 0.55. Two spoiler uprights and a blade 1.40 m wide at y 1.12. Four arch boxes over the wheels. Wheels are 10-segment cylinders, radius 0.36, at x ±0.92 and z ±1.28. Hubs #e8d7b0. Headlights emissive #fff1cc. Taillights emissive #d8452f. Player body #c4512c, door number 14 on a 256×128 canvas. MeshStandardMaterial roughness 0.62 and metalness 0.08. Rivals recolor only the body panels.
   Layout: Simulate on the x,z plane. Speed is along yaw plus a drift slip angle. Each frame, project the car onto the nearest centerline sample, searching only ±40 samples from the last hit so the match cannot jump. Set height from the bank at that lateral offset and roll the mesh to the bank. Camera is a perspective chase: FOV 68, or 76 under 700 px, 7.2 m behind and 2.6 m up, looking 6 m ahead. Under 700 px use 9.2 m behind and 4.0 m up so the bottom sheet and the thumbs do not cover the car. Speed cap is 42 m/s, or 54 m/s while a boost is active. Acceleration is 16 m/s², brake is 28 m/s², and reverse caps at 6 m/s. With no throttle, coast at 3 m/s². Do not add quadratic drag, or the car cannot catch the rivals. Yaw rate eases from 1.6 rad/s at rest to 0.5 rad/s at top speed. Keyboard steer is digital and rate-limited at 3.5 units per second. The steer pad is analog.
   Motion: Fixed step 1/60, accumulator capped at 0.1 s. Wheels spin with speed and the front wheels steer up to 18 degrees. Drift when throttle is held, speed exceeds 18 m/s, and either the drift control is held or the brake is held with steer past 0.4, and a boost is not active. Slip angle moves toward steer times 16 degrees. The boost meter fills 0.40 per second while drifting and falls 0.15 per second otherwise. At 1 it stays armed. Shift, or releasing drift while armed, spends it: 1.2 s at the boost speed, then the meter is 0. Dust specks spawn only while drifting. Reduced motion skips dust and the FOV kick. Camera smoothing is 1 - exp(-8 dt) on position.
4. **Rivals, ghost, gates, and heat rules** — Three rivals use the same mesh on rails. Order in [RIVALS] maps to teal number 7, gold number 22, and dusk blue number 3. Spawn arcs and laterals: first 52 m and -2.4 m, second 36 m and +2.6 m, third 8 m and +0.4 m. The player spawns at arc 20 m, lateral 0, so the grid is first, second, player, third. Rival target speeds in m/s: 34 from 0–180, 27 from 180–460, 32 from 460–700, 18 from 700–860, and 34 from 860–1000, with acceleration limited to 10 m/s². They do not drift or boost. A rival completes a lap when its arc wraps forward past 0, and the first wrap counts as lap 1. Progress for every car is completed laps times 1000 plus current arc. That order drives the HUD place and the podium. If a rival overlaps the player inside 2.4 m, separate them and multiply both speeds by 0.92. The rival then returns to its lateral at 4 m/s. Obstacle hits multiply speed by 0.72, shove lateral 1.2 m away from the obstacle, and never leave speed under 7 m/s. Cooldown 0.45 s.
   Layout: Off asphalt means absolute lateral greater than 8 m: cap speed at 26 m/s, cut acceleration to 0.45, and show Back on the asphalt. If that lasts 1.2 s, or absolute lateral exceeds 20 m, reset to the last cleared gate at lateral 0, heading along the tangent, speed 16 m/s. The last cleared gate starts at arc 0, then updates to 250, 500, and 750 as those gates are passed in order. Show Reset for 0.8 s and ignore obstacle hits during it. Do not clear lap flags and do not end the heat. Gates must be taken forward, in order: 250, then 500, then 750, then arc 0. Ignore arc 0 until the player has reached arc 40 once. Crossing arc 0 with the three flags set completes a lap, stores the lap time, and clears the flags. HUD lap reads completed + 1, clamped to 3. The heat ends when the player completes 3 laps. Each car stores its own finish clock. Unfinished rivals sort behind finishers by progress around the lap. Wrong way, when the heading opposes the tangent for 0.4 s, shows Wrong way and does not reset the car.
   Motion: Record the player's world position and yaw every 100 ms during a lap, capped at 800 samples. If that lap is the best, keep it and write localStorage key [brand-slug]::[track-slug]::best-lap. Replay it as a Coral clone at opacity 0.32, emissive Sun at 0.15, depthWrite false, no shadow and no collision. Restart the ghost each time the player begins a lap. The first visit with no save shows no ghost. Clear ghost deletes the key and hides the clone. Cars stay still through the countdown. On GO the clock starts and input is live. Pause, the hidden document, and the podium freeze the clock and the step.

## Don'ts
- No backend, accounts, database, analytics, external APIs, multiplayer, or a global leaderboard. The best lap stays in localStorage under a key built from the brand and the track.
- No CDN and no remote assets. Self-host fonts, import three from the installed package, use relative paths, and emit minified files to dist/ with source maps off and without copying src/ into dist/. Keep dist under 20 MB.
- No routes, marketing sections, prices, or a scroll landing page. The only page is index.html, and the first screen is the parked car on the circuit.
- Do not copy a source prompt, one-shot HTML page, filmed run, judge note, or playtest reading. Do not show a speed of 105 or a score of 000749. Do not mention Agent OS, GoldieBench, AI Profit Boardroom, Grok, xAI, or Julian Goldie.
- Do not reuse a source car silhouette, circuit layout, gate design, or obstacle placement. Use the control points, gates, bales, tires, and cones specified here. Do not turn the heat into a night city, a hover vehicle, or a brick-breaking game.
- No real racing series, manufacturers, circuits, liveries, sponsor marks, or real drivers. No celebrity or public-figure likeness, no faces on the spectators, and no brand logos. The start stripe is a generic ink-and-paper band, not a series identity.
- No downloaded models, HDRIs, texture packs, stock photos, placeholder-image services, physics engines, or postprocessing bloom. Geometry and the sky stay in code.
- No commercial fonts and no font CDN. Do not hard-code Pennyglass, Wickpenny, Barleycut, Skiff 4, Nell Harp, or the default rivals outside src/content.ts, and do not leave raw variable tokens on screen.
- Do not invent dates, entry fees, online player counts, or any claim about VibeFather. The only VibeFather presence is the optional [CREDIT] link.
- No autoplay audio, camera shake, full-screen flashing, or a permanent stall. Off-track play slows the car and then resets it to the last gate. Honor prefers-reduced-motion without disabling the race.
- Do not let the clock run while paused, hidden, or on the podium. Clear stuck inputs when focus is lost. At 390×844 the steer pad, pedals, minimap, and start sheet must all be usable with no horizontal overflow and no console errors.
- If WebGL fails, show the readable fallback sentence and do not throw in a loop. If frames run long, lower the pixel ratio and the shadow map once, and leave the lap rules, rivals, ghost, and controls in place.
