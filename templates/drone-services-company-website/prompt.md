# Drone Services Company Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A one-page, crisp white services site for [BRAND], an aerial drone services company flying [SERVICE_REGION]. The hero is a full-bleed top-down aerial photograph with white typography and mono telemetry chips, drifting on subtle parallax. Then: three service cards with photos and deliverable chips (inspection, mapping and survey, filming and progress); the before/after mapping slider — one scene, a draggable divider, the processed side drawn live in canvas (survey grid, contours, false-color tint, orange waypoints); a three-step flight-day strip; an industries row with four proof lines; a six-photo gallery with mono captions; and a client-side quote form. All local, all static.

- Audience: [AUDIENCE] who need measurable aerial data or footage, plus drone operators, surveyors and field-services businesses adapting the template for their own book.
- Goal: Prove the quality of the work in one scroll — hero photo, deliverable chips, the interactive map slider and the gallery — and convert project briefs through the quote form.
- Tone: Plain-spoken and measurable. Short sentences, surveyor vocabulary (GSD, orthomosaic, contour), concrete invented numbers. No adventure-sport language, no regulation talk, no hype.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Brand name (default: "Ternbay"). Invented company name; the site brands itself '[BRAND] Aerial'. Never a real drone maker, operator or regulator.
- [SERVICE_REGION] — Service region (default: "the Pacific Northwest"). Where the crew flies. Used in the hero subhead, industries copy and footer; project locations in the gallery echo it.
- [AUDIENCE] — Client type (default: "civil engineering firms, county surveyors and film crews"). Plural noun phrase for who hires the company; drives the hero, industries proof lines and quote-form copy.
- [DAY_RATE] — Starting day rate (default: "$640"). Sample starting price shown as 'from [DAY_RATE] per flight day' on service cards and the quote form. Invented sample, any currency figure with a symbol.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/drone-services-company-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Typography
- Display: H1 clamp(40px, 5vw, 68px) line-height 1.04 letter-spacing -0.02em weight 700; H2 clamp(30px, 3.6vw, 46px) weight 700; card titles 22px weight 600: Space Grotesk Variable (OFL), weights 500/600/700
- Text: body 16px/1.6, subheads 17-18px, nav 14px, buttons 15px weight 600: Archivo Variable (OFL), weights 400/500/600
- Mono: telemetry chips, deliverable chips, section labels (11-12px, letter-spacing 0.12em, uppercase), gallery captions, form labels: IBM Plex Mono (OFL), weights 400/500

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base. Container max-width 1200px with 24px side padding (20px below 640px). Sections 104px vertical padding on desktop, 72px on mobile. Section headers 48px below. Cards pad 24px; the slider frame is edge-to-edge within the container.
- Radius: Cards and photo cards 14px; chips 6px; inputs 8px; the slider frame 16px; primary and ghost buttons are full pills (999px); gallery tiles 10px.
- Motion: Hero parallax: the background photo translates 0 -> 8% of its height over the first 120vh of scroll (rAF, transform only), telemetry chips fade from 1 -> 0.7; disabled under reduced motion.
- Motion: Map slider: pointer or keyboard drag with the position clamped 0-100; a one-time hint sweep (50 -> 62 -> 40 -> 50 over 1.6s) when first 50% in view and not yet interacted; disabled under reduced motion.
- Motion: Scroll reveals: fade up 20px, 0.6s, ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1], once, stagger 0.06-0.1s.
- Motion: Card hovers: photo zooms 1 -> 1.04 (400ms), card lifts 3px; gallery captions underline in signal orange.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: no parallax, no hint sweep, no zooms; reveals render in place; the slider still drags (it is direct manipulation).
- Imagery: Six generated aerial photographs that read as one operator's book: every image is a straight-down (nadir) view from about 120 meters in clear mid-morning light with crisp aligned shadows and a cool natural grade — estuary, solar array, striped farmland, forest-meets-lake, quarry terraces, small harbor. No people, no vehicles with readable plates, no signage, no readable text anywhere in the frames. The white UI sits over them; the survey overlay in the slider is code-drawn, not photographic.
