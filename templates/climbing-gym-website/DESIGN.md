# Climbing Gym Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Chalk** `#F2F1EC` — Page ground everywhere — light warm-gray chalk white.
- **Concrete** `#E4E2DB` — Raised surfaces: comparison panel, form panel, wall-map ground.
- **Chalk line** `#D6D3C9` — Hairlines, table rules, borders, chart cell strokes.
- **Ink** `#23252A` — Primary text — slate near-black for Anton headlines and body.
- **Slate** `#6B6E76` — Secondary text: descriptions, meta, sample notes.
- **Neon magenta** `#E61C7D` — The single accent: grade chips, busy heat, CTAs, active filters, event tags — never decorative.
- **Neon deep** `#C41366` — Pressed and hover states on neon.

## Typography
- **Display — gym name, headlines, wall names, big numbers (heavy condensed, uppercase, tight leading)** — Anton (OFL), weights 400. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body, chips, table, form labels (labels uppercase tracked 0.1em)** — Public Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Athletic and efficient: sections py-24 (96px) desktop / py-16 mobile; content max-w-6xl px-6/px-10; hairlines instead of boxes; the wall map gets full content width and 80px of clear space around it.
- Radius: Hard corners to match the type: 0px on images and panels, 4px on chips and inputs, pills only on the two CTAs.

## Motion
- Snappy reveals: sections, cards and rows fade up (y 16→0, 400ms ease-out) with 60ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once — the sport's tempo, not a spa's.
- Grade filter: non-matching route clusters dim to 15% opacity with grayscale and their chips hollow out (200ms); matching clusters stay in full tape color with neon grade chips.
- Busy chart: cells fill from chalk to neon alpha on first reveal with a 30ms per-cell stagger (fast wave, ~1.3s total); hover shows the sample tooltip.
- Hero: the wall photo zooms 1.04→1 over 1.5s; the Anton headline snaps up 120ms later; the stats row slides in from the left.
- Chips toggle in 150ms; the intro form's saved note pops (150ms); pass rows highlight neon on hover of their column.
- prefers-reduced-motion: no zoom, no chart wave (cells render final instantly), all reveals become 150ms opacity fades.

## Imagery
One gym, one light: indoor climbing photography with bright even gym light and cool daylight from high windows, fine chalk dust drifting in the air, colorful holds on light gray walls, high-contrast energetic framing. Climbers appear only from behind or as silhouettes with faces not identifiable — small on walls, backs to the camera. No readable text anywhere in frame (no banners, boards or route tags with writing), no logos, no brand-identifiable gear marks. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
Direct, dry, a little stoked — gym-beta energy: short lines, concrete numbers, zero fitness-cliché filler.
