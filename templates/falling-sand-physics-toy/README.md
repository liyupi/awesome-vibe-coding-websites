A tactile browser cellular automata sandbox built on a fast typed-array 60fps simulation engine. Paint across a dark slate canvas (#15171C) using 16 vivid, reactive elements—sand, water, fire, smoke, plant, lava, ice, acid, oil, wood, steam, stone, seed, cloner, wind, and eraser—and watch real physics and chemistry unfold: water extinguishes fire, lava meets water to forge stone and billowing steam, fire spreads through wood and oil, seeds sprout into burnable foliage, acid dissolves everything except stone, ice melts near heat, and cloners continuously replicate neighboring grains. Features brush size and shape (circle, square, spray), Play/Pause and Single-Step frame control, a live Temperature Heatmap view, three animated starter scenes (Volcano, Hourglass, Rain Garden), compact URL hash sharing, and effortless touch painting.

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add falling-sand-physics-toy` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A science teacher: set BRAND to your class name, DEFAULT_SCENE to rain-garden, and use the Chemical Reactions legend to walk students through water, fire and acid reactions.
- A creative coding workshop: keep the default Volcano scene and have students edit the material colours and brush sizes in the content file to build their own palette.
- A museum exhibit kiosk: set BRAND to the exhibit title, TAGLINE to a one-line instruction, and open on Hourglass so visitors see motion before touching the screen.
- A game studio prototype: use the Cloner and Wind elements to test particle behaviour, then share scenes by URL with teammates for feedback on the simulation.

## FAQ

### What elements and reactions does the Falling Sand Physics Toy include?
The Falling Sand Physics Toy includes 16 materials: Sand, Water, Fire, Smoke, Plant, Lava, Ice, Acid, Oil, Wood, Steam, Stone, Seed, Cloner, Wind and Eraser. They react to each other: water puts out fire and turns to steam, lava meeting water forges stone and steam, fire spreads through wood, oil and plants, seeds sprout into plants when watered, acid dissolves everything except stone and cloner, ice melts near heat, cloners duplicate neighbouring grains and wind blows airborne grains.

### Is the Falling Sand Physics Toy free to use?
The Falling Sand Physics Toy is a free template. Its prompt and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0. Commercial or client use needs a VibeFather Pro license, which also unlocks the full prompt and the source code of the demos. The page shows the brief, stack and variables publicly either way.

### How do I share a scene I made in the Falling Sand Physics Toy?
The Falling Sand Physics Toy has a Share URL button in the light instrument header that copies a compact URL hash of your current canvas. Anyone who opens that link sees the same arrangement of elements. You can also pick one of the three starter scenes, Volcano, Hourglass or Rain Garden, from the scene switcher, and the template opens on whichever scene you set as DEFAULT_SCENE.

### Can I change the brand name and starting scene in the Falling Sand Physics Toy?
Yes. The Falling Sand Physics Toy is driven by three variables: BRAND for the app title, TAGLINE for the short subtitle in the top toolbar, and DEFAULT_SCENE for which of the three starter scenes loads first. Set DEFAULT_SCENE to volcano, hourglass or rain-garden, then rewrite the headlines and labels in the content file to match your new name and tone.

### How do I add a wine club signup to a winery website built with AI?
The Falling Sand Physics Toy template does not include a wine club form, so you would add one as a new section near the footer. Keep the light porcelain shell and dark slate canvas, place a simple email field and submit button below the simulation, and link it to your mailing list provider. The template has no backend, so the form needs an external signup service.

### How do I build a falling sand physics toy with AI?

Falling Sand Physics Toy is a single-page site built with React and Tailwind CSS, with generative / canvas and micro-interactions. The prompt fixes the stack so every AI tool builds the same kind of project, and the result runs as a static site you can host anywhere.

The Falling Sand Physics Toy prompt and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial projects under CC BY-NC 4.0. Client work and commercial use need VibeFather Pro, which also includes the source code of the live demo.

### Does the Falling Sand Physics Toy template work on mobile?
Yes. Every Falling Sand Physics Toy build was tested on a phone-sized screen as well as desktop before it went live, checking layout, text size and touch targets. Open the live demo on your phone, or switch the preview on this page to mobile view, to check it yourself.
