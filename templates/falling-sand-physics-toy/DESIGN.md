# Falling Sand Physics Toy — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Dark Slate Canvas** `#15171C` — High-contrast cellular simulation chamber
- **Studio Porcelain** `#F6F7F9` — Crisp light page shell and toolbar surface
- **Lab Ink** `#15171C` — High-contrast typography and active tool outlines
- **Molten Lava** `#FF4D1A` — Vivid lava, fire, and thermal highlights
- **Alpine Water** `#3B9EFF` — Fluid water streams and hydro reactions
- **Neon Acid** `#B8FF2E` — Corrosive acid pixels and active reaction badges

## Typography
- **Brand Wordmark & Scene Titles** — Space Grotesk (OFL), weights 600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Material Labels & Tooltips** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Particle Counter, FPS & Brush Size** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Crisp light top instrument bar, full-width framed dark slate simulation canvas in the center of the viewport, and ergonomic 16-material palette + brush/scene controls fitting cleanly on 1440×900 and 390×844.
- Radius: 14px dark simulation frame with crisp 1px slate border; tactile pill swatches for all 16 elements.

## Motion
- Continuous 60fps emergent particle physics already flowing on frame one.
- Smooth interpolated stroke painting between pointer events so fast strokes draw solid streams without gaps.
- Instant Temperature Heatmap color-ramp visualization toggle showing molten hot spots vs icy blues.
- Under prefers-reduced-motion, slow simulation step rate gently while keeping Single-Step and full painting responsive.

## Imagery
100% live pixel-crisp HTML5 Canvas ImageData cellular automata art with vibrant per-grain color micro-variation.

## Tone
Tactile science-lab minimalism—crisp light instrument bar contrasting vivid glowing elemental pixels.
