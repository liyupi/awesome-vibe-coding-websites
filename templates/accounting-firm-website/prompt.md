# Accounting Firm Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [FIRM], an accounting firm in [CITY] focused on [FOCUS], individual returns from $[BASE_FEE]. It must be the calmest money site on the block: services with plain deliverables, a deadline ledger (recurring month-labeled dates, clearly generic), a fee estimator (filer type × complexity × forms → sample range with assumptions), four accountants, a trust strip, and a consultation booking form confirming client-side. Pages: index.html, booking.html. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is submitted anywhere.

- Audience: Individuals with one scary return and small businesses with a shoebox of receipts. They fear jargon and surprise invoices; they want a range, a deadline reminder and a human who says 'bring the shoebox, we've seen worse'.
- Goal: Book the consultation. Within ten seconds a visitor sees the plain-English promise and the next deadline; within two minutes they've tried the estimator and picked a date.
- Tone: Plain, patient, quietly funny about receipts. Concrete deliverables and ranges, zero percent jargon without translation, one joke about the shoebox.

## Variables
- [FIRM] — Firm name (default: "Rowan & Fig"). Invented firm name. Used in the wordmark, title, footer, email (hello@ + firmSlug without hyphens + .com) and localStorage keys. Never a real firm or franchise.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Raleigh, North Carolina"). Where the firm is. Used in the hero, deadlines' context and the footer. Real cities fine; street invented.
- [BASE_FEE] — Base fee (default: "220"). The individual simple-return starting fee as digits. Every estimator range derives from it in src/lib/estimate.ts. Keep it 150–400.
- [FOCUS] — Focus (default: "tax filing and small-business books"). One lowercase phrase naming the practice's core. Drives the hero line, the services columns' balance and one trust line.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/accounting-firm-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the estimator is pure client math in src/lib/estimate.ts with assumptions displayed; the booking saves in the browser only and says so.
- Every internal link points to a file name (./index.html, ./index.html#services, ./index.html#deadlines, ./index.html#fees, ./index.html#team, ./booking.html) — never to a bare directory.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 8 MB.
- No real firms, credentials or professional bodies (generic 'licensed' language, invented team); deadlines are labeled recurring/generic with a confirm-with-authority note; no photos with people, faces, hands or readable forms.
- No dates or years anywhere; the deadline ledger shows recurring month-labeled dates, never this year's actual calendar; the site never shows today's date.
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: no pulses, counts or stamps (instant values); color/border hovers stay.
- The site must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; chips, selects and form work with keyboard only and visible focus rings.
- Keep copy plain: no lorem, no 'synergistic financial solutions'; every fee, deadline and line lives in src/content.ts.
