A single-page website for a management consulting firm: a positioning hero over a war-room session photograph; five numbered practice areas (strategy, operations, pricing, org, due diligence); an insights article grid of six posts with read-times (date-free); three anonymized case examples with sample outcome figures; four leadership partners with AI-generated portraits (fictional people users swap for their own team photos); a trust strip with invented sectors and sample stats; and an engagement request form confirming client-side. The default content follows an invented six-person firm, but the structure suits any boutique consultancy. The look is boardroom modern: ink navy and off-white, a steel-blue accent, sharp serif headlines over a businesslike sans, hairline rules and generous whitespace. Case outcomes carry (sample data) labels and clients stay anonymized to sector. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## Make it yours

- A two-partner strategy boutique: set FIRM and CITY, cut the practice areas to three, and rewrite the hero around the decisions you take on.
- An operations consultancy: set SPECIALTY to operations, lead the practice cards with process and supply chain, and swap cases for throughput and cost examples.
- A pricing advisory: make pricing the first practice card, point the insights grid at pricing posts, and set the engagement form areas to match.
- An independent consultant: use FIRM as your own name, keep two partners, and rewrite the trust strip around the sectors you have actually served.

## FAQ

### What sections and content does the Management Consulting Firm Website template include?
The Management Consulting Firm Website template is one page with five numbered practice areas, an insights grid of six posts with read-times, three anonymized cases with sample outcome figures, four partner portraits, a trust strip with invented sectors and sample stats, and an engagement request form that confirms in the browser. The default content follows an invented six-person firm, and clients stay anonymized to sector.

### How do I change the firm name, city and specialty in the Management Consulting Firm Website template?
The Management Consulting Firm Website template runs on four variables: FIRM, CITY, SPECIALTY and ENGAGEMENTS. Set FIRM to your firm name, CITY to where you are based, SPECIALTY to the two words at the center of your practice, and ENGAGEMENTS to your sample figure between 60 and 300. All copy lives in one content file, so you rewrite headlines, cases and partner bios there without touching the layout.

### Are the case studies and partner portraits in the Management Consulting Firm Website template real?
No. The case outcomes in the Management Consulting Firm Website template are sample figures carrying (sample data) labels, and the clients are anonymized to sector. The four partner portraits are AI-generated fictional people. Before publishing for a real firm, replace the portraits with your own team photos and swap the sample cases for your own anonymized engagements, keeping the labels honest.

### Does the engagement form on the Management Consulting Firm Website template actually send anything?
The engagement request form on the Management Consulting Firm Website template confirms client-side only. Selecting a practice area, filling in company, name, email and a note, then submitting swaps the form for a RECEIVED memo-stamp confirmation reading 'Thank you, [NAME].' Nothing is submitted anywhere and no backend exists, so you connect the form to your own email or scheduling tool before going live.

### How do I adapt the Management Consulting Firm Website template for my own consultancy?
Start with the four variables, then rewrite the copy in the single content file: the positioning hero, the five practice areas, the six insights posts, the three cases and the four partner one-liners. Keep the partner voice short and concrete, keep sample labels on any figures, and replace the AI-generated portraits and stock photography with images of your actual team and offices.
