# Restaurant QR Menu Page — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Paper** `#FAF3E7` — Page ground — warm cream paper, the whole menu.
- **Paper deep** `#F1E6D2` — Sticky tab bar ground, hover tints, desktop page ground outside the column.
- **Linen** `#FFFDF8` — Cards, bottom sheets, photo caption card — near-white linen.
- **Bench line** `#E3D5BC` — Hairlines, borders, dotted price leaders, chip outlines.
- **Walnut** `#4A3628` — Primary ink — dish names, prices, headings, active tab fill; warm dark brown, never black.
- **Walnut soft** `#7A6753` — Secondary text: descriptions, captions, notes.
- **Terracotta** `#C05B3C` — The one accent: add buttons, active filter chips, CTA pills, the signature label, call-waiter outline.
- **Terracotta deep** `#A34830` — Pressed states on terracotta.

## Typography
- **Display — wordmark, dish names, sheet titles (warm serif, italic accents)** — Lora (OFL), weights 500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — descriptions, chips, buttons, notes (friendly rounded sans)** — Asap (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Menu-tight, thumb-friendly: one 480px column; 16px page padding; category blocks py-24px with a Lora heading and a benchline rule; rows 16px tall padding with 12px gaps; every tap target ≥ 44px; the tally bar reserves 72px so the last dish never hides behind it.
- Radius: Friendly and round: 999px chips, 14px cards, 12px photo thumbs, 20px sheet tops; the dotted price leaders are the only straight lines.

## Motion
- Bottom sheets slide up translateY 100%→0 over 320ms ease-out with a linen backdrop fade (250ms); closing reverses in 240ms.
- Sticky tabs: the active chip fills walnut with paper text (200ms); the tab row auto-scrolls to keep it centered; a 2px terracotta underline slides between tabs (240ms).
- Allergen filters: non-matching rows animate to 35% opacity with grayscale(0.5) and their add buttons disable (250ms); matching rows stay full color.
- Add-button feedback: the terracotta plus scales 0.9→1 (180ms) and the tally bar's count pops once; the bar total slides up 80ms after a change.
- Call-waiter state: the outline pill cross-fades to a walnut confirmation card with a bell icon (250ms) — a screen state, never a network claim.
- Reveals: category blocks fade up gently (y 14→0, 500ms, 60ms stagger) via Motion whileInView, once.
- prefers-reduced-motion: sheets fade in place without sliding, pops and underlines become instant, reveals become 150ms opacity fades.

## Imagery
One bistro session: appetizing overhead (top-down) food photography on ceramic plates at a warm walnut table, every shot in the same warm pendant bistro light pooling from the upper left, soft shadows, cream-and-walnut palette with terracotta touches, gentle steam and gloss, honest portions — six dishes only; every other dish is a text row with a dotted leader, like a paper menu. No readable text in frame (no menus, no labels), no logos, no people. The photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
Warm, plain-spoken and a little homely — the voice of a chalkboard, not an app; short dish lines, honest sample-copy notes.
