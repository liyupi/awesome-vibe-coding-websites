# Startup Pitch Deck Website <sup>PRO</sup>

> A 12-slide investor deck as a website: keyboard and swipe slides, live charts, presenter notes, PDF print.

This is a twelve-slide investor pitch built as a website instead of a slide file. Each slide fills the screen, and you move through the deck with the arrow keys, space, the mouse wheel, a swipe on a phone or the dot rail on the right edge. Every slide has its own link, so you can send an investor straight to the traction or market slide. The slides follow the usual seed order: title, problem, solution, product, market size, business model, traction, competition, go-to-market, team, financials and the ask, then a closing contact slide. The look is an editorial dark deck: near-black slides, warm off-white type, one electric blue accent, large serif numerals, thin rules and open space. Charts are drawn in plain SVG and play when their slide opens. The market circles grow one inside the next, the revenue line draws itself, bars rise and headline numbers count up. A thin progress bar and a slide counter move with you, and pressing O opens a grid of all twelve slides. Press P and a presenter window opens with the current and next slide, speaker notes, a timer you can pause and reset, and a clock. It stays in step with the audience window with no server. Printing gives one landscape slide per page on a light background with every chart in its final state, ready to save as a PDF for a data room. It suits founders preparing a seed or pre-seed round, accelerator cohorts and developer advocates who give talks. To make it yours, change the brand, product, audience, round size and contact email, then rewrite the slide copy, chart data and speaker notes in the single content file. The default company is an invented freight-audit startup, and every figure is labeled as illustrative sample data.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Startup name | Ladingly |
| `[PRODUCT]` | One-line product description | Freight invoice auditing that catches carrier overbilling before you pay |
| `[AUDIENCE]` | Target customers | mid-market shippers spending $2M to $50M a year on freight |
| `[ROUND]` | Round being raised | $3.2M seed |
| `[CONTACT_EMAIL]` | Founder contact email | founders@ladingly.example |

## Make it yours

- An accelerator cohort: set BRAND and ROUND to each team, then rewrite the twelve slides and speaker notes so every founder presents from the same deck structure.
- A pre-seed climate startup: swap PRODUCT for your carbon or water technology and rebuild the market and traction charts around your own illustrative figures.
- A developer advocate giving conference talks: keep the slide order but replace financials and the ask with a roadmap slide and your contact email.
- An indie hacker raising a friends-and-family round: set a smaller ROUND, trim the competition slide and point investors to the closing slide's mailto link.

## FAQ

### How do I move through the slides and can I send an investor straight to one slide?
The Startup Pitch Deck Website moves with the arrow keys, space, Page Up and Page Down, the mouse wheel, a swipe on a phone or the dot rail on the right edge. Each of the twelve slides has its own link, so you can send an investor straight to the traction or market slide instead of the title.

### How does the presenter view work when I pitch live on a second screen?
Pressing P in the Startup Pitch Deck Website opens a presenter window showing the current and next slide, your speaker notes, a timer you can pause and reset, and a clock. It stays in step with the audience window without a server, so you can put it on your laptop and project the deck from the same machine.

### Can I print the Startup Pitch Deck Website as a PDF for a data room?
Printing the Startup Pitch Deck Website gives one landscape slide per page on a light background, with every chart in its final state, ready to save as a PDF. That makes the same deck you present usable as a leave-behind document for investors after the meeting.

### How do I turn the freight audit sample into my own startup's pitch?
Change the brand, product, audience, round size and contact email, then rewrite the slide copy, chart data and speaker notes in the single content file. The default company is an invented freight-audit startup and every figure is labeled as illustrative sample data, so replace those numbers with your own before sending the link.

### What charts and animations does the Startup Pitch Deck Website include?
Charts are drawn in plain SVG and play when their slide opens: the market circles grow one inside the next, the revenue line draws itself, bars rise and headline numbers count up. A thin progress bar and slide counter move with you, and pressing O opens a grid of all twelve slides for a quick overview.
