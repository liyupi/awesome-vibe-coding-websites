# University Admissions Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build a confident one-page admissions site for the invented [BRAND] in [LOCATION], aimed at [AUDIENCE]. The university's identity is a strong crest-free wordmark set in an editorial serif — no seals, crests, shields, mascots, or logo-like marks anywhere. The page anchors on a golden-hour campus quad photograph, then delivers a program finder with level, area, and format filters over eleven invented programs, a campus-life photo strip, an apply-review-decide timeline with round deadline labels, a two-slider cost-and-aid estimator with clearly invented sample figures, and a visit-day sign-up saved locally.

- Audience: [AUDIENCE] — prospective students and the people who help them decide — plus the admissions and marketing teams adapting this structure for a college, conservatory, or training institute.
- Goal: Take a visitor from the quad photograph to a shortlisted program and a held visit-day slot: filter programs to their interests in one interaction, make cost legible with a transparent sample estimator, and leave a visit request saved in the browser with no dead ends.
- Tone: Warm, assured, and specific — the voice of a place that knows its worth without boasting. Editorial headlines, plain supporting sentences, believable invented numbers. Never pompous, never a sales pitch, never a real institution.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — University name (default: "Stonewater University"). Invented institution name; the crest-free wordmark and storage keys derive from it.
- [LOCATION] — Location (default: "Millhaven, Vermont"). Town and state the campus sits in; used in copy, address, and alt text.
- [AUDIENCE] — Audience (default: "first-year, transfer, and graduate applicants"). Who the admissions page speaks to.
- [SIGNATURE_STRENGTH] — Signature strength (default: "freshwater science and sustainability studies"). What the university is known for; shapes hero copy, program lines, and campus captions.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/university-admissions-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Design
### Colors
- Collegiate blue `#1E3A5C` — Primary: buttons, hero scrim, footer band
- Ink navy `#14273E` — Headings and body text
- Warm stone `#F5F0E5` — Page ground and section bands
- Deep stone `#E7DECC` — Rails, chips, and hairline borders
- Parchment `#FDFBF5` — Cards and panels on stone
- Brass `#B08A4F` — Accent: rules, active filters, timeline nodes, slider thumbs
- Slate blue `#4A5871` — Secondary text and meta rows

### Typography
- Display: headlines, wordmark, program names, net-cost figure: Fraunces (OFL), weights 600/700
- Body: paragraphs, filters, meta, forms: Source Sans 3 (OFL), weights 400/600/700

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base grid. Content shell max-width 1200px with 32px desktop and 20px mobile side padding. Header 84px. Desktop sections 104px vertical padding, mobile 64px. Program grid gap 20px. Prose max 64 characters per line.
- Radius: Editorial and nearly square: 2px on cards, inputs, and photos; 999px full pills only for filter chips and level tags. Photos sit on a 1px deep-stone border with an 8px brass top rule on hover or featured cards.
- Motion: Hero: the quad photograph settles from scale 1.06 to 1 over 900ms under a navy scrim that fades from 55% to its resting gradient; headline lines rise 18px with a 90ms stagger.
- Motion: Program finder: filtered cards exit and enter with 200ms fade-and-slide via AnimatePresence; the results count crossfades 160ms; the Bookmark toggle pops to 1.12 and fills brass over 180ms.
- Motion: Estimator: the net-cost number tweens over 320ms while the stacked cost bar re-partitions with 320ms width transitions; slider thumbs carry a 2px brass focus ring.
- Motion: Sections reveal once on scroll: 16px rise with a 480ms ease-out fade, sibling cards staggered 60ms.
- Motion: With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, every transition above becomes a 100ms opacity change. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no custom cursor, no autoplay media.
- Imagery: One photographic set, one golden-hour shoot: campus stone architecture, a riverwalk, studios, and green quads in a single warm grade — honeyed highlights, long shadows, soft haze, deep blue shadows, 35mm lens. Students appear only distant and from behind; no identifiable faces, no readable signage, no real landmarks. The wordmark, filter UI, timeline, and estimator are code-drawn; no crest, seal, or emblem is ever drawn.

## Don'ts
- No real universities, colleges, mascots, seals, crests, coats of arms, shields, monograms, or logo-like marks anywhere. The identity is the typographic wordmark derived from [BRAND] only.
- Photos must show architecture, landscapes, and student life from behind or at a distance — no identifiable faces, no readable signage, no real campuses or landmarks presented as this university.
- Cost figures are invented samples: implement the estimator math exactly as specified, keep every output labeled 'sample', and keep the 'not a financial aid offer' note visible next to the net figure.
- Deadlines use round labels (Early / Regular) and relative copy only — never real calendar dates, countdowns, or anything computed from the visitor's clock.
- All programs, stats, buildings, staff, and aid numbers are invented; never reference accreditation bodies, rankings, athletic conferences, or real institutions.
- Forms are local-only: no submissions, APIs, email, or calendar services anywhere; persist the shortlist and visit request to localStorage only and validate restored data.
- No backend, server code, databases, auth, or analytics. Static output in dist/ from the single build command.
- Self-host fonts through the listed Fontsource packages only; never load Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts, or any CDN stylesheet or script.
- No source maps in production, no src/ copied into dist/, keep dist/ under 20 MB, and keep Vite's base relative.
- No Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Google Images, placeholder-image services, or hotlinked files; use only the six generated photos and original code-drawn UI.
- No SEO work: no meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, sitemaps, or keyword-stuffed headings. A plain title with [BRAND] and a favicon are enough.
- A filtered program view must never dead-end: always show the empty state with a working Reset, and every internal link is a file name or in-page anchor, never a bare directory.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere; no scroll-jacking, parallax, custom cursor, or autoplay media.
- Keep all copy in US English and build brand-derived strings (wordmark, storage keys, slugs, alt text) from [BRAND] and [BRAND_SLUG] so alternate variable values never leave default-brand remnants.
- The site must render at 1440×900 and 390×844 with zero console errors.
