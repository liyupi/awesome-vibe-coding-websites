Designed as a true three-breakpoint responsive neighborhood food delivery PWA without any decorative phone bezel, this culinary marketplace ordering app delivers tactile native-style ergonomics on phones while expanding into a multi-column browse grid with a sticky cart rail on tablets and desktops. Warm cream surfaces and deep espresso ink frame appetizing saffron-orange actions and fresh basil-green delivery badges, anchored by six cohesive studio-photographed neighborhood dishes shot under consistent warm daylight. On mobile screens under 768 pixels a fixed bottom tab bar switches cleanly across Explore, Search, Orders, and Account, while tapping any dish opens a slide-up customization bottom sheet where diners choose portion size, spice level, and extra toppings with live price calculation. Shoppers can filter fictional neighborhood kitchens by cuisine chip, browse full restaurant menus, manage items in the cart drawer with a working promo code mock (WELCOME5), complete a mock checkout flow, and watch their meal progress through a live animated order tracker from Confirmed and Cooking to Courier En Route along a stylized SVG neighborhood route map. All active orders, cart items, saved addresses, and favorite kitchens persist automatically in localStorage. Customize the marketplace name, neighborhood, promo code, and delivery fee in one TypeScript data file.

## Make it yours

- A single-location taqueria: set BRAND to your shop name, NEIGHBORHOOD to your pickup area, and replace the six dish photos and cuisine chips with your own menu.
- A meal-prep subscription service: keep the customization sheet for portion size and spice level, and rewrite the seeded orders as weekly plan deliveries in your neighborhood.
- A neighborhood grocery or bakery marketplace: swap the cuisine chips for product categories, set DELIVERY_FEE to your rate, and link the cart drawer to your order intake.
- A food truck with rotating stops: use NEIGHBORHOOD for today's location, keep the live tracker for order status, and set PROMO_CODE to your loyalty offer.

## FAQ

### How does the live order tracker work in the Food Delivery Mobile App template?
The Food Delivery Mobile App template includes a mock animated order tracker that moves a meal through Confirmed, Cooking and Courier En Route along a stylized SVG neighborhood route map. Because the template has no backend or external APIs, the tracker is a front-end demo driven by seeded order data. To use it for real orders, connect the order status in your data file to your own delivery or dispatch system.

### Can I change the promo code and delivery fee in the Food Delivery Mobile App template?
Yes. The Food Delivery Mobile App template keeps PROMO_CODE and DELIVERY_FEE as editable variables in one TypeScript data file. The default promo code applies a $5 discount in the cart drawer, and the delivery fee appears on restaurant cards and in the cart summary. Change both values there and the copy across the browse grid, cart and checkout updates to match.

### Is the Food Delivery Mobile App template usable for a client project or a real restaurant marketplace?
Commercial and client use of the Food Delivery Mobile App template requires Pro, and Pro also includes the full prompt and the demo source code. Free templates' prompts and every DESIGN.md are for personal, non-commercial use only. The template itself is a front-end mock with no backend, so a real marketplace would need payment, ordering and courier systems connected separately.

### What should I change first when adapting the Food Delivery Mobile App template to my own brand?
Start with the five variables in the template's data file: set BRAND to your marketplace name, NEIGHBORHOOD to your delivery district, USER_NAME to the diner profile name, and adjust PROMO_CODE and DELIVERY_FEE. Then replace the six studio-photographed dish images and rewrite the fictional kitchens, menus and seeded orders so the browse grid, customization sheet and tracker all describe your real food offering.
