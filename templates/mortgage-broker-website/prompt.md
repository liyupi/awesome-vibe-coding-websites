# Mortgage Broker Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build a polished one-page website for [BRAND], an invented mortgage brokerage led by the fictional [BROKER] in [LOCATION], serving [AUDIENCE]. The hero contains a working fixed-rate payment calculator initialized from [HOME_PRICE], not a static mockup. Shared calculator state drives a sample rate comparison, an annual principal-versus-interest amortization chart, and a complete accessible schedule. Four loan-type cards, four pre-approval steps, a portrait-led broker profile, a three-step application-start form, and five FAQs complete the page. Every rate and financial figure is illustrative and never an offer; the application draft stays in the browser and is never transmitted.

- Audience: [AUDIENCE] who want to understand a monthly payment and prepare for a broker conversation before sharing documents. They may be comparing down payments, trying to decode loan terminology, or wondering what pre-approval involves. Secondary visitors are independent brokers who need a trustworthy educational site rather than a generic lead-capture page.
- Goal: Let a visitor explore a realistic payment scenario without a credit check, understand how principal and interest change over time, and leave with a locally saved application-start draft. Trust must come from visible assumptions and useful explanations, not claims of guaranteed approval or lowest rates.
- Tone: Warm, precise, and unhurried. Use plain US English, short sentences, and specific examples. Explain necessary terms without condescension. Never use urgency, pressure, jargon-heavy sales copy, or homeownership clichés about dreams coming true.

## Don'ts
- No backend, server code, API routes, database, authentication, external API calls, form transmission, document upload, or third-party service. Calculations run locally and the application draft stays on the device with explicit nothing-sent copy.
- Never request Social Security numbers, full bank details, credit-card data, identity documents, account passwords, or exact credit reports in this static demo. Do not claim an application was submitted, received, approved, or reviewed.
- Every rate, payment, interest total, and financial scenario must be visibly labeled as an example. Never present figures as live rates, personalized quotes, offers, guaranteed savings, eligibility decisions, pre-approvals, or commitments to lend.
- Use the exact amortization formula and zero-rate branch from Setup, preserve full precision until display, and handle invalid or extreme inputs without NaN, Infinity, negative balances, or console errors. Taxes, insurance, fees, and mortgage insurance must never be silently included.
- No real lender names, loan-product brands, lender logos, government seals, official-looking badges, certification marks, or implied institutional endorsement. Program names such as FHA, VA, and USDA may appear only as plain explanatory text.
- The broker, brokerage, testimonial-like statements, history, and phone number are invented. Never use a real person's identity or likeness; the portrait must remain a fictional generated placeholder not resembling any real individual.
