A single-page site that launches one mobile banking app: a hero anchored by a 3D CSS phone mockup playing a three-screen loop (balance, savings goals, card); a feature strip where scrolling swaps the phone's screen; an SVG card section with masked numbers that reveal on hover and a round-up visualization with animated progress rings; a plain-language security section drawn entirely in code; a drawn store-buttons row (no platform marks); a short FAQ; and a footer with invented sample disclaimers. The default content follows an invented app, but the structure suits any consumer finance or wallet app. The look is midnight mint: deep ink navy, glassy panels, one fresh mint accent, everything code-drawn — no photographs anywhere. All copy lives in one content file; swapping the variables rewrites the page.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[APP]` | App name | Fernbank |
| `[RATE]` | Savings rate | 4.6% |
| `[CARD_NAME]` | Card name | Fern Card |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add fintech-app-landing-page` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A couples budgeting app: set APP to your product name, rewrite the balance and goals screens for shared accounts, and point the waitlist at your launch list.
- A prepaid teen card: set CARD_NAME to your card, swap the security section for parental controls, and keep the card reveal as the hero proof.
- A savings and round-up startup: set RATE to your sample rate, rewrite the goal rings for your own goals, and replace the store buttons with your signup call.
- An indie founder's wallet side project: set APP and CARD_NAME, trim the FAQ to your own terms, and link the waitlist form to your mailing list.

## FAQ

### What can I change on the Fintech App Landing Page without touching the layout?
The Fintech App Landing Page template keeps every string in one content file and exposes three variables: APP sets the app name used in the wordmark, title, footer, email address and card face; RATE sets the sample savings figure shown in the hero, goals section and FAQ small print; CARD_NAME sets the debit card name on the card and in the card section. Swapping those rewrites the page copy, and the layout, colors, type and motion stay as designed.

### How does the scrolling phone mockup on the Fintech App Landing Page work?
The Fintech App Landing Page template draws a 3D CSS phone that plays a three-screen loop through balance, savings goals and card, crossfading every few seconds. In the feature strip, scrolling makes each of the four features active and swaps the phone screen to match, so the mockup follows the reader. The loop pauses on hover or focus and stops under reduced-motion settings, where the balance screen stays visible.

### Are there photographs or stock images in the Fintech App Landing Page template?
The Fintech App Landing Page template uses no photographs anywhere. Every visual is drawn in code: the phone mockup, the bank card with its masked number that reveals on hover, the savings goal rings, the round-up progress bars, the security layer stack and the store-button row. That means nothing to download or license before the first build, and the whole page stays in the midnight-mint palette.

### Where does the waitlist signup on the Fintech App Landing Page send emails?
The Fintech App Landing Page template has no backend and no external APIs, so the waitlist form saves the email locally in the browser and shows a success state rather than submitting it anywhere. To collect real signups, connect the form to your own mailing list or form service. The template's FAQ and footer also carry invented sample disclaimers you should replace with your own terms.
