Video Production Company Website is a multi-page portfolio for studios that want the work to lead and the inquiry process to feel straightforward. It suits commercial directors, small production companies, documentary teams, event filmmakers, and post-production studios. The structure moves from a full-screen reel to selected projects, services, production approach, and a detailed brief, with a separate work archive and query-driven case studies.

The visual direction is dark and editorial rather than flashy. Instrument Serif headlines sit against charcoal, bone white, slate, and a restrained ember accent. Six coordinated film stills create the hero reel, project covers, and case-study imagery. The still-image playback treatment gives the site cinematic motion without requiring hosted footage, a video service, or a heavy media bundle. Large imagery, compact production metadata, plain-text client wordmarks, and asymmetric project rows keep the portfolio feeling like a working studio rather than a generic agency page.

The interactions are useful and fully local. Visitors can filter six projects by discipline, open a keyboard-accessible reel modal, read individual project stories through stable file-based links, and prepare a structured project brief. The brief validates required fields, creates a reviewable summary, copies it to the clipboard, and offers an explicit email link without silently submitting data. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences throughout.

Brand, positioning, location, audience, specialty, and contact email are variables. Project names, services, sample prices, process details, and inquiry options live in one content file, so a second studio can replace the commercial-film examples with weddings, events, documentaries, or another suitable focus while preserving the same layout and interaction model.

## Make it yours

- A wedding film studio: set BRAND to your name, SPECIALTY to wedding films, and replace the six commercial projects with real weddings, keeping the filterable archive and brief form.
- An event film collective: point AUDIENCE at conference and festival organisers, rewrite services around same-day edits and multi-camera coverage, and keep the reel modal for highlight stills.
- A documentary production team: use the case-study pages for long-form projects, expand the credits and shoot details in each project record, and adjust the brief options toward funding and broadcast needs.
- A post-production studio: swap the project imagery for colour, sound and edit work, retitle the services around finishing and delivery, and keep the dark editorial layout for a technical audience.

## FAQ

### How does the showreel work if I don't have a video file to host?
The Video Production Company Website template builds its full-screen showreel from six coordinated film stills that crossfade, so no hosted footage, video service or heavy media bundle is required. Replace the stills in the images folder with your own frames, update the alt text in the content file, and the reel modal and project covers pick up the new imagery.

### Can I use this template for a wedding or event film company instead of a commercial studio?
Yes. The Video Production Company Website template ships with commercial-film examples, but every project, client, service, price and detail lives in one content file. Set SPECIALTY to wedding films or event coverage, rewrite the six project records with your own titles, clients, durations and shoot details, and swap the services and process steps to match. The layout and interactions stay the same.

### What does the project brief form actually do when a client fills it in?
The brief on the Video Production Company Website template runs entirely in the browser. It validates required fields, builds a reviewable summary, copies that summary to the clipboard, and offers an explicit email link to CONTACT_EMAIL. Nothing is submitted silently and no data is sent to a server, so the client always chooses whether to send the brief.

### How do I change the studio name, location and contact email across the site?
The Video Production Company Website template exposes BRAND, TAGLINE, AUDIENCE, LOCATION, SPECIALTY and CONTACT_EMAIL as variables. Set them once in the content file and they flow through the hero, studio page, footer and the brief's mailto link. If your values differ from the defaults, rewrite the headlines and paragraphs too so the copy matches your studio rather than the sample one.
