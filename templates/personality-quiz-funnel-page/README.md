Personality Quiz Funnel Page is a complete client-side quiz experience for creators, coaches, marketers, and small studios that want an engaging way to guide visitors toward a personalized recommendation. It combines an editorial landing page, eight visual multiple-choice questions, a brief calculating sequence, a deterministic archetype result, and an email-gated field guide that unlocks on screen. The static demo never transmits answers or email addresses.

The visual direction pairs warm paper tones with coral, lime, lilac, and teal accents. Fraunces headlines, compact DM Sans body copy, offset borders, tactile cards, and original geometric illustrations give the experience a distinctive editorial character. The first screen includes a fully composed quiz-card mockup rather than a decorative placeholder. During the quiz, segmented progress, keyboard-friendly answer cards, Back and Exit controls, and short transitions keep the interaction clear. Results include an original archetype emblem, a transparent answer-based pattern mix, practical recommendations, and a report reveal.

All brand language, questions, answer choices, archetype descriptions, FAQs, and report content belong in one typed content file. Change five variables to adapt the funnel to another audience or topic while retaining the same eight-question structure and four-pattern scoring model. The alternate case turns the creative-work quiz into a weekend adventure quiz without changing the design system.

This template suits lead-generation campaigns, audience engagement, downloadable guides, and lightweight recommendation tools. It is intentionally a reflective exercise, not a scientific or clinical assessment. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences, layouts adapt to mobile, and every asset is local. The included email interaction is a clearly labeled static demonstration, ready for a site owner to replace with a real service outside the demo.

- A nutrition coach: set BRAND to your practice, make QUIZ_TOPIC an eating-habits reflection, and rename REPORT_NAME to a meal-planning starter guide.
- A creative studio: keep the default creative rhythm topic, swap the archetype descriptions for your team's working styles, and point PRIMARY_CTA at your newsletter.
- A weekend travel brand: follow the adventure demo, rewrite the eight questions around trip preferences, and gate a destination shortlist as the report.
- A course creator: use the quiz as a course-fit sorter, set AUDIENCE to prospective students, and unlock a syllabus preview instead of the field guide.

## FAQ

### How do I change the quiz questions and results to fit my own topic?
The Personality Quiz Funnel Page keeps every question, answer choice, archetype description, recommendation and report section in one typed content file. To change the topic, set QUIZ_TOPIC and AUDIENCE, then rewrite the eight questions and four archetype names and descriptions to match. The eight-question structure and four-pattern scoring model stay the same, so the interaction and layout do not need to change.

### Does the email capture on the Personality Quiz Funnel Page actually collect addresses?
No. The email form in the Personality Quiz Funnel Page demo is a clearly labeled static demonstration. It does not send or store answers or email addresses, and the quiz keeps everything in memory in the browser tab. To collect real leads, replace the form with your email service and connect the report reveal to a successful signup, outside the demo.

### What are the five variables I need to set to adapt this quiz funnel?
The Personality Quiz Funnel Page uses five variables: BRAND for the wordmark and footer, QUIZ_TOPIC for the reflective subject, AUDIENCE for the lowercase audience description, REPORT_NAME for the gated resource, and PRIMARY_CTA for the hero and closing quiz-start buttons. Changing them rewrites the topic-specific copy while keeping the editorial layout, colors and motion.

### Is the personality quiz a real psychological assessment?
No. The Personality Quiz Funnel Page is a short reflective exercise, not a scientific or clinical assessment. Results are presented as flexible creative patterns with a transparent answer-based pattern mix, never as fixed identities, diagnoses or rankings. The copy is written to be warm and specific rather than making claims about personality measurement.

### Can I use the Personality Quiz Funnel Page for a lead-generation campaign?
Yes. The Personality Quiz Funnel Page is built for lead generation, audience engagement and downloadable guides. Visitors answer eight visual questions, see a personalized archetype result, and unlock the REPORT_NAME through an email form. For commercial or client campaigns you need Pro, which also includes the demo source code; the prompt and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use.
