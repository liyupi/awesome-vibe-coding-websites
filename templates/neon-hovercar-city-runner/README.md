# Neon Hovercar City Runner <sup>PRO</sup>

> Pilot a hovercar up a wet neon canyon, thread surge arches, and reach the berth.

Neon Hovercar City Runner is a one-screen arcade drive. The page opens on a night canyon: a low wedge hovercar sits on wet asphalt between glowing towers. Hover height stays fixed. The car glides, bends across the lane, can push a faster pace, and can spend a surge meter to kick through an arch. Drones cut the frame on contact. Crystals add haul. The course is a fixed 1.5 km and ends at a berth. An empty frame ends the run early. Results list haul, crystals, arches, peak pace, frame left, and time, then offer the same lane again.

Amber curbs and violet windows are the only neon pair. A dark metal road plus a streak layer reads as wet asphalt. Desktop uses a restrained bloom that phones turn off, and fog tightens on small screens. Pace and frame sit low on the left, surge along the bottom, haul and berth distance up on the right, and a vertical ribbon shows the lane ahead. Keys cover desktop. Phones get large buttons that can be held together. Reduced motion drops bloom, bob, and camera kick, and keeps steering and hazards.

Default copy is a night courier shift: Lumenlane, desk 4 on the lower quay of Amberwharf, a hovercar called the Kite Skiff, relay crystals, and a Roof Berth. Mara Quill is the dispatcher. Names live in one content file. A second set of values makes the same lane a salvage shift and leaves the mesh, colors, placements, and hit numbers unchanged. It suits developers and indie hackers who want a static Three.js game they can reskin by editing words. There is no server or downloaded model. Fonts are self-hosted, the favicon is code-drawn, and the build is minified static files with source maps off.

## FAQ

### What do I actually change to reskin the Neon Hovercar City Runner game for my own brand?
The Neon Hovercar City Runner template keeps every visible word in one content file, driven by six variables: BRAND, CITY, VEHICLE, CARGO, DESTINATION and DISPATCHER. Change those and rewrite the start card, pilot notes, signs, results sentences and flank decal so no default names remain. The mesh, palette, placements and hit numbers stay the same, so the lane plays identically.

### How do the controls work on the Neon Hovercar City Runner, and can phones play it?
The Neon Hovercar City Runner uses keys on desktop and large on-screen buttons on phones, which can be held together for steering plus surge. Pace and frame sit low on the left, surge along the bottom, haul and berth distance up on the right, and a vertical ribbon shows the lane ahead. Reduced motion drops bloom, bob and camera kick while keeping steering and hazards.

### Can I run the Neon Hovercar City Runner game without a server or downloaded models?
The Neon Hovercar City Runner template builds to minified static files with no backend, no external APIs and no downloaded 3D models. The hovercar, canyon and arches are drawn in code with Three.js. Fonts are self-hosted and the favicon is code-drawn, so you can host the output on any static host. Source maps are off in the build.

### How does a run end in the Neon Hovercar City Runner, and what does the results card show?
A run in the Neon Hovercar City Runner ends at the berth after a fixed 1.5 km course, or early if the frame meter empties. The results card lists haul, crystals, arches, peak pace, frame left and time, then offers the same lane again. Drones cut the frame on contact, wall scrapes cost you, and surge is spent to kick through arches.
