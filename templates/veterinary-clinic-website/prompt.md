# Veterinary Clinic Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [BRAND], a small-animal veterinary clinic in [CITY], reachable at [PHONE] and open since [FOUNDED]. It must feel like a good clinic smells: calm, warm, competent. Home: hero with the promise, an emergency band that is impossible to miss and impossible to panic at, six services, three vet profiles, four evergreen care tips. Visit: hours with an open-now indicator, a drawn mini-map, out-of-hours detail and the new-patient form (client-side). Pages: index.html, visit.html. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is submitted anywhere.

- Audience: Pet owners within a short drive: families with dogs and cats, the occasional rabbit, first-time adopters panicking slightly. They want services, hours, an emergency number and a vet they can picture — in that order, quickly, usually on a phone.
- Goal: Book first visits and be the number saved for emergencies. Within ten seconds a visitor sees the care promise, the hours status and the emergency line.
- Tone: Warm, plain, unhurried — the receptionist everyone hopes for. Short sentences, no jargon without a translation, gentle humor about pets (never about owners).

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Clinic name (default: "Pinebrook Animal Clinic"). Invented clinic name. Used in the wordmark, titles, footer, email (hello@ + brandSlug without hyphens + .com) and localStorage keys. Never a real clinic or franchise.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Asheville, North Carolina"). Where the clinic is. Used in the hero, the mini-map label, directions copy and the footer. Real cities are fine; the street stays invented.
- [PHONE] — Phone (default: "(828) 555-0143"). The main line, formatted with a 555 exchange. Used in the header, emergency band (main line), footer and tel: links. The out-of-hours line is a second invented number in content.ts.
- [FOUNDED] — Year opened (default: "2009"). Four-digit year. Drives the wordmark line 'Caring since [FOUNDED]', the story line and one vet's tenure.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/veterinary-clinic-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base unit. Container max-width 1160px, centered, side padding 48px desktop, 32px tablet, 20px under 640px. 12-column grid, column gap 24px. Section vertical padding 104px desktop, 80px under 1024px, 64px under 640px. Type scale: H1 clamp(42px, 6vw, 76px) Comfortaa 700, line-height 1.05; H2 clamp(30px, 4vw, 48px) Comfortaa 700; H3 22px Comfortaa 700; lead Albert Sans 400 18px / 1.7 Ink at 90%; body 16px / 1.7 (max-width 64ch); eyebrow Albert Sans 700 12px uppercase letter-spacing 0.16em Slate with a small drawn paw before it in Teal; caption 13px Slate. Buttons: height 52px, Albert Sans 700 15px. Primary: Teal fill, Card text. Secondary: 1px Teal border, Teal text. Emergency CTA: Coral Alert outline, Coral Alert text (never a fill — calm). Inputs: height 50px, 1px Rule border, Card fill, focus Teal border. Focus ring everywhere: 2px Teal outline with 3px offset.
- Radius: 16px on cards, buttons, inputs, chips, photos, the form panel. 999px only for pills, the open-now dot, vet initials and paw dots.
- Motion: Easing: --ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1) for reveals; --ease-io cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for the form swap and accordion. Gentle, unhurried — a waiting-room pace.
- Motion: Reveal: blocks wrapped in <Reveal> start at opacity 0, translateY(14px) and settle over 700ms --ease-out when 15% visible (Motion whileInView, viewport once). Siblings stagger 80ms.
- Motion: Hero photo: scales 1.05→1.0 over 2400ms on load; a drawn paw print trail (three small SVG paws) fades in along the hero's bottom edge, staggered 200ms (off under reduced motion).
- Motion: Emergency band: the pulse dot breathes (scale 1→1.2, 1800ms loop, off under reduced motion); the band itself is static — urgency without alarm.
- Motion: Service cards: hover lifts 3px and the icon well fills Teal Mist; the icon's stroke re-draws once per hover.
- Motion: Vet cards: hover lifts the avatar 2px and swaps the specialty line to the bedside quote (a 240ms crossfade).
- Motion: Form: species chips fill Teal Mist on select (150ms); invalid submit shakes 4px once with inline hints; submitting slides the panel to the ConfirmationCard (280ms --ease-io) with a drawn heart-plus-paw stamp scaling in; 'Start another' reverses.
- Motion: Hours: the open-now dot pulses only while open; the today-row carries a 2px Teal left rule that slides between days at midnight (crossfade).
- Motion: MiniMap: on view, the road lines draw (900ms), then the pin bobs in with one soft overshoot; the park block fills last.
- Motion: Links: .link-draw grows a 1px currentColor underline over 280ms on hover and focus-visible.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — no pulses, draws, staggers or scales; color/border hovers stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Warm real photography, all six made for this template in one consistent style: soft daylight, warm whites and wood with teal accents, animals relaxed, vet portraits are AI-generated fictional people shown at work with animal patients (consistent warm clinic light); they are placeholders a real clinic swaps for its own staff photos; all other photos stay animal-and-room only. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 16px corners, no shadow; photos on Warm White get a 1px Rule outline. Captions in Albert Sans 13px Slate. Drawn in code: the paw prints, service icons stay lucide, the heart-paw stamp, the mini-map, chips and the favicon. Icons: lucide-react only, stroke width 1.75.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the registration saves in the browser only (localStorage) and the form says so; tel: links are built from [PHONE].
- Every internal link points to a file name (./index.html, ./index.html#services, ./visit.html, ./visit.html#register, ./visit.html#emergency) — never to a bare directory.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- No real clinics, vets, franchises or pets of real people; no photos with human faces or hands — animals and empty clinic spaces only; care tips stay evergreen with no medical advice or dosages.
- No dates or years anywhere except 'since [FOUNDED]'; the site never shows today's date.
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere: no pulses, draws, staggers or scales; color/border hovers stay; the emergency band never flashes.
- The site must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; the form, chips and accordion work with keyboard only, and every interactive element has a visible focus ring.
- Keep copy concrete: no lorem, no medical jargon without translation; every hour, service and tip lives in src/content.ts.
