Corporate Company Website is a multi-page enterprise template designed for diversified industrial, infrastructure, energy storage, and advanced manufacturing groups. Rather than relying on generic stock handshakes, it pairs a cohesive set of dusk architectural photographs with deep navy (#0A1A3A), signal teal (#00B3A6), warm white (#F8F7F4), and oxidized copper (#C27A47) accents set in crisp grotesk typography. Hash-routed views organize the full enterprise narrative: an executive overview with cinematic dusk hero, three dedicated business division deep-dives, an animated by-the-numbers performance band, and a code-drawn SVG global presence map with interactive regional office cards. A dedicated Investor Relations route features an interactive SVG share-price chart with timeframe toggles, a quarterly results table, downloadable report summary cards, and an investor events schedule—every financial figure clearly marked as illustrative sample data. Additional routes cover Sustainability commitments with animated SVG progress rings, a filterable Newsroom with full article reader view, an executive Leadership grid and Careers teaser, and a Contact form that routes submissions dynamically by inquiry type (Investor Relations, Commercial Procurement, Media, Careers) with local confirmation state. Swap the group name, ticker symbol, headquarters city, and CEO name variables to tailor the site to any multi-division industrial enterprise.

## Make it yours

- An infrastructure fund: set [BRAND] and [TICKER] to the fund, swap the three divisions for portfolio sectors and point the investor chart at your own sample series.
- A municipal utility: set [HQ_CITY] to your service area, replace the rail and storage divisions with grid and water programs, and route procurement inquiries through the contact form.
- An engineering employer: keep the Leadership grid and Careers teaser, rewrite the open roles and bios for your teams, and link the sustainability rings to your own targets.
- A trade press or analyst audience: keep the Newsroom filter and article reader, publish quarterly updates as articles, and link report summary cards to your downloadable PDFs.

## FAQ

### What pages and sections does the Corporate Company Website template include?
The Corporate Company Website template is a six-page enterprise site. It opens with an executive overview and cinematic dusk hero, then adds three business division deep-dives, an animated by-the-numbers performance band and an SVG global presence map with regional office cards. Further routes cover Investor Relations with a share-price chart and quarterly table, Sustainability with progress rings, a filterable Newsroom with article reader, Leadership and Careers, and a contact form that routes by inquiry type.

### How do I change the company name, ticker, headquarters and CEO on the Corporate Company Website?
The Corporate Company Website template is driven by four variables: brand name, ticker symbol, headquarters city and CEO name. Set them to your own values, then rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image to match, keeping the same length, tone and structure. All divisions, offices, financial series, sustainability targets, newsroom articles, bios and roles live in one content file keyed from those variables, so the swap stays contained.

### Can I use the Corporate Company Website template for a client project?
Commercial and client use of the Corporate Company Website template needs a Pro membership. Pro templates show the brief, stack and variables publicly and the full prompt to Pro members, and the source code of the demos comes with Pro. Free templates' prompts and every DESIGN.md are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only.

### How do I add a wine club signup to a winery website built with AI?
The Corporate Company Website template is built for industrial and financial groups, not wineries. A winery site needs a different structure: tasting flights, vineyard story, allocation or club signup and a mailing list form. You can still borrow the template's dusk photography, navy and copper palette and routed contact form pattern, then replace the investor and division sections with your wine club form and estate content.

### Is the financial data on the Corporate Company Website template real?
No. Every share-price chart, quarterly results table, report summary card and financial KPI on the Corporate Company Website template is illustrative sample data, and the template marks each one with an explicit badge. Replace the sample series in the content file with your own figures, or keep the badges and use the site as a design and structure reference before wiring in real numbers.
