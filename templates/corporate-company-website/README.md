Corporate Company Website is a multi-page enterprise template designed for diversified industrial, infrastructure, energy storage, and advanced manufacturing groups. Rather than relying on generic stock handshakes, it pairs a cohesive set of dusk architectural photographs with deep navy (#0A1A3A), signal teal (#00B3A6), warm white (#F8F7F4), and oxidized copper (#C27A47) accents set in crisp grotesk typography. Hash-routed views organize the full enterprise narrative: an executive overview with cinematic dusk hero, three dedicated business division deep-dives, an animated by-the-numbers performance band, and a code-drawn SVG global presence map with interactive regional office cards. A dedicated Investor Relations route features an interactive SVG share-price chart with timeframe toggles, a quarterly results table, downloadable report summary cards, and an investor events schedule—every financial figure clearly marked as illustrative sample data. Additional routes cover Sustainability commitments with animated SVG progress rings, a filterable Newsroom with full article reader view, an executive Leadership grid and Careers teaser, and a Contact form that routes submissions dynamically by inquiry type (Investor Relations, Commercial Procurement, Media, Careers) with local confirmation state. Swap the group name, ticker symbol, headquarters city, and CEO name variables to tailor the site to any multi-division industrial enterprise.

## Make it yours

- A regional utility holding: set BRAND and HQ_CITY to your territory, replace the three divisions with generation, distribution and customer operations, and point the investor chart at your own sample series.
- An infrastructure contractor: keep the global map but swap regional office cards for project sites, and rewrite the newsroom filters around contract awards and project milestones.
- An advanced manufacturing group: rename the divisions to your product lines, update the tonnage and capacity metrics in the performance band, and rewrite leadership bios for your executive team.
- A sustainability-led energy storage company: keep the progress rings and reword each commitment to your own targets, then route the contact form's procurement path to your commercial team.

## FAQ

### How do I change the company name, ticker, HQ city and CEO on the Corporate Company Website?
The Corporate Company Website template keys its content from four variables: BRAND, TICKER, HQ_CITY and CEO_NAME. Set them to your group's name, exchange symbol, headquarters city and chief executive, then rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image caption that referenced the defaults. The divisions, regional offices, financial sample series, sustainability targets, newsroom articles, leadership bios and open roles all sit in one content file, so the swap stays contained.

### What does the Investor Relations section of the Corporate Company Website include?
The Corporate Company Website Investor Relations route carries an interactive SVG share-price chart with timeframe toggles, a quarterly results table, downloadable report summary cards and an investor events schedule. Every financial figure on the page is marked as illustrative sample data, so the section reads as a working structure for your own numbers rather than a claim about performance. Replace the sample series with your reported figures before publishing.

### Can the Corporate Company Website contact form send investor, media and careers inquiries to different people?
The Corporate Company Website contact view routes submissions by inquiry type: Investor Relations, Commercial Procurement, Media and Careers. Each path shows a local confirmation state after submission. The template has no backend and no server code, so the routing is a front-end structure you connect to your own form handler or inbox rules when you deploy the static build.

### Who is the Corporate Company Website template built for?
The Corporate Company Website template suits diversified industrial, infrastructure, energy storage and advanced manufacturing groups that need to present several business divisions, an investor story and a newsroom on one site. It is aimed at institutional investors, municipal rail and utility procurement leaders, sustainability analysts, prospective engineering talent and trade press. The tone is measured, engineering-led corporate prose rather than generic stock imagery.

### How do I adapt the Corporate Company Website for a company with different business divisions?
The Corporate Company Website template ships with three division deep-dives covering Advanced Materials, Electric Rail Systems and Grid Energy Storage. Rewrite each division's name, tonnage or capacity metrics and description to match your own segments, then update the global presence map's regional office cards and the by-the-numbers performance band. Keep the illustrative sample data badges on any financial figure until you replace it with your own reported numbers.
