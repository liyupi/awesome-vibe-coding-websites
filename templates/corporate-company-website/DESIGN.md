# Corporate Company Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Deep Navy** `#0A1A3A` — Primary dark architectural band, hero overlay, header/footer anchor, and primary ink on light surfaces
- **Signal Teal** `#00B3A6` — Primary accent, active navigation rule, chart curves, map hub markers, and progress ring highlights (darkened to #007A71 on light text labels for AA contrast)
- **Warm White** `#F8F7F4` — Primary light editorial page canvas
- **Architectural Stone** `#ECEAE4` — Subtle alternating bands, card borders, and table header surfaces
- **Oxidized Copper** `#C27A47` — Secondary warm industrial accent for division tags, key KPI highlights, and report badges
- **Slate Body** `#3A4760` — High-contrast body copy on Warm White surfaces

## Typography
- **Display & Section Headings** — Space Grotesk (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Body & Corporate Copy** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Financial Figures & Tickers** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Measured architectural grid: 68px sticky navy-or-glass header, 1240px max-width container, generous 64px–88px section rhythm, 24px card grids.
- Radius: 4px for structural tables, inputs, and buttons; 8px for photography frames, investor cards, and progress-ring panels.

## Motion
- Smooth count-up entrance on the 'By the Numbers' metrics band (instant final values under prefers-reduced-motion).
- Interactive hover focus on SVG share-price chart points, global presence map hubs, and sustainability progress rings.
- Never use clip-path to hide sections before scroll; all sections render visible by default.

## Tone
Measured, authoritative, engineering-led corporate prose—specific tonnage, megawatt-hour capacities, and division metrics paired with explicit illustrative sample-data notices on every financial figure.
