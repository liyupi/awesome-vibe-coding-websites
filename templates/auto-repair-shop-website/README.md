A two-page website for an independent auto repair shop: a home page with the shop's promise over a clean workshop bay, services grouped with honest price ranges and typical durations, a 24-month warranty strip, reviews from invented drivers with the car and job named, and the beloved 'what's that noise?' FAQ (squeal, knock, hum, grind — plain answers with honest bring-it-in advice); and a booking page whose form captures contact, vehicle (make, model, year, plate), the service, a preferred day and the noise in the driver's own words, confirming client-side. The default content follows an invented three-bay family shop, but the structure suits any independent garage or mobile mechanic: change the shop name, city, phone and price base and every service, warranty line and answer still reads true. The look is honest garage industrial: warm off-white, steel blue ink, one safety-yellow accent, Barlow Condensed headlines over Barlow, tire-track rules and price ranges in tabular numerals. Photography shows bays, lifts, tools and tires — cars generic and de-badged, no people, no readable plates. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add auto-repair-shop-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A mobile mechanic: set SHOP to your service name and SPECIALTY to mobile diagnostics, then rewrite the services list for on-site visits and add your travel radius to the booking form's story field.
- A family-run garage: keep the three-bay structure, set CITY and PHONE to your own, and replace the invented reviews with ones that name your regulars' cars and the jobs you did.
- A brake and tire specialist: set SPECIALTY to brakes and tires, trim the services list to those two groups with your own price ranges, and adjust the noise FAQ to cover squeal and grind.
- A classic car workshop: keep the warranty strip but rewrite the service rows for restoration and tuning, and change the hero photo to one of your own bays with de-badged cars.

## FAQ

### What sections and pages come with the Auto Repair Shop Website template?
The Auto Repair Shop Website template has two pages: a home page and a booking page. The home page includes a hero with the shop's phone number, a services list grouped with price ranges and typical durations, a 24-month warranty strip, reviews that name the car and the job, and a noise-diagnosis FAQ. The booking page has a form that captures contact details, vehicle make, model, year and plate, the service, a preferred day and the noise in the driver's own words, confirming client-side.

### How do I change the shop name, city and phone number in the Auto Repair Shop Website template?
The Auto Repair Shop Website template uses four variables: SHOP, CITY, PHONE and SPECIALTY. Set SHOP to your garage name, CITY to your location, PHONE to your shop line and SPECIALTY to a short phrase describing what you are known for, such as brakes, diagnostics and honest advice. The variables drive the wordmark, page titles, hero, reviews, footer and the tel: link, so changing them updates the site without touching components.

### How does the booking form on the Auto Repair Shop Website template work?
The booking form on the Auto Repair Shop Website template captures name, phone, vehicle make, model, year and plate, the service, a preferred day and a description of the noise in the driver's own words. It confirms client-side and stores the draft in the browser's local storage, so nothing is submitted to a server. You can connect the form to your own booking or email system if you need submissions delivered to the shop.

### What can I change first to make the Auto Repair Shop Website template fit my garage?
Start with the four variables in the content file: SHOP, CITY, PHONE and SPECIALTY. Then rewrite the service list with your own price ranges and durations, update the warranty line to match what you actually offer, and replace the invented reviews with real ones that name the car and the job. The noise FAQ answers should reflect the sounds your customers actually describe.
