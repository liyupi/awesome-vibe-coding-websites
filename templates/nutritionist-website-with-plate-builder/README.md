Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder is a bright one-page site for a nutrition-coaching practice, built around an interactive daily-plate tool. Visitors scan services and program pricing, read a three-step explanation of how coaching runs, then tap food groups to fill a drawn plate ring and watch a friendly balance readout respond — the practice's whole philosophy demonstrated in one interaction. A three-recipe preview, quiet client quotes, an about block, and a discovery-call booking sample complete the page. All copy stays at the food-and-habits level with no medical claims anywhere, and the booking flow is a local-only demonstration.

The look is fresh and sunny: a white and cream ground, leafy green with citrus accents, Baloo 2 display type over Nunito body text, generous pill-shaped controls, and one consistent photographic set — a produce spread, plated dishes, and a coaching kitchen in a single airy daylight grade. The hero is anchored by a market-table produce photograph rather than a face, and the plate ring is drawn entirely in code.

This template suits nutrition coaches, cooking teachers, meal-prep studios, and wellness practitioners who want an interactive, non-clinical feel. It also adapts to any coach-with-packages business: swap the plate ring for another simple visual tool and the structure holds.

Variables cover the brand, practitioner, city, audience, and food focus. All copy lives in one content file, with the plate groups, recipes, packages, quotes, and FAQs written as swappable records. The demo builds as a static Vite + React + Tailwind site with Motion springs, self-hosted fonts, generated images, and localStorage persistence for the plate and the booking sample.

## FAQ

### How does the plate builder tool on the Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder actually work?
The plate builder is an inline drawn donut of eight equal wedges with a white center circle. Visitors tap food groups — veggies and fruit, protein, whole grains, good fats and extras — to fill wedges, and a balance score, readout tier and short suggestion respond as pure functions of the group counts. Each group has targets, maxima and example foods defined in the content file, so the tool demonstrates the practice's food philosophy in one interaction.

### What can I change in the Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder without touching the layout?
The Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder template keeps all copy and data in one content file. You can rewrite the brand, practitioner name, city, audience and food focus, plus the plate groups, recipes, coaching packages, client quotes, FAQs, booking day and slot chips and the hero proof line. Layout, colors, Baloo 2 and Nunito typography and the Motion reveals stay as designed, so the page keeps its sunny, non-clinical feel.

### Does the Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder make any medical or weight-loss claims?
No. The Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder template is written at the kitchen level throughout: coaching talks skills, shopping, weeknight dinners and habits, and it never diagnoses, treats, prescribes or promises weight or medical outcomes. The plate readout is a friendly balance suggestion, not a health assessment. If you adapt the copy for your own practice, keep that same food-and-habits language so the page stays non-clinical.

### How does the discovery-call booking work on the Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder?
The Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder template includes a booking sample where a visitor picks a weekday and time chip and completes the request inline. The request, like the plate state, is saved in the browser with localStorage under keys derived from the brand name, and restored JSON is validated so malformed data recovers silently. There is no backend, so the booking is a local-only demonstration you would connect to your own scheduling flow.

### Can I swap the plate builder for a different interactive tool on the Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder?
Yes. The Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder template is built so the plate ring can be replaced with another simple visual tool while the structure holds: hero, services, a three-step how-coaching-works explanation, package pricing, a three-recipe preview, client quotes, an about block and a booking sample. This makes the layout useful for any coach-with-packages practice, such as cooking teachers, meal-prep studios or wellness practitioners.
