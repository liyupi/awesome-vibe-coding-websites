# Nutritionist Website with Plate Builder

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build a bright, one-page site for [BRAND], the nutrition-coaching practice of [PRACTITIONER] in [CITY], serving [AUDIENCE] through one-to-one coaching, group programs, and [FOOD_FOCUS]. The page presents services and how coaching works, a tap-to-build daily plate tool with a friendly balance readout, a three-recipe preview, quiet client quotes, and a discovery-call booking sample. Every word stays at the kitchen level: no medical, diagnostic, or weight-loss claims anywhere on the page.

- Audience: Primary: [AUDIENCE] — people who want calmer grocery runs, faster weeknights, and steadier food habits without diets, shame, or clinical language. Secondary: freelancers and small studios adapting the layout for any coach-with-packages practice.
- Goal: Move a visitor from 'this looks like my life' to a held discovery-call slot: win them with the produce-first world in one screen, prove the coaching approach with the plate builder in one interaction, and make booking a simple weekday-and-time choice that completes inline and stays in the browser.
- Tone: Warm, plain-spoken, and appetizing — a cook in a sunny kitchen, never a clinic. Short sentences, real food words, gentle humor. Coaching talks skills, shopping, and habits; it never diagnoses, treats, prescribes, or promises weight or medical outcomes.

## Stack
- Framework: Vite 6 + React 18 + TypeScript
- Styling: Tailwind CSS 3.4
- Libraries: react (MIT), react-dom (MIT), vite (MIT), typescript (Apache-2.0), tailwindcss (MIT), motion (MIT), lucide-react (ISC), @fontsource/baloo-2 (OFL-1.1), @fontsource/nunito (OFL-1.1)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Create a Vite 6 + React 18 + TypeScript project with base './', build.outDir 'dist', and build.sourcemap: false. No router, no backend, no server code.
  - Install Tailwind CSS 3.4 with PostCSS and Autrefixer. Extend the theme with the exact palette below, fontFamily.display 'Baloo 2' and fontFamily.body 'Nunito', and the four plate-group colors as named tokens.
  - Self-host Baloo 2 weights 600 and 700 and Nunito weights 400, 600, and 700 through @fontsource/baloo-2 and @fontsource/nunito, imported in src/main.tsx. Never load fonts from a CDN.
  - Use Motion from 'motion/react' (motion, AnimatePresence, useReducedMotion) for reveals, springs, and number tweens; gate every animation on useReducedMotion().
  - Keep all copy and data in src/content.ts: brand strings, hero copy, services, how-it-works steps, plate groups (targets, maxima, examples), recipes, quotes, practitioner bio, booking day and slot chips, FAQ. When variable values change, rewrite the dependent copy there and derive slugs and storage keys from [BRAND] so no default-brand remnants remain.
  - Build the plate as an inline SVG donut of 8 equal wedges with per-group fills and a white center circle; the score, readout tier, and suggestion are pure functions of the group counts defined in content.ts.
  - Persist the plate and the discovery-call request to localStorage with keys namespaced as [BRAND_SLUG] + '-plate' and [BRAND_SLUG] + '-discovery-request'; validate restored JSON and recover silently from malformed data.
  - Use only these lucide-react icons: Leaf, Sparkles, MessagesSquare, Users, ChefHat, Salad, Drumstick, Wheat, Droplets, Clock, CalendarCheck, Check, RotateCcw, Star, Quote, MapPin, ChevronDown, ArrowRight.
  - Download the six generated photos to the exact public paths listed in Assets before building. Create ASSETS.md at the project root listing every file's path, source URL, author or generator, and license.
  - A plain <title> of [BRAND] and a favicon (the code-drawn leaf mark) are enough; no meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, or sitemaps.

### Typography

- Body: paragraphs, controls, chips, meta: Nunito (OFL), weights 400/600/700

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base grid. Content shell max-width 1160px with 32px desktop and 20px mobile side padding. Header 72px. Desktop sections 96px vertical padding, mobile 64px. Card grids gap 24px. Prose blocks max 62 characters per line.
- Radius: Pill 999px for buttons, chips, tags, and the logomark; 24px for photos, the plate card, and large panels; 16px for small cards and inputs. Photos get a 24px radius with a 1px sage border.
- Motion: Sections reveal once on scroll: 14px rise with a 450ms ease-out fade, sibling cards staggered 60ms. Disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Motion: Hero: the produce photo scales from 1.04 to 1 over 700ms while the copy lines rise 16px with an 80ms stagger.
- Motion: Plate builder: a newly added wedge springs in (stiffness 260, damping 20) and the newest wedge pulses once; the readout heading crossfades over 200ms and the score number tweens over 300ms.
- Motion: Hover states lift cards 4px with a soft shadow, 180ms. No page drift, no custom cursor, no autoplay media.
- Motion: With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, replace springs, tweens, and reveals with 100ms opacity changes; every interaction stays fully functional.
- Imagery: One photographic set, one shoot: fresh produce and finished dishes in a consistent airy daylight grade — white marble and light oak surfaces, soft window light from the upper left, high-key whites, gentle warm highlights, leafy greens and citrus dominant. Six generated photos (hero produce spread, three plated recipes, the coaching kitchen, a market basket); no faces, no hands, no readable packaging or logos. The plate ring, icons, logomark, and decorative arcs are drawn in code, never photographed.

## Don'ts
- Never state or imply diagnosis, treatment, cure, prevention, detox, or weight-loss outcomes in any copy, tooltip, alt text, or metadata. Keep every sentence at the food-skills level and keep the footer's food-and-habits disclaimer present.
- Do not name or imply real nutritionists, dietitians, diet systems, or commercial programs. [PRACTITIONER], the Welltable Institute, client quotes, recipes, prices, and stats are invented for this demo.
- Photos must show produce, dishes, and kitchens only — no people's faces, no hands, no readable packaging, labels, or logos anywhere in the imagery.
- The booking flow is a local-only sample: no form submits, no APIs, no email or calendar services, no analytics. Persist to localStorage only and validate restored data.
- No dates tied to the real clock: use weekday chips and static sample slots; never render today's date, a countdown, or season-calendar math against the visitor's clock.
- No backend, server code, databases, auth, or third-party scripts. Static output in dist/ from the single build command.
- Self-host fonts through the listed Fontsource packages only; never load Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts, or any CDN stylesheet or script.
- No source maps in production, no src/ copied into dist/, keep dist/ under 20 MB, and keep Vite's base relative.
- No Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Google Images, placeholder-image services, or hotlinked files; use only the six generated photos and original code-drawn SVG/CSS art.
- No SEO work: no meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, sitemaps, or keyword-stuffed headings. A plain title with [BRAND] and a favicon are enough.
- Do not let the plate builder overflow horizontally, trap keyboard focus, or animate in a way that ignores prefers-reduced-motion.
- No scroll-jacking, custom cursor, autoplay audio, page parallax, or motion beyond the specified reveals, springs, pops, and tweens.
- Keep all copy in US English and build brand-derived strings (storage keys, slugs, alt text) from [BRAND] and [BRAND_SLUG] so alternate variable values never leave default-brand remnants.
- Every internal link is a file name or in-page anchor, never a bare directory; the site must render at 1440×900 and 390×844 with zero console errors.
