# Insurance Agency Quote Website

> A warm independent agency site with guided home, auto, and life quote paths and practical claims support.

Insurance Agency Quote Website is a three-page site for an independent agency that wants to explain coverage without overwhelming visitors. The homepage pairs a wide neighborhood photograph with immediate home, auto, and life quote entry points, then moves through coverage guidance, a three-step working process, three agent profiles, sample client stories, and a claims checklist. A dedicated quote page turns those paths into a short branching form with validation, editable answers, and a useful final summary. The claims page gives visitors calm first steps, coverage-specific document checklists, a general claims timeline, and direct answers to common questions.

The design uses warm parchment, deep forest green, quiet sage, and restrained clay accents. Fraunces headlines bring a human editorial voice while Public Sans keeps forms and checklists highly legible. Six coordinated documentary-style photographs make the agency feel established: a welcoming home, an advisory conversation, three clearly fictional agent portraits, and a hands-on claims scene. Gentle reveals and focused form transitions support the content rather than turning insurance into a spectacle.

Five variables change the agency name, handle, audience, service area, and central promise; all dependent copy and contact details are kept in one content model. The alternate case shifts the same design toward renters, commuters, and new parents in a different invented city. The quote experience does not estimate premiums, bind coverage, transmit requests, or store personal information. It creates a printable, copyable browser-only summary and tells visitors exactly what has and has not happened. This makes the template a trustworthy static front end that an agency can later connect to its own secure systems.

## Make it yours

- A renters-focused agency: set AUDIENCE to renters and commuters, keep the branching quote form, and rewrite the coverage guidance around renters and term life.
- A small-town independent agency: set SERVICE_AREA to your county, swap the three agent profiles for your real team, and link the claims checklist to your phone line.
- A new-parent-focused agency: set AUDIENCE to growing families, lead the homepage with life and home coverage, and rewrite the sample client stories around family milestones.
- An agency owner testing a static front end: set BRAND and PROMISE, keep the browser-only quote summary, and prepare to connect the form to your own secure systems later.

## FAQ

### What is included in the Insurance Agency Quote Website template?
The Insurance Agency Quote Website template is a three-page site: a homepage with home, auto, and life quote entry points, a quote page with a short branching three-step form, and a claims page with first steps, document checklists, and answers to common questions. It also includes three fictional agent profiles, sample client stories, and a claims checklist. The design pairs Fraunces headlines with Public Sans forms and uses warm parchment, forest green, sage, and clay accents.

### Does the quote form actually calculate insurance premiums or send my answers anywhere?
No. The quote form on the Insurance Agency Quote Website template does not estimate premiums, bind coverage, transmit requests, or store personal information. It keeps answers in memory for the current page session only and produces a printable, copyable browser-only summary. The page tells visitors exactly what has and has not happened, so the template works as a trustworthy static front end that an agency can later connect to its own secure systems.

### How do I change the agency name, audience, and service area in the Insurance Agency Quote Website template?
The Insurance Agency Quote Website template uses five variables: BRAND for the agency name, BRAND_SLUG for the handle, AUDIENCE for the primary audience, SERVICE_AREA for the local area, and PROMISE for the central message. Set each one and rewrite every dependent headline, paragraph, label, and image reference to match. All copy and contact details live in one content model, so the brand-dependent values update together rather than being scattered across pages.
