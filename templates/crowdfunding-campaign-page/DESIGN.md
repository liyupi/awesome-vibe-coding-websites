# Crowdfunding Campaign Page — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Plum deep** `#2A1D27` — Page ground everywhere — deep warm plum, the table it all sits on.
- **Plum** `#3A2A37` — Hover tints, tier-card borders on plum, updates rows.
- **Cream** `#F5EBDC` — Panels, tier cards, progress panel, FAQ rows — the paper of the page.
- **Cream line** `#DCCDB6` — Hairlines and rules on cream surfaces.
- **Brass** `#C89B4B` — Money and milestones only: raised figure, progress fill, unlocked nodes, prices, chips, links.
- **Brass light** `#E0BC72` — Hover and emphasis states on brass; the most-popular ribbon.
- **Cocoa** `#3B2F2A` — Primary text on cream — warm dark brown, never black.
- **Mauve ash** `#8D7A82` — Secondary text on plum: captions, sample notes, locked milestone labels.

## Typography
- **Display — campaign title, tier names, chapter titles (chunky storybook serif)** — Young Serif (OFL), weights 400. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body, chips, buttons, FAQ (warm humanist sans; labels uppercase tracked 0.12em)** — Alegreya Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Game-night generous: sections py-28 (112px) desktop / py-20 mobile; content max-w-6xl px-6/px-10; cream panels carry 28px padding with 1px creamline borders; tiers and chapters gap 24px; the progress panel gets 36px padding — it is the money table.
- Radius: Rounded like box corners: 12px on panels, cards and images; 999px pills on chips and buttons; the progress bar itself 999px.

## Motion
- Progress story: on reveal the bar fills 0→111% over 1.8s ease-out while the raised figure counts up (rAF); the 'FUNDED — SAMPLE' chip pops when the fill crosses 100%.
- Stretch goals: as the counting figure passes each threshold ($32k, $38k, $46k), that node pops (scale 0.7→1 spring, 300ms) from locked to unlocked brass; locked nodes rest at 60% opacity and state exactly how much more they need.
- Tier cards hover-lift 3px with the price turning brasslight (200ms); the most-popular ribbon sways once on reveal (rotate ±3°, 600ms).
- FAQ: rows expand with a 280ms height ease and the chevron rotates 180°; keyboard operable.
- Reveals: sections, tiers and chapters fade up (y 22→0, 700ms) with 100ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once.
- Hero: the tabletop macro settles 1.03→1 over 2.5s; the title follows 200ms later.
- prefers-reduced-motion: the bar renders full instantly with the final figure (no count-up), milestone states render final (no pops), FAQ opens without easing, all reveals become 200ms fades.

## Imagery
One tabletop session: warm macro and overhead photography of game components on a deep plum felt table under a single warm lamp glow from the upper left — wooden meeples and lantern pieces, linen-finish cards, brass-toned dice and coins, a box and an unmarked illustrated board corner — soft shadows, shallow depth of field, cozy game-night mood, cream-and-wood palette with brass highlights. Components carry no readable text (blank cards, unmarked board and dice), no logos, and no people ever enter the frame. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
Warm, plainspoken, game-night honest — enthusiastic about wood and linen, quiet about hype; every number cheerfully admits it is sample copy.
