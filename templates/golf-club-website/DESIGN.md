# Golf Club Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Fairway deep** `#1E3D2B` — Page ground everywhere — deep heathland green, the background of the whole club.
- **Fairway** `#2A4E38` — Raised tints on green: tee-grid cells, event rows, hover grounds; the SVG fairway stroke.
- **Ivory** `#F4EFE3` — Panels, hole cards, membership cards, form fields — the club's paper.
- **Ivory line** `#DED4BE` — Hairlines on ivory: table rules, card borders, bunker blobs in the SVG.
- **Brass** `#B08D4A` — The rules and accents: 1px brass hairlines on ivory, hole numbers, par/yardage chips, CTAs, the flag in the SVG.
- **Brass light** `#C9A968` — Hover and emphasis states on brass.
- **Pine ink** `#2B3327` — Primary text on ivory — green-black ink for serif headlines and body.
- **Fescue** `#9BA791` — Secondary text on green: captions, meta lines, sample notes.

## Typography
- **Display — club wordmark, headlines, hole names, prices (refined serif, italic accents)** — Playfair Display (OFL), weights 500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body, chips, tables, form labels (quiet sans; labels uppercase tracked 0.14em)** — Mulish (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Club-letter generous: sections py-32 (128px) desktop / py-20 mobile; content max-w-6xl px-6/px-10; ivory panels carry 32px padding with 1px brass hairline borders; the course tour card gets 48px padding — it is the letterhead.
- Radius: Restrained: 4px on panels, cards and inputs; 999px only on the two CTAs and chips; photographs keep square corners.

## Motion
- Hero: the fairway photograph parallaxes at 0.08 of scroll (the page's one parallax) and settles 1.03→1 over 2.5s on load; the headline follows 200ms behind.
- Hole layouts: the fairway path draws itself stroke-dashoffset 1→0 over 800ms the first time its card is shown; bunkers and green fade in 300ms after.
- Hole tap-through: prev/next crossfades the card content (300ms) with a 12px slide; the hole number in brass ticks over.
- Tee grid: available cells lift 1px with a brass ring on hover (200ms); selecting fills brass with ivory text; the reservation note fades in 300ms.
- Reveals: sections and cards fade up (y 20→0, 700ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)) with 100ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once.
- prefers-reduced-motion: no parallax or load-zoom, hole paths render complete instantly, all reveals become plain 200ms opacity fades.

## Imagery
One morning at the club: golf-course and clubhouse photography in low golden morning sun with soft mist — dew on fairways and greens, one flag in the haze, deep greens under ivory light, brass lanterns in a moody clubhouse interior, heather and bunker detail — tranquil, exclusive, 35mm look. Golfers appear at most as distant figures from behind with no identifiable faces. No readable text anywhere in frame (no signage, scoreboards or scorecards), no logos. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
courteous, unhurried, quietly proud — a secretary's letter, not a brochure; short lines, exact numbers, no superlatives.
