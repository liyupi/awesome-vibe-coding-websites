# Geometric Art School Website

> A primary-color art school with original art, course filters, open-day planning, and local applications.

This art and design school website builds its identity from a strict grid and three decisive colors. Red circles, yellow squares, and blue triangles become a large studio-work composition in the hero, not small decorative accents around a conventional page. Bold sans lettering, visible rules, and square panels carry that language through the course catalog, student showcase, faculty, and application form. Warm white gives the reading sections room to breathe without weakening the colorful opening.

The course catalog contains specific disciplines, formats, durations, fees, and outcomes. Visitors can combine discipline and study-format filters, inspect a course outline, and carry a selected course into the application form. The student wall includes eight original code-drawn projects, each with a title, fictional maker, medium, process note, and larger project view. It demonstrates the sort of work the school teaches instead of using empty colored placeholders.

Faculty profiles use a unified set of original geometric cut-paper portraits of fictional people. They have distinctive shapes and thoughtful composition rather than initials in circles or borrowed photographs. A recurring open-day program includes studio visits, a portfolio discussion, and a materials demonstration. Visitors can assemble a local visit plan, while a separate application flow checks fields and displays a truthful receipt without sending data or pretending to contact admissions.

The second example changes the school, city, teaching focus, course content, faculty biographies, and project stories while retaining the grid and primary palette. One module contains all editable text and sample data. Everything ships as static files with local fonts and original artwork. Keyboard-friendly filters, accessible dialogs, touch controls, and reduced-motion behavior make the site a practical starting point for independent schools and creative education programs.

## Make it yours

- A community print studio: set BRAND and FOCUS to printmaking, rewrite the six course records as press-time workshops, and swap the student wall projects for your members' prints.
- A ceramics or craft school: keep the filterable course finder, change the discipline filters to hand-building, wheel and glaze, and rewrite the faculty portraits as your tutors' stylized cut-paper likenesses.
- A youth art program: set CITY and ADDRESS to your venue, simplify the course fees and durations for after-school terms, and rewrite the open-day plan around parent studio visits.
- An independent design educator launching solo: set BRAND to your practice, replace the faculty section with your own teaching story, and point the application form at your booking or mailing list.

## FAQ

### What can visitors actually do on the Geometric Art School Website template?
Visitors can filter six practical courses by discipline and study format, open a course outline with learning modules and materials, and carry a chosen course into the application form. They can browse eight original student projects with process notes, read faculty profiles, assemble an open-day visit plan, and submit a local-only application that validates fields and shows a receipt without sending data anywhere.

### How do I change the school name, city and teaching focus in this art school template?
The Geometric Art School Website template keeps every editable text and sample record in one content module. Set BRAND, CITY, FOCUS and ADDRESS, then rewrite the matching headlines, course records, faculty biographies, project stories and confirmation copy so nothing still refers to the default school. The grid, primary palette and motion stay as they are, which is why the second live demo can show a different institute with the same visual language.

### Are the student projects and faculty portraits real artwork or placeholders?
Every project on the student work wall and every faculty portrait in the Geometric Art School Website template is original artwork drawn in code for the concept. The eight projects carry invented makers, media and process notes, and the four cut-paper portraits show stylized fictional people rather than photographs. No real school artwork, real designer attribution or borrowed photography is used, so you can replace the stories without clearing image rights.

### Does the application form on the Geometric Art School Website template send anything to the school?
No. The application flow in the Geometric Art School Website template checks the fields and displays a truthful receipt, but it does not send data or contact admissions. The site ships as static files with no backend and no external APIs. To collect real applications you would connect the form to your own form service or admissions system, and adjust the confirmation copy so it describes what actually happens next.
