# Geometric Art School Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Primary red** `#E63946` — Hero circle and large program panels. WCAG relative-luminance calculations: #111111 text on #E63946: 4.53:1; #111111 text on #FFC300: 11.74:1; #FFFFFF text on #1D3FBB: 8.46:1; #111111 text on #FFF9ED: 18.01:1. Use these pairs for every normal-size label/body/button. Decorative overlaps/gradients carry no text; readable text gets a solid backing.
- **Primary yellow** `#FFC300` — Hero square field and open-day/application panels.
- **Primary blue** `#1D3FBB` — Hero triangle field, showcase wall and footer.
- **Warm white** `#FFF9ED` — Explicit brief reading ground.
- **Ink** `#111111` — Body on red/yellow/warm white.
- **White** `#FFFFFF` — Type on primary blue.

## Typography
- **Display** — League Spartan (OFL), weights 700. Self-host the font files.
- **Body** — Public Sans (OFL), weights 400/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: 1200px max, 48px desktop/20px mobile gutters, strict 12-column grid, 1px rules; 8px unit and 96px desktop/56px mobile sections. Display 92px/54px, body 18px/1.6, labels 13px.
- Radius: 0px rectangles and buttons; circles exist only in art/portrait language. No rounded bento dashboard panels.

## Motion
- Geometry enters 16px once over 350ms with only small 4-degree rotation, no looping floating blocks.
- Project hover/focus reveals process label via instant border, not hidden essential text. Reduced motion shows all final geometry and removes slide transitions.

## Imagery
Code-drawn original primary-color student-work artworks: geometric posters, constructed vessels, abstract print sheets and a spatial paper model. Hero is a carefully lit CSS/SVG studio pinboard with overlapping physical sheets, a folded paper model, crop marks, cast shadows and actual visual composition. Faculty use four original AI-created-in-code geometric cut-paper portraits of fictional people, not resembling any real individual: head/shoulder shapes, distinct hair silhouettes and clothing, varied skin-tone shapes and ages, intentionally stylized with no photoreal face. These portraits are permitted and required consistently in imagery/assets/donts. No external photos, real school artwork or initials-only avatars.

## Tone
Clear, encouraging and serious about process; no accreditation, employment or admissions guarantees.
