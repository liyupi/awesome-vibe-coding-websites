# Event Venue Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page site for [VENUE], an event venue in [CITY] known for [STYLE_NOTE], noise curfew [CURFEW]. It must sell the space: three rooms with capacity tables, an interactive seating floor plan (banquet/classroom/cocktail modes redrawn live), a six-photo gallery, three packages, a client-side availability enquiry, a manager portrait (AI-generated fictional person), and a logistics strip. One page: index.html with sections (#spaces, #plan, #gallery, #packages, #enquire). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is submitted anywhere.

- Audience: Couples and corporate event planners shortlisting three venues on a Sunday afternoon. They compare capacity, cost per hour and photos of the room full — the floor plan answers 'will my 120 fit'.
- Goal: Produce enquiries. Within ten seconds a visitor sees the main hall set and the enquiry CTA; within two minutes they've switched floor-plan modes and checked their headcount.
- Tone: Host-confident: precise numbers, warm specifics, zero wedding-industry gush. One dry line about the curfew.

## Variables
- [VENUE] — Venue name (default: "The Foundry Hall"). Invented venue name. Used in the wordmark, title, footer, email (events@ + venueSlug without hyphens + '.com') and localStorage keys. Never a real venue.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Providence, Rhode Island"). Where the venue is. Used in the title, hero, logistics and footer. Real cities fine; street invented.
- [STYLE_NOTE] — Style note (default: "warehouse light and long dinners"). One lowercase phrase naming what the venue is loved for. Drives the hero line and one package line.
- [CURFEW] — Noise curfew (default: "midnight"). The amplified-music cutoff as a time word ('midnight', '11pm'). Used in the logistics strip and one dry FAQ-free line. Keep it short.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/event-venue-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

- Spacing: 8px base unit. Container max-width 1240px, centered, side padding 48px desktop, 32px tablet, 20px under 640px. 12-column grid, column gap 24px. Inputs: height 50px, 1px Rule border, Card fill, focus Champagne border. Focus ring everywhere: 2px Champagne outline with 3px offset.
- Radius: 4px on cards, buttons, inputs, chips and photos — architectural. 999px only for pills, mode chips and the guest stepper.
- Motion: Easing: --ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1) for reveals and gallery; --ease-io cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for plan-mode transitions and the form swap. Composed and unhurried.
- Motion: Reveal: blocks wrapped in <Reveal> start at opacity 0, translateY(12px) and settle over 750ms --ease-out when 12% visible (Motion whileInView, viewport once). Siblings stagger 70ms.
- Motion: Hero: the set-hall photo scales 1.04→1.0 over 2400ms while the overlay lightens 4%; the H1 rises 16px with 140ms stagger; a hairline Champagne rule draws under the promise line.
- Motion: Floor plan modes: tables and rows glide to new positions (Motion layout, 400ms --ease-io); the dancefloor/stage blocks crossfade; the CapacityReadout counts to the new figure over 450ms; mode chips fill Champagne in 150ms.
- Motion: Capacity tables: figures never animate; a hover fills the row Linen and slides a 2px Champagne rule.
- Motion: Gallery: photos scale 1.03 on hover with the caption fading up; clicking opens the Lightbox (Charcoal overlay at 88%, keyboard arrows and Escape, focus returns).
- Motion: Enquiry: guests stepper pops on change; window chips fill Champagne on select; invalid submit shakes 4px with inline labels; sending swaps to SentCard (320ms --ease-io) — a drawn Champagne 'ON THE BOOKS' seal scaling in gently, 'Thank you, [NAME].' 'We reply within a day with real availability.' space/mode/guests echo, 'Edit the enquiry' pre-filled. Persisted in localStorage.
- Motion: Links: .link-draw grows a 1px currentColor underline over 280ms on hover and focus-visible.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — plan modes swap instantly, no scales, counts or staggers; everything at rest; color/border hovers stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Real photography, all six made for this template in one consistent style: high-ceilinged warehouse rooms in soft natural light, photographed empty or set for events — tables dressed, chairs aligned, no guests, no faces, no readable signage or vendor logos. The manager portrait is an AI-generated fictional person (a warm mid-forties host in a linen blazer) — a placeholder a real venue swaps for its own manager's photo. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 4px corners, no shadow. Captions in Jost 13px Stone Gray. Drawn in code: the favicon, the floor plans (spaces, tables, dancefloor, legend), the seal, chips and capacity tables. Icons: lucide-react only, stroke width 1.75.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the enquiry saves in the browser only (localStorage) and says so; tel: links are built from the phone digits.
- All links on this single page are anchors (./index.html#spaces, #plan, #gallery, #packages, #enquire) plus the footer mailto.
- No guest faces or identifiable events in any photo — rooms are photographed empty or artfully set; the manager portrait is an AI-generated fictional person, not any real individual; no vendor logos or readable signage.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- No real venues, caterers or rental companies; capacities and packages are invented and consistent between the space cards, the floor plan and the packages.
- No dates or years anywhere; window chips are month-relative phrases; the site never shows today's date.
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: plan modes swap instantly, no scales, counts or staggers; everything at rest; color/border hovers stay.
- The page must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; space/mode chips, the stepper, selects and the form work with keyboard only and visible focus rings.
- Keep copy host-precise: no lorem, no 'magical day'; every capacity, hour and minimum lives in src/content.ts.
