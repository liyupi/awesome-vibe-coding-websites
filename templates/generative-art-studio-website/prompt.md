# Generative Art Studio Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A three-page site for [BRAND], the studio of [ARTIST] in [CITY], making [PRACTICE]. The site is its own demo: every section background is a live, seeded canvas, and one global seed (input, regenerate button, shareable URL hash) redraws all of them deterministically. Pages: Studio, Gallery of 24 fixed-seed pieces with a large data view, and a Print Shop that draws the chosen seed in CSS frames on a code-drawn wall with a browser-only cart.

- Audience: People who buy art for their walls and enjoy seeing how it is made: designers, architects, developers, collectors of affordable prints. The buyer of the template is a generative artist, creative coder or print seller.
- Goal: Make a visitor play with the seed within ten seconds, find a picture they like, and carry that exact seed to the shop to configure a [PRODUCT].
- Tone: Dry, exact, a little funny. A workshop that keeps good notes. Short sentences, real numbers, no art-speak and no talk of magic.

## Stack
- Framework: Vite 5 + React 18 + TypeScript
- Styling: Plain CSS (custom properties)
- Libraries: react, react-dom 18 (MIT), simplex-noise 4 (MIT), lucide-react (ISC), @fontsource-variable/syne (OFL-1.1), @fontsource-variable/instrument-sans (OFL-1.1), @fontsource-variable/martian-mono (OFL-1.1)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Scaffold with the Vite react-ts template. In vite.config.ts set base: './' and build.sourcemap: false.
  - No router library. Write a small useHashRoute hook. Route format: #/ , #/gallery , #/shop , each with an optional query: #/gallery?seed=4417&piece=1187 , #/shop?seed=4417&algo=flow . A missing or invalid seed falls back to the default 4417; a missing piece means no overlay is open.
  - Keep all copy, piece titles, prices and FAQ answers in src/content.ts. When the variable values differ from the defaults, rewrite that copy to fit the new brand, artist and product, and keep the same structure and lengths. Derive brandSlug in content.ts from [BRAND] (lowercase, hyphenated) and use it for the email address, the cart storage key and PNG file names.
  - Files: src/art/prng.ts, src/art/palettes.ts, src/art/flowField.ts, src/art/circlePacking.ts, src/art/contours.ts, src/art/subdivision.ts, src/art/engine.ts, src/content.ts, src/seed.tsx (seed context), src/components/ (ArtCanvas, TopBar, SeedDock, InstrumentPanel, Panel, FramedPrint, WallScene, CartDrawer, PieceOverlay, Footer), src/routes/ (Studio.tsx, Gallery.tsx, Shop.tsx), src/styles.css.
  - prng.ts, written from scratch: hash the string `${seed}:${salt}` with 32-bit FNV-1a to get an initial state, then return a function producing floats in [0,1) from a multiply-and-xorshift step on that 32-bit state. makeRng(seed, salt) is the only randomness used in art. Math.random is used in exactly one place: choosing a new seed on Regenerate.
  - palettes.ts: the six art palettes listed under Imagery, in that exact order. A section's palette index is (seed + paletteOffset) % 6; a gallery piece uses seed % 6 with no offset. Default seed 4417 therefore gives Ink Riot in the hero.
  - engine.ts exports renderPiece({ canvas, seed, algorithm, salt, paletteIndex, mode }) where mode is progressive or final. Every algorithm is a generator that fills the background, then yields after small batches. The engine runs it in requestAnimationFrame with an 8 ms budget per frame, returns { cancel, done, params } and reports progress from 0 to 1. Starting a new job on a canvas cancels the old one. deriveParams(seed, algorithm, salt) returns the same numbers without drawing, for the instrument panel.
  - Composition must not depend on pixel size. Noise coordinates are x / shortSide and y / shortSide. Stroke widths and radii scale with shortSide / 900. Element counts scale with area / (1440 * 900). Same seed, salt and aspect ratio means the same picture. Canvas device pixel ratio is clamped to 1.5. Guard every getContext call against null.
  - Flow field: createNoise2D(rng) angle field, angle = noise(x*freq, y*freq) * TAU * turns. Derived: freq 1.6-4.2, turns 1-3, particles 2400 at 1440x900, steps 70-190, step length 1.4-2.2, line width 0.5-1.8, alpha 0.85. Color weights across the four inks: 46, 28, 18, 8 percent. Strokes are thin and overlap freely, reading like combed silk. The artist-section instance (salt 'artist') uses 6000 particles and width 0.4-0.9.
  - Circle packing: rejection sampling with a uniform grid hash. Derived: 9000 attempts at 1440x900, radius from 0.004-0.007 up to 0.06-0.14 of the short side, with the local maximum radius modulated by low-frequency noise, gap 1.5 px, at most 1400 circles. Sort largest first and draw in that order. Each circle is one of: solid fill 55 percent, concentric rings 30 percent, half-and-half split disc 15 percent.
  - Noise contours: fractal noise with 2-4 octaves, freq 1.2-3.4, 9-15 levels. Marching squares on a 5 px grid (10 px on phones). For each level from low to high, fill the part of every cell above the threshold with the next palette ink, then stroke the isoline as a 1 px hairline in the palette background. One level is revealed per batch of rows.
  - Subdivision: recursively split the rectangle at a ratio of 0.32-0.68, across the longer side, depth 4-7, with a stop chance of 0.12-0.28 per split. Each leaf gets a fill plus one pattern: none, parallel hatch at 0 or 45 degrees, dot grid, or a quarter arc. A 2 px gap in the background color separates leaves. Leaves appear in shuffled order.
  - ArtCanvas component: absolutely positioned, full-bleed, aria-hidden. It starts a progressive render when within 200 px of the viewport. Fallback: 2500 ms after a route loads, any canvas not yet started renders in final mode, one at a time, so no section is ever left blank. Re-render on seed change and on a width change, debounced by 250 ms. Ignore height-only resizes on phones.
  - Gallery thumbnails render through a single queue at 480x640 (240x320 on phones), start on IntersectionObserver, and cache the result in a Map keyed by seed and algorithm. The print shop, cart and overlay reuse the cache.
  - Regenerate writes the new seed with history.replaceState and updates React state directly. All internal links carry the current seed. Pressing R regenerates when focus is not in an input. A polite aria-live region announces the new seed.
  - Cart: React state mirrored to localStorage under `${brandSlug}-cart`. Wrap every read and write in try/catch, and keep the page working when storage throws. Copy link uses navigator.clipboard inside try/catch and shows a fallback selected text field if it fails.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Studio name (default: "Quillfield"). Invented studio or project name. Used in the wordmark, title tag and all copy.
- [ARTIST] — Artist name (default: "Tove Adeyemi"). An invented person, or yourself. Goes in the bio and hero line.
- [CITY] — Studio city (default: "Rotterdam"). Where the studio is. Goes in the hero line and the address.
- [PRACTICE] — What the studio makes (default: "seeded generative prints drawn by pen plotter and pigment printer"). A plural noun phrase that reads well after 'making'.
- [PRODUCT] — What the shop sells (default: "archival pigment print"). Singular noun phrase for the item that gets framed.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/generative-art-studio-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8 px base. 12-column grid, max width 1360 px, 24 px gutters, 16 px side gutter on phones. Sections are min-height 100svh on desktop, auto with 72 px vertical padding on phones. Panels have 24 px padding (16 px on phones). Art always bleeds to the viewport edge; only content sits on the grid.
- Radius: 0 on art, frames, cards and panels. 2 px on buttons, inputs and chips. Panels get a 1 px border of rgba(244, 239, 255, 0.22). No shadows on UI; only framed prints cast a shadow on the wall.
- Motion: Art is the main motion. On load, Regenerate and scroll entry, each canvas fills with its palette background and draws progressively: hero complete in about 2.4 s, others in about 2-3 s.
- Motion: Instrument panel values change instantly on Regenerate. The draw percent counts up and a 2 px Acid Yellow progress bar fills, then the render time in ms appears.
- Motion: Text panels rise 16 px and fade in over 400 ms ease-out when they enter the viewport, staggered by 80 ms. All are forced visible 2500 ms after route load so nothing stays hidden.
- Motion: Route change: a 180 ms opacity fade on the main content. The cart drawer slides in over 260 ms. The piece overlay fades in over 200 ms.
- Motion: Print shop: changing size animates the framed print's width over 320 ms ease-in-out; changing frame cross-fades the frame style over 200 ms.
- Motion: Regenerate button icon spins once per press (500 ms). Buttons shift 1 px up on hover. Gallery cards show a 2 px Acid Yellow outline on hover and focus.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: render every canvas in final mode without visible stroke drawing and blit the finished frame in one paint. Disable panel rise, route fade, icon spin and frame animation. Regenerate still works.
- Imagery: All artwork is drawn live on canvas by this site's own code. There are no stored art files. Art palettes, in this exact order, as background then four inks. 0 Furnace: #2A0636, #FF2E63, #FFB800, #FF7A1A, #FFF1D6. 1 Ink Riot: #160A33, #FF4D3D, #E6FF2E, #14E0C8, #F4EFFF. 2 Deep Reef: #03203A, #14E0C8, #2E7BFF, #E6FF2E, #FF6FB5. 3 Acid Bloom: #0B2A16, #C8FF00, #00E58A, #FF4D9D, #F6F7D8. 4 Ultra: #1B10A8, #FF5A36, #FFE500, #39F3D0, #F2F0FF. 5 Hot Paper: #FF4D3D, #160A33, #E6FF2E, #1B10A8, #FFF4E8.
