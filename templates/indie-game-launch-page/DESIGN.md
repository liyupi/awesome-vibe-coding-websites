# Indie Game Launch Page — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Dusk** `#141019` — Page ground everywhere — deep violet-black night, never pure #000.
- **Dusk raised** `#1E1822` — Panels, form fields, store buttons, footer band.
- **Ash line** `#3A2F33` — Hairlines, borders, rules — structure instead of shadows.
- **Ember** `#E86A33` — The one accent: CTAs, map dots, play glyph, beat numbers, links, ember motes.
- **Ember deep** `#B34A22` — Pressed and hover-deep states on ember.
- **Parchment** `#F2E6D4` — Primary text — warm candle-lit off-white for the masthead, headings and body.
- **Ash rose** `#A89599` — Secondary text: captions, meta lines, placeholder honesty notes.

## Typography
- **Display — masthead, beat titles, release window (inscription-cut serif with a slightly runic character, tracked wide)** — Cinzel (OFL), weights 600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body, captions, form labels (bookish serif; italics for flavor lines)** — Alegreya (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Breathing room between set pieces: sections py-32 (128px) desktop / py-20 mobile; content max-w-5xl px-6/px-10; paintings bleed full-bleed or sit in 6px frames with 32px gutters; beats alternate sides with 96px between rows.
- Radius: Softly worn: 6px on framed scenes, panels and inputs; 999px pills on CTAs and chips; key art bleeds edge-to-edge at 0 radius.

## Motion
- Hero: the key art settles from scale 1.04→1 over 4s on load, then parallaxes at 0.1 of scroll; the masthead fades up 500ms after; twelve ember motes rise on 8–14s loops.
- Beat scenes: each painting drifts ±2.5% toward the pointer (fine pointers only, lerped) and ±4% with scroll, inside its overflow-hidden frame.
- Map chips: each location dot pulses scale 1→1.3 on a 3s loop, staggered; hovering a chip brightens its label to parchment.
- Trailer placeholder: the ember circle and play glyph breathe subtly (scale 1→1.04, 4s); a click nudges the glyph 2px — deliberately nothing more.
- Reveals: sections and beats fade up (y 26→0, 800ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)) with 110ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once.
- Wishlist form: the ember button presses to emberdeep; the saved note fades in 300ms.
- prefers-reduced-motion: the key art is static, parallax and drift off, motes and pulses stop, every reveal becomes a plain 200ms opacity fade.

## Imagery
One painter, one dusk: hand-painted oil-painting-style illustrations — deep violet-indigo nights warmed by ember-orange light sources, visible expressive brushwork and palette-knife texture, soft glows, atmospheric haze, painterly edges. Figures appear only as distant silhouettes with no readable faces. No readable text, no logos, no watermarks anywhere in the paintings. Six pieces are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
Hushed and literary, like a note pinned inside a lantern: short evocative lines, warm and slightly solemn; the trailer and store rows speak plainly about being placeholders.
