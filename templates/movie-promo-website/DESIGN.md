# Movie Promo Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Near-Black Theater** `#080B0E` — Primary widescreen backdrop
- **Night Teal** `#0E2A33` — Atmospheric shadows, cards, and nocturnal steel tint
- **Sodium Orange** `#FF8C3A` — Anamorphic light leaks, ticket CTAs, and active cue accents
- **Anamorphic Silver** `#CFD6DC` — Primary crisp silver-screen typography and hairline frames

## Typography
- **Film Title & Title Cards** — Cinzel (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Synopsis, Cast & Showtimes** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Timecodes, Cue Lengths & Specs** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Full-bleed widescreen trailer hero followed by disciplined letterboxed sections with dark teal framing and generous breathing room.
- Radius: 14px cinema frames with subtle silver hairlines.

## Motion
- Gentle Ken Burns parallax drift and warm sodium light-leak sweep cycling smoothly across the 4 stills in the hero trailer sequence (respecting prefers-reduced-motion).
- Live animated equalizer bars synced to the active Web Audio soundtrack cue.
- Smooth fullscreen Lightbox inspection for every film still.

## Imagery
Four widescreen 16:9 cinema stills from one nocturnal shoot graded identically in night teal (#0E2A33) and warm sodium orange (#FF8C3A): rain-slicked platform silhouette, empty sleeper corridor, steaming bridge viaduct at midnight, and dimly lit observation lounge—zero close-up faces, zero text.

## Tone
Atmospheric neo-noir cinema—disciplined widescreen framing, crisp silver-screen billing typography, zero clutter.
