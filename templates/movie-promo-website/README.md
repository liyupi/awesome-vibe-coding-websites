Opens directly on a widescreen trailer-like animated hero combining consistently graded 16:9 cinema stills, subtle parallax drift, sodium-orange anamorphic light leaks, and rhythmic title cards. ## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[FILM_TITLE]` | Feature Film Title | THE NIGHT MERIDIAN |
| `[DIRECTOR_NAME]` | Director Name | Elena Lindstrom |
| `[STUDIO_NAME]` | Production Studio | Solstice Filmworks |
| `[TAGLINE]` | Official Film Tagline | Every border crossed erases one passenger's memory. |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add movie-promo-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- An indie festival premiere: set FILM_TITLE and TAGLINE to your short, replace the four stills with your own frames, and swap the release waves for festival screening dates and venues.
- A documentary release: keep the synopsis and billing block, change the score cues to your composer's tracks, and rewrite the Press Kit dossier with your production notes and contact details.
- A film school showcase: set STUDIO_NAME to your program, trim the showtimes form to a single campus screening, and use the gallery Lightbox for student crew portraits shot from behind.
- A streaming launch page: replace the theatrical release waves with platform and territory availability, and point the Watch Showtimes CTA to your streaming link instead of cinema listings.

## FAQ

### What sections does the Movie Promo Website template include?
The Movie Promo Website template is a single page with a trailer-style hero cycling four night-train stills, a synopsis and director's note, a cast and crew billing block, a four-still gallery with a fullscreen Lightbox, a Web Audio score player with three cues, an international release schedule by wave, a Find Showtimes demo lookup, and a downloadable Press Kit.

### Is there a free movie trailer website template I can build with AI?
Yes. The Movie Promo Website template on VibeFather is free, and its prompt and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0. Commercial or client use needs Pro. You can add it with npx vibefather add movie-promo-website and the demos are built from one published prompt.

### How do I change the film title, director and studio in the Movie Promo Website template?
Set FILM_TITLE, DIRECTOR_NAME, STUDIO_NAME and TAGLINE in the template's variables, then rewrite every headline, paragraph and label in the content file to match. The layout, colors, fonts and motion stay as described. Replace the four night-train stills with images from your own film, keeping the same 16:9 grade.

### How does the soundtrack player work in the Movie Promo Website template?
The Movie Promo Website template synthesizes three atmospheric cues directly in the browser with the Web Audio API, so no audio files or external services are needed. Each cue has play and pause, animated frequency bars and volume control. You can swap the cue names and sound design in the score synthesizer to fit your own film.

### Can I use the Movie Promo Website template to promote a real film release?
The Movie Promo Website template works as a promo page for a real film, but commercial use needs Pro. The Find Showtimes form is a client-side demo returning sample cinema listings, so connect it to your own ticketing or a manual territory list. The release schedule uses waves and formats rather than calendar dates, which you can edit in the content file.
