Telehealth App Landing Page is a calm, product-led page for a fictional virtual-care service. It suits healthcare founders, product teams, and marketers who need to explain a mobile care experience without relying on stock photography or unsupported medical promises. Two detailed CSS phone mockups anchor the first screen: visitors can choose a visit reason and time, then explore a video-visit interface with local controls. The layout continues through common visit topics, a three-step care journey, clearly labeled sample pricing for self-pay and insurance, privacy practices, FAQs, and platform download actions.

The visual system pairs warm off-white surfaces with deep green typography, muted teal, soft mint panels, and a restrained coral accent. Fraunces headings add a human editorial note while Inter keeps interface labels compact and readable. Rounded phones sit over a subtle dotted field, with layered UI cards providing depth without turning the page into a generic bento grid. Motion is limited to short screen transitions, small reveal movements, selection states, and accordions, all with reduced-motion support.

The template is designed for adaptation through four variables: brand, audience, care focus, and self-pay visit price. All default names, clinical examples, prices, and interface content are fictional and can be rewritten together from one content file. The alternate case demonstrates routine virtual pet care, showing how the same product story works for a different audience. Every control has a visible local response: topic selection feeds the booking demo, the stepper swaps product screens, pricing changes emphasis, and download buttons explain that no real download exists. The static privacy section avoids certification claims and makes the absence of data collection explicit.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |

| `[AUDIENCE]` | Audience | adults managing everyday health needs |
| `[CARE_FOCUS]` | Care focus | everyday primary care |
| `[VISIT_PRICE]` | Self-pay visit price | $39 |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add telehealth-app-landing-page` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A solo virtual primary care practice: set BRAND to your clinic, CARE_FOCUS to everyday primary care, and rewrite the visit reason chips to the conditions you actually treat.
- A veterinary telehealth service: keep the virtual pet care direction, set AUDIENCE to pet owners, and swap the clinician role and visit notes for pet-specific steps.
- A mental health or therapy app: change CARE_FOCUS to counselling, replace the visit topics with session types, and keep VISIT_PRICE as your illustrative self-pay rate.
- A dermatology or specialist app: narrow CARE_FOCUS to skin care, rewrite the three-step journey around photo intake and follow-up, and relabel the pricing rows.

## FAQ

### What can visitors actually do in the Telehealth App Landing Page booking and video demo?
Visitors pick a visit reason (cold symptoms, skin question, allergy follow-up), a day and a time, and a summary line updates to match, such as Skin question · Tomorrow · 11:00 AM. Review sample visit shows an inline confirmation that no information was sent. The video phone lets them toggle microphone and camera icons and expand visit notes. Every control responds locally, and no real device access or personal data is requested.

The Telehealth App Landing Page template is free. Its prompt and the DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0. Commercial or client work needs Pro, which also gives Pro members the full prompt and the source code of the demos. The brief, stack and the four variables are shown publicly on the page either way.

### How do I change the brand, audience, care focus and visit price in the telehealth template?
The Telehealth App Landing Page template is driven by four variables: BRAND, AUDIENCE, CARE_FOCUS and VISIT_PRICE. All default names, clinical examples, prices and interface copy are fictional and sit in one content file, so you rewrite them together. Set BRAND to your service name, AUDIENCE to the people you serve, CARE_FOCUS to your non-emergency care category, and VISIT_PRICE to your illustrative self-pay price with its currency symbol.

### Can the telehealth landing page be adapted for a vet clinic or another kind of virtual care?
Yes. The template ships with an alternate demo showing routine virtual pet care, built from the same prompt with different variable values. That shows how the product story carries over to another audience: change CARE_FOCUS to the care category, rewrite the visit reasons, time chips, clinician role and FAQs to match, and keep the booking and video phone interactions as they are.

### Does the telehealth template make medical or privacy claims I need to be careful about?
No. The Telehealth App Landing Page template is written for a fictional service and avoids diagnoses, treatment promises, insurance guarantees and certification claims. The privacy section is static, makes the absence of data collection explicit, and the pricing is labeled as sample pricing for self-pay and insurance. If you adapt it for a real clinic, review the copy against your own compliance requirements before publishing.
