> Mix rain, fire, café murmur, vinyl, and birds in a cozy room that responds to every slider.

Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer is a single-page focus space built around an original illustrated room rather than a conventional marketing hero. Rain crosses the window, steam curls from a cup, flames light the fireplace, a record turns, and birds move outside. Each detail responds to its own sound slider, making the mix visible as well as audible. A focus timer and locally saved mixes turn the scene into a useful daily ritual.

This template suits creators, students, developers, and designers who want a calming browser toy, a portfolio interaction, or the starting point for a focus product. It is also a strong example of coordinated SVG animation, Web Audio mixing, accessible controls, and client-side persistence without accounts or a backend. The interface stays compact and tactile: five labeled channels, a circular timer, three useful presets, and a shelf for the visitor's own named atmospheres.

Fraunces headings, DM Sans controls, aubergine walls, warm amber light, and hand-drawn SVG details establish a gentle evening mood. The scene contains no people, branded objects, downloaded illustrations, or stock photography. Five original procedural audio loops are generated locally by a dependency-free Node script, bundled as small WAV files, and released under CC0; sound begins only after explicit activation.

Change the brand, room name, setting, invitation line, or default focus length in one content file and rewrite the supporting copy to match. The layout and interaction model remain intact for a reading nook, writing room, creative break space, or quiet study corner. Saved mixes stay on the current device, reduced-motion preferences are respected, and the complete production site ships as static files with no external media requests.

## FAQ

### How do I change the room, brand and focus length in the Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer template?
The Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer template keeps every visitor-facing string and default value in one content file. Set BRAND to your space name, ROOM_NAME to the room title, ROOM_SETTING to a short lowercase phrase describing the place, ROOM_TAGLINE to the hero invitation, and FOCUS_MINUTES to a whole number from 1 to 120 for the initial timer and middle preset. Rewrite the supporting copy to match the new room.

### What are the five sound channels in the Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer and what does each one change on screen?
The Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer template has five labeled channels: rain, café murmur, fireplace, vinyl crackle and birds. Each slider drives its own bundled local audio loop and a matching part of the illustrated room, so rain crosses the window, flames light the fireplace, steam curls from the cup, the record turns and birds move outside. Sound starts only after someone presses Listen.

### Does the Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer save my mix, and does it need an account?
The Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer template saves current channel levels, mute states and up to eight named custom mixes in the browser's local storage, with no account or backend. Saved mixes stay on the device where they were created. The focus timer may reset on reload. Everything except audio works immediately, and audio begins only after an explicit Listen press.

### Where do the sounds in the Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer come from and can I use them in my own project?
The Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer template generates its five ambient loops locally with a dependency-free Node script and bundles them as small WAV files released under CC0. There are no downloaded samples, stock audio or external media requests. You can regenerate, replace or extend the loops in the script, then adjust the channel definitions and audio paths in the content file to match.

### How do I make the Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer usable for people who prefer reduced motion or keyboard controls?
The Lo-fi Room Ambience Mixer template already respects reduced-motion preferences, showing static rain, flame, steam, record and birds, and changing interface state instantly. Controls keep 44px minimum pointer targets with spacing between them, sliders are labeled, and the focus timer announces completion in an aria-live region. Keep those behaviors when you rewrite labels, and check the room at mobile widths before publishing.

### How do I build a lo-fi room ambience mixer with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add lo-fi-room-ambience-mixer, replace [BRAND], [ROOM_NAME], [ROOM_SETTING] and [ROOM_TAGLINE] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
