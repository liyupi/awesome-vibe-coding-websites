# Market Research Insights Deck — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Editorial Paper** `#FBF9F5` — Primary magazine page background
- **Deep Pine** `#102A24` — Headlines, dark hero stat blocks, and high-contrast typography
- **Forest Teal** `#0D9488` — Primary data series, active segment toggles, and positive adoption curves
- **Burnt Ochre** `#D97706` — Secondary comparison series, regional callouts, and key trend flags
- **Warm Parchment** `#E7E2D8` — Hairline editorial rules and chart grid tracks
- **Pine Muted** `#475E58` — Source notes, captions, and axis labels

## Typography
- **Editorial serif headlines and giant stat numerals** — Fraunces (OFL), weights 600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Body prose and tables** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Figures, chart ticks, and citations** — IBM Plex Mono (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Desktop uses a 16:9 editorial plate (max 1320px, 44px padding) with crisp 1px parchment rules above a 56px bottom bar. Mobile (<768px) stacks cleanly into single-column scrollable report cards with 0px horizontal overflow.
- Radius: 8px for chart frames and callout boxes, 6px for interactive segment switches, 999px for edition badges.

## Motion
- Smooth 220ms directional slide transitions honoring prefers-reduced-motion.
- Interactive hover tooltips and segment toggle highlights on SVG multi-series line and bar charts.
- All content renders visible immediately with no clip-path traps.

## Imagery
Pure magazine-grade code-drawn SVG data visualizations: multi-series adoption line chart, grouped cost-parity bar chart, stylized regional adoption tile-map + bars, executive matrix, and Lucide icons.

## Tone
Authoritative, lucid, empirical, journalistic; clearly notes illustrative research model estimates.
