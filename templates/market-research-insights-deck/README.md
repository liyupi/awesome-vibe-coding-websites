Designed for industry research institutes, policy think tanks, and strategy analysts publishing annual market findings, this 12-slide browser presentation translates complex multi-year sector data into a magazine-grade data journalism deck. Set on warm editorial paper anchored by deep pine ink, forest teal primary series, and burnt ochre comparative highlights, every slide balances authoritative serif storytelling with disciplined quantitative clarity. The narrative opens with a bold cover and single-number hero stat plates before moving into interactive multi-series SVG line and bar charts where presenters can toggle market segments and hover data points for exact annual figures. Additional slides break down regional adoption rates across major clean-mobility markets, synthesize five structural megatrends into scannable editorial cards, compare strategic opportunities on an executive takeaway matrix, and document research methodology, sample sizes, and illustrative model notes transparently. Standard presentation ergonomics come built in: arrow-key, spacebar, touch-swipe, and click navigation, a crisp bottom progress track, a G-key thumbnail overview grid, an N-key speaker notes drawer with a live presentation timer, F-key fullscreen mode, and a clean landscape print stylesheet exporting one slide per page for PDF distribution. Customize the institute name, report edition, headline statistic, and regional series in one central data file to publish your next market report.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Research institute name | Verdant Policy Institute |
| `[REPORT_TITLE]` | Annual report title | Global Clean-Mobility & Urban Transit Outlook |
| `[HERO_STAT]` | Flagship headline figure | 38.4% |
| `[SAMPLE_SCOPE]` | Research coverage note | 42 Metro Regions · 2,180 Fleets |
| `[LEAD_ANALYST]` | Lead researcher | Dr. Hannah Osei, Research Director |

## Make it yours

- A policy think tank: set BRAND to your institute, rewrite REPORT_TITLE for your annual findings, and replace the regional adoption series with your own metro data.
- A strategy consultancy: keep the megatrend cards but swap the five themes for your sector's structural shifts and update HERO_STAT to your flagship figure.
- A climate-tech founder: use the executive takeaway matrix to compare market opportunities and set SAMPLE_SCOPE to your own survey coverage.
- A university research group: set LEAD_ANALYST to your principal investigator and rewrite the methodology slide for your sample sizes and model notes.

## FAQ

### What slides are included in the Market Research Insights Deck template?
The Market Research Insights Deck template is a 12-slide browser presentation. It opens with a bold cover and single-number hero stat plates, then moves into interactive multi-series SVG line and bar charts with segment toggles and hover tooltips. Later slides cover regional adoption bars, five megatrend cards, an executive takeaway matrix, and a methodology slide documenting sample sizes and illustrative model notes.

### How do I change the institute name, report title and headline statistic in the deck?
Set BRAND, REPORT_TITLE, HERO_STAT, SAMPLE_SCOPE and LEAD_ANALYST in the central data file. The Market Research Insights Deck template reads all research datasets, regional series, megatrends, methodology facts and speaker notes from that one file, so changing the institute name, report edition and headline figure updates the cover, stat plates and methodology slide together.

### Can I export the Market Research Insights Deck as a PDF?
Yes. The Market Research Insights Deck template includes a landscape print stylesheet that hides the toolbar controls and prints all 12 slides one per page, so you can export a clean PDF for distribution. Use the browser print dialog to save as PDF; the deck keeps its editorial serif typography and chart colors on each page.

### What keyboard shortcuts and presenter tools come with the deck?
The Market Research Insights Deck template supports arrow keys, spacebar, Home and End for navigation, a G-key thumbnail overview grid, an N-key speaker notes drawer with a live presentation timer, and an F-key fullscreen mode. Touch swipe and click navigation work on mobile, and a bottom progress track shows where you are across the 12 slides.

### Can I use the Market Research Insights Deck for a client report?
The Market Research Insights Deck template is a Pro template, so commercial and client use requires Pro. Pro members see the brief, stack and variables publicly and get the full prompt plus the source code of the demos. You can rebrand it for a client's annual market report by editing the variables and the central data file.

### How do I build a market research insights deck with AI?
Copy the full prompt (Pro) from this page or run npx vibefather add market-research-insights-deck, replace [BRAND], [REPORT_TITLE], [HERO_STAT] and [SAMPLE_SCOPE] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.

### Which AI model built the Market Research Insights Deck demo?
The Clean mobility annual research case was built by Gemini 4 Neon in Antigravity; the Grid storage global report case was built by GLM-5.4 in ZCode. Each build used the published prompt with nothing added, so you can compare the models side by side.

### What tech stack and pages does the Market Research Insights Deck template use?
Market Research Insights Deck is a single-page site built with React and Tailwind CSS, with page transitions and micro-interactions. The prompt fixes the stack so every AI tool builds the same kind of project, and the result runs as a static site you can host anywhere.

### Is the Market Research Insights Deck template free to use?
Market Research Insights Deck is a Pro template: the brief, stack and variables are public, and the full prompt, the source code of the live demo and a commercial license come with VibeFather Pro. Its DESIGN.md is free for personal projects.

### Does the Market Research Insights Deck template work on mobile?
Yes. Every Market Research Insights Deck build was tested on a phone-sized screen as well as desktop before it went live, checking layout, text size and touch targets. Open the live demo on your phone, or switch the preview on this page to mobile view, to check it yourself.
