# AI Image Generator Landing Page

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A one-page, dark, cinematic landing page for [BRAND], [PRODUCT]. The first screen is a working-looking product moment: a glass prompt box types example prompts letter by letter while the large image beside it dissolves into a matching picture through a hand-written WebGL noise-displacement shader. The page then proves the product with a stats strip, a three-step how-it-works with small UI mocks, a feature bento, a draggable before/after slider, a filterable masonry gallery that reveals the prompt behind each image, credit-based pricing, an FAQ and a sign-up band over a blurred collage of results.

- Audience: [AUDIENCE] who want finished images from a sentence without learning a complex tool, and founders or indie developers launching a generative product (image, video, avatar or upscaler apps) who need a believable landing page.
- Goal: Show the product working within two seconds, make the credit pricing easy to understand, and turn visitors into sign-ups through the 'Start free' buttons and the email field in the final CTA band.
- Tone: Calm, confident and concrete. Short sentences, plain verbs, real numbers (render times, pixel sizes, credit costs). No hype words like 'revolutionary', 'unleash' or 'magic'; let the images and the live prompt do the selling.

  - Morph shader (src/hero/morphShader.ts), plain WebGL1, no libraries: a full-screen quad; uniforms uFrom, uTo (sampler2D), uProgress (0..1), uZoom (1.00..1.04), uRes (canvas px), uFromSize, uToSize (image natural px). Compute cover-fit UVs per texture (scale so the image fills the canvas aspect, centered) and apply uZoom around the center. Noise: 2D value noise with 4-octave fbm. In the fragment shader: n = fbm(uv * 3.0); t = n * 0.75 + (1.0 - uv.y) * 0.25; m = smoothstep(uProgress - 0.1, uProgress + 0.1, t) (1 = still showing uFrom); displace the from-UV by (n - 0.5) * 0.10 * uProgress and the to-UV by (n - 0.5) * 0.10 * (1.0 - uProgress); color = mix(texture(uTo), texture(uFrom), m); add an edge glow where the dissolve front is: glow = (1.0 - smoothstep(0.0, 0.05, abs(t - uProgress))) * step(0.001, uProgress) * step(uProgress, 0.999); color += glow * 0.55 * mix(vec3(0.557, 0.424, 1.0), vec3(1.0, 0.478, 0.349), uv.x). Load the five hero images as textures (UNPACK_FLIP_Y_WEBGL true, CLAMP_TO_EDGE, LINEAR) after all have decoded; size the canvas with a ResizeObserver at devicePixelRatio capped at 2; render only while a transition or the zoom hold is running and the hero is visible. If WebGL is unavailable or a context is lost, fall back to two stacked <img> elements crossfading opacity over 700ms.
  - Reveal.tsx wraps sections in Motion whileInView fade-up (opacity 0 -> 1, y 24 -> 0, duration 0.6, ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1], viewport once with amount 0.2) and renders children unanimated when reduced motion is on. Wrap the app in MotionConfig reducedMotion='user'.

  - dist/ holds index.html, hashed JS/CSS, the woff2 files, favicon.svg and the six images; it should stay under 5 MB.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Brand name (default: "Driftframe"). Invented product name. Used in the wordmark, page title, hero subhead, gallery heading, FAQ, footer and the email domain. Never a real AI product, model family or company.
- [PRODUCT] — What the product is (default: "an AI image generator that turns one sentence into a print-ready picture"). One lowercase phrase that completes '[BRAND] is ...'. Drives the hero subhead, step copy, features and FAQ. Any generative visual product fits: avatars, upscaling, concept art, product shots.
- [AUDIENCE] — Who it is for (default: "designers, marketers and indie creators"). Plural noun phrase. Used in the hero subhead and footer line; feature and FAQ examples adapt to it.
- [CREDIT_NAME] — Usage unit (plural) (default: "credits"). The plural word for what users spend per render, e.g. credits, sparks, tokens. Used on the Generate button, pricing headlines, the credit explainer, the CTA and FAQ; the singular drops the trailing s.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/ai-image-generator-landing-page in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

## Don'ts
- No backend of any kind: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, email services, payment providers, image-generation APIs, analytics or third-party APIs. The Generate button only replays the scripted demo; the CTA form calls preventDefault and only changes local UI state; nothing is submitted or sent.
- Do not load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any CDN. Self-host through the three Fontsource packages only. No commercial or custom fonts.
- No CDN <script>, <link> or import maps. Every script, style, font, icon and image is installed or downloaded into the project and bundled into dist/. Never hotlink the media.vibefather.ai URLs at runtime; download them first.
- No source maps in the production build (build.sourcemap false), and src/ is not copied into dist/.
- Relative asset paths (Vite base './', images as ./images/<file>). Internal links are hash anchors (#how, #features, #gallery, #pricing, #faq, #cta, #top) or ./index.html, never a bare directory or a leading slash.
- No names, logos, UI chrome, wordmarks or model names of real AI image products, model families or AI companies anywhere in copy, labels, sample data, alt text or code comments. The product is the invented [BRAND]; refer to the engine generically ('our image model').
- No prompts that name living artists, studios or photographers ('in the style of ...'), real people, celebrities, or copyrighted characters, mascots and game or anime IP. Describe looks with generic terms (film still, matte painting, clay, 3D render).
- No images scraped from AI image communities or showcase feeds, no Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay or other restricted stock, no watermarked images, no placeholder-image services. Use only the six listed files and the code-drawn art.
- No real usage numbers, user counts, model benchmarks, funding, customer logos or testimonials. Stats are invented product facts for the invented brand.
- Do not use code or components from Aceternity UI, Tailwind Plus / Tailwind UI or any purchased template for the compare slider, spotlight, glow border, masonry or any other effect. Re-implement everything as described here.
