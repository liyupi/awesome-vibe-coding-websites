# AI Image Generator Landing Page

> A dark landing page for an AI image app, with a typing prompt hero that morphs the image beside it.

This is a dark, cinematic landing page for an AI image generator, built with React, Tailwind CSS and Motion. The first screen does the selling: a glass prompt box types an example prompt letter by letter, a Generate button fills a progress bar, and the large image beside it dissolves into a matching picture through a small hand-written WebGL shader. Five prompt and image pairs loop, and visitors can jump between them with the thumbnail strip. Below the hero, the page follows the order people expect from a generative product: a strip of product facts, three how-it-works cards with small interface mocks, a feature bento, a before and after slider with three finishing modes, a masonry gallery, credit-based pricing, an FAQ and a sign-up band over a blurred collage of results.

It suits founders and indie developers launching an image, avatar, upscaling or concept art tool, and anyone who wants a polished page for a credit-based creative app. The palette is near-black with soft off-white text and one violet-to-coral gradient kept for buttons, focus rings, the caret and the highlighted plan. Gallery tiles dim on hover to reveal the exact prompt, a style tag and a copy button, and style chips reflow the masonry with smooth layout motion. The comparison slider works with pointer and keyboard. Reduced-motion users get full prompts, static images and a manual next button.

To adapt it, change the brand, the product description, the audience and the name of the usage unit; the prompt asks the builder to rewrite every headline, plan and answer to fit. Pricing math is spelled out (cost per draft, standard and HD image), so swapping in your own numbers is quick. The six images share one dusk-lit look, and four gallery tiles are abstract art drawn in canvas.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Brand name | Driftframe |
| `[PRODUCT]` | What the product is | an AI image generator that turns one sentence into a print-ready picture |
| `[AUDIENCE]` | Who it is for | designers, marketers and indie creators |
| `[CREDIT_NAME]` | Usage unit (plural) | credits |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add ai-image-generator-landing-page` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- An AI avatar app for teams: set PRODUCT to avatar generation, rewrite the how-it-works steps around uploading a photo, and swap the gallery tiles for portrait results.
- A concept art tool for game studios: set AUDIENCE to art directors and illustrators, change the style chips to environment and character tags, and rewrite pricing around concept drafts.
- An upscaling service for photographers: set CREDIT_NAME to upscales, replace the before and after slider modes with your finishing presets, and rewrite the FAQ around file sizes.
- An indie founder launching a product shot generator: set BRAND to your tool, rewrite the hero subhead for ecommerce sellers, and point the sign-up band email field at your list.

## FAQ

### What happens in the first screen of the AI Image Generator Landing Page?
The hero shows a glass prompt box typing an example prompt letter by letter while a Generate button fills a progress bar. Beside it, a large image dissolves into a matching picture through a small hand-written WebGL shader. Five prompt and image pairs loop, and a thumbnail strip lets visitors jump between them. The effect is built with React, Tailwind CSS and Motion.

### What sections come after the hero on this AI image generator template?
The AI Image Generator Landing Page follows a generative product order: a stats strip, three how-it-works cards with interface mocks, a feature bento, a draggable before and after slider with three finishing modes, a filterable masonry gallery, credit-based pricing, an FAQ and a sign-up band over a blurred collage of results. Each section is written to be rewritten for your own product.

### How do I change the pricing and the credit name in this template?
Set the CREDIT_NAME variable to the plural word your users spend per render, such as credits, sparks or tokens. The pricing math is spelled out in the prompt with cost per draft, standard image and HD image, so swapping in your own numbers is quick. The highlighted Creator plan uses the lifted background color and the accent gradient.

### Can I use the AI Image Generator Landing Page for an avatar or upscaling app instead?
Yes. The PRODUCT variable is one lowercase phrase that completes the sentence about your brand, and any generative visual product fits: avatars, upscaling, concept art or product shots. The prompt asks the builder to rewrite every headline, step, feature and FAQ answer to match, while layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.

### What should I swap first when adapting this AI image generator template?
Start with BRAND, PRODUCT, AUDIENCE and CREDIT_NAME, then replace the six images and the four canvas-drawn gallery tiles with your own results. The template keeps all copy, image paths and links in one content file, so you can change the words without touching components. The gallery tiles dim on hover to reveal the exact prompt, a style tag and a copy button.

Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add ai-image-generator-landing-page, replace [BRAND], [PRODUCT], [AUDIENCE] and [CREDIT_NAME] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
