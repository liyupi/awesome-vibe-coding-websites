# Meal Planner & Grocery List App — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Fresh Linen** `#FBF9F4` — Warm kitchen linen background
- **Card White** `#FFFFFF` — Meal slot cards, recipe sheets, and aisle lists
- **Forest Ink** `#1B2A22` — Headings, recipe titles, and primary text
- **Herb Green** `#2D6A4F` — Primary CTAs, active tabs, check-off ticks, and Produce badges
- **Tomato Red** `#DC4A2D` — Quick swap accents, dinner highlights, and Pantry alerts
- **Warm Oat** `#EFEAE0` — Subtle borders, pills, and day selectors

## Typography
- **Editorial recipe titles and headings** — Fraunces (OFL), weights 600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Meal cards, steps, and grocery items** — Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Scaled quantities, prep minutes, and counts** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Mobile (<768px) clears the fixed 64px bottom tab bar comfortably. Desktop (>=1024px) renders a full-height 236px left sidebar, 7-day meal calendar grid, and right live Aisle Grocery rail.
- Radius: 16px for dish cards and recipe sheets, 12px for meal slots and aisle groups, 999px for day pills and servings controls.

## Motion
- Smooth slide-up Swap Meal & Recipe Cook-Mode sheet with live scaled quantity transitions.
- Crisp strikethrough check-off animation on grocery aisle items.
- Respects prefers-reduced-motion; all content visible immediately.

## Imagery
Six cohesive sunlit home-cooking photographs on matte speckled linen stoneware and warm oak countertop, zero text or logos.

## Tone
Warm, organic, appetizing, crisp home-kitchen calm.
