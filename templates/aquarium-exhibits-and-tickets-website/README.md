# Aquarium Exhibits and Tickets Website

> A deep-blue aquarium site with drifting caustic light, zone exhibits, a feeding schedule and timed tickets.

This template builds a one-page website for a city aquarium, run on a dark, deep-blue canvas: exhibits organized into five color-coded zones, animal residents shown as portrait cards, a weekly feeding-and-talks schedule, a timed-entry picker and three quiet membership tiers. The default content is an invented harbor aquarium with four spotlight animals, from a moon jellyfish hero portrait to an aging sea turtle, a drawn SVG floor map of the zones, and visit details on an invented wharf. The look is midnight-navy water under slowly drifting caustic light patterns drawn entirely in CSS and SVG, with one bioluminescent cyan accent for links, chips and ticket states. Headlines sit in a calm, wide sans with mono type reserved for times, depths and zone numbers, and every animal photograph is a single subject lit by one spotlight from above, like a portrait. The timed-ticket picker uses weekday chips and sample time slots with availability states, saves the choice to the browser, and says plainly that it is a sample with no booking behind it. The template suits aquariums, zoos, butterfly houses, botanical gardens and any visit-based attraction selling timed entry and memberships. It is a Vite + React + Tailwind build with Motion for calm, staggered reveals; fonts self-host through Fontsource, and images download into the project so the build stays fully local. To adapt it, change the brand, city, mascot animal and ticket price variables, swap the six photographs for your own animals, and rewrite zones, residents and schedule in one content file; the caustics, map and picker carry over unchanged.

## Make it yours

- A butterfly house or conservatory: set BRAND and CITY to your site, swap the hero animal for your star species, and rewrite the five zones as glasshouse rooms with their own light levels.
- A small city zoo: keep the zone cards and floor map, rename the depths as habitat distances, and replace the feeding schedule with keeper talks and animal encounter times.
- A botanical garden selling timed entry: change TICKET_PRICE to your admission, rewrite the residents as seasonal plant highlights, and point the membership tiers at your own donor levels.
- A science center or nature museum: use the zone map for gallery halls, swap the six photographs for your exhibits, and keep the sample ticket picker until you connect real booking.

## FAQ

### What sections does the Aquarium Exhibits and Tickets Website template include?
The Aquarium Exhibits and Tickets Website template is a one-page site with a sticky header, a hero built around a spotlight animal portrait, five color-coded zone cards beside a drawn SVG floor map, four animal resident portrait cards, a weekly feeding-and-talks schedule, a timed-entry picker that saves the chosen slot in the browser, and three membership tiers. All copy sits in one content file.

### How do I change the animals, zones and feeding times to match my own attraction?
Set BRAND, CITY, HERO_ANIMAL and TICKET_PRICE to your own values, then rewrite the zone list, the resident cards and the schedule inside the single content file. The template's zones, residents and feeding times are invented sample content, so replace them with your real tanks, animals and talk times. The caustics, floor map and ticket picker carry over unchanged.

### Does the timed-entry picker actually book tickets or connect to a payment system?
No. The timed-entry picker on the Aquarium Exhibits and Tickets Website template uses weekday chips and sample time slots with availability states, saves the visitor's choice to the browser, and states plainly that it is a sample with no booking behind it. There is no backend, no server code and no external API, so you would connect your own booking or ticketing service before taking real reservations.

### What does the aquarium vibe look like, and can I change the colors?
The aquarium vibe comes from a midnight-navy canvas, slowly drifting caustic light patterns drawn in CSS and SVG, and one bioluminescent cyan accent for links, chips and ticket states. Animal photographs are single subjects under one spotlight, treated like portraits. Colors, fonts and motion are defined in the Tailwind theme and stylesheet, so you can shift the palette while keeping the calm underwater feel.
