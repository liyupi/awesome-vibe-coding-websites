Open the house before the curtain goes up. This theater box office puts the production first, with a wide photograph of a gold-lit set, a program-style title and the next available performances just below. A visitor can read about the story, explore the company and check access arrangements before choosing a performance. The photographs show one imaginary room from the stage, the wing and the auditorium.

The booking flow follows an actual patron decision. Pick a matinee or an evening, compare prices and examine a hand-drawn plan of the stalls, circles and boxes. The plan distinguishes sold places, wheelchair spaces and seats with a restricted view. A seat inspection includes its price and a sightline sketch. The party finder looks for adjacent places in one row, and a visible timer keeps track of the hold. Programs and interval drinks can be added to the order. A review shows every line before simulated checkout issues a ticket with a scannable confirmation code.

Use the template for a new musical, a repertory season or a neighborhood theater with a permanent home. Set the four content variables for the venue, production, city and entrance before building. Performance inventory, add-on prices and local orders sit behind one small adapter so a developer can connect a box office later. The demonstration already includes a full auditorium of seats, realistic occupancy and several playing times. A visit guide and a group enquiry form give patrons a route into the house even when they are planning a later trip.

## Make it yours

- A new musical in tryouts: set SHOW to your title and BRAND to your producing company, then rewrite the program copy for your cast and creative team.
- A repertory season: keep the seat plan and party finder, but replace the single production with your season list and point each performance at its own show.
- A neighborhood theater with a permanent home: put your venue in BRAND and ADDRESS, and use the visit guide for directions, access and interval details.
- A group bookings coordinator: keep the group enquiry form and seat plan, and adjust the add-on prices for programs and interval drinks to your own bar list.

## FAQ

### How does the seat selection and booking flow work in the Theater Show Ticketing Website template?
The Theater Show Ticketing Website template walks a patron from a performance choice to a scannable ticket. A visitor picks a matinee or evening, compares prices and studies a hand-drawn plan of the stalls, circles and boxes. The plan marks sold places, wheelchair spaces and restricted-view seats. Inspecting a seat shows its price and a sightline sketch, the party finder looks for adjacent places in one row, and a visible timer tracks the hold. Programs and interval drinks can be added before a review screen and simulated checkout issue a ticket with a scannable confirmation code.

### Can I use the Theater Show Ticketing Website template for a real box office and connect it to my ticketing system?
The Theater Show Ticketing Website template ships with a small box-office adapter that holds performance inventory, add-on prices and local orders, so a developer can connect a real box office later. As published it has no backend, no server code and no external APIs: holds, baskets and checkouts run in the browser and no real payments are taken. Commercial and client use requires Pro, which also gives you the full prompt and the source code of the demos.

### What do I need to change first after adding the Theater Show Ticketing Website template?
Set the four content variables before building: BRAND for the theater name, SHOW for the production title, CITY for the location and ADDRESS for the entrance. Then rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for your own values, keeping the same length, tone and structure. Cast, reviews and access details live in one content file, so swap those in the same pass. The default values describe Vesper Arch Theatre, The Gilded Hour and Bellcross.

### Does the Theater Show Ticketing Website template include access information and group bookings?
The Theater Show Ticketing Website template covers access and groups in two ways. The seat plan distinguishes wheelchair spaces and seats with a restricted view, and a seat inspection shows a sightline sketch alongside the price. A visit guide gives patrons a route into the house even when they are planning a later trip, and a group enquiry form lets organizers bring a party. Group requests sit in the same box-office adapter as performance inventory, so they persist locally.

### How do I run the Theater Show Ticketing Website template locally with an AI coding agent?
