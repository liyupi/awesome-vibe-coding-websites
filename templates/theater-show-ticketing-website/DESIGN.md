# Theater Show Ticketing Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Velvet red** `#8C1C2C` — Marquee band, selected seats, velvet accents
- **Gilt gold** `#D4A64A` — Primary booking actions with dark text, rules and price details
- **Deep plum black** `#1A0F14` — Page ground, navigation, auditorium and dark text
- **Program paper** `#F7EDDB` — Display type and light booking surfaces
- **Warm gray** `#CCBFB8` — Quiet body text on plum

## Typography
- **Display and production title** — Cormorant Garamond (OFL), weights 500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Navigation, inventory and form labels** — DM Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Max-width 1440px; 5vw outer gutter, 72px between program spreads, 24px subsections, 8px control rhythm. On small screens 18px gutters and 40px section gaps. Text and controls meet WCAG AA.
- Radius: Square-corner posters and flat program rows; booking controls 4px. Fine 1px gilt rules. No collection of rounded cards.

## Motion
- Subtle 180ms color and underline changes on focus/hover. Show seat preview immediately on focus, touch inspection or hover.
- Use a simple 180ms transition opening drawers and watch reduced-motion preferences. All content is visible initially.

## Imagery
Use all three original photographs as one production shoot. No portraits. Seat sightlines are independent code-drawn miniatures of the photographed stage, shifted by level, row and seat position.

## Tone
Literate and welcoming in program copy. Direct and specific about inventory, access and prices.
