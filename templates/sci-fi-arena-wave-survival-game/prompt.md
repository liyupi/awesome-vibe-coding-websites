# Sci-Fi Arena Wave Survival Game

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page first-person wave game inside a closed industrial yard built from boxes. A glass title card sits over the live yard, with one hovering drone already in view. The visitor then walks, aims, and fires one magazine-fed carbine, collects health and shield cells from the floor, and faces numbered waves of boxy drones until health reaches zero. A retry card shows the wave reached and the drones dropped. Geometry and sound are generated in code.

- Audience: Developers and students who want a finished browser game they can reskin with their own names and ship as static files.
- Goal: A round that plays with a mouse and keyboard, and still plays when pointer lock is refused, including inside a sandboxed iframe: look, shoot, reload, collect cells, lose, and retry, with the score kept in memory and no console error.
- Tone: Dry and physical, like a notice posted at a test range. Short labels, no jokes, no exclamation marks, and no gore.

## Stack
- Framework: Vite 5 + TypeScript + three.js
- Styling: Tailwind CSS 3.4
- Libraries: three (MIT), tailwindcss (MIT), vite (MIT), typescript (Apache-2.0), @fontsource/chakra-petch (OFL-1.1), @fontsource/source-sans-3 (OFL-1.1), @fontsource/jetbrains-mono (OFL-1.1)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Create a Vite 5 + TypeScript app with no React, Vue, or Svelte. The entry is index.html plus src/main.ts. Set the document language to en. The title element reads [BRAND] — [ARENA]. The body contains one canvas and one div#ui for every overlay.
  - In vite.config.ts set base to './', build.sourcemap to false, and build.outDir to dist. Do not copy src into dist.
  - Install tailwindcss 3.4 with postcss and autoprefixer. Point Tailwind content at ./index.html and ./src/**/*.ts. Do not install Tailwind 4 and do not load Tailwind from a CDN. Map the font families and the hex colors below into the Tailwind theme.
  - Install three and import it from the three package. Do not load it from a CDN. Do not use GLTFLoader, TextureLoader, AudioLoader, EffectComposer, or any other postprocessing pass.
  - Install the Fontsource packages and import @fontsource/chakra-petch/600.css, @fontsource/chakra-petch/700.css, @fontsource/source-sans-3/400.css, @fontsource/source-sans-3/600.css, and @fontsource/jetbrains-mono/500.css from src/main.ts.
  - Put every user-facing string in src/content.ts. Build the strings that name the range from [BRAND], [OPERATOR], [ARENA], [WEAPON], and [DRONE]. Pluralize [DRONE] by appending the letter s when the value does not already end in s. When those values differ from the defaults, rewrite the strings and keep every number, layout, and mechanic.
  - Split play code into src/scene.ts, src/player.ts, src/weapon.ts, src/drones.ts, src/pickups.ts, src/waves.ts, src/input.ts, src/hud.ts, and src/audio.ts. Drive them from one requestAnimationFrame loop in src/main.ts. Clamp each frame delta to 0.05 seconds. While document.hidden is true, freeze the simulation.
  - Write public/favicon.svg as a rounded square filled #100F0C with a centered 18 by 4 rounded bar of #3EE0D2 and no letters. Link it with a relative href.
  - Write ASSETS.md at the project root listing each Fontsource package, the OFL-1.1 license, and the author. There are no third-party images.
  - Cap the renderer pixel ratio at 1.5, and at 1.25 when the viewport width is under 768. Resize the drawing buffer to the canvas client size. Make the canvas position fixed and inset 0, with touch-action none. Set the page margin to 0, the background to #100F0C, overflow hidden, and the height to 100dvh.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Game title (default: "Lampwright"). The large name on the title card and in the document title. Invented. It is not a weapon name and not a real studio.
- [OPERATOR] — Range operator (default: "Hale Relay Works"). Short invented company on the title-card eyebrow and inside the premise sentence.
- [ARENA] — Yard name (default: "Sodium Court"). The closed yard. Shown under the title and in the pause card. Also the second half of the document title.
- [WEAPON] — Carbine name (default: "Ash Spool"). Label above the ammo count only. The weapon stays a 12-round magazine carbine whatever this says. Never a real firearm model name.
- [DRONE] — Drone name (default: "Wick"). Singular name of the only enemy. The pause card and the game-over card use the plural, which is the value plus s unless it already ends in s.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/sci-fi-arena-wave-survival-game in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: The canvas fills the viewport. Overlay panels sit 20px from the edges on viewports at least 768px wide, and 14px from the edges below that, plus env(safe-area-inset-*). The title card padding is 28px 32px. Stacked HUD rows use a 6px gap. Touch sticks are 112px across with 16px outer margins, and the Fire and Reload buttons have a 10px gap.
- Radius: Title, pause, and game-over cards are 10px. Text buttons are 8px. Health bars, shield bars, sticks, and the round Fire and Reload buttons are pills at 999px.
- Motion: Before the round, the title drone yaws at 0.25 rad/s and bobs 0.12 m at 0.8 Hz. The bob amplitude is 0 when prefers-reduced-motion is set.
- Motion: While the player is moving faster than 0.4 m/s, the camera bobs 0.016 m vertically and 0.008 m laterally, with 0.004 rad of roll. A shot kicks pitch by 0.008 rad for 70 ms. A hit offsets the camera 0.012 m for 100 ms. Skip all three when prefers-reduced-motion is set.
- Motion: Floor cells spin at 0.7 rad/s on Y. Combat drones bob 0.1 m at 1.4 Hz. Both hold still when prefers-reduced-motion is set.
- Motion: A destroyed drone emits 18 sparks that travel 2.2 m and fade in 0.4 s. A viewport under 768px, or a coarse pointer, uses 8 sparks. Reduced motion uses 6 sparks that fade in place over 0.25 s and do not travel.
- Motion: The reload track fills linearly across 950 ms. A hit on a drone turns the crosshair Tracer for 80 ms. At 30 health or below, the health bar pulses from opacity 1 to 0.45 and back over 0.8 s, unless reduced motion is set, in which case the bar stays solid.
- Motion: Buttons only change background color on hover. They do not scale, bounce, or rise. Cards show and hide with no page transition.
- Imagery: No photographs, textures, or downloaded models. The first screen is the live yard: dark slabs, four warm lamp pools, crate stacks, a gantry, and one hovering drone, with the glass title card centered over that scene rather than a flat color field. The carbine, drones, bolts, cells, tracers, and sparks are three.js meshes and points. The only flat graphic is the favicon SVG.
