# Spend a Billion Dollars Game — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Money Green** `#2EC27E` — Sticky balance vault banner, Buy buttons, and bill stacks
- **Warm Cream** `#FFF8E7` — Primary canvas backdrop and paper receipt surface
- **Print Ink** `#1A1A1A` — Chunky sticker outlines, crisp typography, and offset drop shadows
- **Vault Gold** `#F5B700` — Milestone ribbons, mega-tier highlights, and active tab pill
- **Coral Sell** `#FF6B57` — Sell (-) buttons and budget warning accents

## Typography
- **Game Title & Big Balance Figures** — Bricolage Grotesque (OFL), weights 600/700/800. Self-host the font files.
- **Item Titles, Descriptions & Milestones** — Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Prices, Receipt Lines & Salary Math** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 500/600/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Hero benefactor welcome strip paired with a sticky vibrant green `$100,000,000,000` vault counter bar, category filter pills, 3-to-4-column responsive 60-item sticker grid, and a sticky/collapsible receipt & salary-converter sidebar.
- Radius: 16px rounded sticker cards with 2px solid #1A1A1A ink borders and crisp 3px hard offset shadows; serrated receipt edge at bottom.

## Motion
- Smooth requestAnimationFrame rolling counter animation whenever money drops or refunds.
- Playful sticker pop-tilt on card hover and crisp confetti burst when crossing major milestones or hitting $0 left.
- Animated growing stacks of $100 bills and proportional 3D isometric cubes in the 'Feel the Scale' tab.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion by snapping counters immediately and pausing scale bobbing.

## Imagery
100% original code-drawn flat-sticker SVG illustrations for all 60 items, stylized benefactor medallion portrait, animated SVG/Canvas bill stacks and scale cubes, plus Canvas-rendered thermal receipt PNG.

## Tone
Playful, crisp, editorial-toybox—witty item notes, crisp green-and-gold print ink, and zero preachiness.
