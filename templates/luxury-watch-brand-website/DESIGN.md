# Luxury Watch Brand Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Onyx** `#0A0A0B` — Page ground on both pages — deep neutral near-black.
- **Graphite** `#151619` — Raised surfaces: form panels, footer band, configurator side.
- **Rhodium** `#2A2D33` — Hairlines, table rules, leader lines, input borders — structure instead of cards.
- **Steel** `#8A9096` — Secondary text: spec values, captions, nav links at rest; mid-tone of the drawn watch.
- **Champagne** `#C8A96A` — The single accent: mono labels, prices, active chips, indices, second hand, underlines.
- **Champagne light** `#E3CB96` — Hover and emphasis on champagne: link hover, price emphasis, active-step number.
- **Porcelain** `#EDEDEA` — Primary text — headlines, body, the watch hands on dark dials.

## Typography
- **Display — wordmark, headlines, model names (roman-inscription serif, often tracked)** — Marcellus (OFL), weights 400. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body copy, form labels, buttons (quiet geometric sans)** — Jost (OFL), weights 300/400/500. Self-host the font files.
- **Mono — spec labels, prices, annotation tags, uppercase tracked 0.14em** — IBM Plex Mono (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Generous and rectilinear: sections py-32 (128px) desktop / py-20 mobile; content max-w-6xl px-6/px-10; spec tables and facts rows live on 1px rhodium hairlines with 20px row padding; nothing is boxed unless it is a form panel.
- Radius: Essentially zero: 0px on images, panels and tables; 2px on inputs and selects only; no pills, no rounded cards.

## Motion
- Hero: the macro dial scales 1.04→1 over 3s on load and parallaxes at 0.08 of scroll; the scrim holds constant.
- Craft steps: each step's annotation leader lines draw themselves (stroke-dashoffset 1→0, 600ms, staggered 120ms) as the step crosses 45% of the viewport; the step number brightens to champagne light.
- Balance wheel: the fourth diagram's wheel oscillates ±20° on a 3s ease-in-out loop — the only looping motion on the site.
- Configurator: dial and strap changes cross-fade the drawn watch's fills over 400ms; the price line swaps with a soft 250ms fade; option chips gain a champagne underline (250ms).
- Reveals: sections and cards fade up (y 24→0, 800ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)) with 100ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once.
- prefers-reduced-motion: the balance wheel holds still, parallax and zoom off, leader lines render complete, all reveals become plain 200ms opacity fades.

## Imagery
One studio session, one subject family: museum-grade macro product photography of ORIGINAL generic watches — dials clean with no text (plain baton indices only), movements, bracelets and straps — on a matte black surface under a single soft side light, deep shadows, champagne-gold and brushed-steel highlights, near-black backgrounds, razor-thin depth of field. No people, no hands, no logos, no recognizable real models. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather and downloaded into public/images.

## Tone
Quiet, exact, unhurried — the confidence of a spec sheet delivered like poetry; short declarative lines, mono labels, zero exclamation.
