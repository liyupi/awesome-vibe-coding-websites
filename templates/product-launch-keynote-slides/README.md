Product Launch Keynote Slides is a 14-slide presentation site that runs in a browser tab and paces a launch the way a big-stage event does: an opening tease, the reveal, three feature beats, the numbers, price and date, then a single closing word. Every slide sits on true black with one idea, oversized type and a spotlit product. It suits founders, product marketers and small teams who are announcing something tangible and want to present from a link instead of exporting a deck from a slide tool.

The main design feature is continuity. The product image, the product name, a stat card and the price are shared elements, so they glide, scale and reposition from one slide to the next rather than cutting. Product shots rise out of darkness in front of an ultraviolet glow, the coral product and that glow give the deck its color, and numbers count up in huge tabular figures. Inside a slide, each key press reveals the next line before the deck moves on.

Presenter controls are built in: arrow keys, click zones, swipe on touch screens, a hairline progress bar, an overview grid, fullscreen, optional timed playback and a deep link for every slide. On a phone held upright, the stage switches from 16:9 to 9:16 and each shared element has its own position, so the morphs still read. Visitors who ask for reduced motion get plain crossfades and final values.

To adapt it, change the six variables for brand, product, category, price, ship date and closing word, rewrite the copy in the single content file, and replace the six product images at the same paths with your own shots on black. The default is an invented portable home battery; the second example, a whole-room air purifier, shows the same stage with a different product.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Company name | Norvane |
| `[PRODUCT]` | Product name, one short word that works set huge | Cinder |
| `[PRODUCT_CATEGORY]` | What the product is, in lowercase | home battery |
| `[PRICE]` | Launch price | $1,490 |
| `[SHIP_DATE]` | Date the product ships or goes on sale | March 18 |
| `[CLOSING_WORD]` | The single word on the last slide, with its period | Charged. |

- A hardware startup launching a wearable: set BRAND and PRODUCT to your device, rewrite the three feature beats for your claims, and swap the six product shots for renders on black.
- An agency presenting a rebrand: use PRODUCT_CATEGORY for the service, replace the price slide with a retainer figure, and set CLOSING_WORD to the new tagline.
- A founder pitching at a demo day: keep the reveal, numbers and price slides, cut the feature beats to your two strongest, and set SHIP_DATE to your pilot date.
- A product marketer briefing a sales team: change BRAND to the internal team name, retitle the stat card for pipeline figures, and deep link each slide in the agenda.

## FAQ

### How do I change the product, price and ship date in the Product Launch Keynote Slides template?
The Product Launch Keynote Slides template keeps every value in one content file. Set the six variables for brand, product, category, price, ship date and closing word, then rewrite the headlines, labels and paragraphs so they match the new product. The price is counted up from its string, so keep the currency symbol and number in the same shape, and keep the product name and closing word under ten characters so they set huge without breaking.

### What do I need to replace to put my own product on the stage?
Replace the six product images at the same paths with your own shots on black. The template preloads all six on mount so the shared-element morphs never flash an empty frame, and the product image is one of the elements that glides between slides. Shoot the product free-standing against true black so it rises out of the ultraviolet glow, and keep the framing consistent across the six shots so the scale changes read as one continuous move.

### How do I present the Product Launch Keynote Slides from a link?
Open the published site in a browser tab and press the fullscreen control. Arrow keys, click zones and swipe on touch screens move the deck forward, a hairline progress bar shows position, and each of the 14 slides has its own deep link so you can jump straight to the reveal, the numbers or the price. Inside a slide, each key press reveals the next line before the deck moves on, which lets you pace the build.

### What happens to the animations if someone prefers reduced motion?
Visitors who ask for reduced motion get plain crossfades and final values. The shared product image, product name, stat card and price stop morphing between slides, the slide change becomes a short opacity crossfade, the counting numbers show their final value immediately, and the build lines appear with opacity only. The slow image drift also stops, so the deck stays readable without motion.
