# Vacation Rental Booking Page

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page booking site for [BRAND], a private cabin rental in [REGION] that sleeps [SLEEPS], from $[NIGHTLY] a night. It must work like a real direct-booking page: a gallery with lightbox, amenities in plain groups, a two-month availability calendar showing nightly prices (weekday/weekend) with booked ranges struck through, a price breakdown that updates live with dates, guests and the weekly discount, house rules, an area guide of invented landmarks with a drawn mini-map, and a booking request form that confirms client-side with a reference. One page: index.html with sections (#cabin, #calendar, #rules, #area, #book). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is submitted anywhere.

- Audience: Families and friend groups 28–55 planning a weekend or a week, comparing the big platforms and the owner's page. Phone-first, price-sensitive to fees, allergic to calendars that lie; they want real nightly prices, honest rules and a five-minute booking path.
- Goal: Produce booking requests without a platform fee. Within ten seconds a visitor should see the cabin at dusk, the sleep count and the nightly rate; within two minutes they should see the exact total for their dates.
- Tone: Warm and matter-of-fact, like the owner leaving a note on the fridge. Short sentences, exact numbers (miles, minutes, dollars), no 'nestled' or 'retreat' clichés, no exclamation marks.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Property name (default: "Hemlock Hollow Cabin"). The invented property name. Used in the title, hero, footer, email (hello@ + brandSlug without hyphens + .com), localStorage keys and booking references (initials, e.g. HH-4021). Never a real listing or rental brand.
- [REGION] — Region (default: "the Catskills"). Where the cabin is, lowercase article style ('the Catskills', 'the Blue Ridge Mountains'). Used in the hero line, area guide and footer. Real regions are fine; the lake, trails and town in the guide stay invented.
- [SLEEPS] — Sleeps (default: "8"). The maximum guest count as digits. Drives the hero strip, the guests stepper's max and the sleeping arrangement lines ('sleeps [SLEEPS] across three bedrooms'). Keep it 2–10.
- [NIGHTLY] — Weeknight rate (default: "185"). The base weeknight rate as digits. Weekends (Fri/Sat) compute as +30; the weekly discount is 10% at 7+ nights; cleaning is 95 and service 12%. All money derives from this number.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/vacation-rental-booking-page in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

- Motion: Reveal: blocks wrapped in <Reveal> start at opacity 0, translateY(16px) and animate to rest over 700ms --ease-out when 15% visible (Motion whileInView, viewport once). Siblings stagger by 80ms (index prop).
- Motion: Hero photo: on load scales from 1.06 to 1.0 over 2400ms --ease-out; scrolling translates it 0 to 10% (Motion useScroll, offset ['start start', 'end start']) and the headline block fades to 0.3. H1 rises from translateY(24px) with 140ms stagger. The 'from $[NIGHTLY]' tick slides in last with a small 2px underline draw.
- Motion: Gallery: photos rise 6px and darken their caption bar on hover; clicking opens the Lightbox (Pine overlay at 82%, photo at max 82vh, caption in Card, X closes, arrows and Escape navigate) with a 240ms fade and 8px scale settle.
- Motion: Calendar: month swaps slide 24px horizontally over 280ms --ease-io (AnimatePresence mode 'wait'). Hovering a day lifts the price 2px. Selecting start fills the cell Clay; selecting an end draws the range as a Clay/15% band behind the days with rounded ends; booked cells refuse selection with a 4px shake. The breakdown re-lines item by item (each row fades up 8px, 60ms stagger) and the total counts to the new value over 500ms.
- Motion: Breakdown: the weekly discount row appears with a Moss check drawing when nights reach 7.
- Motion: Rules: each rule row reveals with its icon drawing once (stroke-dashoffset) and 80ms stagger.
- Motion: Mini-map: on view, the road lines draw over 800ms, then the pin drops with a soft overshoot and the guide dots pop with 100ms stagger.
- Motion: Booking form: guest stepper counts pulse 1.0→1.15→1.0 over 240ms; submitting slides the form out and the RequestCard in (16px rise, 320ms --ease-io); the reference (brandInitials + '-' + four digits) types character by character over 700ms.
- Motion: Header: transparent over the hero with Card text; after 40px of scroll it gets Cream at 94% opacity, backdrop-filter blur(10px), Timber text, 1px Rule border, 250ms transition; a small Clay 'Book now' → #book button is always in the header.
- Motion: Links: .link-draw grows a 1px currentColor underline from 0% to 100% width over 300ms on hover and focus-visible.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — disable the hero scale and parallax, H1 rise, map draws, count-ups, type-in, shakes and slides (render at rest). Color and border hover changes stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Real photography only, warm and woodsy, all six made for this template in one consistent style: dusk lantern light and morning mist, timber and moss palette, documentary editorial look, fine grain. Never a person — exteriors, rooms, porch, dock. No readable text or logos anywhere. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 12px corners, no shadow; photos on Cream get a 1px Rule outline. Captions under photos in Work Sans 13px Moss. Drawn in code: the favicon, the wordmark (type only), the mini-map, price pills, the check circle, dividers. Icons: lucide-react only, stroke width 1.5.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the calendar selection, breakdown and booking request live in the browser only (localStorage); nothing is charged or sent, and the form says so.
- All links on this single page are anchors (./index.html#cabin, #calendar, #rules, #area, #book).
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- No real listings, platforms or their badges; no photos with people, faces or hands; no readable text or logos in any frame; the lake, trails, stores and town stay invented.
- The calendar shows nightly prices and booked holds as clearly-labeled sample data; no real dates of any real calendar, and the page never shows today's date (the month view starts at the current month without highlighting today).
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere: disable parallax, draws, count-ups, shakes, type-in and slides; keep color/border hovers.
- The site must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; the calendar and form work with keyboard only, and every interactive element has a visible focus ring.
- Keep copy concrete: no lorem, no 'nestled retreat' clichés; every rate, fee, mile and minute comes from src/content.ts.
