A single-page website for a digital marketing agency: a declaration-sized hero with a scrolling ticker of invented client names; five services in a bold grid; three case studies whose sample result figures count up on scroll (each card labeled sample data once); a four-step process from audit to reporting; five team members as initials cards; a three-line manifesto; and a proposal request form (budget band, services chips, contact) confirming client-side. The default content follows an invented eight-person growth studio, but the structure suits any service agency — brand, performance, content: change the agency name, city and manifesto and every service, case and line still reads true. The look is confident studio bold: off-black and bone, one electric lime accent, Space Grotesk headlines at poster sizes over Inter, chunky cards, marquee energy and counters that mean it. Two studio photographs — a war-room work session and a team huddle (AI-generated fictional people) — join the code-drawn charts and tickers, so the page feels inhabited by an actual crew; still no stock office photos and no real platform logos. To adapt it, swap the variables and rewrite the copy in one content file.

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add digital-marketing-agency-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A freelance brand strategist: set AGENCY to your name, CITY to where you work, and cut the five service tiles down to the three you actually sell, keeping the proposal form.
- A performance marketing consultancy: set SPECIALTY to paid media, rewrite the case study metrics around spend and return, and relabel the service chips to match your channels.
- A content studio: use the brand content demo as a starting point, set MANIFESTO_WORD to taste, and replace the case study charts with editorial and campaign work.
- A small in-house growth team pitching itself internally: set AGENCY to the team name, keep the four-step process, and point the proposal form at an internal request inbox.

## FAQ

### What sections and content does the Digital Marketing Agency Website template include?
The Digital Marketing Agency Website template is a single page with a declaration hero and scrolling client ticker, five service tiles, three case studies with sample figures that count up on scroll, a four-step process from audit to reporting, five team cards shown as initials, a three-line manifesto and a proposal request form with budget bands and service chips. The form confirms in the browser and submits nothing.

### How do I change the agency name, city and specialty in this template?
The Digital Marketing Agency Website template is driven by four variables: AGENCY, CITY, SPECIALTY and MANIFESTO_WORD. Set them in the content file, then rewrite every headline, case line and caption that referenced the defaults so the copy still reads true. The agency name also feeds the wordmark, page title, footer and email address, so changing it updates those in one place.

### Where does the proposal form send submissions, and can I connect it to my inbox?
The proposal form on the Digital Marketing Agency Website template confirms client-side only; nothing is submitted or stored on a server. To receive real enquiries, connect the form to a form-handling service or your own endpoint. The budget band, service chips and contact fields are already built, so you only need to wire the submit action to your chosen destination.

### Are the client names and case study numbers in the template real?
No. The client names in the scrolling ticker are invented, and the three case study result figures are sample data, each card labeled as such once. Replace them with your own client names and verified results before publishing. The counters animate whatever figure you enter, so your real numbers will count up the same way.

### What should I edit first to make this agency template my own?
Start with the four variables: AGENCY, CITY, SPECIALTY and MANIFESTO_WORD. Then rewrite the hero declaration, the five service tiles and the three case studies in the content file, since those carry the most persuasion. After that, swap the team initials and manifesto lines, and check the proposal form labels match the services you actually offer.
