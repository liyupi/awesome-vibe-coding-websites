Team Wiki Workspace App is a self-contained collaborative notes, documentation, and lightweight database workspace crafted for small product and design studios. Opening directly onto a complete product roadmap—never a blank canvas—it combines a nested sidebar page tree (with drag-to-reorder, Favorites, Trash with restore, and one-click templates for Meeting Notes, Product Spec, and Roadmap) with a tactile block editor. Typing '/' inside any block opens a command menu to insert headings, to-do checklists, collapsible toggles, callouts, pull quotes, code blocks, dividers, geometric visual figures, inline tables, and embedded kanban boards. Every block includes a drag handle plus keyboard move shortcuts, recognizes live Markdown shortcuts as you type, and surfaces a selection bar to bold, link, or attach inline comments. An embedded team deliverables database switches instantaneously between Table, Board, and 4-week Sprint Calendar views with status filters and sorting. Breadcrumbs, live backlinks between pages, a Cmd+K quick-jump palette, full Undo/Redo history, one-click Export to Markdown, and a dark mode toggle all persist cleanly in browser localStorage. Styled in warm soft white (#FAF9F6), crisp charcoal (#2B2B2B), energetic tangerine (#FF7A2F), and pastel database pills, the app collapses cleanly into a slide-out sidebar drawer on 390px phones.

## FAQ

### What can I edit in the Team Wiki Workspace App after the AI builds it?
The Team Wiki Workspace App seeds a full workspace from four variables: BRAND sets the app name, STUDIO_NAME the seeded team, LEAD_NAME the workspace lead, and CYCLE_NAME the roadmap page that opens on load. The default content is a studio handbook and roadmap, so you rewrite the page tree, block text and database records for your own team while the layout, colors and interactions stay as built.

### How does the block editor in the Team Wiki Workspace App work?
Typing a slash inside any block in the Team Wiki Workspace App opens a command menu for headings, to-do checklists, collapsible toggles, callouts, pull quotes, code blocks, dividers, geometric figures, inline tables and embedded kanban boards. Each block has a drag handle and keyboard move shortcuts, live Markdown shortcuts apply as you type, and a selection bar adds bold, links or inline comments. Edits persist in browser localStorage with Undo and Redo.

### Can I use the Team Wiki Workspace App for client work?
Commercial and client use of the Team Wiki Workspace App requires Pro, since it is a Pro template. Pro membership shows the brief, stack and variables publicly and gives you the full prompt plus the source code of the demos. The free DESIGN.md and free templates' prompts are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only, so a client wiki needs Pro before you deploy it.

### What database views does the Team Wiki Workspace App include?
The Team Wiki Workspace App embeds a team deliverables database that switches between Table, Board and a 4-week Sprint Calendar view, with status filters and sorting on each. Records are seeded in the prompt's data file and persist in browser localStorage, so the views hold their state between visits. You can rename statuses and deliverables to match your own sprint rhythm without touching the view logic.
