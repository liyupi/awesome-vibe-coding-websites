# Team Wiki Workspace App — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Soft White** `#FAF9F6` — Primary light-mode document canvas
- **Warm Sidebar** `#F2EFE9` — Left page tree rail, breadcrumbs bar, and database header surface
- **Charcoal Ink** `#2B2B2B` — Primary text, headings, and dark-mode elevated surface (#1E1E1E dark canvas)
- **Tangerine Accent** `#FF7A2F` — Active page indicator, primary CTA, drag drop line, and slash menu highlight
- **Pastel Sage** `#D8F3DC` — Done status pill and Shipped tag (ink #1B4332)
- **Pastel Sky** `#DBEAFE` — In Progress status pill and Engineering tag (ink #1E3A8A)
- **Pastel Lavender** `#EDE9FE` — Design Review status pill and Systems tag (ink #4C1D95)
- **Pastel Butter** `#FEF3C7` — Planned status pill and Callout background (ink #78350F)

## Typography
- **Page Titles & Headings** — Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL), weights 600/700/800. Self-host the font files.
- **Editor Body & Sidebar** — Inter (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Code Blocks & Shortcuts** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Balanced workspace geometry: 264px collapsible sidebar, 52px sticky top breadcrumb/action bar, 820px centered page canvas (expanding cleanly to 960px for embedded databases).
- Radius: 6px for sidebar items, block controls, and buttons; 10px for covers, callouts, database frames, and slash-command menu.

## Motion
- Crisp 140ms hover reveal on block drag handles and page actions.
- Smooth 160ms expand/collapse on toggle blocks, sidebar tree folders, and Cmd+K search overlay.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion across all transitions.

## Imagery
Bespoke geometric architectural SVG cover banners (Tangerine/Charcoal studio risograph bands, grid topography, and Bauhaus shapes) plus Lucide page icons—never emoji icons or blank white headers.

## Tone
Warm, organized, studio-crafted clarity—concrete deliverables, owners, and sprint milestones with zero corporate jargon.
