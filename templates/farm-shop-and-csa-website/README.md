# Farm Shop and CSA Website

> A warm farm website with seasonal produce, flexible CSA shares, a local shop, and visitor events.

Farm Shop and CSA Website is a warm, editorial starting point for small farms, market gardens, orchards, and cooperatives that sell directly to their community. It brings the jobs of a working farm website into one clear experience: showing what is in season, explaining how a CSA share works, offering a few farm-shop staples, and inviting people to visit.

The homepage opens with a generous harvest photograph and direct routes to the share builder and shop. A season-led produce calendar makes availability easy to scan without pretending to be live inventory. The farm story uses a working portrait, specific growing details, and a restrained visual rhythm rather than a wall of rustic badges. Event rows include practical times, capacity, and a local reservation flow.

The CSA page centers on a useful configurator. Visitors choose a box size, pickup point, and frequency while the contents, per-box price, and estimated monthly total update immediately. The shop page adds category filters, product quick views, a persistent cart, pickup selection, and an honest device-only order confirmation. No backend or payment service is required.

Adapt the brand, location, farm focus, share season, and pickup area through the content variables, then rewrite the sample crops, products, people, and events in the single content file. Six coordinated photographic assets provide a complete starting look. Earthy colors, expressive Fraunces headings, compact data rows, and gentle motion keep the result grounded and readable on both desktop and mobile. The result should feel like a dependable farm that knows its customers, with every control useful and every sample detail ready to replace rather than decorative storefront theater.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Farm name | Hazel & Furrow Farm |
| `[LOCATION]` | Farm address | 18 Meadow Gate, North Elms, Vermont |
| `[FARM_FOCUS]` | Farm focus | vegetables, herbs, and pasture-raised eggs |
| `[CSA_SEASON]` | CSA season name | Summer Share |
| `[PICKUP_AREA]` | Pickup area | the Upper Valley |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add farm-shop-and-csa-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A family orchard: set FARM_FOCUS to apples, pears and cider, swap the seasonal calendar for your picking months, and rewrite the shop products as fruit boxes and preserves.
- A market garden cooperative: point PICKUP_AREA at your town collection points, rename CSA_SEASON, and list each member grower in the farm story section.
- A dairy and egg farm: change FARM_FOCUS to milk, cheese and pasture-raised eggs, then replace the CSA box contents and shop staples with your weekly dairy list.
- A farm hosting workshops: keep the visit events section, set times and capacity for your own classes, and link reservations to your mailing list instead of device-only confirmation.

## FAQ

### How does the CSA share configurator work on the Farm Shop and CSA Website?
The CSA page on the Farm Shop and CSA Website template centers on a client-side configurator. Visitors choose a box size, a pickup point and a frequency, and the contents, per-box price and estimated monthly total update immediately. The chosen share plan is saved in the browser with localStorage, so no backend or payment service is required and the confirmation is honest about being device-only.

### Can I take orders and payments on the Farm Shop and CSA Website template?
The Farm Shop and CSA Website template handles ordering entirely in the browser. The shop page includes category filters, product quick views, a persistent cart and pickup selection, and it shows an on-device order confirmation rather than processing payment. There is no backend and no payment service, so you would connect your own checkout or take orders by email if you need real transactions.

### What do I need to change first after downloading the Farm Shop and CSA Website template?
Start with the five content variables: BRAND, LOCATION, FARM_FOCUS, CSA_SEASON and PICKUP_AREA. Rewriting them triggers a coherent pass over headlines, labels and imagery. Then replace the sample crops in the seasonal calendar, the four shop products, the people in the farm story, and the visit events, all of which sit in the single content file the template ships with.

### How do I turn the Farm Shop and CSA Website into a site for an orchard or a market garden?
Set FARM_FOCUS to your actual produce, such as apples and pears or salad greens and herbs, then rewrite the seasonal calendar rows, the shop products and the CSA box contents to match. Change LOCATION and PICKUP_AREA to your address and collection towns, rename CSA_SEASON, and update the visit events with your real times and capacity. The layout, colors and motion stay as they are.
