# Summer Camp Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Craft Cream** `#FBF2DC` — Page ground under the grain overlay.
- **Paper White** `#FFFDF6` — Cards, the fees table and the registration panel.
- **Cocoa** `#4A3728` — Text, hand-drawn outlines and strokes.
- **Sun Yellow** `#FFC53D` — Primary accent, CTAs, water-activity chips and avatar discs.
- **Lake Blue** `#3F8DB4` — Secondary accent, day cards, table header and land-activity chips.

## Typography
- **Display / headlines** — Baloo 2 (OFL), weights 600/700/800. Self-host the font files.
- **Body / UI text** — Nunito (OFL), weights 400/600/700. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Friendly and roomy: 88px section padding on desktop and 56px on mobile, a 1120px container, 24px card gaps; the hero scene is 62vh.
- Radius: Extra rounded: 24px cards and photo frames, 999px chips, buttons and avatar circles, 16px inputs and table corners.

## Motion
- Hero paper layers drift: each SVG layer floats on its own 9-13s ease-in-out loop, translateY up to 8px, staggered; all loops freeze under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Scroll reveals: cards rise 20px and fade in over 0.45s whileInView, staggered 70ms.
- Micro-interactions: activity chips lift 2px on hover, the save button presses in like paper, inputs and selects get a Sun Yellow focus ring.
- The grain overlay is static; nothing on the page loops faster than 9s.

## Imagery
One hand-built paper-cut world: layered flat SVG shapes with long soft shadows and Cocoa ink outlines for the hero scene, program discs, cabin glyphs and staff avatars. Three real-camp photographs (lake, cabins, campfire) share one warm golden-hour documentary grade and hang like taped prints; staff and campers are always illustrated, never photographed.

## Tone
Warm, proud, a little bit funny; speaks to parents and kids at once. Concrete details over slogans, safety stated plainly and never scary. Rewrite programs, schedule, staff, fees and FAQs to fit the supplied variables.
