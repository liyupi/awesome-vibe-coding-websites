# Summer Camp Website

> Paper-cut lake-camp charm: age-group programs, a week-at-camp schedule, and sign-ups saved in the browser.

Summer Camp Website is a hand-crafted site for a fictional lakeside sleepaway camp, made for camp directors and program coordinators who need warmth and reassurance on one page. The hero is a layered paper-cut scene drawn entirely in SVG — a cut sun, blue hill bands, a dock with canoes, pines, an A-frame cabin and tents — with each layer drifting on its own slow loop. Cream craft paper, sun-yellow and lake-blue accents, rounded Baloo 2 type and a static grain overlay carry the hand-made feel through the whole page. Content does the reassuring: programs split into three age bands with activity chips, a Monday-to-Sunday week-at-camp strip with an everyday rhythm bar, cabin cards and a plainly written safety list, an illustrated staff row, and a session-labeled fees table. Three real-camp photographs in one warm golden-hour grade hang like taped prints, proving the place exists; people appear only as illustrated avatars. The registration form saves to the browser through localStorage and says so in plain words — no backend, no emails, no payments. Parents get honest FAQ answers, kids get a week that sounds worth packing for. Adaptation runs through four variables — camp name, focus, age range and session length — with every list kept in one content file. The alternate case reworks the template for a farm day camp, showing how the same paper-craft system absorbs a different program without losing its shape. Motion stays gentle throughout — drifting paper layers, soft reveals and chip pops — and everything calms under prefers-reduced-motion while the page holds cleanly from a 1440px desktop down to a 390px phone.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Camp name | Camp Bluejay |
| `[CAMP_FOCUS]` | Camp focus | canoeing, forest crafts and campfire cooking |
| `[AGE_RANGE]` | Age range | 7 to 14 |
| `[SESSION_LENGTH]` | Session length | one week |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add summer-camp-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A farm day camp: set BRAND to your farm name, rewrite CAMP_FOCUS around animals, garden beds and harvest cooking, and swap the lake activities in the program chips for farm chores and barn crafts.
- A nature preschool or forest school: set AGE_RANGE to your youngest group, shorten SESSION_LENGTH to half days, and rewrite the safety cards to cover outdoor supervision and pick-up routines.
- A music or arts summer program: keep the paper-cut hero but rewrite CAMP_FOCUS around instruments, rehearsal and a final showcase, and relabel the week strip with practice, workshop and performance days.
- A youth sports camp: set CAMP_FOCUS to your sport, replace the water and land activity chips with drills and match days, and rewrite the fees table by session length for each age band.

## FAQ

### What comes with the free Summer Camp Website template?
The Summer Camp Website template is a free single-page site for a lakeside sleepaway camp. You get the full prompt, the DESIGN.md and the live demos built from it. The prompt covers the layered paper-cut hero, age-band programs, a Monday-to-Sunday week strip, cabin and safety cards, an illustrated staff row, a session-labeled fees table and a registration form that saves to the browser. The prompt and DESIGN.md are free for personal, non-commercial use; commercial or client work needs Pro.

### How does the registration form work if there is no backend?
The registration form on the Summer Camp Website saves each sign-up in the visitor's own browser through localStorage under the camp-registration key, and the page says so in plain words. There is no backend, no email sending and no payment step. To take real bookings you would connect the form to your own form service or booking system after generating the site, or treat it as a demo request form.

### What do the four variables change on this camp template?
The Summer Camp Website template runs on four variables: BRAND sets the camp name in the wordmark, copy, fees and footer; CAMP_FOCUS sets what camp days are built around and drives the program lists, week schedule and hero subline; AGE_RANGE sets the campers' ages that the three program bands split between them; and SESSION_LENGTH names and prices sessions by length, never by calendar dates. Every list lives in one content file so you can swap copy without touching layout.

### Can the Summer Camp Website template be reworked for a farm day camp or another program?
Yes. The Summer Camp Website template's alternate demo is a farm day camp built from the same prompt by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash in DeepSeek Harness, showing how the paper-cut system absorbs a different program without losing its shape. Set BRAND to the camp name, rewrite CAMP_FOCUS around farm days, adjust AGE_RANGE and SESSION_LENGTH, then rewrite the programs, week strip, cabins, staff, fees and FAQs to match. Colors, type and motion stay as described.

### What should I change first after generating the Summer Camp Website?
After generating the Summer Camp Website, start with the four variables: set BRAND to your camp name, rewrite CAMP_FOCUS around your actual activities, confirm AGE_RANGE and set SESSION_LENGTH. Then rewrite the three age-band programs with your real activity chips, the Monday-to-Sunday week strip, the cabin and safety cards, the staff row, the fees table and the FAQs. Replace the three camp photographs with your own golden-hour shots and point the registration form at your booking process.
