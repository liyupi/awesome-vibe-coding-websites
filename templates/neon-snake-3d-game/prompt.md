# Neon Snake 3D Game

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build [BRAND], a complete single-page 3D snake game on a dark 16 by 16 grid called [ARENA]. A four-segment glowing capsule line and one pulsing bead are already in view behind a frosted start card. The line glides from cell to cell, the camera follows from the south, and the bead pulses with a short loop synthesized in Web Audio. Two floor rules, Wrap and Walls, speed steps 1 to 5, and local bests are part of the game. [STUDIO] presents it. Opening sentence: [TAGLINE].

- Audience: Indie hackers and front-end developers who want a finished static Three.js game with keyboard, swipe, and on-screen controls, readable on a desk and a phone.
- Goal: Ship one replayable run: a start card over a living grid, immediate play, growing speed, wrap or rail rules, pause, game over with initials, and bests stored in the browser. A second set of names must keep the same grid, snake, colors, and rules.
- Tone: Calm, precise, and a little playful. Short US English. Talk about the line, the floor, and the bead. No military ranks, no hype, and no cyber slang.

## Stack

- Styling: Vanilla CSS
- Libraries: three (MIT), lucide (ISC), @fontsource/syne (OFL), @fontsource-variable/outfit (OFL), @fontsource/ibm-plex-mono (OFL)
- Build: `npm run build` → static files in `dist/`. No backend, no server code, no external APIs.
- Setup:
  - Scaffold Vite's vanilla TypeScript template. Scripts: dev=vite, build=tsc && vite build, preview=vite preview. Package name is the slug of BRAND, not a fixed template id. Set vite base to './', build.outDir to dist, build.emptyOutDir to true, and build.sourcemap to false. tsconfig: strict true, noEmit true.
  - Install only three, lucide, @fontsource/syne, @fontsource-variable/outfit, and @fontsource/ibm-plex-mono as runtime dependencies, plus Vite and TypeScript as dev dependencies. Do not add React, a physics engine, a game framework, postprocessing, a tween library, or an audio decoder.
  - Import Syne 700 and 800 from @fontsource/syne, Outfit variable weights from @fontsource-variable/outfit/wght.css, and IBM Plex Mono 500 from @fontsource/ibm-plex-mono. Copy only the Lucide Pause, Volume2, and VolumeX icon nodes into src/icons.ts and render them with lucide createElement at 18px, currentColor, aria-hidden. Wait up to 800ms for document.fonts.ready, then show the card even if fonts are still swapping.
  - Use modules src/content.ts, main.ts, game.ts, scene.ts, audio.ts, ui.ts, and styles.css. Keep every proper name, button label, hint, and sentence in content.ts. Grid size, speeds, coordinates, and colors are named constants in game.ts and scene.ts.
  - World axes: +x east (screen right), +y up, +z south (toward the camera, screen down). North is decreasing iz and points up the screen during play. Cell (ix, iz) center is ((ix - 7.5) * 1.2, 0, (iz - 7.5) * 1.2) with ix and iz from 0 to 15. Playable lip is x and z of ±9.6. Wrap period is 19.2.
  - Use BRAND, STUDIO, PICKUP, ARENA, PLACE, and TAGLINE for all player-facing names. Sentence patterns: Collect ${PICKUP} on the ${ARENA}. The ${ARENA} is holding. Document title is BRAND, an em dash, and ARENA. When values differ from the defaults, rewrite TAGLINE so it does not name the old floor or the old pickup. Never show raw bracket placeholders. The capsule line stays a snake even if TAGLINE uses a metaphor.
  - Persist scores under the key slug(BRAND) + '-runs' and mute under slug(BRAND) + '-muted'. slug lowercases and turns every non-alphanumeric run into one hyphen, then trims hyphens. Lumen Coil becomes lumen-coil-runs. Wrap localStorage in try/catch and keep playing if it throws. No calendar dates.
  - One index.html, lang=en, viewport with viewport-fit=cover, a full-view canvas, and HTML overlays. Body margin 0, overflow hidden, background #07080A, height 100dvh. Touch-action none on the canvas. No router and no second page.
  - Read prefers-reduced-motion on load and on change. Reduced motion holds the bead at scale 1 and halo opacity 0.32, freezes the title drift and the grid breathe, sets follow smoothing to the slower taus, and skips the score pop and the death color flash. Audio may still play.
  - Cap device pixel ratio at 1.75, and at 1.25 below 800px. Antialiasing on, shadows off, alpha false. If the rolling frame time stays above 28ms for three seconds after the first two seconds of play, lower the cap by 0.25 once and not below 1. Do not change speeds when you do that.

- [TAGLINE] — Opening sentence (default: "Steer the sea-glass line across the Relay Floor."). One sentence under the game name. Rewrite it when the floor or the pickup changes. The 3D line stays capsules.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/neon-snake-3d-game in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: Use a 4px base. Desktop card padding 28px, inset 36px from the left. Mobile card inset 12px plus safe-area insets. HUD chip gap 8px, top-bar padding 12px 16px. Buttons are at least 44px tall. The pad keys are 64px. The center of the play view stays clear of the pad.
- Radius: Cards 20px, buttons 12px, radios 12px, HUD chips fully round, pad keys 14px. The snake and the bead are round capsules and spheres. The rail is a sharp low box.
- Motion: Simulation uses the frame delta capped at 0.05 seconds, with at most 3 cell crossings processed per frame. Render after the step.
- Motion: All capsules share one offset from 0 to 1 and glide together between cell centers. The play camera never yaws. Its position eases toward a southern offset with tau 0.28 seconds, or 0.62 when reduced motion is on.
- Motion: The bead swells on each quarter note at 96 beats per minute: scale 1 to 1.2 in 0.09 seconds, back to 1 by 0.32 seconds. Reduced motion holds scale 1 and halo opacity 0.32.
- Motion: Title camera drifts by a sine of 0.08 radians over about 28 seconds and stops when the run starts. Buttons darken or lighten on hover. A pressed control scales to 0.98 in 80ms unless reduced motion is on.
- Motion: Pause freezes the offset, the beat phase, and the title drift. The hidden tab pauses the same way and does not resume until Resume.
- Imagery: Draw the floor, the line, the bead, and the rail from Three.js primitives and one grid shader. No photographs, generated bitmaps, textures, HDRIs, or downloaded models. The first screen is that 3D floor beside or above a frosted card, never type on a flat field. The head has no face. No people and no portraits.

3. **Stepping, growth, and the two rules** — Speed from length, including the head: step 1 at length 4 to 7 is 3.4 cells a second and 10 points; step 2 at 8 to 11 is 4.4 and 20 points; step 3 at 12 to 16 is 5.5 and 30 points; step 4 at 17 to 22 is 6.6 and 40 points; step 5 at 23 or more is 7.8 and 50 points. Points are awarded at the step in effect when the bead is taken, then length grows by one and the step is recomputed. Filling all 256 cells ends the run as a clear floor after that last score.
   Layout: One shared offset advances by cellsPerSecond times dt. Every segment i glides from its origin cell toward its target: the head targets head plus direction, and each later segment targets the cell in front of it. Short delta: subtract or add 16 so each axis delta is -1, 0, or 1. World position is the origin center plus short delta times 1.2 times offset. In Wrap, if x or z leaves the ±9.6 lip, add or subtract 19.2 so the capsule reappears on the far lip instead of streaking across the middle. In Walls, do not wrap that coordinate. When offset reaches 1, resolve one crossing before subtracting. On a successful commit, subtract 1 and continue, at most 3 commits per frame. On rail, cross, or clear, leave offset at 1 and stop. Apply the queued turn first. The upcoming cell is the head plus that direction. In Walls, a cell outside 0 to 15 ends the run with reason rail and does not commit, so the head rests on the rail. In Wrap, wrap the cell with modulo 16. If the upcoming cell is occupied by any body cell, except the tail when the grow queue is 0, end with reason cross and do not commit. Otherwise commit. If the cell is the bead, add 10 times the current step, add 1 to the grow queue, and play the eat blip before moving the cells. Unshift the new head. If the grow queue is positive, decrement it and keep the tail; otherwise pop the tail. If this crossing ate a bead, pick the next bead uniformly at random among cells not in the updated body. If none remain, end with reason clear and hide the bead. A new tail capsule scales from 0.2 to 1 in 0.18 seconds, or appears full size when reduced motion is on. Ignore a queued turn that is the reverse of the current direction. One queued turn, replaced by the latest legal key. Apply it at the crossing, then clear it. Direction map, world and screen: north (0, -1) is up, south (0, +1) is down, west (-1, 0) is left, east (1, 0) is right.
   Motion: Segments move together for the whole step, including the tail. Only a capsule that is crossing an open edge jumps to the far lip. Pause, the hidden tab, and game over stop the offset. A hitch must not skip the collision check: the while-loop of at most 3 crossings is what prevents that.
