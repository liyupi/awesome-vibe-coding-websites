## FAQ

### What does the WebGL fluid hero on the Fluid Simulation Agency Website actually do?
The Fluid Simulation Agency Website runs a real-time Navier-Stokes fluid simulation across the full viewport. Moving the cursor or dragging a finger stirs glowing electric dyes under the cutout studio wordmark, and clicking sends a radial splash through the fluid. The simulation is seeded with multi-color swirls on the first frame, pauses when scrolled off-screen, and shifts palette as visitors move between sections.

### How do I change the studio name, city and tagline in the Fluid Simulation Agency Website?
Set [BRAND] to your studio wordmark, [CITY] to your city pair, [STUDIO_TAGLINE] to your one-line positioning and [CONTACT_EMAIL] to your inquiry address, then update the copy in the content file so headlines, case studies, services, awards and contact text match your studio. The wordmark is rendered as the fluid cutout mask, so keep it short and uppercase for the strongest effect.

### Can I use the Fluid Simulation Agency Website for a client project?

The template is a Pro template, so its brief, stack and variables are shown publicly while the full prompt and the demo source code come with Pro membership. Free templates' prompts and every DESIGN.md are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only.

### What happens on phones or low-power devices with the fluid simulation?
The Fluid Simulation Agency Website includes a floating Fluid Settings chip with a low-power mode, and it falls back to a slow-moving gradient whenever WebGL is unavailable or prefers-reduced-motion is active. Touch gestures stir the fluid on mobile, the simulation pauses when off-screen, and the layout stacks the hero, Selected Work cards, Services, Process Timeline, Awards and Contact into a single readable column.

### Which sections and interactive details are included in the Fluid Simulation Agency Website?
The Fluid Simulation Agency Website is a one-page creative studio site with a fluid hero, four Selected Work case cards with code-animated previews and a detail drawer, Services, a Process Timeline, Awards and Contact. A floating Fluid Settings chip offers a palette selector, dye intensity slider, bloom toggle and low-power mode, and scrolling shifts the active fluid palette per section.
