# Animal Shelter Adoption Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [SHELTER], a no-kill shelter in [CITY] with [PETS] animals currently looking for homes. It must be honest and warm: a filterable adoptable grid (species, age, size), pet detail pages with real stories and an application, donate tiers that say what money does, volunteer roles with time commitments, and sample-labeled statistics. Pages: index.html, pet.html?pet=<slug> (first pet without a query). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is submitted or charged anywhere.

- Audience: Families and singles deciding to adopt, weekend volunteers, and donors who give $25 when a story lands. They browse on phones, fall for one animal, and need the next step to be obvious.
- Goal: Start applications and shift the stats. Within ten seconds a visitor sees the animals and the mission; within two minutes they've filtered the grid and opened one story.
- Tone: Warm, funny about animals, serious about care. Every pet gets a real personality description, not a breed chart; no guilt-tripping.

## Variables
- [SHELTER] — Shelter name (default: "Second Snout Shelter"). Invented shelter name. Used in the wordmark, title, footer, email (hello@ + shelterSlug without hyphens + .org) and localStorage keys. Never a real shelter or rescue.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Madison, Wisconsin"). Where the shelter is. Used in the hero, visit strip and footer. Real cities fine; the street invented.
- [PETS] — Pets count (default: "12"). The number of animals currently adoptable, as digits. Drives the hero strip, the grid's live count and one stat. Keep it 8–20 (the roster in content.ts has 12; adjust proportionally if changed).
- [FOCUS] — Focus (default: "dogs and cats"). The animals the shelter takes, lowercase. Drives the hero line, filter defaults and two stats. Keep it dog/cat-centric; the roster can include one small-and-furry.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/animal-shelter-adoption-website in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

Buttons: height 50px, Nunito 700 14px. Primary: Meadow fill, Card text. Donate: Tangerine fill, Card text. Secondary: 1px Meadow border, Meadow text. Inputs: height 50px, 1px Rule border, Card fill, focus Meadow border. Focus ring everywhere: 2px Meadow outline with 3px offset.
- Radius: 16px on cards, buttons, inputs, chips and photos — friendly corners everywhere. 999px only for chips, pills, stat dots and paw dots.
- Motion: Easing: --ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1) for reveals and grid shuffles; --ease-io cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for the application swap. Gentle and warm.
- Motion: Reveal: blocks wrapped in <Reveal> start at opacity 0, translateY(14px) and settle over 700ms --ease-out when 15% visible (Motion whileInView, viewport once). Siblings stagger 75ms.
- Motion: Hero: the shelter photo scales 1.04→1.0 over 2400ms; the headline rises; three paw glyphs walk in beside the mission line (staggered 200ms, off under reduced motion).
- Motion: Grid filtering: cards use Motion layout with AnimatePresence mode 'popLayout'; leaving cards fade 180ms, survivors glide 380ms --ease-io; the live count crossfades; an empty filtered grid shows 'No friends match — loosen a filter, they''re all good ones.' with Reset.
- Motion: Pet cards: hover lifts 3px and the photo scales 1.03; the one-line personality ticks from Bark to Meadow.
- Motion: Pet detail: the story's first line reveals with a Meadow underline draw; trait tags stagger 40ms; the 'good with' checks draw once.
- Motion: Application: the preselected pet's chip pulses once; invalid submit shakes 4px with inline labels; sending swaps to Confirmed (300ms --ease-io) — a drawn heart+paw stamp scaling in, 'Application received' and 'The shelter replies within two days — usually with more photos.'
- Motion: Stats: figures count up once on reveal (600ms); the '(sample)' caption never animates.
- Motion: Donate tiers: hover lifts the tier 2px; the Tangerine 'what it buys' line brightens.
- Motion: Links: .link-draw grows a 1px currentColor underline over 280ms on hover and focus-visible.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — no scales, staggers, counts or stamps; everything renders at rest. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Warm real photography, all six made for this template in one consistent style: soft natural light, cream and warm gray backgrounds, animals relaxed and well-lit — portraits, action and shelter corners; never a person (no faces, no hands). No readable text or signage. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 16px corners, no shadow; photos on Cream get a 1px Rule outline. Captions in Nunito 13px Bark. Drawn in code: the paw glyphs, heart stamp, trait tags, chips and the favicon. Icons: lucide-react only, stroke width 1.75.

## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API, no payments: the application and donate clicks save or confirm in the browser only (localStorage); the donate caption says the demo takes no payment.
- Every internal link points to a file name (./index.html, ./index.html#adopt, ./pet.html, ./pet.html?pet=mochi, ./pet.html#apply) — never to a bare directory; pet.html without a query shows the first pet, never an error.
- Self-host everything: fonts through Fontsource, icons as npm packages or inline SVG, images downloaded into public/images/. No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no Google Fonts, no analytics.
- Production build: minified, no source maps, no src/ copied into dist/, single command npm run build, output in dist/ under 10 MB.
- No real shelters, rescues, staff or animals; no photos with people, faces or hands; stats are sample-labeled; no real payment marks anywhere.
- No dates or years anywhere; ages are in years ('2y') without birth dates; the site never shows today's date.
- Never mention VibeFather or any other brand inside the site except the optional credit footer link; all copy is invented US English.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: no scales, staggers, counts or stamps; grid filters change instantly; color/border hovers stay.
- The site must work at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile with zero console errors; filters, chips, selects and the application work with keyboard only and visible focus rings.
- Keep copy concrete and kind: no lorem, no guilt-tripping; every fee, trait and story line lives in src/content.ts.
