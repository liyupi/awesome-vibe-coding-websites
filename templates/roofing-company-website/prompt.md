# Roofing Company Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
Build a one-page site for [BRAND], the roofing company founded by [FOUNDER] and run out of [CITY], with [EXPERIENCE] of reroofing behind it. The page sells finished craft with a golden-hour hero, proves it with a before/after slider and a materials table, and wins the lead with two working tools: a roof cost estimator that computes a visible price range and days on site, and a storm damage checklist that grades what the homeowner saw. A license-and-warranty block, a drawn service-area map, sample financing note and an inspection request form close the page. Every price, rate and review is invented sample data.

- Audience: Primary: homeowners in and around [CITY] comparing roofers after a storm, an aging roof or a purchase — people who want a real number before they talk to anyone. Secondary: trades contractors adapting the layout and estimator to siding, windows or solar.
- Goal: Move a visitor from 'this company looks solid' to a submitted inspection request: earn trust with specifics in one screen, let the estimator answer the money question honestly without capture, then convert that momentum into the short form at the bottom.
- Tone: Confident, plainspoken trades talk — short sentences, exact numbers, no chest-thumping. Writes like the owner [FOUNDER] explaining the job across a kitchen table, never like an ad agency.

- Setup:

  - Install Tailwind CSS 3.4 with PostCSS and Autrefixer. Extend the theme with the exact palette below, fontFamily.display 'Oswald' and fontFamily.body 'Inter'.
  - Self-host Oswald weights 500, 600 and 700 and Inter weights 400, 500, 600 and 700 through @fontsource/oswald and @fontsource/inter, imported in src/main.tsx. Never load fonts from a CDN.
  - Use Motion from 'motion/react' (motion, useInView, useReducedMotion, animate) for section reveals, stat count-ups and slider handle spring; gate every animation on useReducedMotion().
  - Keep all copy, rates and data in src/content.ts: brand strings, hero copy, service cards, estimator config (pitch profiles with area factors and labor surcharges, material rate ranges, squares-per-day divisors, tear-off rates), checklist items with weights and tier copy, materials table rows, reviews, service towns with drive times, financing note, form town options. When variable values change, rewrite the dependent copy there and derive storage keys and the tel: link from [BRAND] and [PHONE] so no default-brand remnants remain.
  - Estimator math, exactly: squares = ceil(footprint × pitchAreaFactor ÷ 100) where pitchAreaFactor is 1.05 / 1.12 / 1.20 / 1.41 for the four profiles; installed rate per square is asphalt $425–515, metal $725–875, tile $950–1,150; steep surcharge adds 8% (Steep) or 15% (Very steep) to both bounds; tear-off, when on, adds $95–130 per square; days = ceil(squares ÷ divisor) + 1 if tear-off and squares over 18, divisor 15 asphalt / 12 metal / 9 tile, minimum 1, shown as 'N–N+1 days'; round both price bounds to the nearest $100. Every input, the chosen pitch name and material persist to localStorage under [BRAND_SLUG]-estimate.
  - Build the four pitch profiles as inline SVG gable cross-sections drawn in code (a right-triangle roof with the rise labeled by angle only — 4/12, 6/12, 9/12, 12/12 — no text beyond the name and pitch fraction), each in a selectable card that highlights in safety orange when active.
  - Build the service-area map as an inline SVG: a charcoal panel, [CITY] as an orange star with a 'Shop' label, six sky-blue town dots connected by thin dashed roads, one large dashed sky-blue circle labeled '40-mile service radius'. Invent the dot positions; towns and drive times come from content.ts.

  - Use only these lucide-react icons: Phone, CalendarCheck, ShieldCheck, HardHat, Home, Wind, Droplets, CloudRain, Wrench, CheckCircle2, Star, MapPin, ChevronRight, ArrowRight, Clock, Menu, X, ChevronLeft.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: Sections separated by 96px on desktop, 64px on mobile; section headers use a 14px letter-spaced orange eyebrow above an Oswald 600 title at clamp(30px, 3.4vw, 46px); cards carry 28px internal padding and 24px gaps in grids.
- Radius: Hard-edged: 2px on cards and chips, 0 on buttons and tables — square corners are the identity; only photos get 6px.
- Motion: Fade-rise reveals: sections and cards enter with a 16px rise and 500ms ease, children staggered 60–90ms, driven by useInView once.
- Motion: Stat numbers count up over 900ms when the proof bar enters view; the estimator price tweens over 150ms on change.
- Motion: Before/after handle follows the pointer directly while dragging and settles with a 200ms spring on release; checklist checks pop 1.15× over 150ms.
- Motion: Every animation is disabled instantly under prefers-reduced-motion; no parallax, no scroll-jacking, no autoplay media.
- Imagery: Golden-hour architectural photography from one shoot: finished roofs, roofline details and material textures, raking warm light, deep shadows, no crews, no tools, no vehicles, no readable text or signage anywhere. Charcoal shingles and warm terracotta tones echo the palette.

## Don'ts
- No real roofing manufacturers, product lines, shingle color names or warranty programs — materials are described generically as architectural asphalt, standing-seam metal and clay tile.
- No real contractors, franchises, certification badges or inspection seals; [BRAND], license #RC-88412, reviews, towns and the address are invented sample data.
- Photos must show roofs, rooflines and materials only — no crews, no faces, no vehicles, no readable text, signage, license plates or brand marks anywhere.
- All prices, rates, financing terms and drive times are labeled sample data for illustration; never present them as quotes or an offer of credit, and never call the financing section a lender's terms.
- No dates anywhere on the page: no year in the footer, no '© 2026', no date pickers tied to the real clock; time references stay relative ('same-week', '48-hour response').
- Forms are local-only samples: no submit to any server, no APIs, no analytics, no email or calendar services. Persist to localStorage only and validate restored data.
- No backend, server code, databases or auth. Static output in dist/ from the single build command, under 20 MB, with Vite's base relative.
- Self-host fonts through the Fontsource packages only; never load Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts or any CDN stylesheet or script.
- No source maps in production and no src/ copied into dist/.
- No Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Google Images, placeholder-image services or hotlinked files; use only the six generated photos and code-drawn SVG art.
- No SEO work: no meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, sitemaps or keyword-stuffed headings. A plain title with [BRAND] and a favicon are enough.
- The estimator must never guess silently: keep the rate table and every surcharge visible in content.ts and reflected in the on-screen line items.
- No scroll-jacking, page parallax, custom cursor or autoplay media; motion limited to the specified reveals, count-ups, tweens and the slider spring, all respecting prefers-reduced-motion.
- Keep the mobile header on one line at 390px (logomark, call button, menu button) and never let the sticky bottom bar cover the inspection form's submit button.
- Build brand-derived strings (storage keys, tel: link, slugs) from [BRAND] and [PHONE] so alternate variable values never leave default-brand remnants.
- Every internal link is a file name or in-page anchor, never a bare directory; the site stays one index.html.
