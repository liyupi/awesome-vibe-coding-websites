Roofing Company Website is a single-page site for a residential roofing contractor, built around one signature tool: a roof cost estimator that turns home footprint, roof pitch, material and tear-off into a realistic price range and days-on-site figure, with the rate math shown on screen. Around it sit the things homeowners actually look for — a storm damage checklist that grades what you saw, a materials comparison table with sample per-square rates, a draggable before-and-after reroof slider, a license and warranty block with specific numbers, and a service-area map drawn in code. The look is trade-confidence, not blue-corporate: slate charcoal ground, safety orange signals, sky-blue data accents and a bold condensed display face, with golden-hour photography of finished roofs. The page ends in an inspection request form that completes inline and stays in the browser. It suits any contractor or trades business that quotes jobs — roofers, but also siding, windows or solar installers happy to re-point the estimator's rate table. Swap the brand, city, owner, phone and experience years through five variables; every rate, town and review is sample data in one content file, ready to be replaced with the real numbers.

## Make it yours

- A storm-restoration roofer: set BRAND and CITY, keep the storm damage checklist, and rewrite the estimator rates to match your insurance-driven tear-off and decking jobs.
- A siding and window contractor: swap the materials table rows for siding and window products, re-point the per-square rates, and keep the before-and-after slider for exterior transformations.
- A solar installer: replace the roof pitch profiles with roof-type options, change the estimator to panel counts and installed rates, and keep the service-area map for your coverage towns.
- A multi-town roofing company: add your towns and drive times to the service map, set CITY to your main shop, and update the license and warranty block with your own numbers.

## FAQ

### How does the roof cost estimator on the Roofing Company Website template calculate a price?
The estimator on the Roofing Company Website template turns home footprint, roof pitch, material and tear-off into a price range and a days-on-site figure. It converts footprint to squares using the pitch area factor, applies the installed rate per square for asphalt, metal or tile, adds a steep surcharge and tear-off cost, then rounds both bounds to the nearest $100. The rate math is shown on screen, and every rate is sample data in the content file.

### What should I change first when setting up the Roofing Company Website template for my business?
Start with the five variables: BRAND, CITY, FOUNDER, PHONE and EXPERIENCE. The Roofing Company Website template rewrites the hero, service map, towns list, footer address and warranty copy around them, and derives the tel: link and storage keys from BRAND and PHONE. Then replace the sample rates, towns, drive times and reviews in the content file with your real numbers before publishing.

### Can I adapt the Roofing Company Website template for a siding, window or solar installer?
Yes. The Roofing Company Website template is built for any trades contractor that quotes jobs. Re-point the estimator's rate table, pitch profiles and tear-off assumptions to siding, windows or solar work, rewrite the materials comparison rows and the storm damage checklist wording, and keep the inspection request form as the lead capture. The layout, charcoal-and-orange palette and before-and-after slider stay as they are.
