# Spa and Massage Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [BRAND], an independent day spa on [STREET] in [CITY], offering [SPECIALTIES]. It must feel like a real, restful local business: atmospheric treatment-room photography, six clearly priced treatments with durations, three therapist profiles, three bookable packages, a customizable gift-card preview saved on the device, first-visit guidance, opening hours, and a four-step booking flow — treatment, therapist, time, confirm — ending in a persistent browser-only reference card. Pages: index.html (home) and booking.html (book a treatment). Everything runs in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

- Audience: Adults within a short drive choosing a first massage, regular guests looking for their preferred therapist, and people buying a thoughtful gift. Many visit on phones after work; they want clear prices, a sense of the room, practical first-visit answers and a booking path that does not require a phone call.
- Goal: Turn quiet interest into a confident appointment. Within ten seconds a visitor should see the spa atmosphere, understand that treatments start at $65, and find the booking action. The complete flow should take under a minute and remain understandable without accounts or online payment.
- Tone: Calm, warm and precise, like a considerate receptionist who never rushes a guest. Short sentences, concrete minutes and dollars, no mystical wellness language, no medical promises, no exclamation marks. Use US English throughout.

- Motion: Hero: the photograph scales from 1.045 to 1 over 1800ms on load; the two headline lines rise 24px and fade in with 140ms stagger. The small appointment note fades in after 500ms. No scroll parallax is required.
- Motion: Treatment rows: hover shifts the title 6px right, changes the border to Clay and reveals the arrow over 250ms. Keyboard focus produces the same state without hiding the price or duration.
- Motion: Therapist cards: portrait scales to 1.025 over 600ms on hover and the booking underline draws left to right; no card tilt.
- Motion: Gift card: amount changes crossfade the price over 200ms and the recipient name updates immediately; saving opens a small confirmation drawer that slides up 12px and fades over 300ms.
- Motion: Booking: step changes use AnimatePresence mode 'wait', crossfading with a 16px horizontal slide over 280ms. Selection chips fill over 150ms. The confirmation check stroke draws over 500ms and the reference fades in; no typewriter effect.
- Motion: FAQ: height 0 to auto and opacity 0 to 1 over 320ms --ease-io; the Plus icon rotates 45 degrees.
- Motion: Header: solid Porcelain at 96% with backdrop blur 12px from the start; after 24px scroll a 1px Rule bottom border fades in. Mobile menu slides from the right over 280ms.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — render every reveal and hero element at rest, disable image scaling, step slides, check drawing and smooth scrolling; retain instant state changes, color hovers and focus indicators. Use CSS media queries and Motion useReducedMotion.

## Don'ts
- No backend, server code, API routes, database, authentication, analytics or third-party API calls. Booking and gift-card data stay in localStorage; no payment, email, text or reservation is actually sent.
- Every internal link points to an HTML file or hash target such as ./index.html#treatments and ./booking.html?treatment=warm-stone, never a bare directory. booking.html works when opened directly and invalid query values fall back safely.
- Self-host every asset: images downloaded into public/images/, fonts through Fontsource and icons through lucide-react. No CDN scripts or links, Google Fonts, stock-photo hotlinks, map embeds or placeholder-image services.
- Production output must be minified static files in dist/ from npm run build, with no source maps and no src/ copied into dist/. Keep dist/ under 12 MB and use Vite base './'.
- Use only the invented spa identity and original copy. No real spa logos, slogans, treatment menus, prices, branded products or recognizable landmarks. All visible copy and alt text is US English.
- The three therapist portraits must remain fictional people not resembling any real individual. Never substitute celebrity, client or identifiable real-person photographs. No real person likenesses elsewhere.
- No medical, diagnostic or therapeutic outcome claims. Massage is presented as a relaxation service; pregnancy, injury and health conditions are referred to a qualified healthcare professional.
- Do not imply that a browser-saved booking or gift card is a transmitted reservation or paid order. The confirmation and gift preview must clearly say they are local demonstrations.
- Never mention VibeFather inside the site except the optional [CREDIT] footer link. Do not add pricing, membership or license copy for VibeFather.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere: remove reveal offsets, image scaling, step slides and check drawing while preserving usable state changes and focus styles.
- The site must work at 1440x900 and 390x844 with zero console errors. Every step is keyboard usable, the mobile menu and accordions manage focus correctly, all tap targets are at least 44px, and sticky UI never covers fields or confirmation content.
- Keep all copy, services, schedules, prices and sample data in src/content.ts. No lorem ipsum, filler testimonials, hard-coded content outside the content file or wellness clichés without practical detail.
