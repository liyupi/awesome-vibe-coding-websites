# Funeral Home Website

> A gentle, dignified funeral home site with transparent pricing, a pre-planning checklist and a local guestbook.

Funeral Home Website is a single-page site for a family funeral home, in soft sage, deep slate and warm ivory with a graceful serif — quiet, warm rather than grey, and designed so that nothing on the page adds work at the worst time. The practical spine: arrangement options in family language (burial, cremation, green burial, celebration of life); a transparent price list presented kindly, with what's included and a plain note that no one has to decide today; a five-step pre-planning checklist that saves progress in the browser; an obituaries list with a sample tribute page and a browser-local guestbook where visitors can light a candle; grief resources written as help, not upsell; and a 24/7 contact block that leads with the phone number. Motion is nearly invisible — slow fades only. Photography shows a calm chapel, memorial gardens, flowers and candlelight — no caskets in close-up, no people. It suits funeral homes, memorial chapels and celebrant services. Five variables swap home name, town, director, phone and years; every family, price and tribute is invented sample data.

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add funeral-home-website` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A memorial chapel: set BRAND and CITY to the chapel, keep the arrangement options and price list, and replace the obituaries with tributes for services held in your own chapel.
- An independent celebrant: change FOUNDER to your name, rewrite the arrangement options around celebration-of-life ceremonies, and link the contact block to your own phone line.
- A family funeral home taking pre-planning enquiries: keep the five-step checklist, rewrite the financial step for your prepaid options, and point the conversation booking at your director.
- A green burial provider: lead the arrangement options with green burial, keep the garden photography, and rewrite the price rows to describe natural burial plots and services.

## FAQ

### How do I change the funeral home name, town, director and phone number in this template?
The Funeral Home Website template is driven by five variables: BRAND, CITY, FOUNDER, PHONE and EXPERIENCE. Set them once and every headline, the pre-planning copy, the obituary service lines, the footer and the tel: link follow. The prompt asks you to rewrite the dependent copy in the content file so no default name, town or number from Willowmere, Ashton Mill or Eleanor Voss remains.

### Does the pre-planning checklist and guestbook actually save what visitors type?
The Funeral Home Website template keeps both in the visitor's own browser. The five-step pre-planning checklist, with its progress line and sage bar, persists under a storage key built from BRAND, and the guestbook entries and candle count persist under a second key. Nothing is sent to a server, and the page says the demo guestbook lives in your browser only, so treat it as a demonstration of the flow.

### What does the price list show and can I put my own funeral packages in it?
The Funeral Home Website template shows three sample packages: Simple cremation, Traditional service and Celebration of life, each with what is included, plus a note that itemized lists are always provided and no one decides today. All prices are invented sample data. Replace them with your own packages and figures in the content file, keeping the same kind, unhurried presentation and the note underneath.

### How do I adapt this funeral home site for a memorial chapel or a celebrant service?
The Funeral Home Website template suits funeral homes, memorial chapels and celebrant services. Set BRAND and CITY to the chapel, change FOUNDER to the celebrant or director, and rewrite the arrangement options and price rows in their language. Swap the chapel and garden photography for your own rooms, and adjust the obituaries and tribute copy so the sample families read as plausible for your area.
