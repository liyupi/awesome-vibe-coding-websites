# Safari Lodge Website — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Charcoal** `#1C1A16` — Page ground everywhere — warm near-black, never pure #000; base of the hero scrim.
- **Charcoal raised** `#26231D` — Rates table header, suite card hovers, inquiry panel ground.
- **Field line** `#3A372F` — Hairlines, table rules, timeline track, card borders — structure instead of shadows.
- **Ochre** `#C98A3B` — Primary golden accent: section labels, prices, timeline dots, active chips, the CTA underline.
- **Ochre light** `#E4B36A` — Hover and emphasis states on ochre: link hover, price emphasis, the active timeline stop's time.
- **Olive** `#6E6F4C` — Secondary accent: suite tags, experience icons' color, the 'sample data' chips.
- **Bone** `#EFE7D8` — Primary text — warm off-white for headlines and body on charcoal.
- **Sand mist** `#A79C88` — Secondary text: intros, captions, meta rows, table body copy.

## Typography
- **Display — headlines, suite names, timeline stop titles (refined serif)** — Fraunces (OFL), weights 500/600. Self-host the font files.
- **Text — body copy, table cells, form labels, uppercase section labels (tracked 0.16em)** — Work Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Wide and slow: sections py-32 (128px) desktop / py-20 (80px) mobile; content max-w-6xl px-6/px-10; photos bleed full-width; 1px fieldline hairlines carry structure instead of boxes.
- Radius: Almost square: 2px on images, cards, buttons and inputs — the cinematic look depends on it; nothing rounder anywhere.

## Motion
- Hero: the photograph scales 1.05→1 over 3s on load and parallaxes at 0.12 of scroll (capped 14%); the bottom scrim deepens slightly as the headline settles.
- Reveals: sections, cards and table rows fade up (y 26→0, 700ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)) with 90ms stagger via Motion whileInView, once.
- Day timeline: each dot fills ochre (200ms) as its stop crosses 45% of the viewport, and that stop's time label brightens to ochre light; past dots rest at 50% ochre.
- Suite and experience cards hover-lift 2px and turn their fieldline border to ochre (200ms); arrows nudge 2px.
- Inquiry chips toggle to ochre ground with charcoal text (200ms); steppers tick with a small y-bounce.
- The grain overlay never animates — it is the constant of the page.
- prefers-reduced-motion: parallax and load zoom off, all reveals become plain 200ms opacity fades, timeline dots fill without transition.

## Imagery
One shoot, one hour of light: cinematic golden-hour landscape photography — warm ochre sun low over olive bush and charcoal shadow, hazy amber skies, backlit dust, visible warm grain, wide filmic compositions. Animals only distant in frame; people at most a ranger silhouette from behind; no identifiable faces, no text, no logos. The six photographs are generated by VibeFather, downloaded into public/images, with the warm grain overlay sitting over them too.

## Tone
Quiet, warm, unhurried; short declarative sentences with the precision of a field notebook; luxury expressed through restraint and light, never superlatives.
