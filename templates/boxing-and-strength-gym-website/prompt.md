# Boxing and Strength Gym Website

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A two-page website for [BRAND], a boutique gym in [CITY] teaching [DISCIPLINE] to [AUDIENCE]. It looks bold and gritty: near-black and concrete grey with one blood-orange signal accent, huge condensed uppercase headlines, grainy duotone-graded photographs of bags, gloves, ropes, rings and kettlebells (never faces), and diagonal tape-stripe dividers. Page 1 (index.html) is a long single page: hero, class types, today's sessions, membership pricing with a monthly / 10-class pack toggle, coaches with monogram avatars, results and member quotes, a validated free-trial booking form with an on-page ticket confirmation, a beginner FAQ and a visit band. Page 2 (timetable.html) is a filterable weekly timetable (days × times on desktop, day tabs on mobile) with a slide-in class detail drawer. Everything is static and client-side.

- Audience: [AUDIENCE] in [CITY] who are curious about boxing but nervous about walking into a fight gym, plus members checking tonight's classes on their phone. They want to see what each class is like, whether it suits their level, what it costs, and how to try it free.
- Goal: Turn a nervous visitor into a booked free trial class. The hero, class cards, timetable drawer and pricing all lead to the trial form; the FAQ and coach cards remove the fear of getting hurt or being out of place. Secondary goal: make the timetable the fastest way for members to find a session by type and level.
- Tone: Short, direct and warm under the grit, like a good coach: imperative headlines in caps, plain sentences in the body, concrete numbers (times, prices, kilos, capacities), no hype words, no fight-promo bravado, no exclamation marks. Never mocking beginners.

## Don'ts
- No backend of any kind: no server code, API routes, databases, auth, analytics, booking services, payment links, email services or third-party APIs. The trial form calls preventDefault and only changes local React state; it never submits, stores or sends anything.
- Do not load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any CDN. Self-host through the Fontsource packages only.
- No CDN <script>, <link> or import maps. Every script, style, font, icon and image is in the project and bundled into dist/.
- No hotlinked images: every photo is downloaded into public/images/ once and served from there, and every third-party file is listed in ASSETS.md. No Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay or other stock sites with their own licenses.
- No source maps in the production build (build.sourcemap false), and src/ is not copied into dist/.
- Relative asset paths (Vite base './'). Internal links are hash anchors or file names such as ./index.html, ./index.html#trial, ./index.html?class=mon-1730#trial and ./timetable.html?type=engine, never a bare directory or a leading slash.
