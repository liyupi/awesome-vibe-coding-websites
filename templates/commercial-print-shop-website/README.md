Commercial Print Shop Website is a single-page site for a local print shop, dressed in its own trade: process cyan, magenta and yellow on black-and-white structure, with registration marks, CMYK bars and halftone fields drawn in code and deliberate misregistration in the headings. The signature tool is the instant quote calculator: product, size, paper, quantity and turnaround produce a price with every factor visible — unit rate, quantity break, turnaround multiplier — all from one table in the code. Around it: a five-product catalog in print-buyer language (business cards to booklets, each quotable in one tap), a file-prep guide written like a pressman's checklist, an honestly simulated file-upload chip attached to the quote request, a turnaround promise with real cutoffs, and pickup-or-delivery logistics. Photography shows ink, paper and presses — no people, no readable job tickets. It suits print shops, sign makers and promo-product houses re-pointing the calculator. Five variables swap shop name, town, foreman, phone and years; every price and spec is invented sample data.

## Make it yours

- A sign maker: keep the calculator, replace the five print products with banner and rigid-sign sizes, and rewrite the file-prep checklist around vector artwork and bleed.
- A promo-product house: re-point the quote table to apparel and branded goods, swap the catalog photos for product shots and keep the pickup-or-delivery logistics section.
- A university print bureau: set BRAND to the campus shop, use CITY for the building, and add student-friendly turnaround copy alongside the same-day cutoff.
- A family print shop: put the owner's name in FOUNDER, set EXPERIENCE to the shop's years, and rewrite the hero to lead with counter service and local pickup.

## FAQ

### How does the instant quote calculator on the Commercial Print Shop Website work?
The quote calculator on the Commercial Print Shop Website takes product, size, paper, quantity and turnaround and returns a price with every factor printed on screen: the unit base, size factor, stock factor, quantity break and turnaround multiplier. All of it reads from one table in the code, so you can change a rate without touching the layout. Prices are invented sample data.

### Can I change the print products and prices in the Commercial Print Shop Website template?
Yes. The Commercial Print Shop Website template keeps its catalog and quote table in one content file, so you can edit the five products, their sizes, paper chips, quantity breaks and turnaround multipliers in one place. Swap business cards, flyers, banners, stickers and booklets for your own range, and replace the sample rates with your shop's real numbers.

### What are the CITY, BRAND, PHONE, FOUNDER and EXPERIENCE variables for on the Commercial Print Shop Website?
The five variables on the Commercial Print Shop Website set the shop name, town, phone number, press foreman and years of experience. They appear in the hero, the file-prep guide, the pickup copy, the footer and the tel: link. Change them and rewrite the dependent copy so no default-brand text remains anywhere on the page.

### Does the Commercial Print Shop Website actually upload customer files?
No. The file-upload chip on the Commercial Print Shop Website is honestly simulated: clicking it attaches a fixed sample chip and a note saying nothing uploads. No real file input sends data and there is no backend. For live jobs you would connect the quote request to your own form service or email, then replace the demo chip with a real upload.

### What should I change first in the Commercial Print Shop Website template?
Start with the quote table in the Commercial Print Shop Website template, because every number on the page flows from it. Replace the sample unit bases, stock factors, quantity breaks and turnaround multipliers with your shop's rates, then set CITY, BRAND, PHONE, FOUNDER and EXPERIENCE. After that, rewrite the file-prep checklist and turnaround cutoffs to match how your press actually runs.

### How do I build a commercial print shop website with AI?
Copy the prompt from this page or run npx vibefather add commercial-print-shop-website, replace [BRAND], [CITY], [FOUNDER] and [PHONE] with your own content, and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent. The live demos were built from exactly that text.
