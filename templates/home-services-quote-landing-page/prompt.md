# Home Services Quote Landing Page

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page lead-generation website for [BRAND], a [TRADE] company serving [CITY], reachable 24/7 at [PHONE]. It must convert like a real trades landing page: a sticky emergency call bar that survives scrolling, an instant quote estimator above the fold (job type, home size, urgency) that computes a live price range, six service cards with flat-rate from-prices, a service-area ZIP checker with an invented neighborhood list, a guarantees strip, homeowner reviews, a four-step process strip and a call-now finale. One page: index.html. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is submitted anywhere.

- Audience: Homeowners in an emergency (burst pipe, dead boiler, no hot water) searching on a phone at 9pm, plus planners comparing quotes for renovations. Skewed 35–70, price-sensitive but primed by urgency; they want a price range, a real human on the phone and proof the company is licensed and local.
- Goal: Produce phone calls and booked estimate visits. Within ten seconds a visitor should see the emergency number, the arrival promise and the start of the estimator; within two minutes, a price range for their job.
- Tone: Plain, fast and honest, like a good dispatcher. Short declarative sentences, concrete numbers (minutes, dollars, years), no exclamation marks, no 'family owned and operated' clichés without specifics.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Company name (default: "Trueflow Plumbing & Heating"). Invented trade company name. Used in the wordmark, title tag, footer, email (dispatch@ + brandSlug without hyphens + .com) and localStorage keys. Never a real franchise or company.
- [TRADE] — Trade (default: "Plumbing & Heating"). What the company does. Drives the six service cards, the estimator's job list, the process strip wording and the FAQ. Keep the same counts: 6 services, 8 job types, 3 sizes, 3 urgencies.
- [CITY] — City (default: "Columbus, Ohio"). Where the company works. Used in the title, hero, area map label and footer. Neighborhoods and ZIP prefixes stay invented-sounding; only the city may be real.
- [PHONE] — Phone (default: "(614) 555-0184"). The 24/7 line, formatted like a local number with a 555 exchange. Used in the emergency bar, hero, CTA buttons (tel: links built from digits) and the footer.
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/home-services-quote-landing-page in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — disable the bar slide, pulse, count-ups, draws, staggered pins and layout animations (render at rest). Color and border hover changes stay. Use CSS @media in index.css plus Motion's useReducedMotion in components.
- Imagery: Real photography only, documentary and workmanlike, all six made for this template in one consistent style: natural daylight, navy and white with orange tool accents, clean job sites, fine grain. Never a person: vans, tools, installs, parts and details — no faces, no hands. No readable text, logos or branding on vans, tools or parts. Every photo uses object-fit cover with the object-position given under Assets, 10px corners, no shadow; photos on Mist get a 1px Rule outline. Captions under photos in Barlow 13px Slate. ## Don'ts
- No backend, no form submission, no fetch to any API: the estimator, ZIP check and lead form all resolve in the browser only (localStorage); nothing is sent anywhere, and the form says so.
