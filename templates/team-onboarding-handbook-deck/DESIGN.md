# Team Onboarding Handbook Deck — Design System

Apply this design system to an existing project. It describes the look and feel only; for the full site, use prompt.md.

## Colors
- **Warm Cream** `#FAF7F2` — Primary editorial paper slide background
- **Espresso Ink** `#1E1B18` — Headings, dark anchor card, and high-contrast typography
- **Sage Green** `#2F855A` — Active checks, completed milestones, and primary accents
- **Soft Apricot** `#F6AD55` — Warm highlights, ritual tags, and spotlight badges
- **Warm Taupe** `#6B6259` — Secondary labels and notes

## Typography
- **Warm editorial slide headlines** — Fraunces (OFL), weights 600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Readable handbook prose and UI** — Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL), weights 400/500/600/700. Self-host the font files.
- **Slide indices, checklists, and ritual times** — JetBrains Mono (OFL), weights 400/500/600. Self-host the font files.

## Spacing & shape
- Spacing: Generous 16:9 widescreen composition with bold 52px desktop serif headline paired with a warm dark espresso 90-day preview anchor card.
- Radius: 20px for slide canvas, 14px for directory and checklist cards, 999px for pills.

## Motion
- Smooth checklist completion progress ring & slide transitions.
- Honors prefers-reduced-motion; all content visible immediately.

## Imagery
100% code-drawn warm editorial visuals: 30/60/90 milestone progression arc, weekly rituals grid, checklist progress bar, and Lucide icons.

## Tone
Warm, human, transparent, reassuring, and structured.
