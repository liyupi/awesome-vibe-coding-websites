Team Onboarding Handbook Deck is a warm, welcoming 11-slide 16:9 browser presentation built for remote-first studios and product teams welcoming new hires. It combines editorial book-like typography (Fraunces paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono) with full presentation controls: keyboard arrow and Spacebar navigation, touch swipe support, a G-key thumbnail overview grid, an N-key presenter notes drawer with live session timer, F-key fullscreen mode, and clean @media print landscape PDF export. Across 11 thoughtfully paced slides, new teammates explore the studio welcome, mission and operating principles, a filterable interactive org and who-to-ask directory, core toolstack and async communication norms, a visual weekly rituals schedule, 30 / 60 / 90-day onboarding milestones, an interactive First-Week Checklist whose check-off progress saves persistently in localStorage, a benefits and time-off snapshot, and a curated helpful links closing slide. Anchored in warm cream (#FAF7F2) and espresso ink (#1E1B18) with sage green (#2F855A) and soft apricot (#F6AD55), it feels human and calm from the very first slide. Update the studio name, welcome headline, onboarding buddy, and headquarters label in src/content.ts.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Studio name | Hearthstone Studio |
| `[WELCOME_TITLE]` | Cover headline | Welcome Home: Your First 90 Days With Us |
| `[BUDDY_NAME]` | Onboarding host | Hannah Lin, People Lead |
| `[STUDIO_LOCATION]` | Remote footprint | Remote-First · 14 Cities |
| `[CORE_VALUE]` | Culture principle | Write it down, protect deep work, assume goodwill. |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add team-onboarding-handbook-deck` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A remote-first design studio: set BRAND to your studio, WELCOME_TITLE to your welcome line, and swap the org directory filters for your real departments and who-to-ask contacts.
- A distributed engineering guild: replace the four operating principles with your engineering values and rewrite the weekly rituals slide for your standup, review and demo cadence.
- A people operations team at a growing startup: keep the 30/60/90 milestones and first-week checklist, then edit the benefits and time-off slide to match your actual policies.
- A founder welcoming a first hire: set BUDDY_NAME to yourself, shorten the org directory to a few roles, and point the closing helpful links slide at your internal docs and coffee chat calendar.

## FAQ

### What slides are included in the Team Onboarding Handbook Deck?
The Team Onboarding Handbook Deck is an 11-slide 16:9 browser presentation. It covers a welcome cover with a 30/60/90 readiness overview, mission, four operating principles, a filterable org and who-to-ask directory, the toolstack and async norms, weekly rituals, 30/60/90 milestones, an interactive first-week checklist, benefits and time off, feedback and growth reviews, and a closing helpful links slide.

### How does the persistent First-Week Checklist work for new hires?
The Team Onboarding Handbook Deck's first-week checklist is eight clickable tasks on slide eight. Check-off progress saves in the browser's localStorage, so a new hire can close the deck and return to the same checked items. A live completion bar shows how far through the checklist they are, and the checks use the sage green accent.

### What keyboard shortcuts and presenter tools does the onboarding deck include?
The Team Onboarding Handbook Deck supports arrow keys, Spacebar, Home and End for navigation, plus touch swipe on mobile. Press G for a thumbnail overview grid, N for presenter notes with a live session timer, and F for fullscreen. Print styling exports the slides as a landscape PDF, and on screens under 768px the shortcut hints are replaced with a swipe instruction.

### How do I change the studio name, buddy and welcome headline in this deck?
Edit the variables in the deck's content file: set BRAND to your studio name, WELCOME_TITLE to your cover headline, BUDDY_NAME to the onboarding host, STUDIO_LOCATION to your remote footprint, and CORE_VALUE to your culture principle. The default copy is written for the sample studio, so rewrite each headline, paragraph and label to match your values while keeping the same length and tone.

### Can I customize the org directory and weekly rituals for my own team?
Yes. The Team Onboarding Handbook Deck's who-to-ask directory has department filters for Product, Design, Engineering and Ops, and the weekly rituals slide lists a Monday kickoff, Tuesday crit, Thursday demo and Friday quiet focus. Swap the people, departments and ritual names for your own team's structure and meeting rhythm in the content file, keeping the card layout and tags.
