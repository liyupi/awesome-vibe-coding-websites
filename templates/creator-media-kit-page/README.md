# Creator Media Kit Page

> An editorial media kit with animated audience stats, partnership cases, rates, and a sponsor inquiry flow.

Creator Media Kit Page is a one-page sponsorship site for independent video makers, newsletter writers, educators, and creative personalities. It gives a prospective partner the whole story in a deliberate sequence: a personal introduction, a compact reach snapshot, animated audience charts, channel formats, campaign examples, transparent packages, and a focused inquiry form. All people, partners, prices, and performance figures are fictional; sample-data labels remain visible wherever numbers appear.

The visual system pairs warm paper surfaces and fine editorial rules with dark ink, persimmon accents, expressive Newsreader headlines, and compact mono labels. A spacious working portrait anchors the first screen, while a second studio photograph adds texture to the collaboration story. Hand-built SVG charts animate into view and remain readable without motion. Campaign covers are original code-drawn compositions, not client logos or borrowed advertising.

Three rate packages connect directly to the inquiry form. Choosing a package updates the form, and valid entries produce a copyable project brief with an explicit notice that nothing has been sent. This keeps the demo useful without a backend or false submission state. Responsive layouts, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion behavior are part of the specification.

Change the creator name, niche, audience, location, and hero tagline through five variables, then replace the channel data, case studies, and rates in one content file. React and Tailwind CSS support reusable cards, chart components, accessible disclosures, and client-side form state. The finished site builds to static files with local fonts and images, no external APIs, and no analytics.

## Variables

Replace these in the prompt with your own values; everything else stays as written.

| Variable | What it is | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| `[BRAND]` | Creator name or public identity | Nora Vale |
| `[CREATOR_NICHE]` | Creator subject and content niche | small-space gardening and mindful city living |
| `[AUDIENCE]` | Primary content audience | urban renters, first-time gardeners, and slow-living enthusiasts |
| `[LOCATION]` | Creator home base | Portland, Oregon |
| `[MEDIA_KIT_TAGLINE]` | Media kit hero tagline | Make room for a greener everyday. |

## How to use it

1. Copy [prompt.md](prompt.md), or run `npx vibefather add creator-media-kit-page` in your project.
2. Replace the `[VARIABLES]` with your own brand, copy and details.
3. Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another AI coding agent and let it build. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds the colors, type and spacing on their own if you only want the look.

## Make it yours

- A newsletter writer: set BRAND to your publication name, swap channel formats for newsletter placements, and rewrite the rate packages around sponsored issues and classified slots.
- A freelance educator: use CREATOR_NICHE for your teaching subject, replace campaign cases with workshop and course partnerships, and label audience charts with student and subscriber figures.
- A video essayist: keep the audience charts, change channel formats to long-form and short-form video, and rewrite partnership cases around brand integrations and series sponsorships.
- A podcast host: set MEDIA_KIT_TAGLINE to your show promise, replace channel formats with episode and segment placements, and adjust rates for host-read ads and bonus content.

## FAQ

### What sections does the Creator Media Kit Page template include?
The Creator Media Kit Page template is a one-page site with a header and footer, a creator introduction hero, a compact reach snapshot, animated audience charts, channel formats, fictional partnership cases, three rate packages, and a sponsor inquiry form. The page also includes a collaboration section with a second studio photograph. All sample people, partners, prices, and performance figures are fictional, and sample-data labels stay visible wherever numbers appear.

### How do I change the creator name, niche, and audience in the Creator Media Kit Page template?
Set the creator name, niche, audience, location, and hero tagline through the five variables BRAND, CREATOR_NICHE, AUDIENCE, LOCATION, and MEDIA_KIT_TAGLINE. Then replace the channel data, case studies, and rates in the single content file. When variables change, rewrite every dependent headline, paragraph, label, image alt text, and default-only reference so the copy stays coherent with the new creator identity.

### How does the sponsor inquiry form work on the Creator Media Kit Page template?
The sponsor inquiry form on the Creator Media Kit Page template is a client-only draft. Choosing one of the three rate packages updates the form selection, and valid entries produce a copyable project brief with an explicit notice that nothing has been sent. The template has no backend and no external APIs, so it never shows a false submission state. You can connect the copied brief to your own email or form service later.

### Are the audience charts and stats on the Creator Media Kit Page template real or animated?
The audience charts and stats on the Creator Media Kit Page template are illustrative sample data, not real analytics. Numeric values count from zero to their final displayed figures when the audience section first enters, horizontal bars scale from the left, and the line chart draws with a stroke animation. Under reduced-motion settings, final numbers and complete charts appear immediately. Screen readers receive the final values right away.

### Can I use the Creator Media Kit Page template for a newsletter writer or educator instead of a video creator?
Yes. The Creator Media Kit Page template is built for independent video makers, newsletter writers, educators, and creative personalities. Set CREATOR_NICHE to your subject and AUDIENCE to your readers or students, then rewrite the channel formats, partnership cases, and rate packages to match your formats. The two live demos show the same layout applied to small-space gardening and seasonal home cooking.
