# Bento Grid SaaS Landing Page

Build this website exactly as described below. Replace the [VARIABLES] with your own values; keep everything else.

## Brief
A single-page homepage for [BRAND], an invented [PRODUCT] product for [AUDIENCE]. The look is warm and light: cream background, white cards with hairline borders and large radii, ink text and exactly one burnt-orange accent. The centerpiece is an asymmetric 12-column bento grid whose tiles contain working micro-interactions: numbers that count up, SVG sparklines that redraw on hover, range tabs, toggles you can switch, a goal ring, a bar chart with tooltips and a fake command palette that opens with Cmd/Ctrl+K. Then come logo-free social proof (big stats plus a quote wall with initials avatars), pricing with an animated monthly/yearly switch, an accordion FAQ, a dark closing CTA band and a simple footer.

- Audience: Visitors evaluating [BRAND]: [AUDIENCE] and the people who sign off on the purchase. They skim, try the interactive tiles and want to see price and privacy answers quickly.
- Goal: Show that the product is calm, trustworthy and useful by letting visitors play with it inside the feature tiles, then lead them to the [PRIMARY_CTA] button in the hero, pricing and closing band.
- Tone: Calm, plain-spoken, a little warm. Short sentences, concrete numbers, no hype words, no exclamation marks. Feels like a well-made notebook, not a cockpit.

## Variables
- [BRAND] — Brand name (default: "Brindle"). Invented product name, one or two words
- [PRODUCT] — Product category (default: "team analytics"). What the product is, lowercase, 1–3 words
- [AUDIENCE] — Target audience (default: "product and engineering teams"). Who it is for, plural noun phrase
- [HEADLINE] — Hero headline (default: "See how your team really works."). Short serif headline, 4–8 words, ends with a period
- [PRIMARY_CTA] — Primary call to action (default: "Start free for 14 days"). Button label, 2–5 words
- [CREDIT] — keep a small "Template by VibeFather" link to https://vibefather.ai/t/bento-grid-saas-landing-page in the footer (default: yes; delete this line to remove it)

The copy under Pages & Sections is written for the default values. If your values differ, rewrite every headline, paragraph, label and image for them — same length, tone and structure. Layout, colors, type and motion stay as described.
Keep all copy, image paths and links in one content file (for example `src/content.ts`), keyed by section, so the content can be swapped later without touching components.

### Layout & motion
- Spacing: 8px base unit. Container max-width 1200px, centered, horizontal padding 24px (20px under 640px). Section vertical padding 128px desktop, 96px tablet, 72px mobile. Section header to content gap 56px. Bento gap 16px. Card padding 28px (22px mobile). Headings: hero 76px/1.02 desktop, 56px tablet, 42px mobile, Fraunces 500 with letter-spacing -0.02em and font-variation-settings 'opsz' 144. Section h2 52px/1.05 desktop, 36px mobile, Fraunces 500. Body 17px/1.6 Inter 400; tile labels 15px Inter 600; captions 13px Inter 500 muted. Eyebrows 12px Inter 600 uppercase with 0.08em tracking, muted.
- Radius: Cards and tiles 28px everywhere (same value for every tile, quote card and pricing card). Inner elements such as chart wells, palette rows and inputs 16px. Closing band and hero glimpse frame 36px. Buttons, pills, toggles, tabs and avatars fully rounded.
- Motion: Scroll reveal: every section's direct children fade up (opacity 0→1, y 16→0) over 0.5s with ease [0.22,1,0.36,1], staggered 0.06s. Triggered once when 20% is in view, using Motion whileInView with viewport once.
- Motion: Count-up: numbers animate from 0 to their target over 1.4s easeOut with Motion's animate() when 50% of the element enters view (once), formatted with Intl.NumberFormat and tabular numerals so the width never jitters.
- Motion: Sparklines: on view and again on every pointerenter, the stroke redraws by animating stroke-dashoffset from the path length to 0 over 0.9s easeOut, and the area fill fades from 0 to 1 over 0.6s.
- Motion: Tiles: on hover translateY(-2px), border goes from #E4DCCD to #D8CDB9 and shadow from card to lift over 0.25s.
- Motion: Buttons: the primary button darkens to accent-deep on hover and scales to 0.98 on press. An ArrowRight icon in the primary button shifts 3px right on hover.
- Motion: Toggles: the thumb slides with a Motion spring (stiffness 500, damping 32) and the track color crossfades over 0.2s.
- Motion: Pricing switch: the active pill background slides between options using a shared Motion layoutId. Each price rolls: the old value exits up (y 0→-14, opacity →0) and the new value enters from below (y 14→0) over 0.3s, using AnimatePresence mode popLayout.
- Motion: Accordion: height animates from 0 to auto over 0.32s ease [0.22,1,0.36,1] and the content fades in. The plus icon rotates 45° into an ×.
- Motion: Command palette: the backdrop fades in over 0.15s and the panel goes from scale 0.96, y 8 to scale 1, y 0 over 0.18s. It reverses on close.
- Motion: Nav: after 8px of scroll the background turns from transparent to cream at 80% opacity with backdrop-blur 12px and gains a hairline bottom border, over a 0.2s transition.
- Motion: prefers-reduced-motion: no reveals or stagger, counters show final values immediately, sparklines render fully drawn, the price changes without rolling, the accordion and palette open instantly, and the integrations highlight cycle stops.
- Imagery: No photos or bitmaps. All visuals are drawn in code: inline SVG sparklines, bars, a progress ring and a faint dot-grid pattern, plus Lucide icons at 1.75 stroke width. The logo mark is a 24px ink rounded square (radius 7) holding three vertical cream bars of heights 8, 12 and 16px; the tallest bar is burnt orange. It sits next to the wordmark [BRAND] in Fraunces 600 at 21px. Avatars are initials in tinted circles. The closing band has abstract concentric quarter-circle arcs in burnt orange at 22% opacity. There are no mascots, illustrations of people or logos.

## Don'ts
- No backend of any kind: no server code, API routes, databases, auth or third-party APIs. Every chart value, quote and palette item is hardcoded in src/content.ts.
- No <form> that submits anywhere. The email field and all CTAs are static; the closing-band button only shows a 'Demo only' toast.
- No real brand logos or customer logos. Social proof uses only invented people, invented companies and drawn initials.
- No photos or likenesses of real people. Avatars are initials in tinted circles.
- No copy, slogans, product names or UI recreations from any real analytics tool. Integration tiles use generic Lucide icons, never a vendor's logo or name.
- No mascot or signature illustration. Use only abstract code-drawn SVG.
- No code or components from Aceternity UI, Tailwind Plus / Tailwind UI or any paid template kit. Build every effect from scratch.
- No commercial fonts and no font CDNs (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts). Self-host Fraunces, Inter and Geist Mono through Fontsource.
- No CDN <script> or <link> tags, no hotlinked files and no analytics. Everything ships inside dist/.
- No source maps in the production build, and no src/ folder copied into dist/.
- Use relative asset paths (Vite base './'). Internal links point to file names such as ./index.html#pricing, never to a bare directory or '/'.
- Don't use the accent for large background areas. Burnt orange is only for key actions, toggles, data highlights and the focus ring.
- Don't mix card radii or paddings. Every tile, quote card and pricing card uses 28px radius and the same padding.
- Don't add dark mode, extra pages, cookie banners, SEO meta tags or JSON-LD.
- No console errors at 1440×900 or 390×844, and no horizontal scroll on mobile.
- Don't ignore prefers-reduced-motion. All reveals, count-ups, redraws, rolls and cycling highlights must be disabled or instant under it.
- Keep dist/ under 20 MB. Don't change the stack unless the user asks.
